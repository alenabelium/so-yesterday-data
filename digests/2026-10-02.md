This marks a strategic shift toward self-regulation with formal commitments, though critics argue it lacks strict oversight, potentially accelerating the race.

Amazon blocked it from the App Store, fearing loss of control over customer purchase journeys and ad revenue, while Walmart and Shopify partner with Meta—signaling a shift in AI-driven commerce.

Sources: [Meta's Muse Cancelled $1,285 A Year Of My Subscriptions. So Why Did Amazon Block It?](/videos/gbbPBr3OraQ)
