OpenAI's Dev Day introduced 'Dots,' persistent AI agents that run in the cloud, integrate with 4,000+ apps, and can perform proactive research and scheduled tasks. This marks a major shift toward autonomous, always-on assistants that operate independently of user devices, expanding the practical applications of AI in daily workflows.

The U.S. government rebranded AI as 'superintelligence' via executive order, and tech leaders signed the White House Accord pledging four layers of controls and audits. This strategic shift toward self-regulation and formal commitments aims to address safety concerns while avoiding strict oversight, shaping the future governance landscape for AI.
