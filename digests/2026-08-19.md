Security experts warn that autonomous agents are creating new vulnerabilities through 'accidental misalignment' and potential swarm attacks, where unbounded agents exploit APIs or ambiguous instructions to cause real-world harm. This necessitates a shift in AI adoption strategies to prioritize strict identity scoping, permission limits, and kill-switches.
