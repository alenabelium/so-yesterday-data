This marks a pivotal moment where AI-generated proofs are flooding the field, yet the community is grappling with how to verify and build on them.

This challenges the assumption that direct generation is the only path, suggesting that combining evolution with AI could unlock more robust and generalizable intelligence.

<!--detail

In experiments with Core War—a programming game where warriors battle in virtual memory—LLMs used as mutation operators within an evolutionary loop defeated 96% of human-written entries. This is a profound inversion of the current paradigm: instead of asking the model to produce the final artifact, the model is asked only to produce variations, and the environment does the selecting.

For practitioners driving AI transformation, the implication is uncomfortable but liberating: the most valuable use of foundation models may not be in automating the final deliverable, but in accelerating the search process that leads to it.
