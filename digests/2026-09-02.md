Architect Labs announced 'Redwood,' the world's first chip designed entirely by AI, which achieved zero bugs on first silicon and 3.4x better performance per watt than Nvidia's Jetson. This marks a pivotal shift toward recursive self-improvement in hardware design, reducing reliance on traditional human-led engineering cycles.

Apple launched a new desktop Mac lineup featuring up to 512GB of unified memory, enabling powerful local inference and agentic workflows without cloud dependency. This signals a strategic bet that users prefer owning their compute infrastructure for privacy and cost control over renting frontier cloud agents.
