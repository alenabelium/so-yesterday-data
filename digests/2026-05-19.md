## 2. Strategic Pivot to Physical Hardware and Robotics

As digital AI capabilities saturate, the industry is accelerating toward physical hardware, robotics, and re-industrialization to address supply chain security and military needs. This shift requires new engineering approaches, emphasizing generalist talent and first-principles design to overcome the high iteration costs and reliability constraints inherent in physical systems.

The economy is transitioning from attention-based to interpretation-based models, where AI agents mediate consumer and hiring decisions.
