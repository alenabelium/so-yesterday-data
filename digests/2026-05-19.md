With digital AI capabilities saturating, the industry is pivoting to robotics, manufacturing, and hardware to address supply chain and energy constraints. Successful AI adoption is shifting from model-centric to workflow-centric strategies, utilizing levers like automate, build, buy, hire, and wait. Leaders are advised to define workflows in plain English and use an investment matrix based on work specificity and market maturity to avoid capital allocation failures.

As AI agents mediate consumer and hiring decisions, the economy is transitioning from attention-based to interpretation-based models. Individuals and businesses must establish a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data to remain legible to AI agents while maintaining distinct human brand loyalty to avoid being averaged out.
