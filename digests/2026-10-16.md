A comparison video and Microsoft's hybrid AI approach with Nvidia show that cloud computing is often cheaper and faster than running models locally. This challenges the assumption that local models are more economical, pushing enterprises to reconsider their AI infrastructure investments.

This is not merely a marginal advantage but a structural one: the cloud's economies of scale in GPU utilization, power efficiency, and dynamic resource allocation often render local deployment a premium luxury rather than a cost-saving measure.
