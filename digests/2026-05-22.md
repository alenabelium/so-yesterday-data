## 5. Shift to 'Data Room' Workflows and Questioning Methodologies for Reliability

Practical AI adoption is moving beyond prompt engineering toward structured 'data room' workflows and 'questioning' mental models to mitigate hallucinations. By having agents first audit and organize source data before generation, and treating AI as a senior partner for strategic inquiry, organizations can ensure reliability in complex knowledge work and legal contexts.
