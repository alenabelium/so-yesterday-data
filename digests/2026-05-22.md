# Daily Digest — May 22, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Financialization of Compute via First Major Exchange Futures

The launch of the first major exchange-listed compute futures on the ICE, backed by the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI), marks the financialization of GPU infrastructure. This allows institutional investors to hedge risks and finance the $7 trillion compute buildout using established commodity derivatives frameworks, fundamentally changing how AI hardware is valued and traded.

Coupled with an 8x increase in capital expenditure to $190 billion and next-gen TPUs, this cements Google's position in the race for multimodal dominance and physical AI integration via Android XR.

## 3. Standardization of the Agent Protocol Stack (MCP, A2A, AGUI)

Developers are converging on a core stack of protocols—MCP for tool access, A2A for cross-agent delegation, and AGUI for human-in-the-loop control—to build reliable agentic systems. This shift moves the industry beyond simple model selection to managing the complex substrate of agent coordination, security, and state management.

## 4. Rise of Infrastructure Control Points in the Agent Economy

Power in the AI agent economy is shifting from model providers to infrastructure layers controlling runtime, identity, data, payments, and observability. Companies like Cloudflare, Okta, Snowflake, Stripe, and Datadog are establishing dominance by providing the necessary governance, security, and operational constraints required for safe enterprise agent deployment.
