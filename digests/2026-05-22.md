# Daily Digest — May 22, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Financialization of Compute via First Major-Exchange GPU Futures

The launch of the first major exchange-listed compute futures on the ICE, backed by the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI), marks the financialization of AI infrastructure. This allows institutional investors to hedge risks and finance the $7 trillion compute buildout using established commodity derivatives frameworks, fundamentally changing how AI hardware is capitalized and traded.

## 2. Establishment of the Core Agent Protocol Stack (MCP, A2A, AGUI)

Developers are converging on a standardized substrate for AI agents, with MCP, A2A, and AGUI emerging as the core layers for tool access, cross-agent delegation, and human-in-the-loop control. This shift moves the industry beyond model selection to protocol architecture, defining how agents discover tools, coordinate tasks, and maintain security in production environments.

Companies controlling these control surfaces (e.g., Cloudflare, Okta, Stripe) are becoming the critical gatekeepers for safe, scalable agent deployment, necessitating new governance frameworks for enterprise adoption.
