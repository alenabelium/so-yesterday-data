# Daily Digest — May 22, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Financialization of Compute via First Major-Exchange GPU Futures

The launch of the first major exchange-listed compute futures on the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) marks a pivotal shift in AI infrastructure finance. By establishing the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI) and listing futures for H100, B200, and other GPUs, institutional capital can now hedge risks and finance the $7 trillion compute buildout using traditional commodity derivatives frameworks.

## 3. Emergence of the Core Agent Protocol Stack (MCP, A2A, AGUI)

The industry is consolidating around a core stack of agent protocols: MCP for tool access, A2A for cross-agent delegation, and AGUI for human-in-the-loop supervision. Understanding these standards is now critical for developers, as they dictate the security, reliability, and customer experience of agentic systems, moving beyond simple model selection to infrastructure governance.

## 4. Infrastructure Control Shifts to Runtime, Identity, and Observability Layers

Strategic power in the AI agent economy is shifting from model providers to infrastructure companies controlling runtime, identity, data, and payments. Firms like Cloudflare, Okta, Snowflake, Stripe, and Datadog are establishing dominance by providing the necessary governance, security, and monitoring layers required to deploy agents safely in enterprise environments.
