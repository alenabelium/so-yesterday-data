# Daily Digest — May 22, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Financialization of Compute via First Major Exchange Futures

The launch of the first major exchange-listed compute futures on the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), backed by the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI), marks a pivotal shift in AI infrastructure finance. This development allows institutional investors to hedge GPU hardware risks using the same regulated derivatives framework as traditional commodities, accelerating the $7 trillion compute buildout by providing essential financial plumbing for hyperscalers and lenders.

## 3. Emergence of the Core Agent Protocol Stack (MCP, A2A, AGUI)

Developers are moving beyond model selection to adopt a standardized substrate for AI agents, with MCP, A2A, and AGUI emerging as the core stack for tool access, cross-agent delegation, and human-in-the-loop control. Understanding these protocols is now critical for enterprise adoption, as they dictate security boundaries, latency, and the ability to manage 'supervision debt' in long-running autonomous workflows.

## 4. Infrastructure Dominance Shifts to Runtime, Identity, and Observability Layers

Strategic control in the AI agent economy is consolidating around infrastructure providers managing runtime, identity, data, payments, and observability, rather than model providers alone. Companies like Cloudflare, Okta, Snowflake, Stripe, and Datadog are establishing dominance by defining the governance and operational constraints necessary for agents to act safely and effectively in production environments.
