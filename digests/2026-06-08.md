This trend emphasizes the importance of token efficiency, local inference, and tool-agnostic frameworks like RAMP for practical AI adoption.

<!--detail

The June 2026 landscape report makes clear that general-purpose agents remain unreliable for marathon engineering tasks, yet specialist systems are now achieving verifiable breakthroughs in math formalization, chemistry, and early cancer detection. For practitioners, the implication is clear: stop waiting for the perfect general agent and start building around specialized workflows. The organizations that win will be those that identify their highest-value, most repeatable tasks — a specific diagnostic, a particular code refactor, a defined chemical synthesis — and wrap them in a structured framework like RAMP, deployed on hardware they control. The compute supply chain tangles between Google, SpaceX, and xAI are a distraction; what matters is that token efficiency and local inference are becoming competitive advantages.
