# Daily Digest — May 17, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Enterprise AI Value Shifts to Implementation and Agentic Workflows

The industry is moving past generic model capabilities to focus on the implementation layer, where custom workflow design, governance, and integration create the true competitive moat. This shift is driven by hyperscalers moving down-stack and private equity funding agentic deployment solutions, signaling that sustainable value lies in executing specific business processes rather than owning the base model.

## 2. New SaaS Pricing Models for Autonomous Agents

Major vendors like Salesforce and Microsoft are introducing a 'second meter' that bills for autonomous agent actions and work units rather than just per-seat licenses. This transition forces procurement teams to negotiate complex usage rights and caps upfront, as costs are now tied to operational outcomes and API access rather than simple token volume.

The release of Mammal, a unified foundation model for chemistry, genetics, and protein data, has demonstrated the ability to predict drug efficacy and antibody binding with higher accuracy than specialized models. Its successful prediction of existing drug repurposing for solid tumors, confirmed by physical experiments, marks a significant leap toward faster, cheaper, and more accurate personalized medicine.
