As AI-generated videos become visually flawless, a new video explains that detection now relies on C2PA digital signatures, invisible watermarks like Google SynthID, and independent verification via reverse image search and official sources. This marks a mindset shift: there is no single 'real' or 'fake' button, but rather a process of gathering evidence that an event actually occurred.

This development highlights how AI and human collaboration can accelerate mathematical progress, though the limit of improvements remains contingent on human choices, raising philosophical questions about the nature of mathematical truth.
