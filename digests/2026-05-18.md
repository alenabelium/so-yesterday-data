## 1. Shift to Agentic Workload Billing and Contractual Gatekeeping

Major SaaS vendors like Salesforce and Microsoft are transitioning from per-seat licensing to billing for 'agentic work units,' charging for autonomous actions rather than just token usage. This shift forces procurement teams to negotiate strict caps and usage definitions upfront, as API restrictions are becoming the primary gatekeeper for agent access and failed actions may now count toward bills.
