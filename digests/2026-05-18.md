# Daily Digest — May 18, 2026

The 5 most important AI developments from recent content.

## 1. Shift to Agentic Work Unit Billing in Enterprise SaaS

Major vendors like Salesforce, Microsoft, and ServiceNow are transitioning from per-seat licensing to billing for autonomous agent actions and work units. This structural change forces procurement teams to negotiate specific caps and clarify whether failed actions incur costs, fundamentally altering the economics of AI adoption for enterprises.

The industry is shifting from single-model chat interfaces to autonomous 'digital employees' like Meta's Muse Spark and self-improving agents like Hermas. These systems utilize parallel agents and persistent memory to perform complex, multi-step execution, marking a significant leap in AI capability and autonomy.
