## 1. Shift to Agentic Billing and 'Second Meter' Pricing Models

Major SaaS vendors like Salesforce, Microsoft, and ServiceNow are transitioning from per-seat licensing to billing for autonomous agent actions and work units. This structural change forces procurement teams to negotiate specific caps and clarify whether failed actions count toward bills, fundamentally altering the cost structure of enterprise AI adoption.
