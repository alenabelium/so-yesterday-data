This development challenges the closed-source monopoly of US giants and forces rivals like OpenAI and Anthropic to navigate new regulatory and competitive landscapes.

This cultural shift recognizes that illuminating and managing shadow AI is more effective for security and productivity than attempting to suppress it.

## 5. Chinese Models Reshape Global AI Economics via MoE and Distillation

Chinese labs are leveraging mixture-of-experts architectures and distillation of US frontier models to offer drastically lower costs and alternative data sovereignty options, challenging US dominance. Organizations are now advised to conduct rigorous, task-specific testing to determine if models like Kimi K3 or DeepSeek offer better value based on cost-per-result and deployment feasibility.
