Alex Wissner-Gross's Chain Theory posits that reality is built from finitely many maximally entangled quantum pairs, deriving a parameter-free value for the fine structure constant that matches CODATA 2022 to 10 significant digits. This challenges string theory and anthropic reasoning, offering a testable, axiom-driven alternative that could reshape fundamental physics.

The derived inverse value of 137.035999189566 aligns with the CODATA 2022 measurement to ten significant digits, sitting just 0.60 standard deviations away—a precision that, if robust, would be unprecedented in theoretical physics.
