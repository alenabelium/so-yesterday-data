## 1. Anthropic's IPO triggers $1.3B crypto liquidation, exposing AI-crypto capital rotation

Bitcoin fell 18% amid Anthropic's IPO, but analysis shows the crash was driven by forced liquidations of leveraged longs, not direct selling to buy shares. The event highlights how major AI capital events can destabilize crypto markets, and the real test is whether ETF outflows persist post-listing.
