## 4. Conditional proof of Goldbach conjecture emerges — with possible AI fingerprints

A new paper claims a conditional proof of the binary Goldbach conjecture assuming the generalized Riemann hypothesis plus an additional spectral hypothesis, using a novel operator-based approach. The presenter suspects AI involvement due to unusual phrasing and a logical redundancy in the abstract, highlighting how AI is increasingly influencing mathematical research — both in generating results and in the need for scrutiny.

<!--detail
A new preprint by Jamine Lee and Jenny Leo claims a conditional proof of the binary Goldbach conjecture, a 300-year-old problem stating every even integer greater than 2 is the sum of two primes. The proof departs fundamentally from the classical circle and sieve methods that have dominated attempts for a century, instead leveraging Dirichlet characters and a self-adjoint operator to control the error term in the weighted Goldbach asymptotic formula. The result is conditional on the generalized Riemann hypothesis (GRH) plus one of two additional spectral hypotheses, making it a significant but heavily contingent advance.

The presenter does not claim the mathematics is wrong, only that its provenance is questionable, which itself is a novel kind of scrutiny for the field.

For practitioners driving AI transformation in organizations, this development is a double-edged signal.
