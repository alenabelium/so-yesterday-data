## 2. Industry Pivot to 'Harness Engineering' and Context Layers

With raw model intelligence plateauing and frontier releases constrained, competitive advantage is shifting to 'harness engineering'—the orchestration of models, memory, and tool calls. Providers like Anthropic (Claude Tag) and Apple (Siri) are locking in users by integrating AI deeply into existing workflows and private data contexts, making the 'last mile' integration and context management the primary bottleneck for adoption rather than model capability.
