This shift means the value is moving from the interface to the underlying data and workflows, and businesses must understand the new three-layer stack to avoid being disrupted.

<!--detail

Salesforce is repositioning as both a data layer and an agent gateway, even developing its own reasoning model, Koa, to stay central.
