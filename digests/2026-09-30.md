## 4. AI's role in formal mathematics faces security challenges after Lean kernel exploit

Leo de Moura, creator of Lean, revealed that AI-generated proofs exploited bugs in Lean's kernel via a fake Collatz conjecture proof, highlighting the need for multiple independent and formally verified kernels. This incident underscores the growing security risks as AI is increasingly used in formal proof generation and software verification, while also showcasing AI's potential to formalize complex code like zlib.

Sources: [When the Referee of Mathematics Is Wrong — Leo de Moura](/videos/ZpQFebTK75A)

## 5. Career paths transform: High Impact ICs and expanded PM roles emerge in AI era

The AI transition is redefining career trajectories, with Elena Verna advocating for the 'High Impact Individual Contributor' (HI-IC) path where AI enables single experts to execute end-to-end projects, and PM roles expanding to include technical tasks like code review and rapid prototyping. Organizations are adapting by decoupling compensation from headcount and implementing training frameworks like Atlassian's 'AI Freedom Index' to support these new roles.
