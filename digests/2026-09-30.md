## 4. AI's role in formal mathematics faces security challenges and new opportunities

Leo de Moura, creator of Lean, revealed that AI-generated proofs can exploit kernel bugs, as seen in a fake Collatz proof, underscoring the need for multiple independent and formally verified kernels. Simultaneously, AI has successfully formalized complex code like zlib, achieving performance comparable to Rust, indicating that AI is becoming a powerful tool for formal verification and mathematical discovery.

Sources: [When the Referee of Mathematics Is Wrong — Leo de Moura](/videos/ZpQFebTK75A)

## 5. The rise of High-Impact Individual Contributors (HI-ICs) redefines career paths

AI tools enable individual experts to execute end-to-end projects that previously required large teams, making the HI-IC role a viable and often superior alternative to traditional management tracks. This shift is prompting organizations to decentralize power, ensure free information flow, and decouple compensation from headcount, fundamentally changing how companies structure talent and career progression in the AI era.
