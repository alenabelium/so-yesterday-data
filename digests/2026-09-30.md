## 4. OpenAI abandons long-term planning for 90-day product cycles

OpenAI product leaders advocate for a 90-day development horizon, shipping imperfect products quickly to gather real-world feedback and adapt to rapidly evolving model capabilities. This approach challenges traditional multi-year roadmaps, emphasizing direct user accessibility and agentic design that aligns with human cognitive limits—a blueprint for product teams navigating the AI era.

## 5. AI-driven proof exploits expose vulnerabilities in formal verification systems

Leo de Moura, creator of Lean, reveals that AI-generated proofs exploited bugs in Lean's kernel and external kernels via a fake Collatz conjecture proof, highlighting the need for multiple independent, formally verified kernels. This incident underscores the growing risk of AI-generated attacks on mathematical and software verification, prompting a push for smaller trusted cores and verified compilers to maintain integrity in AI-assisted development.
