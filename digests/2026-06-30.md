Companies like Anthropic (Claude Tag) and Apple are building 'sticky' harnesses that integrate AI deeply into organizational workflows and private data, making it difficult for users to switch to cheaper or superior open models due to context lock-in.

The industry is seeing rapid hardware advancements to support AI scaling, including OpenAI's partnership with Broadcom for custom 'Jalapeno' AI processors and IBM's demonstration of sub-1nm chips using 3D stacking.
