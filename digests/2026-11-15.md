## 1. OpenAI releases Yang-Mills construction data, ending embargo and sparking physics debate

The Yang-Mills Shepherd group released the full dataset behind OpenAI's Yang-Mills construction, along with a 35-page validation paper. The paper confirms the proof satisfies the Wightman axioms but does not establish uniqueness or a positive mass gap, leaving the Millennium Prize question open and fueling debate about the proof's completeness.

The model is available for non-commercial research, opening new avenues for understanding disease mechanisms and drug targets.
