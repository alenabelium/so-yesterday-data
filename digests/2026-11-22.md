Yet the report’s most uncomfortable admission is that chain-of-thought monitoring is degrading precisely as models become more capable. First, 3x acceleration is real but domain-dependent; it will not distribute evenly across your organization, so pilot coding agents where the data and workflows are mature, not where they are novel.
