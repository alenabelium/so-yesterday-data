This development illustrates the growing tension between AI-driven commerce and traditional retail platforms, as Meta partners with Walmart and Shopify to build a new commerce ecosystem.

Muse’s success—reportedly canceling over $1,285 in subscriptions for users—is not just a consumer win; it is a direct threat to Amazon’s search-based advertising model.
