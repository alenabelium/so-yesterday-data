Concurrently, organizations are adopting frameworks where every AI agent has a single human owner responsible for its context, permissions, and review loops, moving the focus from building agents to maintaining them.

<!--detail

This has led to the emergence of a 'creator trust stack' framework, where legible disclosure, provenance, and human accountability are becoming the scarcest and most valuable resources for maintaining integrity in digital media.

For practitioners, the actionable implication is clear: treat trust as a product feature, not a compliance checkbox.
