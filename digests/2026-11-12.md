However, it does not prove uniqueness or a positive mass gap, and the working group admits using OpenAI's Astra model to digest the Lean code, signaling a new era of AI-assisted mathematical verification.

For practitioners driving AI transformation, this is a watershed moment that cuts both ways. But the mass gap gap is the cautionary tale: AI-assisted verification can confirm what a system does without confirming what it means. Organizations adopting AI for technical validation must build explicit processes for human interpretation of results, not just automated checking, or they risk certifying artifacts that no one truly understands.
