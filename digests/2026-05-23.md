## 4. Shift to 'Questioning' and Data Room Workflows

Industry best practices are evolving from static prompting to dynamic 'questioning' methodologies, treating AI as a senior partner for complex reasoning. Concurrently, professionals are adopting 'data room' workflows where agents audit and organize source materials before generation, a critical step to eliminate hallucinations and ensure reliability in high-stakes knowledge work.

## 5. Infrastructure Control Points for Agent Deployment

The focus of AI enterprise adoption is shifting from model capability to infrastructure governance, specifically runtime, identity, data, payments, and observability. Companies like Cloudflare, Okta, and Stripe are establishing dominance by providing the necessary control surfaces and safety mechanisms required to deploy autonomous agents securely in production environments.
