Practical adoption strategies are evolving beyond prompt engineering. Experts advocate for a 'questioning' mental model that treats AI as a senior partner for complex reasoning, and 'data room' workflows where agents audit and organize source data before generating content. These methods address hallucination risks and enable reliable integration of AI into high-stakes professional workflows.
