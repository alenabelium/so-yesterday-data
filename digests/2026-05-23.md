## 4. New Methodologies for Reliable AI: Questioning and Data Rooms

Practical adoption is shifting from prompt engineering to 'questioning' frameworks that treat AI as a senior partner, and implementing 'data room' workflows to audit sources before generation. These methods address hallucination risks by ensuring agents operate on verified, organized data rather than messy inputs, critical for professional knowledge work.
