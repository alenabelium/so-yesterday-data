# The Generation That Grew Up With Algorithms Just Called Bullshit on AI

## Executive Summary

The video argues that Gen Z's sabotage of corporate AI strategies is not technological resistance but a rational response to a power grab disguised as innovation. Executives are deploying performative AI strategies to protect their own jobs, despite admitting these tools have negligible productivity impact, while simultaneously creating a two-tier workforce that concentrates power and wealth. Gen Z, having witnessed the exploitative nature of previous algorithmic platforms, recognizes this pattern and uses sabotage as a form of labor resistance against a system that threatens their autonomy and security.

## Key Points

- Executives admit that 90% of AI deployments have no measurable impact on productivity, yet 75% of strategies are performative, driven by leadership's fear of being replaced rather than genuine business value [00:04:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yMzHGzLyqgE&t=276).
- Gen Z workers are actively sabotaging AI adoption not out of ignorance, but because they recognize the poor strategy and the concentration of power, with 44% admitting to actions like feeding proprietary data to public bots or producing low-quality outputs [00:06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yMzHGzLyqgE&t=403).
- A deliberate 'AI elite' is being cultivated by 92% of executives, creating a class divide where super-users gain promotions and pay raises while resisting workers face layoffs, mirroring the exploitative dynamics of previous tech platforms [00:09:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yMzHGzLyqgE&t=572).
