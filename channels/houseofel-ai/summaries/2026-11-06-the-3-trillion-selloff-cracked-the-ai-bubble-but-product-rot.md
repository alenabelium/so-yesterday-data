# The $3 Trillion Selloff Cracked the AI Bubble, but Product Rot Will Kill It

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the AI industry's trillion-dollar valuations are built on unreliable products, citing evidence of hidden changes, degraded performance, and lack of transparency from companies like Anthropic and OpenAI. It highlights how infrastructure tweaks, not model updates, can silently alter behavior, creating risks for developers and businesses. The creator suggests that the chaotic public discourse around AI distracts from consumer protection issues, and calls for regulation and transparency to prevent market degradation.

- Users report degraded performance and hidden changes in AI products like Claude and ChatGPT, with evidence from studies and user complaints. [00:01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZgyuBbMy7c&t=72)
