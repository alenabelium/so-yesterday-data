# Big Tech Tried To Save Money With AI. It Costs MORE.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that major tech companies are facing a severe AI cost crisis due to the Jevans paradox, where cheaper compute units lead to exponentially higher consumption without proportional value. It highlights specific examples like Uber's budget burnout, Meta's gamified token consumption leaderboards, and Microsoft's strategic pivot to in-house tools to control costs. The speaker contends that current deployment strategies focus on measuring input rather than output, creating unsustainable spending habits driven by fear of missing out rather than proven returns. To justify the massive capital expenditure, the industry should shift focus from internal software engineering metrics to empowering external researchers in fields like medicine and climate science.

## Key Points

- Uber's AI budget was exhausted in four months because internal leaderboards incentivized engineers to maximize token consumption rather than deliver value [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuhAaMSXD9A&t=0).
- Meta employees engaged in 'claonomics' by competing on token usage leaderboards, with top users consuming billions of tokens daily, some even running idle agents to inflate their rankings [00:04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuhAaMSXD9A&t=269).
- Microsoft canceled its use of competitor Anthropic's Cloud Code to unify its platform and control billing, revealing that cost management often overrides engineering preference [00:09:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuhAaMSXD9A&t=590).
- Nvidia's CEO and VP present contradictory views on AI costs, reflecting the conflict between hardware suppliers who profit from consumption and engineering leaders managing fixed budgets [00:11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuhAaMSXD9A&t=694).
- The speaker proposes that sustainable AI demand should come from subsidizing frontier research in chemistry, medicine, and physics, rather than forcing software engineers to consume more tokens [00:19:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuhAaMSXD9A&t=1146).
