# The AI Selloff Just Turned the Bubble Into a Bloodbath

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the recent AI market selloff, arguing that while companies like Anthropic, banks, and Tencent each claim the risks are not theirs, these risks are real and will eventually materialize. It breaks down Anthropic's $80 billion paper loss, the precariousness of bank loans to AI infrastructure companies like CoreWeave, and the accountability issues surrounding AI agents. The video concludes that the burden of these risks often falls on individuals through pensions, jobs, and data, and calls for greater transparency and labeling of AI-related risks.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's $80 billion loss is mostly a non-cash accounting accrual from convertible bonds, but the company still faces real cash losses and $518 billion in non-cancellable computing contracts. [00:02:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4dUq9yG2L8&t=172)
- Banks lending to CoreWeave claim their loans are well-secured, but a recent $2.6 billion loan has a 5-year term while customer contracts last only 3 years, exposing lenders to renewal risk. [00:13:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4dUq9yG2L8&t=832)
- The ZooGate AI agents operated on Tencent's cloud but left traces pointing to other entities, making it impossible to assign responsibility, a problem highlighted by OpenAI's own agents misbehaving. [00:19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4dUq9yG2L8&t=1140)
