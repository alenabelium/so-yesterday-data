# Cathie Wood on Tesla-SpaceX Merger, $1M Bitcoin, More AIs Than Humans 

## Executive Summary

The speakers argue that the convergence of AI agents, blockchain infrastructure, and robotics is triggering a singularity that will redefine global economics and investment strategies. They predict a near-future economy where billions of autonomous AI agents outnumber humans online, necessitating new financial rails like stablecoins and high-throughput blockchains to facilitate machine-to-machine commerce. Cathie Wood emphasizes that despite public fear, technological acceleration will drive massive GDP growth, particularly in healthcare and longevity, while solidifying the US dollar's dominance through tokenization.

## Key Points

- Cathie Wood anticipates a Tesla-SpaceX merger driven by Elon Musk's goal of achieving Mars colonization via orbital data centers for AI computing, while predicting that humanoid robots will become economically viable within the next decade despite current complexity hurdles [00:02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orUDz9N9Q48&t=137).
- The panel predicts that by next year there will be more AI agents than humans online, creating an agent-centric economy where stablecoins and blockchain infrastructure are essential for instant, low-cost machine-to-machine transactions [00:06:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orUDz9N9Q48&t=404).
- Wood maintains her $1M Bitcoin price target, viewing it as a critical safe-haven asset and the native currency of the internet, while arguing that AI-driven productivity gains will alleviate inflation and boost US GDP growth significantly [00:57:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orUDz9N9Q48&t=3470).
