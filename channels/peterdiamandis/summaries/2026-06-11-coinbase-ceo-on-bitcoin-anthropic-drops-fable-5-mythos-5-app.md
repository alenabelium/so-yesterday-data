# Coinbase CEO on Bitcoin, Anthropic Drops Fable 5 & Mythos 5, Apple Hands Siri to Gemini 

## Executive Summary

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong discusses Bitcoin's role as digital gold, the emerging agentic economy where AI agents transact autonomously via crypto wallets, and the potential for government stakes in AI companies. The episode also covers Anthropic's release of superior Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models, Apple's strategic shift to use Google's Gemini for Siri, and SpaceX's ambitious plans for orbital AI data centers. Finally, the hosts explore breakthroughs in longevity science, specifically epigenetic reprogramming and gene editing, signaling a rapid approach to longevity escape velocity.

- Brian Armstrong explains that Bitcoin is transitioning into 'digital gold' and that Coinbase is building financial rails for an agentic economy where AI agents autonomously transact using stablecoins and crypto wallets, potentially bypassing traditional banking infrastructure [00:04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=isd2y37j8v4&t=265).
