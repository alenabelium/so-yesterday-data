# Thanksgiving Special: The Moments That Almost Ended Us (And Why We’re Grateful) 

## Executive Summary

In this Thanksgiving special, Peter Diamandis and his co-hosts share personal stories of near-failures and pivotal moments that shaped their lives, emphasizing gratitude and resilience. They discuss how challenges, rejections, and unlikely coincidences led to their greatest successes, from stolen pig embryos to founding major companies. The conversation also touches on the exponential future, the importance of overcoming adversity, and the unique perspective of those who understand the coming technological transformation.

## Key Points

- Peter recounts stealing a pig embryo from a lab, nearly getting expelled, and being forgiven by Dr. Price, a moment that changed his life's trajectory. [00:04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcC1VjJ4jU&t=265)
- Dave shares how a near-firing from his first company, due to Amazon's lawsuit with Walmart, turned into a billion-dollar liquidity event and taught him resilience. [00:11:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcC1VjJ4jU&t=686)
- Salim explains how his startup's collapse led to unexpected opportunities, including meeting Peter at Singularity University, highlighting the role of coincidence and gratitude. [00:14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcC1VjJ4jU&t=878)
- Alex describes how being rejected from an MIT summer program redirected him from AI to nanotechnology, shaping his interdisciplinary career. [00:37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcC1VjJ4jU&t=2251)
- Peter shares the story of founding the X Prize, facing 150 rejections before finding Anousheh Ansari as the perfect sponsor, leading to a lasting partnership. [00:43:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcC1VjJ4jU&t=2626)
