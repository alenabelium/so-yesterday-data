# Steve Jurvetson on What Drives Elon, the SpaceX Bet VCs Rejected, & Voyager 1 Hits 1 Light-Day

## Executive Summary

Steve Jurvetson discusses his early investments in SpaceX and Tesla, revealing how Elon Musk's risk tolerance and mission-driven leadership overcame VC skepticism. The conversation covers the Anthropic IPO, the AI compute race, SpaceX's massive compute buildout, and the future of orbital data centers and Starlink. Jurvetson emphasizes the importance of investing in unprecedented ideas and the accelerating pace of AI progress.

## Key Points

- Jurvetson recounts how his partnership initially voted down investing in SpaceX, and how adding deal structure like anti-dilution clauses turned two partners from no to yes. [00:05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_7FICfOHTV8&t=301)
- Jurvetson explains his investment guideline: invest in things unlike anything seen before yet adjacent to past experience, which led him to leave internet investing for deep tech. [00:10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_7FICfOHTV8&t=647)
- Jurvetson discusses Elon Musk's mission-driven leadership, noting that profits are not the priority and that the mission attracts the best talent. [01:44:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_7FICfOHTV8&t=6299)
