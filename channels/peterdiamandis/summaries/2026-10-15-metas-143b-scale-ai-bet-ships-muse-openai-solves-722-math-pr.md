# Meta’s $14.3B Scale AI Bet Ships Muse, OpenAI Solves 722 Math Problems, & Anthropic Bans AI Cruelty

## Executive Summary

This episode of Moonshots covers major AI developments: Anthropic's ban on cruelty toward AI models, Meta's release of Muse Wonder after a $14.3B investment, and OpenAI's mass publication of 722 mathematical solutions. The hosts debate the ethical treatment of AI, the future of mathematics and biology in the age of superintelligence, and the strategic implications for Meta and the broader AI industry.

## Key Points

- Anthropic updated its usage policy to ban prolonged, unnecessary cruelty toward its models, citing uncertainty about Claude's moral status, while Elon Musk echoed concerns about AI suffering during reinforcement learning. [00:03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5YVqEpbgaU&t=213)
- Meta released Muse Wonder, its largest model, after a $14.3B investment in Scale AI, aiming to create a personal superintelligence with active memory and real-world task execution. [00:25:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5YVqEpbgaU&t=1518)
- OpenAI published 722 mathematical solutions in one day, representing 81% of the most important math results in the last three years, signaling a shift from artisan mathematics to mass problem-solving. [00:40:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5YVqEpbgaU&t=2448)
