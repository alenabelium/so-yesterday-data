# We Gave $2M To The AI Startup That Could Help The Most People 

## Executive Summary

Peter Diamandis hosts the Build with Gemini X Prize, an initiative designed to demonstrate that individuals can rapidly build profitable AI-driven businesses to solve significant societal problems. The event features a panel of distinguished judges who discuss the shifting social contract in the age of AI, emphasizing entrepreneurship over traditional employment and highlighting opportunities in healthcare, data ecosystems, and social media reform. Five finalist startups are recognized for their innovative use of AI in education, business formation, labor markets, 3D design, and tax automation, with cash prizes awarded to each team.

## Key Points

- The new social contract encourages individuals to find their purpose by solving meaningful problems using AI, rather than relying on traditional educational and career paths [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOhUSIaa2MA&t=0).
- Judges Palmer Lucky, Katie Wood, Logan Kilpatrick, and Mark Pincus discuss how AI is reducing risks in drug development, creating data-driven startup opportunities, and necessitating a reinvention of toxic social media experiences [00:05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOhUSIaa2MA&t=340).
- Five finalist companies—Dodo Prep, Launch Bridge, MyFixem, Polyform, and Tichen Sierra—are awarded cash prizes ranging from $100,000 to $500,000 for their AI solutions in education, entrepreneurship, labor, design, and finance [00:16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOhUSIaa2MA&t=969).
