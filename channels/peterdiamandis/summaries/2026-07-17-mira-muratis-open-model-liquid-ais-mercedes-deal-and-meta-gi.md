# Mira Murati's Open Model, Liquid AI's Mercedes Deal, and Meta Gives 3.5B People a Doctor 

## Executive Summary

The episode analyzes the shifting landscape of AI regulation, arguing that incumbent-led frameworks risk regulatory capture and stifling open innovation, while highlighting Mira Murati's new open-weight model as a strategic counter-movement. It explores Liquid AI's neuromorphic small language models deployed in automotive settings, demonstrating the viability of efficient, on-device AI. Finally, the hosts discuss breakthroughs in recursive self-improvement, AI avatars for civic engagement, and a scientific advance in reversing biological aging.

## Key Points

- CEOs like Sam Altman and Demis Hassabis are pushing for US-led AI regulation, but the panel warns this creates barriers for entry-level labs and risks regulatory capture by incumbents [00:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=335).
- Mira Murati launched Inkling, an open-weight model betting on customization and fine-tuning over leaderboard dominance, signaling a shift toward enterprise-owned AI infrastructure [00:26:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=1579).
- Liquid AI demonstrates the power of small language models through a Mercedes-Benz partnership, enabling private, offline, and energy-efficient intelligence directly on car hardware [00:22:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=1370).
- A startup claims evidence of recursive self-improvement, though experts note true architectural evolution remains computationally intractable compared to simple code optimization [00:44:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=2660).
- Malaysia's adoption of an AI digital twin for its Prime Minister illustrates the future of bidirectional civic engagement and the potential for organizational digital twins [01:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=3821).
- Palmer Luckey proposes expanding classified patents to protect national security, but the panel argues this undermines open innovation and that enforcement, not secrecy, is the real issue [01:40:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=4803).
- Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 provides free, high-accuracy medical diagnostics to 3.5 billion users, while new research demonstrates the enzymatic reversal of aging-related protein damage [01:49:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAoXVyibE6Q&t=5356)
