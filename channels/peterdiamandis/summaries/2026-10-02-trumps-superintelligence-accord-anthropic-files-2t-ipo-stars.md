# Trump’s Superintelligence Accord, Anthropic Files $2T IPO, Starship Reaches Orbit 

## Executive Summary

This episode of Moonshots covers seven major AI and space stories from a 48-hour window, including the White House Superintelligence Accord signed by nearly 20 tech leaders, Anthropic's $2 trillion IPO filing, OpenAI's Dev Day announcements, AMD's acquisition of World Labs, and Starship's first orbital flight. The hosts analyze the political dynamics behind the accord, debate the implications of Anthropic's massive compute commitments and safety disclosures, and discuss the broader themes of AI abundance, alignment, and moral status. The conversation balances optimism about exponential technological progress with skepticism about regulatory theater and the concentration of power.

- The White House Superintelligence Accord, reportedly drafted by Mark Zuckerberg, establishes self-policing controls and audits but is seen as 'morally binding' theater that preempts regulation rather than enforcing safety. [00:10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=619)
