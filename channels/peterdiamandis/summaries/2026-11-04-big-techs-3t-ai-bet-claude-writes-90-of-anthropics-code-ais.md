# Big Tech’s $3T AI Bet, Claude Writes 90% of Anthropic’s Code, AIs Editing AI Brains 

## Executive Summary

Peter Diamandis and his co-hosts discuss the massive $3 trillion AI infrastructure investments by Big Tech, the rapid progress of AI benchmarks like GDPX and LifeVal, and the emergence of recursive self-improvement where AI models write and edit code, including Anthropic's Claude writing 90% of its code. They also explore the implications of AI on elections, the shift toward thin clients and centralized computing, and the potential for AI to edit neural network weights directly, which could accelerate AI development. The conversation touches on cybersecurity risks, space exploration, and the broader societal impact of AI, emphasizing optimism about the singularity while acknowledging challenges.

## Key Points

- Big Tech's $3 trillion AI investment is unprecedented, with Alphabet alone committing over $1 trillion, and the scale is comparable to 10 Apollo programs, but there are concerns about financial sustainability and potential bubbles. [00:18:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x09f_BuJsoY&t=1111)
- Anthropic's Claude now writes over 90% of the company's code, and internal AI usage has increased 250 times in nine months, signaling a shift toward recursive self-improvement. [00:49:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x09f_BuJsoY&t=2990)
- MIT CSAIL researchers demonstrated that LLMs can edit neural network weights directly, improving a model's math score from 7.9% to 48.1% by altering just 0.016% of weights, opening a path to AI self-improvement. [01:02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x09f_BuJsoY&t=3720)
