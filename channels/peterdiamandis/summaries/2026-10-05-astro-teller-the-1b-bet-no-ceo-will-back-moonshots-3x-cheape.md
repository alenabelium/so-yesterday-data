# Astro Teller: The $1B Bet No CEO Will Back, Moonshots 3x Cheaper in 16 yrs, and Clean Water at 1¢/L

## Executive Summary

Astro Teller, co-founder of X (Alphabet's moonshot factory), discusses the principles behind launching ambitious 'moonshot' projects, emphasizing the need for a huge global problem, a science-fiction-like solution, and a breakthrough technology. He shares insights on the importance of courage and humility, the low success rate (about 2%), and the decreasing cost of moonshots over time. Teller also highlights the role of AI in accelerating innovation and the critical need for a supportive organizational culture that tolerates failure and encourages radical thinking.

## Key Points

- Moonshot projects require a huge global problem, a science-fiction solution, and a breakthrough technology, plus a culture of courage and humility. [00:01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FO8VXvS8aw4&t=66)
- X takes on 100-200 ideas a year, but only about 2% become successful moonshots, emphasizing the importance of failing fast and cheaply. [00:05:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FO8VXvS8aw4&t=317)
- The cost of achieving results at X has decreased about three times over 16 years, partly due to AI and better processes. [00:20:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FO8VXvS8aw4&t=1246)
