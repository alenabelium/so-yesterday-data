# White House Director of Science: Genesis Mission & Golden Age of Science (Michael Kratsios) 

## Executive Summary

Michael Kratsios, White House Director of Science, outlines a transformative vision for American science and technology, centered on the 'Genesis Mission' and the 'Golden Age of Science' report. He argues for a fundamental shift in how the government approaches scientific discovery, advocating for AI-native processes, deregulation, and bold national missions to accelerate progress in AI, quantum computing, and space exploration. Kratsios emphasizes the need to overcome bureaucratic inertia, improve public perception of AI, and leverage private sector innovation to maintain US global leadership.

## Key Points

- Kratsios proposes the 'Genesis Mission' to double scientific productivity by applying AI across all government agencies, aiming for a 10x acceleration in discovery rates rather than the initially stated 2x target [01:05:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHv-vNM_XtU&t=3914).
- The 'Golden Age of Science' report recommends radical reforms such as long-duration grants, fast-track reviews, and 'golden tickets' to fund high-risk, unconventional ideas that traditional peer review often rejects [00:40:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHv-vNM_XtU&t=2403).
- To counter the negative narrative surrounding AI, the administration focuses on tangible benefits in healthcare and job creation, while promoting 'innovation zones' where states can test technologies like drones and EVs with relaxed regulations [00:54:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHv-vNM_XtU&t=3266).
- The US strategy involves leading both open and closed-source AI ecosystems and exporting a turnkey American AI stack globally to counter Chinese influence, while maintaining strict export controls on advanced semiconductors [00:20:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHv-vNM_XtU&t=1231).
- Kratsios envisions a future of autonomous, AI-driven laboratories that operate at machine speed, democratizing access to scientific infrastructure and enabling rapid hypothesis testing without human bottlenecks [01:01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iHv-vNM_XtU&t=3675).
