# We Paid $3.5 Million for Sci-Fi That Isn't Dystopian

## Executive Summary

Peter Diamandis announces the winners of the $3.5 million Future Vision X Prize, a competition to create sci-fi films that portray a positive, techno-optimistic future rather than dystopian narratives. The five finalist trailers—'Nobody Dies on Mars,' 'Senti,' 'Slingshot,' 'The Thorne Protocol,' and 'The Gifted'—each explore themes of human-AI collaboration, collective problem-solving, and hope. The winning film, 'The Gifted,' reframes the antagonist as fear of AI rather than AI itself, emphasizing the power of positive storytelling. Diamandis argues that the media we consume trains our minds like neural networks, so we must feed ourselves optimistic visions to shape a better future. He also announces a crowdfunding opportunity for fans to invest in the winning film and plans to hold the competition again in 2027.
