# Why We're Living in a Biological Singularity With Ben Lamm 

## Executive Summary

Ben Lamm, founder of Colossal Biosciences, discusses the convergence of AI and synthetic biology, arguing that de-extinction and genetic engineering are advancing exponentially and will lead to a 'biological singularity' within a decade. He emphasizes the importance of surrounding oneself with experts, the value of unique datasets over models, and the need for global biorepositories to preserve biodiversity. Lamm also addresses ethical considerations, the potential for artificial wombs, and the role of AI in unifying biological research.

## Key Points

- Ben Lamm explains that his lack of synthetic biology expertise was an advantage, allowing him to ask fundamental questions and attract top talent like George Church. [00:01:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHg3oX1ytYQ&t=114)
- Lamm predicts that AI will become a critical tool for unifying biological terminology and bridging gaps in research, with applications in experimental design and literature analysis over the next 3-5 years. [00:07:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHg3oX1ytYQ&t=428)
- Colossal is working on artificial wombs for mammals, aiming for full extrauterine birth within 24 months, and has already achieved over 300 gene edits with high efficiency. [00:22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHg3oX1ytYQ&t=1324)
- Lamm emphasizes the importance of global biorepositories and open-access technology for conservation, citing partnerships with governments and organizations to preserve biodiversity. [00:36:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHg3oX1ytYQ&t=2211)
- He advises aspiring innovators to embrace curiosity, admit what they don't know, and ask for help, as people are generally willing to assist. [00:40:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VHg3oX1ytYQ&t=2431)
