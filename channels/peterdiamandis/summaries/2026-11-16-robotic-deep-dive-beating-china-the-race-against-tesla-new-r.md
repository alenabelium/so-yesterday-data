# Robotic Deep-Dive: Beating China, the Race Against Tesla, & New Robotics Timeline With Brett Adcock

## Executive Summary

Brett Adcock, founder of Figure, discusses the company's progress in humanoid robotics, emphasizing a four-stage roadmap from hardware to scaling intelligence and robots. He critiques Chinese robotics as focused on dancing and teleoperation, not practical general-purpose work, and positions Figure as leading in zero-shot, long-horizon tasks. Adcock outlines plans for Figure 4, aiming for an 'iPhone moment' with human-level dexterity, and projects home robots by 2027-2028, with a vision of 20 billion humanoids by 2040. He also addresses safety, vertical integration, compute scaling, and the ethical need for robots to prevent a dystopian future where AI bosses humans.

## Key Points

- Figure has over 1,000 robots on campus, more than humans, and is testing in 20-100 new homes, aiming for zero-shot, long-horizon tasks within six months. [00:06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E864U582FVY&t=364)
- Adcock dismisses Chinese robotics as 'dancing' and teleoperated, not practical for general robotics, and sees Tesla as not a current threat. [00:16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E864U582FVY&t=1007)
- Figure 4 will be a major step up, with hands nearly indistinguishable from human hands, and is designed for lights-out manufacturing. [00:35:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E864U582FVY&t=2113)
- Home robots will launch only when they are 'Apple-style quality,' with a lease price of $300-500/month, and Adcock expects to see the full picture by end of 2027. [00:05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E864U582FVY&t=312)
- Adcock argues robotics is necessary to avoid a dystopian future where AI bosses humans, and sees humanoids as the solution to scale work ethically. [01:17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E864U582FVY&t=4656)
