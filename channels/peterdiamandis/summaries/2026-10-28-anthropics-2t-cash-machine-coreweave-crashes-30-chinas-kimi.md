# Anthropic’s $2T Cash Machine, CoreWeave Crashes 30%, & China’s Kimi Catches Up 

## Executive Summary

The podcast discusses the AI economy's rapid growth and its risks, highlighting Anthropic's record-breaking revenue and IPO plans, the neocloud debt crisis exemplified by CoreWeave's crash, and China's Kimi model narrowing the gap with US frontier models. The hosts debate whether the AI buildout is a bubble, the implications of Anthropic's governance structure, and the accelerating pace of AI capabilities, including new physics theories and AI-driven scientific discoveries. They also touch on geopolitical issues like European protests and the need for sovereign AI strategies.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's revenue surged to $16.3B in H1 2026, and its IPO could be the largest in history, but concerns remain about compute commitments and governance structure. [00:13:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXELsIk4o0g&t=787)
- CoreWeave and other neoclouds crashed due to massive debt and overbuilding, drawing parallels to the dot-com fiber bust, but the technology itself is still winning. [00:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXELsIk4o0g&t=966)
- China's Kimi K3.1 Pro is now only about five weeks behind US frontier models, with open weights planned, shrinking the open-closed AI gap and increasing competitive pressure. [00:55:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXELsIk4o0g&t=3352)
