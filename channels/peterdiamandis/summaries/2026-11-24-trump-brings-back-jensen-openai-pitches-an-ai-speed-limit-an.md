# Trump Brings Back Jensen, OpenAI Pitches an AI Speed Limit, and AI Passes the Mirror Test 

## Executive Summary

The episode covers the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia CEO after an 11-day ouster, framed as a quasi-nationalization influenced by President Trump and Elon Musk. It also analyzes OpenAI's release of its perplexity chart as a potential move to establish an AI 'speed limit' and form a safety cartel, while discussing the implications of AI solving the Riemann hypothesis and passing the mirror test. The hosts debate the balance between national security, corporate power, and the accelerating singularity.

- Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO after an 11-day ouster, with the board citing government input and a unanimous vote; the stock jumped $1.09 trillion, and the hosts debate whether this represents a 'quasi-nationalization' of Nvidia. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CDN5X5z4gLw&t=0)
