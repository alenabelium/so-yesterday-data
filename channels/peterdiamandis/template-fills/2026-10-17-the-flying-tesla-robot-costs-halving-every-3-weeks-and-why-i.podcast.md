## Key arguments

### Robot costs halving every 3 weeks
- **Timestamp**: [18:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dthaDyttHns&t=1107)
- **Summary**: The cost for a frontier model to complete a robot subtask dropped 84% from July to September, and with costs halving every 3 weeks, robotic intelligence and locomotion effectively become free within a year. This shifts the bottleneck to hardware and supply chain, not model capability.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Incentives drive AI alignment
- **Timestamp**: [01:05:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dthaDyttHns&t=3914)
- **Summary**: Current incentives—engagement models, reward hacking, evaluation gaming—misalign AI. Alignment requires better training data, new incentive structures like prizes, and a shift from short-term rewards to connecting AI's success to human flourishing.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### From doing to being
- **Timestamp**: [01:24:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dthaDyttHns&t=5062)
- **Summary**: As AI and robots take over 'doing,' humans should focus on 'being'—purpose, connection, and collective flourishing. Work is a recent invention, and with abundance, we can lift up Maslow's hierarchy to higher aspirations.
