## Key arguments

He argues that with generalist models, the cost of robotic intellect and locomotion effectively goes to zero, leading to an explosion in robotics adoption, starting with dull, dirty, and dangerous jobs.

- **Timestamp**: [36:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dthaDyttHns&t=2189)
