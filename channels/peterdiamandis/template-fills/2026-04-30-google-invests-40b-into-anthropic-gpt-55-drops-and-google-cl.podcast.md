## Key arguments

### Compute is the bottleneck, not model innovation
- **Timestamp**: [40:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCbJKkG_mZs&t=2452)
- **Summary**: Dave argues that the AI race is bottlenecked at TSMC and energy supply, not model innovation. Only Elon Musk is publicly discussing this constraint, and the hyperscalers' investments are all about securing compute, not picking winners.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Google's TPU dominance and recursive self-improvement
- **Timestamp**: [32:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCbJKkG_mZs&t=1934)
- **Summary**: Dave and Alex highlight Google's 8th-gen TPUs, which are 3x faster for training and designed for millions of agents. Alex notes that TPUs are now designed by TPUs, creating a recursive self-improvement loop that cements Google's lead.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Anthropic's revenue could hit $70B, limited by compute
- **Timestamp**: [39:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCbJKkG_mZs&t=2357)
- **Summary**: Dave reveals that Anthropic internally projects $40-70B revenue by year-end, but only if they can secure enough compute. This explains their deals with Google and Amazon, and why they're holding back models like Mythos.

### Math is cooked: 1% monthly gains on Frontier Math
- **Timestamp**: [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCbJKkG_mZs&t=1689)
- **Summary**: Alex argues that GPT-5.5's 2% leap on Frontier Math Tier 4 shows a 1% monthly gain in research-level math. At this pace, all professional math problems will be solved within 4-5 years, signaling a broader 'cooked' trend in cognitive tasks.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### The economic value per token drives AI strategy
- **Timestamp**: [54:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCbJKkG_mZs&t=3259)
- **Summary**: Alex explains that Anthropic's projects, like Project Deal, are all about maximizing economic value per token. This principle explains why codegen is the focus—it's more valuable per token than consumer plays—and why OpenAI pivoted to enterprise.
