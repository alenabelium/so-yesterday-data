- **Anchor Quotes**: [0, 1]

### Inference cost collapse and Jevons paradox
- **Timestamp**: [19:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoW13oUTTV0&t=1181)
- **Summary**: Wood explains that the collapsing cost of AI inference (99% per year) will lead to explosive unit growth, not deflationary GDP decline. She invokes Jevons paradox: as intelligence becomes cheaper, demand becomes insatiable, driving overall economic expansion. She also notes that trueflation is already at 1.2-2%, and expects inflation to go below 2% within a year.

### US-China AI race and open source
- **Timestamp**: [45:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VoW13oUTTV0&t=2735)
- **Summary**: Wood argues that the US forced China into open-source AI, and China has capitalized, now leading in open-source models. She highlights that China's investment as a share of GDP is 40% vs. 20% in the US, and that China is pouring money into 'new productive forces.' She believes competition is good and that the US must stay vigilant.

### Bitcoin bull case and stablecoin impact
