## Key arguments

### The End of Coasean Hierarchy
- **Timestamp**: [04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9c8STV7Hnw&t=268)
- **Summary**: Salem argues Ronald Coase's 1937 theory that firms exist to lower transaction costs is broken by AI. Execution costs have dropped so low that the internal hierarchy is no longer cheaper than external coordination. Companies must shift from hierarchy-based to [[intelligence-native]] architectures where AI agents handle execution, leaving humans to manage the 'fiduciary wedge' of legal and ethical
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### The Edge Innovation Strategy
- **Timestamp**: [29:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9c8STV7Hnw&t=1790)
- **Summary**: Legacy companies cannot transform from within due to 'organizational drag' and immune system responses. The only viable path is to build an [[ai-native-digital-twin]] at the edge of the business as a separate entity. This allows the new AI-native workflows to achieve recursive self-improvement without being bottlenecked by legacy approval chains, eventually replacing the core.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### The 100x Performance Leap
- **Timestamp**: [34:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9c8STV7Hnw&t=2050)
- **Summary**: By implementing a full [[recursive-self-improvement]] loop, companies can achieve 100x performance improvements annually. Salem cites examples like contact centers and marketing where AI-native workflows have replaced agency-heavy or human-centric models, allowing teams to focus on high-value judgment rather than manual coordination.

### Workforce Compression and Roles
- **Timestamp**: [25:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9c8STV7Hnw&t=1514)
- **Summary**: The new model compresses the workforce to 10-25% of its current size. Middle management coordination roles vanish, while the suite shifts to accountability holders. Entry-level grunt work is automated, necessitating aggressive [[apprenticeship-model]] programs to train future leaders, as the traditional path of learning via spreadsheet drudgery disappears.

### Dynamic Org Structures as Protocols
- **Timestamp**: [49:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I9c8STV7Hnw&t=2968)
- **Summary**: Static org charts and five-year plans are obsolete. The organization itself becomes a dynamic protocol that adapts constantly to the situation, similar to an amoeba. This shift requires the Massive Transformative Purpose (MTP) to be encoded as a protocol for AI agents, ensuring alignment without human intervention for every decision.
