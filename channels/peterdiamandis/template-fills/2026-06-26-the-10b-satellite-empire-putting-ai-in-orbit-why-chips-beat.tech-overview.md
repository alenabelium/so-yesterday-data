## Wider context

The competitive landscape for AI infrastructure is shifting from raw launch capacity to compute efficiency. While SpaceX dominates launch costs, Google's TPUs offer superior inference efficiency, making orbital data centers economically viable sooner than anticipated. Simultaneously, China's GLM 5.2 demonstrates that open-weight models can achieve parity with US frontier labs, signaling a multipolar shift in AI capability.
