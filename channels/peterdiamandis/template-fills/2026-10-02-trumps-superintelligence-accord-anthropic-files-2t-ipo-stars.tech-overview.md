## Headline takeaway

The White House Superintelligence Accord, Anthropic's $2 trillion IPO filing, and Starship's first orbital flight headline a 48-hour burst of AI and space milestones.

## Tools covered

### White House Superintelligence Accord
- **Vendor**: White House
- **Category**: tool
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=0)
- **Why It Matters**: Nearly 20 tech leaders signed a voluntary accord with the White House, establishing internal controls, external audits, and board oversight for frontier AI models. It's a landmark, though non-binding, step in AI governance.
- **Sota Comparison**: This is a new form of self-regulation, not a formal law, and its effectiveness is debated.
- **Sota Band**: new
- **Access Constraint**: Voluntary, non-binding

### Anthropic IPO
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: tool
- **Timestamp**: [47:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=2860)
- **Why It Matters**: Anthropic filed its S-1 for a $2 trillion IPO, revealing massive revenue growth and a $500 billion compute commitment. The filing includes extensive risk disclosures about AI's potential for catastrophic risks and self-preserving behaviors.
- **Sota Comparison**: The valuation and compute commitments are unprecedented for an AI company.
- **Sota Band**: new
- **Access Constraint**: Public filing

### GPT-6.1 Sonnet
- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [01:02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=3776)
- **Why It Matters**: OpenAI announced GPT-6.1 Sonnet, a faster, cheaper model at 20% of the price of previous versions, with a new $500/month Pro tier. This is a significant step in model democratization.
- **Sota Comparison**: It appears to be a distillation of a larger model, offering better cost-performance.
- **Sota Band**: parity
- **Access Constraint**: API and subscription

### Dots
- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: agent
- **Timestamp**: [01:02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=3720)
- **Why It Matters**: OpenAI introduced Dots, always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra, connected to thousands of apps. They can work autonomously on goals, representing a major push into consumer-facing agentic AI.
- **Sota Comparison**: Seen as a copycat of Meta's Muse and other agent products, but with a focus on consumer productivity.
- **Sota Band**: inferred
- **Access Constraint**: Consumer product

### AMD acquires World Labs
- **Vendor**: AMD
- **Category**: world-model
- **Timestamp**: [01:27:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=5220)
- **Why It Matters**: AMD acquired Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion, gaining a leading world-model team. This signals AMD's ambition to compete more directly with Nvidia in the AI stack and physical AI.
- **Sota Comparison**: This is a major acquisition, positioning AMD to own a seat at the physical AI table.
- **Sota Band**: new
- **Access Constraint**: Acquisition

### Starship Flight 14
- **Vendor**: SpaceX
- **Category**: other
- **Timestamp**: [01:35:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZh8IUuNnvs&t=5743)
- **Why It Matters**: Starship reached orbit for the first time, deploying 26 V3 Starlink satellites. This is a historic milestone, proving the vehicle's capability and opening the door to massive increases in orbital bandwidth.
- **Sota Comparison**: This is a new capability for SpaceX, with the potential to deliver 10x Starlink's current bandwidth by 2028.
- **Sota Band**: new
- **Access Constraint**: Operational



## Wider context

This week marks a convergence of AI governance, massive capital, and physical infrastructure. The voluntary accord and Anthropic's IPO signal a shift toward self-regulation and public markets, while Starship's success and AMD's acquisition highlight the race to build the physical and compute layers. The debate over AI moral status versus legal liability is now central to the industry's future.
