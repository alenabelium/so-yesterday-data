## Wider context

This week underscores a shift from raw model releases to control and distribution: the White House accord and Anthropic's IPO both grapple with governance and liability, while OpenAI's Dev Day focuses on consumer agents and cheaper models. The AMD-World Labs deal and Starship's orbit signal a race to own the physical and orbital infrastructure of AI.
