## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from pure model parameter wars to infrastructure and power constraints. With Apple's M5 chips optimizing for local LLMs and Leopold Aschenbrenner's portfolio highlighting Bloom Energy, the industry recognizes that watts, not weights, are the binding constraint. Simultaneously, specialized models like Kos1 Light are beginning to outperform generalist giants in specific domains.
