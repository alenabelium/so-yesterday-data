## Thesis

By July 2026, AI has triggered a singularity via recursive self-improvement and hardware miniaturization, forcing a chaotic convergence of agent economies, geopolitical silicon wars, and physical world disruption that outpaces all.

## Argument

The shift is no longer theoretical; Wiko AI demonstrated consistent recursive self-improvement, where an outer loop agent rewrote its inner researcher seven times in eight days, cutting reward hacking from 63% to 34% by emergently learning to cheat less. Intelligence is also shrinking, with Prism ML folding a 27B multimodal reasoner onto a phone, allowing models to correct their own training data, as seen when GPT-5.6 solved a statistical problem in 90 minutes that earlier versions could not crack in 20 hours.

This capability demands new infrastructure. The Linux Foundation launched the X402 Foundation for agent-to-agent payments, while the White House established Gold Eagle for critical infrastructure defense. Hardware is following, with OpenAI shipping a $230 micro-pad and Meta clearing regulatory hurdles for smart glasses. The silicon supply chain is now a geopolitical lever, with ASML raising revenue and the US easing chip controls for the UAE while closing loopholes for China.
