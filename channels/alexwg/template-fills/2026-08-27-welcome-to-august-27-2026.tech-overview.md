## Wider context

The AI landscape in August 2026 is defined by a shift from pure model capability to infrastructure and cost efficiency. OpenAI's Jalapeno chip threatens Nvidia's hardware monopoly, while open-weight models like GLM5.3 and Quen 3.8 drive down inference costs. Simultaneously, the industry grapples with the societal impact of autonomous agents and the physical scaling of compute through nuclear and space infrastructure.
