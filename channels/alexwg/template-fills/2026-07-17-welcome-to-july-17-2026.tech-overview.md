## Wider context

The AI industry is shifting from a compute-scarcity model to one where open-weight models like Kimmy K3 democratize frontier capabilities. This forces closed labs to justify their high costs and restrictive safety measures, while hardware and regulatory landscapes evolve to support or constrain these advancements.
