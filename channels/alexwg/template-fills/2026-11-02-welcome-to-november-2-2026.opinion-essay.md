## Thesis

By November 2026, AI recursive self-improvement is a line item at Anthropic, and the gap between AI's real-world work performance and its life-related capabilities is reshaping both industry and human expectations.

## Argument

The mechanism is financial. Anthropic's IPO filing shows internal Claude usage, priced at retail, has outvalued its monthly cash payroll since April, after surging 250-fold this year. Claude writes over 90% of its makers' new code, making recursive self-improvement a line item. Revenue grew 86 times while headcount grew five times, and the company has committed $895.4 billion through 2045 to compute, including deals with Google and SpaceX. This is not a hypothetical; it's a budget line.

Life is proving easier than work.
