## TL;DR

The massive global investment in AI infrastructure is stalled by a lack of transparent pricing for GPU compute, preventing efficient risk management and capital allocation. To solve this, the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI) establishes a standardized price based on actual cleared transactions, mirroring how electricity markets function. By enabling derivatives and futures contracts, this index unlocks trillions in institutional capital.

## The argument

### Capital flows blind without a price
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Nearly $7 trillion in data center investment is projected through 2030, but lenders, insurers, and investors cannot underwrite, cover, or mark positions without a standard price for GPU compute. Capital is flowing on trust and guesswork instead of transparent markets.
- **Role**: definition

### Commodities follow a predictable arc
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Every critical commodity—oil, natural gas, electricity—followed the same path: vital, then opaque, then a benchmark that enables the financial stack. GPU compute is stuck at step two, and without a benchmark, the capital needed for half of the $7 trillion buildout stays on the sidelines.
- **Role**: evidence

### OCPI settles like electricity, not like a stock
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:01:34']
- **Claim**: GPU hours cannot be warehoused, so OCPI futures settle Asian-style, averaging volume-weighted prices of executed transactions over the contract period. Separate indices track each GPU type (H100, H200, B200) and regional variations reflect geographic price differences.
- **Role**: definition

### Built on cleared prices, not estimates
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:09']
- **Claim**: OCPI is built on actual cleared prices from live GPU markets, not surveys or rate cards. The company executed the first compute swap in December 2025, and the index is now live on the Bloomberg terminal, making it a credential for institutional capital.
- **Role**: evidence

### The financial stack follows the benchmark
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:09']
- **Claim**: Once the benchmark exists, forward curves become constructable, residual value risk becomes insurable, and derivatives referencing OCPI are already live on Kshi and Robin Hood. This mirrors how the shale revolution began when financial infrastructure caught up, not when drilling technology was ready.
- **Role**: synthesis
