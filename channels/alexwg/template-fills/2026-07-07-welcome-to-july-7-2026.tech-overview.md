## Wider context

The week marks a shift from pure capability races to interpretability and infrastructure. Claude Fable 5's dominance is notable, but the real delta is [[access-consciousness]] via JSpace, which turns internal model states into debuggable artifacts. Simultaneously, the energy bottleneck is becoming the primary constraint, with [[Fision]] achieving criticality and [[DeepSeek]] designing custom silicon, signaling that hardware and power are now the gating factors for frontier AI progress.
