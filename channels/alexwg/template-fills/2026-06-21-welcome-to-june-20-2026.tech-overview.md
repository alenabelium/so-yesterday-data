## Wider context

The AI landscape is bifurcating into a closed duopoly (Anthropic/OpenAI) and an open Chinese insurgency. While frontier models like GPT-5.6 loom, open-source models like GLM-5.2 are achieving parity or beating closed systems on specific benchmarks like code maintainability. This shift is driven by talent migration (Jumper to Anthropic) and the 'dark forest' retreat of open-source contributors from frontier labs. Simultaneously, physical AI (robotics touch, nuclear power) and space infrastructure are accelerating to support this computational demand, highlighting a move from pure software competition to hardware and energy sovereignty.
