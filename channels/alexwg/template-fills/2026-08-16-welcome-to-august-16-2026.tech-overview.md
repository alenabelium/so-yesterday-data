## Wider context

The singularity is defined by capital flows arbitraging bottlenecks like power and compute. As models commoditize, value shifts to data provenance and physical infrastructure (megawatts). Open-source leadership has shifted east, while US labs focus on agents and hardware. Trust and regulation remain the primary constraints on deployment speed.
