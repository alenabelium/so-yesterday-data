## Wider context

The week's releases signal a shift from model capability to economic autonomy: agents now earn their own keep, and infrastructure costs are consolidating around a few hyperscalers. As per-seat licensing gives way to consumption pricing, the unit of account moves from users to tasks and tokens, reshaping SaaS economics.
