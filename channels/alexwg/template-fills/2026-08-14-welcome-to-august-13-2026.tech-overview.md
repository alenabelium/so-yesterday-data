## Wider context

The frontier has shifted from raw model capability to [[recursive-self-improvement]] and infrastructure dominance. Claude Opus 5's ability to self-correct and rebuild codebases signals a move toward autonomous scientific discovery, while the massive capital expenditure in memory and compute (SK Hynix, Cerebras) indicates that the bottleneck is now physical substrate and energy. This creates a widening gap between those who control the [[compute-infrastructure]] and those who merely consume it.
