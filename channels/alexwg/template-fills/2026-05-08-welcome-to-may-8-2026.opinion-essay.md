## Thesis

By May 2026, the AI singularity's insatiable compute demand forces a physical pivot to orbital data centers, reordering global economics, energy geography, and even government transparency.

## Argument

The mechanism is physical: terrestrial power, land, and cooling can no longer match the cadence of AI growth. Anthropic's partnership with SpaceX, handing over Colossus 1 and unlocking 300+ megawatts and 220,000 Nvidia GPUs, is the first step toward gigawatt-scale orbital compute. SpaceX's launch economics and constellation experience make this a near-term engineering program, not a research concept. The demand is extreme: Anthropic grew 80x annualized in Q1 against a planned 10x, and compute can't catch up. Capital markets concur—pre-IPO valuation hit $1.2 trillion, up 900% since October. Naive ARR extrapolation has Anthropic absorbing 100% of global GDP in 21 months, absurd until you recall the product is cognition itself. Models keep earning the spend: Opus 4.7 tops Scalab's refactoring leaderboard at 48.57, and the harder program bench—rebuilding code bases from binary—shows 3% almost resolved, a humbling reminder that the latter still has rungs above us.
