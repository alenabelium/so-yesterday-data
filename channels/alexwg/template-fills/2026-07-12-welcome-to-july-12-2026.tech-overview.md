## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from model-centric to agent-centric competition as base models become commoditized. Anthropic and OpenAI are pushing [[context-window]] limits and reasoning benchmarks, while infrastructure costs drive innovation in local hosting like GLM 5.2. This commoditization fuels a war at the agent layer, exemplified by Cursor's acquisition, and impacts labor markets with new job structures and regulatory responses.
