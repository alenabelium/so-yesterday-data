## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from pure capability races to structural and financial entanglements. Public-private equity stakes and tangled compute supply chains (e.g., Apollo/Blackstone funding Google TPUs for Anthropic) indicate a maturation where infrastructure and governance are as critical as model weights. While general agents struggle with long-horizon tasks, specialists like Lean and Claude Opus 4.7 demonstrate that domain-specific breakthroughs are currently outpacing general-purpose reliability, forcing enterprises to adopt hybrid routing strategies.
