## TL;DR

Sarama is being announced as the first consumer-scale foundation model for non-human minds, starting with a dog collar. It exists because, unlike text or images, animal cognition was never written down and has to be measured into existence. That makes it the first AI frontier where the training record must be generated, not read.

## The argument

### The Record Has To Be Measured Into Existence
- **Anchor Timestamps**: [0]
- **Claim**: Every domain AI mastered came pre-documented; it only had to read. Non-human cognition is the first frontier with no written record, and synthetic data cannot help because it only interpolates distributions a model has already seen. The data must be measured into being.
- **Role**: definition

### Instrument Self-Labels In The Home
- **Anchor Timestamps**: [86]
- **Claim**: Rather than learn from researcher archives like Earth Species or Dolphin Gemma, Sarama generates data with an in-home instrument. A universal bark dictionary fails because meaning is individual, so it trains a separate model of each dog on that dog's own life.
- **Role**: evidence

### Three Sensors Label One Another
- **Anchor Timestamps**: [169]
- **Claim**: The collar fuses camera, microphone and motion sensors and transmits feature vectors, not raw media, keeping collection private. When the camera frames the bowl, motion logs the lunge and the mic the bark, three readings label each other with no annotator in the loop.
- **Role**: evidence

### Catching Pain A Dog Evolved To Hide
- **Anchor Timestamps**: [169]
- **Claim**: Dogs mask pain by instinct and illness can go unseen for weeks. A model watching continuously catches early what a dog has evolved to hide, already reading core needs and identifying dogs by voice at roughly 93% accuracy on emotion and intent.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Real Inheritance Is The Method
- **Anchor Timestamps**: [246]
- **Claim**: The dog co-evolved to read human intent and the law is shifting it from property toward personhood. But the durable payoff is a protocol for modeling a mind that cannot say what it wants, generalizing from horses to herds to the non-carbon minds we are building.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

**Prior art:** Earth Species' NatureLM Audio, Google's Dolphin Gemma, and Project CETI all learn from researcher-collected archives; Sarama's bet is the opposite — data generated and self-labeled by an instrument in the home.

**Why dogs first:** they co-evolved to be legible to humans, are naturally labeled in the home, and exist at consumer scale. The collar ships a two-mode app: a third-person AR overlay and a first-person collar view.

**Caveats (per the speaker's own disclosure):** the speaker has a financial interest in Sarama. Performance figures, including the ~93% accuracy and the target of 1 million annotated bark sequences by 2026, are company-reported and forward-looking, subject to risk and uncertainty.
