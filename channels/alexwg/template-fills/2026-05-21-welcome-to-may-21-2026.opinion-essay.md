## Thesis

The singularity is no longer a distant theoretical horizon but an active, accelerating reality reshaping mathematics, infrastructure, and capital in 2026.

## Argument

The shift is evidenced by an internal OpenAI model disproving Erdős' planar unit distance conjecture, a milestone that collapsed Epoch AI's median prediction for solving Millennium Prize problems to 2032. This mathematical acceleration forces the White House to treat frontier model releases like FDA submissions. Simultaneously, the physical bottleneck has migrated from logic to power and concrete, with Nvidia conceding the China market to Huawei and Anthropic bankrolling SpaceX's compute infrastructure. Capital is reorganizing around this new reality: OpenAI is token-maxing YC founders, while massive philanthropic pledges mature. The singularity is not just changing what we build, but how we value and verify it, as seen in the forensic debates over AI-generated literature and the imminent IPO of the very labs driving the change.

## Counterpoints

- Local zoning laws, like the ban in Saint Charles City, Missouri, still constrain the singularity's physical expansion.
- Orbital compute timelines are debated, with Bezos calling Musk's two-to-three-year timeline ambitious.
- The authenticity of human authorship is under forensic scrutiny, as seen with the Commonwealth Short Story Prize winner.
