## Wider context

The AI landscape is bifurcating between open-weight accessibility and closed geopolitical control. While tools like [[unsloth]] and [[glm-5.23r]] democratize access to massive models, export bans on [[mythos]] and [[fable-5]] highlight the weaponization of frontier capabilities. Simultaneously, the convergence of [[codeex]] and [[aws-continuum]] signals a shift from experimental AI to entrenched enterprise infrastructure, even as labor markets react with both shortages and layoffs.
