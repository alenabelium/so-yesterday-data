## Wider context

The industry is shifting from building static models to systems that can recursively improve themselves, as evidenced by the Post-Train Bench results. This self-improvement loop is supported by massive infrastructure investments and cheaper compute, while legal and economic structures are rapidly restructuring to accommodate autonomous agents and AI-driven displacement.
