## TL;DR

The singularity is an engineering problem, not a prophecy, because intelligence is fundamentally compression. By viewing AI through information theory, we can map internal 'droplets' of thought in model derivatives. This perspective shifts development from training to recursive self-improvement via targeted data injection.

## The argument

### Intelligence Is Compression
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:15']
- **Claim**: Prediction and compression are one operation. Intelligence is literally compression, turning the arrival date from prophecy into engineering.
- **Role**: definition

### Droplets in Derivatives
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:01:45']
- **Claim**: Thoughts live in Jacobian derivatives, not activations. Intermediate steps like planned rhymes run silently in slopes, condensing into namable droplets.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Indicator Diagram
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:23']
- **Claim**: The Jacobian lens is the 'indicator diagram' of minds. Mapping where thought condenses reveals where to dig, similar to how Watt's diagram improved engines.
- **Role**: synthesis

### Recursive Self-Improvement
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:24']
- **Claim**: Progress moves from improving models to improving the improver. We must map droplets and feed them as data to the next model, closing the loop.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Skeptics argue emergence is a metric mirage, but the speaker counters that sharp transitions depend on the order parameter, just as density jumps when steam condenses. The analogy extends to superposition as degeneracy pressure and potential black hole-like density. The speaker notes that Grokking resembles supercooling, waiting for a seed to nucleate early generalization.
