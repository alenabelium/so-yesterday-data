## Wider context

The frontier has moved from raw scale to [[self-modeling]] and orchestration. Models like Fugu and Fable prioritize directing work over generating it, while hardware efforts like Fab 2 focus on cost reduction. This convergence allows AI to handle complex discovery tasks, shifting human value toward accountability and judgment rather than execution.
