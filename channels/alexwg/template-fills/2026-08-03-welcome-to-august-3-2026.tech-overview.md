## Wider context

The AI landscape is bifurcating between [[open-weight-competition]] and [[regulatory-enforcement]]. Alibaba's Quen 3.8 Max and Deepseek's V4 Flash are driving costs down, forcing proprietary models to compete on utility rather than just access. Simultaneously, the EU AI Act's enforcement marks a shift from voluntary guidelines to mandatory disclosure, while physical constraints like energy demand and humanoid robotics patents define the next phase of infrastructure buildout.
