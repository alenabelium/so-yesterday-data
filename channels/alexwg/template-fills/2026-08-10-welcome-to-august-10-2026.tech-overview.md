## Wider context

The internet's infrastructure is undergoing a fundamental shift as machine traffic overtakes human traffic, forcing adaptations like Cloudflare's Kiturf. Simultaneously, the AI model race is pivoting from chat interfaces to physics-based world models, a domain where Chinese systems currently dominate video generation and humanoid robotics. This geopolitical and technological realignment is compounded by massive energy demands for data centers and new frontiers in space and UAP investigations.
