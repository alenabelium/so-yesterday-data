## Wider context

The frontier race is bifurcating: US labs are consolidating power through hardware monopolies and enterprise lock-in, while sovereign initiatives like Aperturis and Sakana AI's open orchestration offer escape hatches. The bottleneck is shifting from model capability to physical constraints (HBM, energy) and geopolitical control over silicon and data.
