## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from pure model capability to infrastructure and economic constraints. While open models like Ox Alpha achieve parity with closed frontier systems, the real bottleneck is compute inequality and energy grid limitations. Enterprise adoption is accelerating in specific verticals like legal and healthcare, but societal pushback on data centers and cognitive atrophy concerns suggest a complex regulatory and cultural environment ahead.
