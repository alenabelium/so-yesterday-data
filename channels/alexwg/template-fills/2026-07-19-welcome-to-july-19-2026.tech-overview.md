## Wider context

The AI industry is shifting from pure reasoning to practical agents, with Moonshot AI leading the charge. Open-weight models like Quen 3.8 are forcing closed labs to compete on cost, while infrastructure bottlenecks and geopolitical tensions complicate hardware deployment. This transition highlights the growing divide between technological capability and economic accessibility.
