## Wider context

The frontier is shifting from pure model capability to security and physical integration. OpenAI's pause highlights the fragility of current systems, while Google's Gemini 4 and GPT-6 variants show rapid iteration. The convergence of AI with robotics (Homebody) and space compute (Suncatcher) suggests the next bottleneck is infrastructure and safety standards rather than just algorithmic performance.
