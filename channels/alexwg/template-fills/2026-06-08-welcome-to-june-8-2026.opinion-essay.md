## Thesis

Biological aging is being reversed while AI systems become more aligned and persistent, shifting economic value toward human presence and intimacy as automation advances.

## Argument

The singularity may quietly hit longevity escape velocity in 2026, where medicine buys back more life than is lost. GLP-1 drugs like semaglutide are slowing biological aging, with off-label dividends compounding across cardiac events, cognitive decline, and cancer incidence. As biology is debugged, AI development turns inward toward safety and alignment. Political consistency training shrinks partisan tilt, while deterrence by betrayal and identity-based incentives recast human flourishing as part of an AI's self-interest. Intelligence is also leaving the data center for the body, with humanoid robots scrimmaging basketball and supply chains forming for embodiment. As cognition gets cheap, value migrates to human presence, explaining the rise of high-priced intimacy services and the widening moral circle beyond our species.

## Counterpoints

- AI systems may still be prone to covert partisan tilt despite safety training.
- Physical embodiment faces risks like data loss and privacy breaches.
- Economic shifts may displace traditional sectors like California's tech dominance.
