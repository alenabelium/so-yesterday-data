## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from pure software dominance to physical and infrastructural constraints. With OpenAI's strategic isolation and the rise of persistent agents like Codeex, the industry is prioritizing hardware efficiency and power management. This transition is evident in the development of AI-designed chips and robotic platforms, signaling a move towards integrated physical-world applications.
