GPU compute has transitioned from a speculative equity play to a regulated commodity via the first exchange-listed futures on ICE. This infrastructure allows institutional capital to hedge risks and finance the $7 trillion buildout using proven derivatives mechanics. The singularity now has a direct price signal, mirroring the financialization of oil and gas that powered previous industrial revolutions.
