## Wider context

The industry is shifting from raw intelligence races to infrastructure and control. With [[frontier-model-commoditization]] rendering capability gaps less decisive, capital is rotating toward physical silicon, data security, and quantum [[new-physics-of-value]]. This marks a structural break where institutional memory and traditional economic models are being rewritten by AI-driven consolidation and sovereign wealth proposals.
