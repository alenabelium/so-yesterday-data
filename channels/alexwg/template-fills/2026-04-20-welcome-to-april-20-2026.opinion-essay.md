## Thesis

The Singularity now has bureaucratic momentum, and AGI has been demoted from prophecy to a product roadmap.

## Argument

The mechanism is bureaucratic capture. Anthropic's Mythos preview is being run by the NSA and DO even after the Department of War flagged Anthropic as a supply chain risk, revealing that frontier models are too useful to refuse. This cascade runs downstream: Mythos floods open source maintainers with bug reports, conscripting volunteers into a global red team, while eight European National Cyber agencies are locked out and the UK's AI security institute quietly tests and acts on its findings. AGI is no longer a distant prophecy; it's a product roadmap. Elon announced Grock 4.4 at 1 trillion parameters for early May, Grock 4.5 at 1.5 trillion for late May, and Grock 5 as full AGI. Superintelligence is being packaged for mass creativity with Anthropic's Claw Design, powered by Opus 4.7. The effect ripples through distribution: worldwide app releases jumped 60% year-over-year in Q1 2026, confounding the thesis that chatbots would kill apps. Silicon is returning with a vengeance. Intel shares erased every dollar lost in the 2000 crash, and Google is in talks with Marll to co-develop inference chips. The scarcity is structural: global DRAM supply meets only 60% of demand through 2027, pushing memory to 40% of a low-end smartphone's manufacturing cost by mid-2026. Robots have broken out of the lab and onto the track, with ProRL hosting America's first professional humanoid and quadruped robot races in Boston, and Beijing's second annual robot half marathon. Tesla's robo taxi is rolling into Dallas and Houston, and the Iran War's gas price spike pushed used EV sales up 12%

## Counterpoints

- The thesis that chatbots would kill apps is confounded by a 60% year-over-year increase in app releases.
- The Department of War flagged Anthropic as a supply chain risk, yet the NSA and DO run Mythos, showing a contradiction.
- The attention liberation movement offers a small counterpoint, with people dropping phones into colanders to practice noticing real life.
