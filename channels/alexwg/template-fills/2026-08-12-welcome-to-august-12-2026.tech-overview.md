## Wider context

AI capabilities are shifting from pure generation to [[autonomous-agents]] and [[scientific-reasoning]], with models like GPT 5.6 Cyber and Claude's math proofs demonstrating functional utility. Simultaneously, the industry is undergoing [[infrastructure-financialization]], where GPU revenue streams are securitized as an asset class, mirroring 2008 subprime structures. This dual pressure of capability and capital is forcing regulatory responses, from EU watermarking mandates to US Senate pauses, as the gap between AI power and human oversight widens.
