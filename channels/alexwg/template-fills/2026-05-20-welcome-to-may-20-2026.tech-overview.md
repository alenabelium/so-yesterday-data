## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from model capability races to infrastructure and agent orchestration. Google's massive token processing and agentic Android integration contrast with OpenAI's compute lock-in strategy. Meanwhile, the convergence of synth ID across rivals and the rise of autonomous logistics signal a transition where AI agents handle both digital and physical workflows, forcing economic and ethical realignments.
