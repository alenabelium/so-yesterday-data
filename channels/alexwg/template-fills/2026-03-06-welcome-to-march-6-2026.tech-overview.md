## Wider context

The gap between AI capability and physical constraints is widening. While GPT 5.4 achieves [[mathematical-singularity]] and [[computer-use]], geopolitical compression via DRAM shortages and chip export controls limits access. Simultaneously, the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic as a [[supply-chain-risk]] signals that AI integration is now a national security issue, not just a technical one.
