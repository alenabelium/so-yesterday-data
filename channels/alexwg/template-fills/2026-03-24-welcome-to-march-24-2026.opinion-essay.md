## Thesis

By March 2026, AGI will be a declared reality running on consumer devices, and the resulting scramble for energy, silicon, and orbital infrastructure will reveal that the singularity may have a fossil record predating humanity.

## Argument

The declaration lands with the weight of the substrate: Jensen Huang, the man who manufactures the chips, says AGI is achieved. That claim is backed by recursive architecture—Meta's hyper-agents fuse task solving with self-modification, so the system improves the very mechanism of improvement. The same intelligence now fits in a pocket: the anmll open-source project runs a 400B model on an iPhone 17 Pro at 0.66 tokens per second. And it's solving what humans couldn't: GPT-5.4 Pro cracked the first open problem on the Frontier Math Open Problems Benchmark, a conjecture posed by Will Brian in 2019. Epoch AI notes the pattern: experts see the general approach but get stuck executing; the AI finishes. The frontier shifts from individual breakthroughs to sustained inquiry—Anthropic's physical superintelligence recommendation turns Claude into a persistent research engine.

The agent becomes the storefront: GAP partners with Gemini for direct checkout, Walmart rolls out electronic price labels. Claude takes control of your computer via app connectors or direct keyboard/mouse.
