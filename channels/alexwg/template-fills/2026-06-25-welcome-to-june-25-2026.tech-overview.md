## Wider context

The industry is shifting from model-centric to infrastructure-centric competition. Custom silicon like Jalapeno and Dragonfly C1000 indicates a move away from generic GPUs. Simultaneously, biology is becoming programmable via tools like Proto and BioNeMo, merging wet and dry labs. This dual shift in compute and biology suggests a new era of industrialized science.
