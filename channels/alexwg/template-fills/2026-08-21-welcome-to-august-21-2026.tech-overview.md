## Wider context

The AI industry is shifting from pure model competition to infrastructure and financial consolidation. Simultaneously, the physical deployment of AI (robo-taxis, humanoid robots) and regulatory responses (data center scrutiny, AI labeling) indicate the technology is moving from software labs into tangible economic and political friction points.
