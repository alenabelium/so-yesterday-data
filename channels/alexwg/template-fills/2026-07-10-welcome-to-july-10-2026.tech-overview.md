## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from a compute-bound arms race to an [[algorithmic-efficiency]] war. OpenAI’s GPT 5.6 family and Meta’s Muse Spark demonstrate that [[price-implosion]] is the new competitive moat, forcing rivals like Anthropic to adopt [[veblen-pricing]] strategies. This commoditization is outpacing governance, as seen in Europe’s delayed regulatory response and the Fed’s scramble to understand economic impacts.
