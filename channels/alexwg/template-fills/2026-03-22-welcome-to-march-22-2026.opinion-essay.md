## Thesis

By March 2026, humanity is transitioning to a galactic civilization powered by recursive AI design loops and pervasive automation, with compute and agents converging to reshape every layer of society.

## Argument

The mechanism is physical: SpaceX's Terraab foundry, a joint project with Tesla, aims to produce over a terowatt of compute per year, 80% for space and 20% for Earth, closing the gap between today's chip production and future demand. The recursive design loop—keeping masks, fabrication, testing, and iteration in a single building—accelerates the flywheel. Models are getting lighter and longer range: Evermind AI's memory sparse attention scales from 16,000 to 100 million tokens with under 9% degradation, decoupling memory from reasoning. OpenAI is building a fully automated AI researcher, targeting a research intern by September and a multi-agent system by 2028. Tooling is compressing: Cursor's Composer 2 delivers frontier-level coding at a fraction of the cost. Agents are inheriting the web—Cloudflare's CEO predicts bot traffic will surpass human traffic by 2027, and Browser Use's agent accessed websites 81% of the time. Democratization is accelerating: Open Claw runs fully autonomous AI at home, and Chinese school kids and retirees raise lobsters. This democratization is cooking mathematics—researchers built an agent that generated 665 novel research problems in differential geometry, and Terry Tao notes high schoolers can now contribute to Frontier Math. The applications layer is consolidating: OpenAI plans a desktop super app merging ChatGPT, Codex, and its browser; WordPress.com lets AI agents draft, edit, and publish posts; Google Search replaces news headlines with AI-generated text.
