## Wider context

The industry is bifurcating: frontier labs are voluntarily pausing for safety containment while open-source models like Quen and GLM achieve parity. Simultaneously, physical infrastructure (memory, compute, drones) is seeing massive capital reallocation, suggesting the bottleneck is shifting from software generation to physical deployment and energy constraints.
