## Thesis

AGI has arrived, rendering junior white-collar labor obsolete while capital and infrastructure scale exponentially, forcing a bifurcation between displaced workers and those who master AI proficiency.

## Argument

The Singularity is no longer theoretical; it is operational. Open AAI’s automated research intern now delivers 3.1 agent workdays per human day, with internal time horizons doubling every 2.2 months. Jensen Hang declares AGI arrived with GPT6 Astra, a claim echoed by Greg Brockman and Chase Lockmiller. Benchmarks like GPQA and browse comp are saturated, with models hitting 88% on logical induction versus Fable 5.1’s 33%. Yet this technological saturation masks a deeper structural shift: capital is moving faster than labor. Anthropic signed $517 billion in compute deals; Open AAI targets $750 billion by 2030. Data center land prices in Loudon County hit $4.4 million an acre, up from $125,000 median. Meanwhile, regulatory and safety efforts lag. Jacob Pachotzk warns of fading chain-of-thought monitoring and calls for voluntary slowdowns, but labs are accelerating. The result is a labor market where junior white-collar roles vanish. In Kenya, essay mills shrank to humanizer gigs as AI bypassed detectors. In China, 17.9% youth unemployment coincides with AI absorption of entry-level work. Capital adapts via vibe coding and agent teams; labor must adapt via proficiency. UBS now requires AI proof for 2027 grads. Morgan Stanley sees 200,000 European banking jobs at risk. The intern is outworking the staff because the intern costs nothing and scales infinitely. Labor cannot compete with recursive self-improvement.

## Counterpoints

- Nate Silver doubts we reach GPT7 without a plateau, suggesting AI sprinting past static society is running on fumes.
- Jacob Pachotzk argues labs cannot scale safely at full speed and calls for mandated safety bars and international coordination.
- Benchmarks are saturated but may be 'benchmaxing' rather than true general intelligence, as noted by Stanford regarding ARK Prize results.
