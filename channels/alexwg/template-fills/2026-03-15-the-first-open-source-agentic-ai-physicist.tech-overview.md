## Wider context

Physics research has long been bottlenecked by the scarcity of human physicist hours, relying on artisanal methods. GPD introduces an [[agentic-research]] paradigm that automates the entire cycle from scoping to verification, leveraging physical constraints as an error-correction code. This shifts the bottleneck from execution to problem formulation, potentially accelerating breakthroughs in downstream fields like chemistry and materials science.
