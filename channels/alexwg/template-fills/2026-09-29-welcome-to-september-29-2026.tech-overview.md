## Wider context

The convergence of [[space-economics]] and [[agentic-ai]] signals a shift from model capability races to infrastructure and delegation. Starship's launch density enables massive satellite constellations, while models like Claude Sinet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 push reasoning limits, forcing enterprises to adopt agent supervision platforms like Answers in Silicon. The bottleneck is no longer just compute, but the safety and economic integration of autonomous agents.
