The convergence of cheap orbital launch (Starship) and autonomous agent safety (Answers in Silicon) signals a shift from model capability races to infrastructure and delegation. While AI reasoning benchmarks are being shattered by models like Sinet 5.5, the real bottleneck is now user adoption and physical world integration, as seen with Optimus and Muse's enterprise rollout.
