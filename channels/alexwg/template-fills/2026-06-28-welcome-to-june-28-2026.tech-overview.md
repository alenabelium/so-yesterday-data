## Wider context

The AI landscape has shifted from raw capability races to **[[cost-efficiency]]** and **[[agent-specialization]]**. Models like GPT 5.5 and Opus 4.8 are commoditized, forcing enterprises to use specialized agents like Fable 5 for coding and GPT 5.6 for experimentation. This fragmentation is driven by **[[compute-scarcity]]** and **[[energy-constraints]]**, pushing adoption of open weights like Deepseek GLM5.2 and physical infrastructure like **[[humanoid-robotics]]** and **[[fusion-energy]]** to sustain growth.
