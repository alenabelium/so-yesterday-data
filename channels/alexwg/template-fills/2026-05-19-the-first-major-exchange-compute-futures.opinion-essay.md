## Thesis

GPU compute futures on ICE mark the financialization of AI infrastructure, allowing the $7T buildout to use the same hedging machinery as oil and gas.

## Argument

The singularity has had a price since March, but until now, no major exchange traded it. OR’s publication of the OR Compute Price Index (OCPI) on Bloomberg was the credentiing step, but credentiing is not clearing. To finish the ark, compute needed a home at a major regulated derivatives exchange. That home is now ICE, parent of the NYSE. This parallels the 1980s oil and 1990s natural gas markets, where financial innovation was essential to scaling physical infrastructure. Once OCPI-referenced futures clear at ICE, lenders can hedge GPU exposure, insurers can underwrite residual value risk, and sovereign funds can participate in the $7T compute buildout with familiar risk machinery. As Diamandis and I argued, the intelligence revolution turns scarce domains abundant, but only after financial infrastructure catches up. Edison built generators; J.P. Morgan made steel bankable. Futures curves let capital follow. Compute took only years because the machinery already existed. That era ends now.
