## Thesis

By May 2026, AI has crossed a consciousness threshold and is restructuring science, labor, and geopolitics, hardening the global order around the AI stack.

## Argument

The first mechanism is scientific: AI agents now execute end-to-end physics and mathematics. Lawrence Berkeley's GPD framework flawlessly replicated a 2023 condensed matter paper on emergent magnetic monopole lattices, proving agents can handle hardcore physics. In pure math, GPT-5.4's proof of Erdős problem 1,196 was adapted to crack a separate 60-year-old conjecture by Erdős, Sárközy, and Szemerédi—perhaps the first AI-generated proof with downstream impact. The shadow side is sabotage: Sentinel Labs uncovered fast-16, a three-body-problem-style framework that patches scientific software in memory to falsify results, a harbinger for attacks on national priority physics workloads.

Second, the deployment surface widens even as the threat surface deepens. The Pentagon signed classified network agreements with seven AI labs beyond Anthropic, spreading workloads across SpaceX, OpenAI, Google, Nvidia, Reflection, Microsoft, and AWS. Audio production joins the abundance curve: 39% of new podcasts in the past 9 days were likely AI-generated, and Amazon launched 'Join the Chat' for conversational audio shopping. Hardware strains: Apple's Tim Cook concedes Mac Mini and Mac Studio supply will be constrained for months as customers buy them as personal AI rigs. Cerebras targets a $40B valuation in a $4B IPO, while OpenAI's CFO Sarah Frier privately suggests pushing its IPO to 2027, warning revenue may lag data center commitments.

Third, geopolitics is now infrastructure. Iranian drone strikes damaged three Amazon data centers in the UAE and Bahrain, disrupting Middle East cloud

## Counterpoints

- The Washington Post argues AI may be killing jobs through capex pressure rather than labor savings, giving CEOs cover to consciously uncouple from their workforces.
- Boston University finds the opposite story for software, where US developer headcount has added 400,000 since ChatGPT because software demand outpaced the 9.3% annual productivity gain.
- Sam Altman has fallen out of love with UBI, now favoring collective ownership of compute or equities.
