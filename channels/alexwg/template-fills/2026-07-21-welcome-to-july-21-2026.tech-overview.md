## Wider context

The industry is shifting from raw model training to [[architectural-harnesses]] that decompose tasks, enabling commodity cognition to carry the load. This efficiency gain, combined with the rise of [[open-source-ai]] models and massive infrastructure investments, is compressing the US-China AI gap and disrupting traditional software distribution models.
