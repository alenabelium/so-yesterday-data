## Wider context

The week's news shows AI's frontier shifting from raw pre-training to post-training and agentic self-improvement, with compute now a strategic asset. The commoditization of math proofs and the rise of AI decompilation signal a broader trend where AI-generated content becomes indistinguishable from human work, forcing industries to adapt. Geopolitically, compute is becoming statecraft, with nations and corporations vying for control over the means of computation, as seen in SpaceX's massive compute deal and the strike on Yandex's data center.
