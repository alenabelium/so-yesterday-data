## Thesis

The construction of a Dyson swarm for AI compute has already begun with Lonear Space's deployment of the first node on the Moon.

## Argument

The singularity's agenda is to put solar system matter and energy to their highest use: thinking. This culminates in a Dyson swarm of compute nodes around the sun. While Freeman Dyson imagined this in 1960, the AI age has made it tangible. The swarm is assembled one node at a time, outward from home. The leading edge is defined by how far from Earth the compute actually runs. Lonear Space is the lead builder. Unlike SpaceX's Starlink and StarCloud, which operate in low Earth orbit, Lonear operates compute, storage, and bandwidth to the lunar surface, 300,000 km from Earth. Lonear's history includes the first software-defined data center in space, the first AI-generated artwork in space, and the first commercial lunar spectrum claim. In February 2024, Lonear flew the first software-defined data center to the lunar surface aboard Intuitive Machines Odysius. It transmitted the Declaration of Independence for storage and the Constitution and Bill of Rights back. In 2025, Lonear carried the first solid-state drives to the moon, 8 terabytes of storage, and the first risk 5 flight chip. It ran continuously through cis-lunar space and 39 lunar orbits. It held disaster recovery data for eight governments and computed the first knowledge graph off Earth. This is the full stack at lunar distance: compute, storage, and bandwidth. Compute in low Earth orbit is racing to commodity pricing. Lonear holds the premium sovereign tier uncontested at its distance with the pricing power that scarcity confers. Demand is already here with eight government customers and a $120 million agreement

## Counterpoints

- SpaceX's Starlink and StarCloud are flying compute in low Earth orbit, with Star Mind planning to fly compute in 2027.
- StarCloud has already flown a single GPU in low Earth orbit, a few hundred km up.
- The accomplishments and performance figures described were provided by Lonear and other third parties and have not been independently verified.
