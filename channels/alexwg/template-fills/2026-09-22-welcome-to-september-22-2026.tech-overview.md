## Wider context

The AI landscape is pivoting from pure model capability to [[infrastructure-abundance]] and [[agent-swarm-efficiency]]. While proprietary labs like OpenAI and Anthropic negotiate safety frameworks, the real bottleneck has shifted to physical constraints: power, compute, and bandwidth. The rise of open-weight models and agent swarms suggests a democratization of access, but geopolitical tensions and hardware limits (HBM4, chips) remain the primary gating factors for future advancement.
