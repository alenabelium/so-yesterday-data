## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from pure parameter scaling to efficiency and embodiment. Grok 4.5's cost-performance ratio and SWE1.7's open access democratize frontier capabilities, while GPT Live's full-duplex voice marks a UI paradigm shift. Simultaneously, hardware constraints drive custom silicon and open-source robotics, suggesting the next bottleneck is physical integration and economic distribution rather than raw model intelligence.
