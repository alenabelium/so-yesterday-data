## Wider context

The narrative shifts from pure software capability to physical and economic constraints. AI's growth is no longer just about model parameters but about [[on-device-efficiency]] (Bonsai 4B), [[hardware-scaling]] (Nvidia N1), and [[energy-infrastructure]] (SoftBank, Lithium). The financialization of AI via [[anthropic-ipo]] and the militarization of robotics (Foundation Future) suggest the industry is maturing into a heavy-industry sector where physical resources and capital markets dictate the pace of innovation more than algorithmic breakthroughs alone.
