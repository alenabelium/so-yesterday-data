# Welcome to November 6, 2026

## Executive Summary

The video presents a speculative future scenario set on November 6, 2026, where AI development faces a political backlash through voter referendums and moratoriums, causing market turmoil for major AI companies. Despite regulatory friction, the narrative emphasizes that recursive self-improvement and compute efficiency are accelerating faster than legislation can contain, with frontier labs like Anthropic and OpenAI making rapid progress. The video also highlights the shift toward compute as a metered utility, the rise of agentic coding, and the eventual move of data centers to space, suggesting that technological momentum will ultimately outpace political constraints.
