# Welcome to October 12, 2026

## Executive Summary

This video provides a rapid-fire overview of the AI landscape as of October 12, 2026, highlighting the profound impact of AI on mathematics, the shift of compute toward post-training, and the emergence of agentic systems. It covers the commoditization of mathematical proofs, the rise of AI-generated content and decompiled games, the massive profitability of AI infrastructure, and the broader societal and geopolitical implications of AI's expansion. The narrative underscores a world where AI is reshaping professions, energy, transportation, and even biology, while raising concerns about oversight and the concentration of power.
