# Welcome to May 21, 2026

## Executive Summary

The video outlines a hypothetical future on May 21, 2026, where AI has achieved mathematical breakthroughs previously thought impossible, signaling a rapid approach to the technological singularity. This acceleration drives massive shifts in infrastructure, with compute power moving to space and energy becoming the primary bottleneck, while financial markets and philanthropy reorganize around AI tokenization and new funding models.
