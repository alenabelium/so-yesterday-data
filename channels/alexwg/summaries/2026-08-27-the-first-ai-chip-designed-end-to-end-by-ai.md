# The First AI Chip Designed End-to-End by AI

## Executive Summary

Architect Labs has unveiled Redwood, the first AI accelerator designed, verified, and deployed end-to-end by an AI system without human intervention below the initial specification. This breakthrough addresses the mismatch between slow chip design cycles and rapidly evolving AI workloads, allowing for rapid iteration and optimization. Early projections suggest Redwood offers significant performance-per-watt gains over existing solutions like Nvidia's Jetson Orin Nano when mapped to Samsung 8nm technology. The development marks a shift toward a 'designless industry' where custom silicon is derived directly from workload specifications rather than traditional human-led design teams.

## Key Points

- Redwood was generated entirely by AI from a high-level specification, producing RTL, verification environments, and firmware in under two weeks with zero bugs in the first FPGA drop. [00:01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujM7vgYZuwQ&t=65)
- Projected performance on Samsung 8nm shows Redwood achieving 49 tokens per second at 1.3 watts, offering a 3.4x improvement in performance per watt compared to Nvidia's Jetson Orin Nano. [00:02:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujM7vgYZuwQ&t=175)
- The AI system continuously optimizes its own design, absorbing hundreds of merge changes daily and exposing the running model as an endpoint to find further timing and kernel improvements.
