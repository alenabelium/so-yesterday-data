# The First Frontier AI Token Price Index

## Executive Summary

The video introduces the OR Token Price Index (OTPI), a new benchmark designed to measure the actual cost of AI inference tokens based on real transaction data rather than published rate cards. By addressing the structural gap between posted prices and realized costs, OTPI provides enterprises and investors with a transparent view of the AI economy's output side. This index complements existing compute pricing metrics, offering a comprehensive look at both the input (GPU) and output (token) costs of the AI industry. The launch marks a significant step in standardizing the pricing of intelligence as a commodity, similar to how oil barrels were standardized in the 19th century.

## Key Points

- OTPI launches as the first benchmark pricing frontier AI tokens from real transactions, addressing the structural gap between posted rate cards and actual costs caused by caching and model mixing [00:01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9tOOZjuMh4&t=101).
- The index uses a unit value methodology that weights models by transacted volume, providing a live market signal of how traffic splits across models and how quickly new releases gain adoption [00:02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9tOOZjuMh4&t=176).
- Together with the existing OR Compute Price Index (OCPI), OTPI allows stakeholders to read both sides of the AI cost curve, measuring both the cost of GPU input and the value of the intelligence output [00:04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9tOOZjuMh4&t=248).
