# The First Orbital Sovereign AI Model

## Executive Summary

The video introduces the concept of 'orbital sovereign AI models' as a solution to data sovereignty issues in the age of artificial intelligence. By hosting AI models and their training data in space, entities can ensure their information remains under their own legal jurisdiction, beyond the reach of foreign laws like the US Cloud Act. This approach allows nations and corporations to retain control over their institutional memory while still leveraging the power of large foundation models through a bifurcated architecture.

## Key Points

- Sovereignty is redefined from physical territory to data control, leading to the creation of AI models that operate under the owner's law rather than the host country's jurisdiction [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLZDzXTfR8c&t=0).
- Lonear is launching the first sovereign AI models from orbit, ensuring that the model lives beside the data it was trained on, creating a physical and legal firewall against external seizure [00:01:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLZDzXTfR8c&t=104).
- Orbit is chosen over terrestrial bunkers because space law ties the satellite to its registry state, preventing foreign powers from accessing the hardware or data, thus preserving true data sovereignty [00:04:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLZDzXTfR8c&t=288).
