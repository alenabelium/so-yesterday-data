# The First Consumer-Scale Interspecies Foundation Model

## Executive Summary

The video introduces Sarama, the first consumer-scale interspecies foundation model designed to decode non-human animal cognition by generating data where no prior records exist. Unlike previous models that rely on existing text or audio archives, Sarama uses a smart dog collar to self-label data through multimodal sensor fusion, creating personalized models for each individual dog. This technology aims to improve animal welfare by detecting hidden pain and illness, while also laying the groundwork for understanding other species and potentially non-carbon intelligences in the future.

## Key Points

- Sarama addresses the lack of written records for non-human animals by using a smart collar that self-labels data via synchronized camera, microphone, and motion sensors, eliminating the need for human annotators [00:01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxw6Z_Im-1Q&t=86).
- The system creates individualized models for each dog rather than a universal dictionary, achieving 93% accuracy in identifying emotion and intent while enabling early detection of masked pain or illness [00:02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxw6Z_Im-1Q&t=169).
- The methodology for modeling minds that cannot speak is positioned as a critical precursor to understanding future non-carbon intelligences, shifting the legal and social frame of companion animals from property to personhood [00:04:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxw6Z_Im-1Q&t=246).
