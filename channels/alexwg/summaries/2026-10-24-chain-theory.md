# Chain Theory

## Executive Summary

Chain Theory is a new research program by Alex Wissner-Gross that aims to rebuild fundamental physics from quantum information, proposing that all of reality consists of finitely many maximally entangled pairs of finite-dimensional quantum systems. The theory derives a parameter-free candidate for the fine structure constant that matches the measured value to 10 significant digits, and it interprets the standard model gauge group, the finite information bound, and the point-like nature of particles as clues pointing to a new foundational framework. The program contrasts with string theory and the anthropic approach, arguing that the constants of nature can be derived from a single axiom rather than being environmental or un-derivable.
