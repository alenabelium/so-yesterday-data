# The Sun is Not Enough

## Executive Summary

The video presents a poetic vision of a future where humanity expands beyond Earth by harvesting the matter and energy of other celestial bodies. It describes the transformation of planets like Mercury, Venus, and Mars into infrastructure for a vast, distributed intelligence. The narrative emphasizes that the Sun alone cannot sustain this future growth, necessitating the utilization of the entire solar system. Ultimately, it portrays a scenario where humanity evolves into a multi-planetary, energy-harvesting entity, turning the cosmos into a medium for expansion.

- The lyrics describe the initial stages of solar system engineering, where Mercury, Venus, and Mars are dismantled and repurposed into mirrors, circuitry, and engines to support human expansion [00:00:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zubH0fPk7aw&t=50).
- Jupiter is depicted not as a destroyed planet but as a resource for immense power, with its storms and pressure converted into satellites and distributed minds in orbit [00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zubH0fPk7aw&t=123).
