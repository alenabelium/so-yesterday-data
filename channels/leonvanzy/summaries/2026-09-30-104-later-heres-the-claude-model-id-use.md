# $104 Later, Here's the Claude Model I'd Use

## Executive Summary

The creator conducts a comparative analysis of Claude Sonnet 3.5, Opus 3.5, and Fable 3.0 by tasking each with building a complete 3D browser-based minigame. While benchmarks suggested Sonnet 3.5 would offer similar performance to Opus at a lower cost, the real-world experiment revealed that Sonnet incurred costs nearly identical to Opus without delivering superior quality. Consequently, the creator concludes that Opus 3.5 remains the more efficient choice for complex agentic programming tasks due to its better balance of speed, cost, and output fidelity.

- The experiment tasked all three models with creating a polished 3D minigolf game to compare real-world performance against benchmark claims [00:01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NzyP4VjkrvM&t=70).

- The creator ultimately recommends Opus 3.5 over Sonnet 3.5 for complex coding tasks due to better quality at a comparable price point [00:10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NzyP4VjkrvM&t=614).
