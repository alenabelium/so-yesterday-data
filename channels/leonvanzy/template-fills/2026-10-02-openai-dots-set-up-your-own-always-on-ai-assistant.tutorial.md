## Headline takeaway

Set up your own always-on AI assistant with OpenAI Dots, a ChatGPT Pro feature that runs on GPT-6 Astra, connects to 4,000+ apps, and gives your agent its own computer and browser.

## Prerequisites

### ChatGPT Pro subscription
- **Kind**: account
- **Note**: Required for Dots access; Plus plan does not include it. Cost is $100/month.

### ChatGPT desktop app
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: Must be installed and updated; Dots setup is not available on mobile.

### Supported region
- **Kind**: knowledge
- **Note**: Dots may not be available in all regions; check if your country is supported.

## Steps

### Download and install the ChatGPT desktop app
- **Timestamp**: [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=294)
- **Action**: Go to the ChatGPT desktop app download page (linked in the video description) and click 'Download and install the app'. Then open the app and log in to your OpenAI account.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Download and install the app'
- **Choice Branch**: If you already have the app, update it to see the Dots option.

### Create your Dot and choose its name and avatar
- **Timestamp**: [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=346)
- **Action**: In the ChatGPT desktop app, click 'Continue' to start creating your Dot. Then choose where the Dot should work (separate computer, local computer), give it a name (e.g., Luna), and pick an avatar.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Continue', then select 'Separate computer' and 'Local computer', enter name, choose avatar, click 'Save'
- **Choice Branch**: You can skip giving access to your local computer if you prefer.

### Connect your Dot to apps
- **Timestamp**: [06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=402)
- **Action**: During setup, connect your Dot to over 4,000 apps. The host recommends granting access to Gmail and calendar, but not contacts or Google Drive.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Select apps to connect (e.g., Gmail, Calendar)
- **Choice Branch**: You can choose which apps to connect; the host suggests avoiding contacts and Drive.

### Test your Dot's computer and browser
- **Timestamp**: [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=496)
- **Action**: After setup, view your Dot's computer by clicking on it. You can take control of the computer to help the agent if needed. The host demonstrates opening Blender and asking the Dot to create a 3D avatar.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Take control' to manage programs; open Blender and send a request
- **Choice Branch**: You can use the Dot's computer for tasks like 3D modeling.

### Call your Dot and set a reminder
- **Timestamp**: [09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=544)
- **Action**: Use the call feature to talk to your Dot. The host calls Luna and asks for a reminder to check OBS recording in 5 minutes, preferring a call. The reminder arrives as a chat message instead.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Call' and speak to your Dot
- **Choice Branch**: The calling feature may not be fully enabled yet.

### Use Spaces for collaborative work
- **Timestamp**: [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=659)
- **Action**: Create a Space (e.g., 'YouTube Video Ideas') and ask your Dot to research and generate content. The Dot updates the Space automatically when done.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Go to 'Spaces', create a new page, then ask your Dot to fill it
- **Choice Branch**: You can use Spaces for any collaborative project.
