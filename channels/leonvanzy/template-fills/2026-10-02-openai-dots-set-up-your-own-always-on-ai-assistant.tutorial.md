## Headline takeaway

Create a persistent, always-on AI assistant with OpenAI Dots that runs on its own virtual computer and browser, all within your ChatGPT Pro subscription.

## Prerequisites

### ChatGPT Pro subscription
- **Kind**: account
- **Note**: Dots require a Pro plan ($100/month) at the time of recording; Plus plans do not have access.

### ChatGPT desktop app
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: Must be installed and updated; setup is not possible on mobile.

## Steps

### Install and update the ChatGPT desktop app
- **Timestamp**: [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=294)
- **Action**: Download the ChatGPT desktop app from the link in the video description, install it, and log in with your OpenAI account. Ensure the app is updated to see the Dots option.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Visit the linked website and click 'Download and install the app'.
- **Choice Branch**: If you don't see the Dots option, your region may not be supported yet.

### Create your Dot
- **Timestamp**: [05:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=346)
- **Action**: In the desktop app, click 'Continue' to start creating your Dot. Connect it to apps like Gmail and calendar, and choose where it should work—on a separate computer and optionally your local computer.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Continue', then select apps and workspace.
- **Choice Branch**: You can grant access to your local computer or keep it on the separate one.

### Name your Dot and customize avatar
- **Timestamp**: [06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=402)
- **Action**: Give your Dot a name (e.g., Luna) and choose an avatar. Save your choices to finalize the setup.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Type a name, pick an avatar, click 'Save'.
- **Choice Branch**: You can use a pet photo or a preset avatar.

### Explore the Dot's computer and take control
- **Timestamp**: [08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=496)
- **Action**: View the Dot's virtual computer by clicking the computer icon. You can take control to manage programs manually, useful if the agent gets stuck or needs login help.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'take control' to interact with the virtual desktop.
- **Choice Branch**: Use this for troubleshooting or manual tasks.

### Test calling and reminders
- **Timestamp**: [09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=544)
- **Action**: Call your Dot and ask it to remind you in 5 minutes to check if OBS is recording. Note that the reminder may come as a chat message instead of a call.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Call the Dot, then say: 'Can you remind me in about 5 minutes to check if OBS is still recording?'
- **Choice Branch**: The calling feature may not be enabled yet.

### Use Spaces for collaborative tasks
- **Timestamp**: [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=659)
- **Action**: Create a new Space (e.g., 'YouTube Video Ideas') and ask your Dot to research and generate a list of video ideas. The Dot will update the Space automatically when done.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Go to Spaces, create a new page, then ask the Dot to research and list ideas.
- **Choice Branch**: The Dot can work in the background and refresh the Space.

## Gotchas

### Dots are only available on the Pro plan ($100/month) at the time of recording; Plus users won't have access.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [02:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=152)

### Dots cannot be set up from the mobile app; you must use the ChatGPT desktop app.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [04:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=294)

### The calling feature may not work as expected; reminders might come as chat messages instead of calls.
- **Severity**: heads_up
- **Timestamp**: [09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=niv2BEcOkR8&t=544)
