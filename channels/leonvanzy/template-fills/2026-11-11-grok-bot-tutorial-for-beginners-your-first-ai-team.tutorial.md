## Headline takeaway

Create a team of autonomous AI agents in Grok Bot that can design a house, share context, and deploy to GitHub.

## Prerequisites

### X Premium, Super, or Ultra subscription
- **Kind**: account
- **Note**: Free and Plus tiers do not include Grok Bot access; Premium starts at ~$30/month.

### Desktop app for your OS
- **Kind**: os
- **Note**: Download from x.ai/bot; available for major operating systems.

## Steps

### Visit x.ai/bot and install the desktop app
- **Timestamp**: [05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=357)
- **Action**: Go to the official Grok Bot website, review pricing, and download the installer for your operating system.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Navigate to x.ai/bot

### Upgrade your X subscription to Premium or higher
- **Timestamp**: [06:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=390)
- **Action**: On the pricing page, click 'Get Premium' or 'Get Super'. Log in with your X account, add a credit card, and complete the upgrade.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Get Premium' or 'Get Super'
- **Choice Branch**: Choose Premium, Super, or Ultra based on your needs.

### Create your first bot
- **Timestamp**: [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=420)
- **Action**: Open the Grok Bot app, log in with your X account, name your bot, pick an avatar and color, then create it.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Name bot: 'Leon's Bot'

### Create a scheduled routine
- **Timestamp**: [07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=460)
- **Action**: Ask your bot to create a routine that reminds you to stretch every 30 minutes. The bot will set it up and display upcoming procedures.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'Please create a routine that reminds me to stretch my legs every 30 minutes.'

### View and control the agent's computer
- **Timestamp**: [08:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=490)
- **Action**: Click the computer icon to see the agent's virtual machine. You can take control, open apps like Chrome, and assist with logins.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click the computer icon

### Generate an image with the bot
- **Timestamp**: [09:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=560)
- **Action**: Ask the bot to generate an image, e.g., 'a dog eating ice cream'. Grok Bot automatically selects an image generation model.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'Please generate an image of a dog eating ice cream.'

### Create additional specialized bots
- **Timestamp**: [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=600)
- **Action**: Dictate or type a request for the bot to create two new bots: an architect and a 3D home designer, each with specific roles.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'Hi, can you please create two more bots? ...'

### Create a project group
- **Timestamp**: [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=660)
- **Action**: Ask the bot to create a new group, add itself and the two new bots, and assign itself as project manager.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'Please create a new group and add yourself and these two agents...'

### Delegate a house design task to the team
- **Timestamp**: [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=680)
- **Action**: Send a message to the group asking for a three-bedroom house with a pool and garden. The project manager delegates to the architect and developer.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'Okay, team, I'd like you to design a three-bedroom house...'

### Review the architect's floor plan
- **Timestamp**: [12:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=740)
- **Action**: Open the PDF floor plan generated by the architect, which includes a 3D render and layout.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Open the PDF from the group media

### Connect GitHub and create a repository
- **Timestamp**: [14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=870)
- **Action**: In Connected Apps, connect GitHub. Then ask the bot to create a public repository named 'Architecture Demo' and push changes.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Go to Connected Apps → GitHub → Connect; Prompt: 'Please create a GitHub repository for this project called "Architecture Demo" and make it public.'

### Deploy the project to Vercel
- **Timestamp**: [15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=920)
- **Action**: Import the GitHub repository into Vercel to deploy the 3D house demo and view it in the browser.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Vercel → Import 'Architecture Demo'

## Gotchas

### Free and Plus X subscriptions do not include Grok Bot access; you must upgrade to Premium or higher.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [05:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=304)

### The pricing page lists free and Plus plans, but they don't actually give access to the bot — ignore them.
- **Severity**: heads_up
- **Timestamp**: [06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=360)

### Grok Bot may not reveal which model it uses; if you need a specific model like Opus 5.5, you may have to ask directly.
- **Severity**: heads_up
- **Timestamp**: [12:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b6cMUBxUtw&t=770)
