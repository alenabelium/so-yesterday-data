# The integers are Diophantine in the rationals, this result is HUGE!

## Executive Summary

This video discusses a groundbreaking mathematical result proving that the integers are Diophantine in the rationals, meaning there exists a polynomial with integer coefficients whose rational zeros project exactly onto the integers. The result, initially announced with a polynomial of about 300 billion variables, has been rapidly improved by a collaborative grassroots effort to a quartic polynomial with 962 variables. The video highlights the role of OpenAI's earlier work on Hilbert's tenth problem for rational numbers, the refutation of Mazur's conjecture, and the ongoing verification in Lean. The presenter emphasizes the unprecedented speed of mathematical progress and the shift towards collaborative, AI-assisted mathematics.

## Key Points

- The integers are Diophantine in the rationals: there is a polynomial with integer coefficients such that a rational number is an integer iff it can be extended to a rational zero of the polynomial. [00:01:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxeX6v2Cjlk&t=64)
- The original polynomial had about 300 billion variables and absurd coefficient bounds, but rapid collaborative improvements reduced it to a quartic with 962 variables. [00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxeX6v2Cjlk&t=123)
- This result refutes Mazur's conjecture and, combined with the DPRM theorem, solves Hilbert's tenth problem for rational numbers. [00:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxeX6v2Cjlk&t=728)
- The proof is based on OpenAI's earlier work on Hilbert's tenth problem for rational numbers, and the mathematics community is rapidly iterating on the result. [00:03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxeX6v2Cjlk&t=187)
- Lean verification is still ongoing; the proof currently relies on some published theorems that have not been fully verified in Lean. [00:28:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxeX6v2Cjlk&t=1688)
