# VP≠VNP, a baby version of the P vs. NP question, proved with AI

## Executive Summary

This video discusses Anthropic's recent proof that VP is not equal to VNP over every field, a significant result in algebraic complexity theory that serves as a baby version of the P vs. NP problem. The proof, discovered by an internal Claude model and formalized in Lean, shows that the permanent and Hamiltonian cycle polynomials require super-polynomial circuit size, providing weak abductive evidence that P is not equal to NP. The video also explores the implications for the future of mathematics, the role of human mathematicians, and community reactions on Hacker News.

## Key Points

- Anthropic announced a proof that VP is not equal to VNP over every field, a result discovered by an internal Claude model and formalized in Lean, providing weak evidence that P is not equal to NP. [00:01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTPTDEObCY0&t=83)
- The proof shows that the permanent and Hamiltonian cycle polynomials require more than m^(c * cube root of log m) gates, establishing a super-polynomial lower bound and separating VP from VNP. [00:04:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTPTDEObCY0&t=293)
- The result does not settle P vs. NP because arithmetic circuits must work over all field extensions, unlike Boolean circuits, but it is a significant step in algebraic complexity theory. [00:06:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTPTDEObCY0&t=364)
