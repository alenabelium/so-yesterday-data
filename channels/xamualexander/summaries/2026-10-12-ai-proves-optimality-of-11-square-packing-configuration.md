# AI proves optimality of 11-square packing configuration

## Executive Summary

This video discusses a recent breakthrough where AI was used to prove the optimality of the 11-square packing configuration, a long-standing open problem in mathematics. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, explains the significance of the proof, which was formalized in the Lean proof assistant rather than in plain English, highlighting AI's role in tackling complex mathematical proofs. He also explores other square packing configurations, noting that many remain unproven, and expresses excitement about AI's potential to solve these problems in the future.

## Key Points

- AI proved the optimality of the 11-square packing configuration, a long-standing open problem, with the proof formalized in Lean. [00:04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFkFN3PlM80&t=241)
- The optimal configuration, known as the Trump configuration, was found by Walter Trump but its optimality was unproven until now. [00:02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFkFN3PlM80&t=168)
- Many other square packing configurations, such as those for 17 or 1765 squares, remain unproven, but AI's infinite patience makes it likely they will be solved soon. [00:10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFkFN3PlM80&t=623)
