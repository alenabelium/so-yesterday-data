# Mathematics foundations problem settled using AI: Medvedev logic is undecidable

## Executive Summary

This video discusses a groundbreaking paper proving that Medvedev logic, a foundational superintuitionistic logic, is undecidable—a problem open for 47 years. The proof was achieved using AI, specifically ChatGPT and Claude Opus, which found the result in 45 minutes. The video highlights the paper's methodological innovations, including the use of AI agents in a positive/negative team setup and the authors' commitment to transparency by releasing all prompts and verification documents. The presenter argues this work challenges fears about AI-generated mathematics and sets a precedent for future AI-assisted research.

## Key Points

- The paper proves Medvedev logic is undecidable, settling a 47-year-old open problem, with the AI finding the result in 45 minutes. [00:01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3LTVA3nay4&t=70)
- The proof was obtained using ChatGPT and Claude Opus, with the authors taking responsibility for correctness but not credit for the ideas, challenging the stance of 25 Fields Medalists. [00:10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3LTVA3nay4&t=600)
- The authors propose a new precedent for AI-assisted mathematics, including full transparency of prompts and verification ledgers, and emphasize the need for systematic research into prompt engineering. [00:14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3LTVA3nay4&t=844)
