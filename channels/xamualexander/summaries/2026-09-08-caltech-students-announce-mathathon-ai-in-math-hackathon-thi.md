# Caltech students announce "Mathathon", AI-in-Math hackathon, THIS is mathematics post-2026

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the Caltech Mathathon, an AI-in-mathematics hackathon scheduled for October 30 to November 1, 2026, where 100 teams will use frontier AI models to solve open mathematical problems. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, praises the event's transparency, equity, and verification principles, and engages with Hacker News comments debating the role of AI in mathematics, the changing incentives in academia, and the future of the mathematical profession. He argues that mathematics should focus on understanding and problem selection rather than just proof difficulty, and sees the Mathathon as a positive step toward adapting to AI-driven mathematics.

## Key Points

- Caltech students are organizing the first AI-in-math hackathon, Mathathon, with 100 teams, $2 million in AI credits, and support from major AI labs, aiming to solve open problems and promote responsible AI use. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iR3rD0RMYR0&t=0)
- The event emphasizes transparency and verification: all AI chat logs will be open-sourced, participants must declare AI usage, and results will be published for community review, with prizes for both promising results and well-explained presentations. [00:02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iR3rD0RMYR0&t=165)
- Organizers stress that selecting impactful problems and understanding proofs are more important than just generating proofs with AI, and they encourage participants to formulate their own conjectures. [00:07:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iR3rD0RMYR0&t=471)
- The video highlights a Hacker News debate about whether AI will put mathematicians out of work, with the host arguing that mathematics' purpose is to unveil the universe's structure, not to provide jobs, and that AI should shift academic incentives toward problem selection and verification. [00:10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iR3rD0RMYR0&t=623)
- The host sees the Mathathon as a grassroots, student-led initiative that represents the future of mathematics, contrasting it with traditional academic gatekeeping and encouraging adaptation to AI-driven methods. [00:22:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iR3rD0RMYR0&t=1341)
