# Classification of Finite Simple Groups Formalized In Lean by an AI Swarm

## Executive Summary

This video discusses the formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (CFSG) in the Lean proof assistant by an AI swarm from Axiom, a project that generated 43.1 million lines of code in 24 days. The formalization uncovered and corrected errors in the original 15,000-page human proof, including a significant flaw in the unbalanced group theorem. The video highlights the interactive proof map visualization, the collaborative swarm methodology, and the implications for the future of mathematics, where AI and human mathematicians work together to verify and extend mathematical knowledge.

## Key Points

- The Classification of Finite Simple Groups, a 15,000-page human proof, was fully formalized in Lean by an AI swarm, generating 43.1 million lines of code in 24 days. [00:10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZklSBoLC30&t=646)
- The formalization uncovered and corrected errors in the original proof, including a significant flaw in the unbalanced group theorem, demonstrating AI's ability to verify and improve human mathematics. [00:14:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZklSBoLC30&t=888)
- The project used a swarm of AI agents coordinated by humans, with a proof map visualization that allows navigation of the entire proof structure, akin to Google Maps for mathematics. [00:24:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZklSBoLC30&t=1464)
- The formalization revealed that the original proof contained 34 significant discrepancies, including a counterexample in Aschbacher and Smith's quasithin groups work, which were corrected with expert input. [00:31:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZklSBoLC30&t=1916)
- The success of this project signals a new era in mathematics where AI can formalize and verify massive collaborative proofs, potentially making the lone mathematician era obsolete. [00:37:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZklSBoLC30&t=2260)
