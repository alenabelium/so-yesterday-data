# Five Stages of Grief for Mathematicians Facing AI

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander reviews Kevin Buzzard's article on the five stages of grief mathematicians are experiencing as AI transforms the field. Buzzard argues that while many mathematicians are in denial, anger, bargaining, or depression, he remains optimistic, believing AI will push mathematics to new frontiers. He challenges the notion that mathematics is solely about human understanding, advocating for releasing AI-generated results to the public. Alexander endorses Buzzard's perspective, encouraging mathematicians to embrace the change and move forward.

## Key Points

- Kevin Buzzard outlines the five stages of grief—denial, anger, bargaining, depression, and acceptance—as a framework for how mathematicians are reacting to AI's impact on the field. [00:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TFHKXLzGh38&t=67)
- Buzzard challenges the idea that mathematics is primarily about human understanding, pointing out that even he doesn't fully understand his own work, and argues that the theorem tally is a more measurable goal. [00:19:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TFHKXLzGh38&t=1183)
- Buzzard calls for OpenAI to release its collection of theorems publicly, and Alexander endorses this, arguing that mathematicians have a right to know what is already known. [00:20:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TFHKXLzGh38&t=1245)
