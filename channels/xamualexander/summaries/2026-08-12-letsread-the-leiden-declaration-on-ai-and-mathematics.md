# LetsRead The Leiden Declaration on AI and Mathematics

## Executive Summary

The video is a critical reading of the Leiden Declaration on AI and Mathematics, a document drafted by mathematicians to address the impact of AI on the field. The speaker summarizes the declaration's preamble, values, and recommendations for individuals, organizations, governments, and AI developers, while interjecting skepticism about its practical impact. He argues that the declaration is largely a defensive reaction by established mathematicians to protect their status, and he doubts it will influence the commercial AI industry. The video concludes with the speaker's view that such declarations are futile against the tide of technological progress.

## Key Points

- The Leiden Declaration originated from a pub conversation and was developed over eight months by a working group, aiming to address AI's threats to mathematical values. [00:01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCrL38ebnLE&t=72)
- The declaration outlines core values of mathematics—proof, authorship, transparency, evaluation, and community—and warns that AI threatens each of these, especially for young mathematicians. [00:07:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCrL38ebnLE&t=428)
- Recommendations for mathematicians include disclosing AI use, maintaining responsibility for correctness, citing properly, and choosing tools carefully, while organizations are urged to lead on publication policies and support public research labs. [00:12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCrL38ebnLE&t=778)
- The declaration calls on governments to protect authors' rights, regulate AI, and invest in public computing infrastructure, and asks AI developers to respect the values outlined. [00:20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCrL38ebnLE&t=1213)
- The speaker criticizes the declaration as a futile attempt by established mathematicians to preserve their status, noting that it won't affect the commercial AI industry and that even the AMS doesn't follow its own advice. [00:25:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCrL38ebnLE&t=1511)
