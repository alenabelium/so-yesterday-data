# AI sets a new record for best result on lengths of prime gaps, verified in Lean

## Executive Summary

An AI system, GPT-5.6, has achieved a new record in mathematics by improving the best-known lower bound for large prime gaps, surpassing the previous result by Ford, Green, Konyagin, Maynard, and Tao. The proof, which introduces a novel sieving procedure, has been formally verified in the Lean proof assistant, making it unconditionally correct. While the exposition is criticized as poor and partly derivative, experts like Ben Green confirm the result is a genuine and independent improvement, potentially worthy of an Erdős prize. This development highlights the growing role of AI in mathematical discovery and the challenges of translating machine-generated proofs into human-readable work.
