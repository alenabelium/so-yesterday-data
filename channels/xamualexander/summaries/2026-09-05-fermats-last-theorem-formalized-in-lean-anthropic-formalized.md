# Fermat's Last Theorem formalized in Lean! Anthropic formalized math's most famous proof in 11 days

## Executive Summary

Anthropic's AI, Claude, formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in the Lean proof assistant in just 11 days, a task that was expected to take years and was the subject of a £1 million grant to mathematician Kevin Buzzard. The proof, which is over 13 million lines of Lean code, follows a simplified version of Wiles's proof and was verified by Lean, marking the completion of a 20-year-old formalization challenge. While the formalization adds no new mathematical insight, it demonstrates the potential of AI to auto-formalize complex mathematics, which could revolutionize proof verification and the review process. The achievement also raises questions about the future role of human mathematicians and the feasibility of formalizing modern research on the fly.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's Claude formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in 11 days, completing a 20-year-old benchmark and outpacing Kevin Buzzard's 5-year, £1 million project. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6mkOu6Zlnk&t=0)
- The proof, over 13 million lines of Lean code, follows a simplified version of Wiles's proof and was verified by Lean, but it only covers primes ≥17, with the remaining cases already formalized by others. [00:02:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6mkOu6Zlnk&t=156)
- Kevin Buzzard emphasizes that while the formalization adds no new mathematical insight, it demonstrates the potential for AI to auto-formalize large swaths of mathematics, which could transform proof verification and the review process.
