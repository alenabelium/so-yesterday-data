# AI progress on Twin Primes Conjecture: Three competing breakthroughs in the last 3 days

## Executive Summary

In this video, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses three rapid breakthroughs on the twin primes conjecture within a few days, where AI-driven groups like OpenAI and Axiom Math improved the bound on prime gaps from 246 to 186. He highlights the competitive dynamics, the role of AI in accelerating mathematical research, and the implications for the future of mathematics.

## Key Points

- Three major developments in two days: Julia Stollmann's 240 bound, Axiom Math's 212 bound, and OpenAI's 186 bound, showing rapid AI-driven progress. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6d04h0UqyH0&t=0)
- Axiom Math, a small lab, improved Stollmann's method to 212, likely using AI, and OpenAI rushed out 186 shortly after, highlighting competitive pressure. [00:02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6d04h0UqyH0&t=163)
- OpenAI's 186 result includes a Lean formalization, though not fully standalone, and the video emphasizes that AI in mathematics is here to stay. [00:18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6d04h0UqyH0&t=1080)
