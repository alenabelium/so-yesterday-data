# The better-than-O(n log n) integer multiplication community (math of the future)

## Executive Summary

The video discusses a recent OpenAI proof that integer multiplication can be done in better than O(n log n) time, and highlights a grassroots community website called 'Beyond Enlaw' where AI-generated proofs are progressively improving the algorithm's exponent. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, explores the philosophical implications of this community-driven, version-controlled approach to mathematics, contrasting the contingent, human-dependent nature of the community's progress with the Platonic ideal of mathematical truth. He also presents a theoretical meta-algorithm that could achieve the limit of these improvements, but notes it is impractical and requires an oracle or a fixed AI and proof checker to be considered a purely mathematical result.

## Key Points

- OpenAI published an AI-generated proof on October 6, 2026, showing integer multiplication can be done in O(n log^(1-2^-182) n) time, a tiny improvement over O(n log n). [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uwX_0fIhDtk&t=0)
- A community on the 'Beyond Enlaw' website has rapidly improved the exponent, with the best kappa now at 1.48 * 2^-11, using a Git-based workflow and AI-generated proofs. [00:01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uwX_0fIhDtk&t=76)
- The limit of these improvements (kappa infinity) is contingent on human choices and is a physical, not mathematical, question; a purely mathematical version requires fixing an AI and a version of Lean. [00:08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uwX_0fIhDtk&t=484)
