# 25(!) Fields Medalists co-author "A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics"

## Executive Summary

25 Fields Medalists signed a declaration warning that AI companies' use of mathematical problem-solving as a benchmark is severely misaligned with the goals of the mathematical community, which values conceptual understanding and insight over mere problem-solving. The declaration argues that the mass production of solutions without proper write-ups and community integration could destroy the fertile ground of mathematics and raise attribution issues. The video host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, analyzes the declaration, compares AI to telescopes for exploring the Platonic realm of ideals, and discusses community reactions, predicting that mathematics will evolve into a more collaborative, less credit-driven field.

## Key Points

- The declaration, signed by 25 Fields Medalists, argues that AI companies' push to solve mathematical problems as a benchmark is detrimental to mathematics, as it prioritizes problem-solving over conceptual understanding and insight. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IBw_tyshNgY&t=0)
- The host compares AI to telescopes, suggesting that mathematics will become more like astronomy where amateurs can make significant discoveries, and that mathematicians will use AI to gain deeper insights into the Platonic realm of ideals. [00:11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IBw_tyshNgY&t=687)
- The host predicts that the mathematical community will split into those who refuse AI and those who embrace it, with the latter likely becoming more productive, leading to a shift away from credit-driven mathematics toward a more collaborative approach. [00:23:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IBw_tyshNgY&t=1406)
