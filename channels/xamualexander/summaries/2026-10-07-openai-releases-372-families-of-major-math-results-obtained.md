# OpenAI releases 372 families of major math results obtained using AI!

## Executive Summary

OpenAI has released 372 families of major mathematical results produced by an internal frontier model, including solutions to significant open problems like Hilbert's 10th problem over the rationals and the quasi-Riemann hypothesis. The results are published on GitHub with some proofs formalized in Lean for computer verification. This release marks a historic moment for AI in mathematics, though some results lack formal verification and may be subject to error.

## Key Points

- OpenAI releases 372 families of major math results, including Hilbert's 10th problem over the rationals and the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, marking a historic day for mathematics. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQ3ejiLdMug&t=0)
- The results are published on GitHub with some proofs formalized in Lean, enabling scalable verification, unlike fields like anthropology. [00:02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQ3ejiLdMug&t=177)
- Some results, such as Catalan's constant irrationality, are Lean-verified, while others like Hilbert's 10th problem lack formal verification, leaving room for potential errors. [00:15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQ3ejiLdMug&t=929)
