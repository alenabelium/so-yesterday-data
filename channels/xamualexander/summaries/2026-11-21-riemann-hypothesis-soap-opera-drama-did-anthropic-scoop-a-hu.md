# Riemann Hypothesis Soap Opera Drama! Did Anthropic scoop a human mathematician?

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses the recent claim that Anthropic's AI proved the generalized Riemann Hypothesis, which was quickly followed by a human mathematician's competing proof announcement. He analyzes the drama surrounding the human proof by Alessandro Masulo, endorsed by Ravi Vakil and Brian Conrad, and highlights an AI audit that found fundamental errors in the 940-page draft. Alexander predicts the human proof will fail but welcomes the increased public interest in mathematics that such controversies generate.
