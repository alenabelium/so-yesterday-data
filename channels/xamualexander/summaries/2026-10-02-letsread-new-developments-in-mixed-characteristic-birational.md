# LetsRead "New Developments in Mixed Characteristic Birational Geometry and Commutative Algebra"

## Executive Summary

This video is a read-through of a survey article on recent advances in mixed characteristic birational geometry and commutative algebra, focusing on the arithmetic minimal model program. The presenter explains the minimal model program's goal of decomposing algebraic varieties into simpler building blocks, the role of singularities and splinters, and the recent proof of the three-dimensional minimal model program over mixed characteristic rings. The discussion highlights key breakthroughs like the direct summand conjecture, Bhatt's theorem on Cohen-Macaulay absolute integral closures, and the use of perfectoid and prismatic cohomology.

## Key Points

- The minimal model program aims to decompose algebraic varieties into simpler pieces (Fano, Calabi-Yau, and general type) via fibrations, up to birational transformations. [00:05:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94TAGx_Oub0&t=327)
- The direct summand conjecture, proven by André in 2018 using perfectoid spaces, is a key driver of recent advances in mixed characteristic commutative algebra. [00:23:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94TAGx_Oub0&t=1407)
- Bhatt's theorem on the Cohen-Macaulay property of absolute integral closures is a fundamental component in proving the mixed characteristic minimal model program. [00:28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94TAGx_Oub0&t=1695)
