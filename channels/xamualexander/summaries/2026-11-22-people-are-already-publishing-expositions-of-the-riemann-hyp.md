# People are already publishing expositions of the Riemann Hypothesis proof

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses the recent AI-generated proof of the Riemann Hypothesis by Anthropic's AlpGate, which was released as a 13-million-line Lean archive. He reviews three human-readable expositions of the proof and uses an AI teaching harness called Learn Harness to get a 20-minute overview of the proof's strategy. The video highlights the rapid pace of AI-driven mathematical progress and the potential of AI tools to make complex proofs accessible to non-experts.
