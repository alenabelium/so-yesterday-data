# LetsRead "Fundamentals and Applications of Coding Theory" (by Hiram H. López)

## Executive Summary

This video is a walkthrough of a survey article on coding theory by Hiram H. López, published in the Notices of the AMS. The presenter, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, explains the fundamental concepts of error-correcting codes, including Hamming distance, linear codes, finite fields, and key families like Reed-Solomon and Reed-Muller codes. He also covers advanced topics such as algebraic geometry codes, evaluation codes, and modern research questions like list decoding and coded matrix multiplication. The video serves as an accessible introduction to the mathematical foundations and applications of coding theory.

## Key Points

- Coding theory began over 75 years ago to transmit information reliably, and now underpins technologies like QR codes, 5G, and solid-state drives. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da7z0w2wC-w&t=0)
- The Hamming distance between two vectors is the number of positions where they differ, and it is central to designing codes that can correct errors. [00:02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da7z0w2wC-w&t=131)
- Reed-Solomon codes are maximum distance separable (MDS) codes used in QR codes, DVDs, and deep-space communication. [00:19:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da7z0w2wC-w&t=1186)
- Reed-Muller codes are another important family, used in early deep-space missions and 5G networks for small data packets. [00:25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da7z0w2wC-w&t=1507)
- Algebraic geometry codes, like the Hermitian code, provide asymptotically good families that surpass the Gilbert-Varshamov bound. [00:32:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Da7z0w2wC-w&t=1962)
