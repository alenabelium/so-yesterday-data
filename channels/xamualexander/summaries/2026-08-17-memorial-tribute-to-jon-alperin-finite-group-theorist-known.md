# Memorial Tribute to Jon Alperin, finite group theorist known for simple elegant proofs

## Executive Summary

This memorial tribute honors Jonathan L. Alperin, a distinguished finite group theorist known for his simple yet powerful proofs, who passed away in 2025. The video recounts his life, education, and major contributions, including Alperin's fusion theorem and the Alperin weight conjecture, which have had a lasting impact on group theory. It also highlights his mentorship, collaborative spirit, and the deep respect he earned from colleagues and students.

## Key Points

- Alperin's fusion theorem, a simple but powerful result, unified approaches to simplicity criteria and inspired Frobenius categories and p-fusion systems. [00:03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KJ8cg3FfCqI&t=194)
- The Alperin weight conjecture, proposed in 1986, remains open and has driven extensive research in modular representation theory. [00:06:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KJ8cg3FfCqI&t=415)
- Alperin mentored 23 doctoral students and was known for his open-mindedness, humor, and willingness to listen, leaving a lasting legacy in the mathematical community. [00:09:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KJ8cg3FfCqI&t=574)
