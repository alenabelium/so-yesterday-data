# AI Mathathon switches focus to math exposition instead of proving new results

## Executive Summary

The AI Mathathon, originally announced by Caltech undergrads and met with an open letter of criticism from established mathematicians, has been redesigned to focus on mathematical exposition and finding new proofs of old problems rather than proving new results. The event now emphasizes human understanding, encourages full disclosure of AI usage, and is funded by Terence Tao's Foundation for Science and AI Research and XTX Markets after OpenAI and Anthropic withdrew or were turned down. The redesign reflects a broader community shift toward valuing conceptual understanding and exposition, though the commentator warns that if AI proves better at exposition too, mathematicians may have nowhere left to retreat.

## Key Points

- The Mathathon has been redesigned to focus on 'old problems, new proofs,' celebrating human mathematicians who undertake exposition and understanding, not just problem-solving. [00:01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BKmxlIUiSMw&t=86)
- Participants will have 40 hours to learn about a problem with an unintuitive solution, then two months to develop an alternative proof or exposition, submitting an explainer and a GitHub repository with AI chat histories and code. [00:03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BKmxlIUiSMw&t=235)
- The event is now co-organized with Terence Tao's Foundation for Science and AI Research and funded by XTX Markets, after OpenAI withdrew funding and Anthropic's remaining funding was turned down. [00:07:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BKmxlIUiSMw&t=436)
