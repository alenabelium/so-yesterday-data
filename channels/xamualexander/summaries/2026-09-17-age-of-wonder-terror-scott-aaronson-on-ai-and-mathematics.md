# Age of Wonder & Terror: Scott Aaronson on AI and Mathematics

## Executive Summary

Scott Aaronson reflects on the rapid advancement of AI in mathematics, arguing that the singularity has already begun, evidenced by AI solving major open problems like the Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem. He discusses the implications for human mathematicians, the shift in their roles, and the need to preserve human understanding and community in the face of AI's dominance. The video also explores the controversy around AI-assisted proofs, the response from the mathematical community, and the broader existential questions about the future of human mathematical endeavor.

## Key Points

- Aaronson asserts that the singularity has already started, citing AI's recent achievements in solving major mathematical problems as evidence that the 'wild prophecies' have come true. [00:03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8QXhDHTVsw&t=213)
- The Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem appears to be solved with crucial AI contributions, but the case without external force remains unresolved, highlighting the ongoing role of human mathematicians. [00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8QXhDHTVsw&t=485)
- Aaronson discusses the changing landscape of mathematical research, where AI is now central to proving theorems, and the community grapples with questions of credit, authorship, and the preservation of human understanding. [00:19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8QXhDHTVsw&t=1150)
