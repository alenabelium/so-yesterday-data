# Could YOU be the ancestor of almost all future humans? The Identical Ancestor Point Axiom

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander presents the Identical Ancestor Point (IAP) axiom, a mathematical principle suggesting that for any biological species, each organism is either the ancestor of almost all future members or of almost none, assuming the species persists indefinitely. He argues that this axiom, formalized from earlier work by Joseph Chang and Willy Hennig, is plausible through three arguments: the unfair advantage of descendant groups, population modeling, and cladistic principles. The objective species theorem further shows that different species definitions converge when they satisfy the IAP axiom and convexity, revealing an objective, genealogical basis for species. The talk concludes that if your descendants never go extinct, you will eventually become the ancestor of nearly all future humans.

## Key Points

- The IAP axiom states that for any organism in a species, either its descendants eventually dominate the species or go extinct, assuming the species persists forever. [00:02:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPWUEK8CZW0&t=149)
- Three arguments support the IAP axiom: the unfair advantage of descendant groups, Joseph Chang's population modeling showing common ancestry within ~33 generations, and Willy Hennig's principle that species never split permanently. [00:07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPWUEK8CZW0&t=429)
- The objective species theorem shows that any two species definitions satisfying the IAP axiom and convexity yield essentially identical species, making species more objective than previously thought. [00:19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPWUEK8CZW0&t=1150)
