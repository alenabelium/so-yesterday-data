# Good Proofs Make Us Wiser: Old interview with Yuri Manin touching on computerized proofs

## Executive Summary

This video analyzes a 1998 interview with mathematician Yuri Manin, highlighting his prescient views on mathematics, proofs, and the role of computers. Manin argues that proofs are essential to mathematics and that good proofs make us wiser, while also discussing the cultural role of mathematics and the challenges of funding pure research. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, contextualizes Manin's insights with modern developments in AI and formal proof systems like Lean, offering both agreement and critique.

## Key Points

- Manin argues that proofs are the only way to communicate mathematical truth, and that good proofs make us wiser, while computer calculations only prove tested cases. [00:11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cc52_khwNx8&t=688)
- Manin predicts that mathematics will be increasingly influenced by computers, but he believes that proofs will remain central, and that computer-assisted proofs are acceptable if they are good proofs. [00:13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cc52_khwNx8&t=798)
- Manin discusses the cultural role of mathematics, arguing that it is a special linguistic activity and that the public's alienation from Enlightenment values threatens funding for pure mathematics. [00:27:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cc52_khwNx8&t=1635)
