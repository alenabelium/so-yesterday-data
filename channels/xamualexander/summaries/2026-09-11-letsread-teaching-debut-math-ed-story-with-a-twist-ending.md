# LetsRead "Teaching Debut" (math ed story with a twist ending)

## Executive Summary

This video is a reading of Alvaro Lozano Robledo's article 'Teaching Debut' from the Notices of the AMS, which recounts the author's nervous first day as a math teaching assistant at Boston University. The story captures his anxieties about teaching in a second language, the unhelpful TA orientation, and the small personal details of his life at the time. The narrative takes a dramatic turn when, immediately after his first class, the September 11 attacks unfold, putting his worries into perspective. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, adds personal reflections on his own teaching experiences and the recurring dreams that haunt math teachers.

## Key Points

- The article begins with the author's intense nervousness before his first math TA class, compounded by teaching in English and his lack of public speaking experience. [00:00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npyY8yebVdQ&t=58)
- The TA orientation was largely useless, with the main advice being 'do not date your students,' and the teaching sample feedback was just 'good' and 'next.' [00:03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npyY8yebVdQ&t=212)
- After his first class, which went better than expected, the author discovers the September 11 attacks unfolding, which drastically shifts his perspective on his trivial worries. [00:13:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npyY8yebVdQ&t=828)
