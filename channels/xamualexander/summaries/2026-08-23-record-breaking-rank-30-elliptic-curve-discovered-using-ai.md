# Record-breaking rank-30 elliptic curve discovered using AI

## Executive Summary

A record-breaking elliptic curve with rank at least 30 was discovered using AI, surpassing the previous rank-29 record. The discovery, attributed to Levent Alpige and Eva Howell, was likely made with a universal AI like Claude, highlighting AI's growing role in mathematical research. The finding challenges existing heuristics about the boundedness of elliptic curve ranks and underscores the rapid pace of AI-driven mathematical breakthroughs.

## Key Points

- A new elliptic curve with rank ≥30 was discovered, breaking the previous record of 29, and under the generalized Riemann hypothesis and BSD, the rank is exactly 30. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-baZfeHNBPI&t=1)
- The discovery was made by Levent Alpige and Eva Howell, and it appears to have been found using a universal AI, similar to how AlphaZero learned chess without specialized knowledge. [00:01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-baZfeHNBPI&t=71)
- The finding challenges existing heuristics that suggested high-rank curves like 28, 29, and 30 were unlikely, and it may push the field toward believing ranks are unbounded. [00:04:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-baZfeHNBPI&t=261)
- The rank of an elliptic curve is the number of generators of its infinite part, and higher-rank curves are extremely rare, with most having rank 0 or 1. [00:05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-baZfeHNBPI&t=350)
- The discovery highlights how quickly AI is advancing mathematics, as this record-breaking result would have been front-page news months ago but now barely registers on Reddit. [00:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-baZfeHNBPI&t=772)
