# Expository Normal Form Proofs for Post-AI Math 2.0

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander proposes 'expository normal form' as a new standard for mathematical proofs in a post-AI era where machines solve open problems. This format requires theorems to be preceded by lemmas that are either intuitively obvious or supported by compelling narratives, making the main proof easy to follow. He argues this approach addresses the overwhelming volume of AI-generated papers by prioritizing human understanding over exhaustive formal detail, while acknowledging criticisms about rigor and the risk of plausible but false statements. The video includes examples (fundamental theorem of calculus and algebra) and a discussion of Hacker News comments debating the trade-offs between narrative and verification.

## Key Points

- Expository normal form requires a theorem to be preceded by lemmas that are either intuitively obvious or explained via a compelling narrative, making the main proof easy to follow. [00:02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QXHfiXl2B0&t=154)
- The fundamental theorem of calculus is proved using the mean value theorem as an intuitively obvious lemma, with the proof reduced to a telescoping sum. [00:05:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QXHfiXl2B0&t=316)
- The fundamental theorem of algebra is proved using Cauchy's integral formula and Liouville's theorem, where the non-obvious lemmas are supported by a narrative about the holographic nature of complex analysis. [00:09:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QXHfiXl2B0&t=563)
- Critics on Hacker News raise concerns about the format's reliance on plausible narratives over rigorous verification, but supporters argue it improves accessibility and aligns with how most readers engage with mathematics. [00:16:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QXHfiXl2B0&t=980)
- The format is proposed as a constructive response to the 'mathematical apocalypse' of July 2026, where AI solves open problems and mathematicians must shift focus to exposition. [00:00:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QXHfiXl2B0&t=48)
