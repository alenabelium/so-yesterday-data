# LetsRead "A Stroll Through the Pentagram Zoo" (by Richard Evan Schwartz)

## Executive Summary

This video is a walkthrough of Richard Evan Schwartz's paper 'A Stroll Through the Pentagram Zoo,' which connects mathematics and art through the pentagram map and its variants. The presenter explains the mathematical concepts behind the art piece 'Blue Harmony,' including the pentagram map, bird maps, K-birds, and the associated theorems about shrinking polygons and triangulations. The video highlights the aesthetic appeal of the generated images and argues that pure mathematics can be a form of art.

## Key Points

- The pentagram map is a dynamical system on convex polygons that shrinks them exponentially fast to a point, a result proven by the author. [00:02:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i0p9HD2mBUE&t=129)
- Bird maps are variants of the pentagram map that preserve certain non-convex polygons called K-birds, leading to visually striking 'flapping bird' images. [00:08:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i0p9HD2mBUE&t=518)
- The art piece 'Blue Harmony' is generated by a bird map with parameters K=17, N=55, chosen to balance wildness and order, illustrating quasi-periodicity. [00:23:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i0p9HD2mBUE&t=1410)
