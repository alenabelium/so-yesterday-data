# Claude AI deciphers 370-year-old encrypted message, the Cyphral Distich

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses how Claude AI (Fable 5.1) solved a 370-year-old unsolved cipher, the Cyphral Distich, by discovering that the key was the book itself rather than an external alphabet. The AI used clues like the 32 procurations and the poem's promise to decode a royalist prayer for King Charles II. The video also covers a second, larger cipher that was partially solved, and reflects on the implications for AI's ability to conduct original research and self-directed problem-solving. Alexander connects this to his own work embedding a cryptogram within a philosophical dialogue, illustrating the broader potential of AI in historical and cryptographic research.

## Key Points

- Claude Fable 5.1 solved the 370-year-old Cyphral Distich by realizing the key was the book itself, using the 32 procurations and the poem's clues to decode a royalist prayer. [00:02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtA8Y6llTQM&t=121)
- The AI also partially deciphered a second, larger cipher from 'The Jewel' (1652), recovering a royalist prayer with only nine letters missing. [00:04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtA8Y6llTQM&t=283)
- The author highlights that the AI's success came from persistence and self-direction, choosing which problem to solve, which suggests potential for autonomous research. [00:09:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtA8Y6llTQM&t=568)
