# LetsRead: "No Strangers At This Party" (by Veselin Jungić), a math education article

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander reviews an article by Veselin Jungić about teaching Ramsey theory to non-mathematics majors in a course called Mathematical Journeys at Simon Fraser University. The article highlights the use of creative semester projects, such as podcasts, videos, and games, to engage a diverse student body. Alexander reflects on the value of making advanced mathematics accessible, the importance of popularizing math, and shares personal connections to Ramsey theory.
