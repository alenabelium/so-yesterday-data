# Math 2.0: Terence Tao responds to the recent OpenAI flood of proofs

## Executive Summary

Terence Tao responds to OpenAI's release of nearly 400 AI-generated mathematical proofs, arguing that the traditional ecosystem of talks, workshops, and community building around breakthroughs is being lost. The video's host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, critiques Tao's perspective as elitist, defends the value of AI-generated proofs, and explores broader implications for mathematics, academia, and creativity. The discussion includes Hacker News comments debating AI's role in original thinking, the future of mathematical exposition, and whether AI will eventually replace human mathematicians.

## Key Points

- Terence Tao describes the traditional 'Math 1.0' process where breakthroughs generate talks, workshops, and community activity, contrasting it with the current AI-driven flood of proofs that lack follow-up engagement. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBUCv-nimL4&t=0)
- The host argues Tao's view is biased because elite mathematicians like Tao receive attention that average researchers do not, and that AI is actually bringing new people into mathematics. [00:01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBUCv-nimL4&t=89)
- Tao suggests 'Math 2.0' should decenter raw problem-solving and elevate exposition, community building, and new directions, but the host warns this strategy relies on AI not becoming better at exposition than humans. [00:06:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBUCv-nimL4&t=405)
