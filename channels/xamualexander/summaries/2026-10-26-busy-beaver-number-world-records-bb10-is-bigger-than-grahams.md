# Busy Beaver Number World Records. BB(10) is bigger than Graham's number!

## Executive Summary

The video discusses recent breakthroughs in the busy beaver function, a central concept in computability theory, where new records have been set with the aid of AI. It highlights that a 230-state Turing machine's halting is independent of ZFC, and that the 10th busy beaver number exceeds Graham's number, a result achieved by Jeffrey Ketcheson using Claude code agents. The video also explores the controversy within the busy beaver community about AI's role, and concludes with the presenter's own research on 'biological busy beaver numbers'.

## Key Points

- A 230-state Turing machine has been found whose halting is independent of ZFC, meaning set theory cannot determine its behavior. [00:03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mUslD_jtV4&t=218)
- Jeffrey Ketcheson used Claude code agents to prove that the 10th busy beaver number is larger than Graham's number, a significant improvement over the previous 13th bound. [00:10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mUslD_jtV4&t=652)
- The busy beaver community debates the role of AI, with some questioning the human contribution, but the presenter argues that AI cannot fully solve the uncomputable busy beaver function. [00:16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3mUslD_jtV4&t=1017)
