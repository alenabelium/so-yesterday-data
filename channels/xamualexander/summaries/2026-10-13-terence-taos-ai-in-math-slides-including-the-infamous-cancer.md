# Terence Tao's AI-in-Math slides (including the infamous cancer slides *facepalm*)

## Executive Summary

The video is a critical commentary by Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander on Terence Tao's slides about AI's impact on mathematics. Alexander praises the potential of AI to expand mathematical frontiers but strongly criticizes Tao's slides for being disingenuous, politically motivated, and scientifically flawed, particularly the infamous cancer thought experiment. He argues that Tao's concerns about AI harming mathematics are actually about protecting mathematicians' jobs and institutional incentives, and he rejects claims that AI cannot produce consistent applied mathematical solutions. Alexander also highlights the irony that mathematicians are now trying to appropriate concepts like experimental mathematics that were developed in the AI/ML community.

## Key Points

- Alexander welcomes the era of abundant proofs and AI's potential to expand mathematical frontiers, comparing it to the invention of photography leading to cinema. [00:09:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9YXOgvP34&t=558)
- He criticizes Tao's claim that AI harms mathematics as disingenuous, arguing it's about protecting mathematicians' salaries and institutional incentives. [00:10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9YXOgvP34&t=637)
- Alexander rejects Tao's slide implying AI cannot produce consistent applied mathematical solutions, calling it a bold statement lacking evidence. [00:22:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9YXOgvP34&t=1320)
- He condemns the cancer thought experiment as showing profound ignorance of medicine, noting that terminally ill patients routinely accept experimental treatments. [00:25:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9YXOgvP34&t=1554)
- Alexander points out that mathematicians are now trying to appropriate concepts like experimental mathematics that were developed in the AI/ML community. [00:36:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9YXOgvP34&t=2193)
