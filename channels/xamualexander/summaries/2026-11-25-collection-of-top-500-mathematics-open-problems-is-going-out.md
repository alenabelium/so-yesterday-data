# Collection of Top 500 mathematics open problems is going out of date faster than they're updating it

## Executive Summary

The video critiques a website called Proof Atlas that ranks the top 500 open mathematical problems by importance using AI, arguing that the list is already outdated because major problems like the Riemann hypothesis and Navier-Stokes equations have recently been solved by AI. The creator emphasizes the rapid pace of mathematical progress, which makes static rankings obsolete quickly, and calls for a more frequently updated alternative. He also reflects on the organic nature of mathematical importance versus centralized rankings, and notes the increasing overlap between mathematics and computer science.

## Key Points

- The video introduces Proof Atlas, an AI-ranked list of the top 500 open math problems, and immediately notes that the Riemann hypothesis has already been solved, making the list outdated. [00:01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML90bg1vaAA&t=68)
- The list ranks P vs. NP as the most important problem, which the creator agrees with, but he criticizes the superficial explanation and notes the complexity of related problems like P vs. PPAD. [00:02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML90bg1vaAA&t=134)
- The creator points out that the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem is also already solved by Anthropic, further proving the list's obsolescence. [00:05:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML90bg1vaAA&t=339)
- He calls for a competitor to Proof Atlas that updates regularly, emphasizing the need for real-time tracking of mathematical progress. [00:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ML90bg1vaAA&t=685)
