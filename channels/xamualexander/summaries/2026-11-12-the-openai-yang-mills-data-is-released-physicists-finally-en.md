# The OpenAI Yang-Mills data is released! Physicists finally end the embargo

## Executive Summary

The OpenAI Yang-Mills construction data has finally been released by the Yang-Mills Shepherd group after a month-long embargo, which had sparked controversy. The release includes a 35-page validation paper by the working group, which confirms that the submission proves the existence of a quantum field theory satisfying the Wightman axioms via a scaling limit of lattice gauge theory, but does not prove uniqueness or a positive mass gap. The working group admits they used OpenAI's Astra model to assist in digesting the Lean code and do not claim to fully understand the proof yet. The video discusses the implications, the community's reaction, and the ongoing debate about whether this constitutes meaningful progress toward the Yang-Mills Millennium Prize problem.
