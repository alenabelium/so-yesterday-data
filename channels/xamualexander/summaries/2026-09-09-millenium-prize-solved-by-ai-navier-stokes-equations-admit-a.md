# Millenium prize solved by AI! Navier-Stokes equations admit a singularity, OpenAI announces

## Executive Summary

OpenAI announces that its internal AI system has solved the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem by proving that the equations can develop a singularity in finite time, a result formalized in Lean. The video discusses the technical nature of the proof, the massive multi-agent effort behind it, and the controversy over whether OpenAI improperly used prompts from Anthropic researchers. The host also reflects on the broader implications for AI progress, the future of mathematics, and the ethical landscape of training on user data.

## Key Points

- OpenAI claims its internal model solved the Navier-Stokes problem, proving finite-time singularity, with a Lean formalization. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Irl9aAZaJfk&t=0)
- The solution was found by a multi-agent system with ~10,000 concurrent agents, using 130 billion output tokens over 88 hours. [00:05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Irl9aAZaJfk&t=350)
- OpenAI acknowledges concurrent work by Anthropic and NYU researchers but denies accessing their data, while admitting de-identified data may have been used. [00:10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Irl9aAZaJfk&t=651)
