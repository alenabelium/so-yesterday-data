# AI classifies the biologically unavoidable sequences

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses how an AI system solved an open problem he posed in 2013, proving that biologically unavoidable gender sequences are exactly the eventually periodic ones. He explains the classification theorem, the elegant construction for avoiding non-periodic sequences, and the AI's verification of the proof in Lean. The video highlights the growing role of AI in mathematical discovery and reflects on the significance of this achievement.

## Key Points

- AI solved an open problem posed by Dr. Alexander, proving that a sequence is biologically unavoidable if and only if it is eventually periodic. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGnh24_zuEw&t=0)
- The proof constructs a population that avoids any non-eventually periodic sequence, using a clever graph where each vertex has two parents with complementary genders. [00:11:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGnh24_zuEw&t=686)
- The AI's proof is verified in Lean, and the construction is elegant enough to be considered part of Erdős's 'Book'. [00:24:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGnh24_zuEw&t=1487)
