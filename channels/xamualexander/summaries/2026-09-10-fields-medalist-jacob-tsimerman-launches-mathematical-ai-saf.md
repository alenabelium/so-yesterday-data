# Fields medalist Jacob Tsimerman launches Mathematical AI Safety Institute (MAISI)

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the launch of the Mathematical AI Safety Institute (MAISI) by Fields medalist Jacob Tsimerman, which aims to bring mathematicians into AI safety research. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, reviews the institute's goals, structure, and key personnel, while raising questions about funding and the practicality of relocating to the Bay Area. He also critiques the subjective nature of current AI risk assessments and emphasizes the need for rigorous mathematical foundations. The video highlights the potential for mathematicians to contribute to formalizing AI safety concepts, though it notes the challenges and uncertainties involved.

## Key Points

- Jacob Tsimerman, a 2026 Fields Medalist, announces the founding of MAISI to bring mathematicians into AI safety research, with plans to hire 10-30 mathematicians by January and scale to 30-100 by September 2027. [00:01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6yKoqCxl_0&t=79)
- The institute aims to provide rigorous mathematical foundations for AI safety, addressing the lack of precise definitions and measurements in the field, similar to how mathematics underpins physics and statistics. [00:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6yKoqCxl_0&t=366)
- MAISI will function as a research institute with visiting positions, fostering collaboration between mathematicians and AI safety experts, and will prioritize rapid sharing of ideas over traditional academic publishing timelines. [00:12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6yKoqCxl_0&t=723)
