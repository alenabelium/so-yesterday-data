# ETH conjecture disproved by OpenAI AND Anthropic on the same day?!

## Executive Summary

This video discusses the simultaneous, independent disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH) by OpenAI and Anthropic on October 16, 2026. The presenter explains the significance of the result, which invalidates hundreds of papers that relied on ETH, and highlights that AI has created new open problems rather than just solving them. He compares the two papers, noting differences in presentation and space guarantees, and discusses the implications for complexity theory, including the lack of practical algorithms due to their 'galactic' nature. The video also touches on priority disputes and the need for cryptographic hashes to establish discovery dates.

## Key Points

- OpenAI and Anthropic independently disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis on the same day, October 16, 2026, with both results formalized in Lean. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbxC0412cys&t=0)
- The disproof invalidates over 800 papers that assumed ETH, turning their results into open problems and demonstrating that AI can create new mathematical questions. [00:29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbxC0412cys&t=1740)
- The new algorithms are 'galactic'—they only outperform brute force for inputs with more than 10^10,000 variables, so they have no practical application. [00:30:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbxC0412cys&t=1801)
- The presenter suggests that both companies should have published cryptographic hashes to prove their discovery dates, as the coincidence of the same day raises questions about priority. [00:05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbxC0412cys&t=308)
