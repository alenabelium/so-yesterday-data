# OpenAI announces independent AI-in-Math Advisory Group

## Executive Summary

OpenAI announced an independent advisory group on mathematics and AI, following its claim of solving over 100 open mathematical problems, including the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem. The group, hosted at the Institute for Advanced Study, includes prominent mathematicians like Timothy Gowers and Edward Witten, and aims to advise on the review and communication of results while operating independently from OpenAI. The video discusses the controversy surrounding this move, including concerns about AI benchmarking, the role of mathematicians, and the potential for AI to disrupt the field. The host analyzes reactions from the mathematical community, weighing the risks of cooperation versus inaction, and reflects on the future of mathematics as a discipline.
