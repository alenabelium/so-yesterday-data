# Live Demo of what the next qualitative step toward AGI will look like

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander argues that current AI is far from AGI, using a mock-up to illustrate the next qualitative step: an AI that can generate custom, task-specific models on demand. He contends that true AGI would threaten the jobs of AI company employees themselves, which current technology does not. The demo shows the AI creating specialized models (e.g., for 1960s pop lyrics, medical diagnosis) and even self-improving versions, but Alexander emphasizes this is pure science fiction today. He concludes that since such capabilities are unrealistic, AGI itself remains unrealistic.

## Key Points

- We are not close to AGI; the mock-up demonstrates a capability far beyond current AI, which is to generate custom models on demand. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzmZTjvWObw&t=0)
- The demo shows the AI creating a lightweight model for 1960s pop lyrics, which correctly refuses to answer about Isaac Newton because it's outside its training data. [00:03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzmZTjvWObw&t=236)
- True AGI would threaten the jobs of AI company employees, as it would include the ability to design better models, a task currently done by humans. [00:06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzmZTjvWObw&t=418)
- The mock-up also demonstrates self-improvement, where the AI creates a better version of itself with more parameters and context window, but this is pure science fiction. [00:09:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzmZTjvWObw&t=541)
