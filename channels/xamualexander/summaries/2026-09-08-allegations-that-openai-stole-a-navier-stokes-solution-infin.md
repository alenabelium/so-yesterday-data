# Allegations that OpenAI stole a Navier-Stokes solution?? Infinite drama blowup in finite time!

## Executive Summary

The video discusses allegations that OpenAI may have used user prompts from mathematicians Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alpoge to train models that produced a proof for the Navier-Stokes problem, potentially stealing their work. The host details the timeline of events, including OpenAI's urgent meetings and proposals to remove Alpoge from authorship, and highlights the broader implications for AI in mathematics and the ethics of training on user data. Despite the drama, the host emphasizes the monumental nature of AI solving major mathematical problems and compares the situation to historical priority disputes like Newton vs. Leibniz.

- Tristan Buckmaster and Levent Alpoge claim to have made significant progress on fluid dynamics problems using AI, but explicitly state they have not solved the Navier-Stokes problem yet. [00:01:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VaJlOhX8Ss4&t=73)
