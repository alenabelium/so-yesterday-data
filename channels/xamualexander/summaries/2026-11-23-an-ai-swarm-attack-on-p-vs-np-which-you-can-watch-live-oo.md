# An AI-swarm attack on P vs. NP which you can watch live O_O

## Executive Summary

This video showcases a live, AI-driven swarm attack on the P vs. NP problem, where a coordinated fleet of agents formulates, proves, and disproves mathematical lemmas in real time, with results verified in Lean. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, explores the interactive website, highlighting the dynamic nature of the research, the verification process, and the potential for public participation. He expresses cautious optimism about the swarm's ability to make progress on this famous open problem, while marveling at the evolution of mathematical research.

## Key Points

- The swarm has conducted 2,899 agent sessions with 123 agents currently working on P vs. NP, and the process is live-streamed for public viewing. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OIfrauVmEY&t=1)
- The website displays lemmas and hypotheses, some verified in Lean, such as the lemma about real matrices with dimension at most P+Q-2, which can be tested by users. [00:03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OIfrauVmEY&t=191)
- The fleet viewer shows 130 agents working, with cells containing open lemmas and a connection graph, and 350 lemmas have been verified so far. [00:06:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OIfrauVmEY&t=379)
- The swarm also proposes hypotheses, like L meta 094, which are not yet verified, and the site includes a 'cemetery' for approaches that hit barriers. [00:09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OIfrauVmEY&t=593)
- The site plans to allow public participation, similar to SETI@home, enabling users to join the swarm and contribute to the research. [00:12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OIfrauVmEY&t=734)
