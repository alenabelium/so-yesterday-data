# AI makes progress on the Riemann Hypothesis! Huge improvement on lower bound of zeroes on the line!

## Executive Summary

This video discusses a recent AI-driven improvement on the Riemann Hypothesis, where Anthropic's Claude model increased the proven lower bound of zeros on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.25%. The result, verified in Lean, was achieved through a massive multi-agent effort involving 60 subagents and 31 million tokens. The video highlights the significance of this breakthrough, the methodology used, and the broader implications for AI's role in mathematical research.

## Key Points

- AI improved the lower bound of zeros on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.25%, a significant leap in progress on the Riemann Hypothesis. [00:01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qebxsdjT5c&t=74)
- Claude generated and rejected ~650 ideas, coordinated 60 subagents, executed 2,400 shell commands, and used 31 million tokens to achieve the result. [00:02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qebxsdjT5c&t=179)
- The result was formally verified in Lean, and the paper is only five pages long, showcasing the efficiency of AI-driven mathematical discovery. [00:15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qebxsdjT5c&t=927)
