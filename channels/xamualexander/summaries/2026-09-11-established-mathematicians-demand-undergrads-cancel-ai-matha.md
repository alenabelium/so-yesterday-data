# Established mathematicians DEMAND undergrads cancel AI Mathathon. NO FUN ALLOWED!

## Executive Summary

The video discusses a controversy at Caltech where 771 mathematicians signed an open letter demanding the cancellation of the AI Mathathon, an event where undergrads use AI to solve open math problems. The host criticizes the letter as gatekeeping and fear of job loss, praising the organizers' professional response that addresses concerns with added verification and publication requirements. He argues that mathematics has intrinsic value regardless of how results are obtained and that the event is a legitimate experiment in AI-assisted research.

## Key Points

- An open letter with 771 signatories demands Caltech cancel the AI Mathathon, calling it destructive and accusing AI companies of research misconduct. [00:00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgvECyNdugY&t=24)
- The Mathon organizers respond professionally, adding a six-month verification period and requiring arXiv publication to address concerns. [00:14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgvECyNdugY&t=871)
- The host argues that mathematics has objective value independent of human effort, and that the Mathon is a legitimate experiment that should be welcomed. [00:25:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgvECyNdugY&t=1510)
