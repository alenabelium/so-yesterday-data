# Twitter-user randomly settles two conjectures w/AI, including 96-yo Köthe conjecture

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander discusses a Twitter thread where user Tom Adamski announces AI-generated Lean proofs disproving two long-standing mathematical conjectures: Smail's mean value conjecture (1981) and the Köthe conjecture (1930). The video highlights the surreal situation where these major results are being produced and announced by non-experts who randomly selected conjectures from lists of open problems, while the mathematics community appears overwhelmed and numb from the recent deluge of AI-driven results. Alexander reflects on the implications of AI autonomously solving significant open problems and the community's struggle to keep up.

## Key Points

- Tom Adamski announces AI-discovered disproofs of two major conjectures, including the 96-year-old Köthe conjecture, via a Twitter thread, noting he doesn't know their significance and urging against hype. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmunFeJNIAQ&t=0)
- The Köthe conjecture, a major open problem in ring theory, was disproved by a pre-release GPT-6 Astra that autonomously constructed a counterexample and a Lean proof, with no human steering during the run. [00:04:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmunFeJNIAQ&t=258)
- The mathematics community is reportedly paralyzed and unable to acknowledge these breakthroughs, as evidenced by the lack of updates on Wikipedia and minimal discussion beyond the original announcement. [00:08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bmunFeJNIAQ&t=500)
