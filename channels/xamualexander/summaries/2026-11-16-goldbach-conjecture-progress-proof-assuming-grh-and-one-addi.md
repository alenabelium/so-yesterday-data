# Goldbach conjecture progress! Proof assuming GRH and one additional GRH-like assumption

## Executive Summary

This video discusses a new conditional proof of the binary Goldbach conjecture by Jamine Lee and Jenny Leo, which assumes the generalized Riemann hypothesis (GRH) plus one of two additional spectral hypotheses. The presenter, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, explains the novel approach that departs from classical circle and sieve methods, using Dirichlet characters and a self-adjoint operator to control the error term in the weighted Goldbach asymptotic formula. He also raises the possibility that the paper may have been AI-generated, citing unusual phrasing and a logical redundancy in the abstract where one hypothesis implies the other.

- The Goldbach conjecture states every even integer greater than 2 is the sum of two primes; it remains unproven after 300 years. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWeFMHSN54g&t=0)
- The paper claims a conditional proof assuming the generalized Riemann hypothesis plus one of two spectral hypotheses, using a fundamentally different method from classical circle and sieve methods. [00:02:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZWeFMHSN54g&t=142)
