# Mathocalypse! Scott Aaronson on OpenAI's flood of AI math breakthroughs

## Executive Summary

The video discusses OpenAI's release of nearly 400 AI-generated mathematical breakthroughs, including a proof of the Unique Games Conjecture and other major results in complexity theory. The host, Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, reacts to Scott Aaronson's blog post, highlighting the shock and awe of the mathematical community, the creative nature of the proofs, and the differing publication strategies of OpenAI and Anthropic. The video explores the implications for mathematicians, the potential for AI to solve even more problems, and the broader philosophical and practical questions raised by these developments.

## Key Points

- OpenAI released 372 major mathematical results, including a proof of the Unique Games Conjecture, which was solved in about three hours by AI, shocking experts like Scott Aaronson's wife Dana Moshkovitz. [00:02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StluAE0HQOM&t=144)
- The AI's proofs are described as 'alien' and creative, challenging the notion that AI cannot invent new mathematics, and many results, like Hilbert's 10th problem over the rationals, are considered monumental. [00:05:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StluAE0HQOM&t=322)
- OpenAI and Anthropic took different approaches to releasing AI math results: OpenAI dumped raw proofs, while Anthropic paid experts to digest them, with OpenAI's approach winning more public attention. [00:17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StluAE0HQOM&t=1058)
