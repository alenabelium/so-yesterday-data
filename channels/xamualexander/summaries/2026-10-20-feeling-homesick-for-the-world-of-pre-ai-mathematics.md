# Feeling homesick for the world of pre-AI mathematics

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander reflects on the emotional transition from pre-AI to post-AI mathematics, expressing both excitement for the rapid breakthroughs and a deep sense of homesickness for the old world of mathematical discovery. He discusses the obsolescence of traditional mathematical skills, the democratization of mathematics through AI, and the challenges faced by independent researchers. Despite the grief and fear, he finds consolation in his pre-AI publications and looks forward to the new era, acknowledging that the change is irreversible.

## Key Points

- The speaker feels homesick for pre-AI mathematics, comparing it to moving from a poor country to a rich one, and notes that this change is irreversible. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjq04yzWDdI&t=0)
- AI has made traditional mathematical skills obsolete, with AI proving theorems better than humans, and the speaker shares personal experiences of struggling with problems that AI now solves trivially. [00:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjq04yzWDdI&t=145)
- The speaker expresses grief and fear for the future of mathematicians, especially independent researchers, and finds solace in his pre-AI publications, which now hold a special status. [00:15:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjq04yzWDdI&t=912)
