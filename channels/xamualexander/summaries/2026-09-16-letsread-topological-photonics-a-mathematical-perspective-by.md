# LetsRead "Topological Photonics: A Mathematical Perspective" (by Parker and Aceves"

## Executive Summary

Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander reviews the paper "Topological Photonics: A Mathematical Perspective" by Parker and Aceves, which introduces the field of topological photonics and its mathematical foundations. The paper explains how topological invariants like winding numbers, Berry phases, and Chern numbers characterize robust photonic edge states, using the SSH model, Haldane model, and a photonic Floquet topological insulator as examples. Alexander highlights the deep connections between continuous and discrete mathematics and emphasizes the potential for mathematicians to contribute to this emerging field.

## Key Points

- Topological photonics uses topology to design photonic materials with robust, unidirectional light transport, analogous to topological insulators in condensed matter physics. [00:01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJUss1crtaU&t=79)
- The SSH model demonstrates how a winding number distinguishes topologically distinct phases, leading to edge modes in finite lattices. [00:10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJUss1crtaU&t=615)
- The Haldane model extends these concepts to 2D, where the Chern number changes at a topological transition, and the photonic Floquet topological insulator realizes these ideas experimentally. [00:36:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJUss1crtaU&t=2207)
