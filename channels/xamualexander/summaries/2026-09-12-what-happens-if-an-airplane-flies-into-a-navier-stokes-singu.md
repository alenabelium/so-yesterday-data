# What happens if an airplane flies into a Navier-Stokes singularity?

## Executive Summary

The video explores a hypothetical scenario where an airplane encounters a singularity in the Navier-Stokes equations, which AI has reportedly solved. The narrator, a mathematician, uses a flight simulation to illustrate how such a singularity would manifest as extreme, localized turbulence and structural failure. The video explains the mathematics behind the singularity, including vorticity, pressure, and energy scaling, while emphasizing that the aircraft would disintegrate under finite but unequal loads before reaching the mathematical infinity. It concludes with philosophical reflections on the aircraft's material becoming part of the fluid.

- The singularity creates extreme velocity gradients and vorticity, leading to unequal aerodynamic loads that threaten to tear the aircraft apart. [00:07:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OL4HSxHVDWk&t=467)
