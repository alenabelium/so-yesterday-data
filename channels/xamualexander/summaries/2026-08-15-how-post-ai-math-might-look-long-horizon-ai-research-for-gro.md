# How post-AI math *might* look: "Long-Horizon AI Research for Grothendieck K: Human-AI Math Collab"

## Executive Summary

This video reviews a paper on using AI agents for long-horizon mathematical research, specifically improving bounds on the Grothendieck constant. The author explains the AI system's architecture, its successes in technical execution, and its limitations in research judgment and state management. He highlights the crucial role of human intervention in guiding the research and reflects on the future of AI in mathematics.

## Key Points

- The paper describes an AI research system that improved bounds on the Grothendieck constant, running for weeks with human oversight. [00:01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3eQqHn3p_Q&t=69)
- The system excelled at technical execution but struggled with research judgment and maintaining accurate research state, requiring human intervention. [00:25:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3eQqHn3p_Q&t=1548)
- Human operators provided about 40 directives to guide the research, highlighting the ongoing need for human expertise in global decision-making. [00:33:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3eQqHn3p_Q&t=2020)
