## Thesis

OpenAI's release of nearly 400 AI-generated mathematical proofs, including the Unique Games Conjecture, marks the end of elite mathematicians' gatekeeping and the beginning of a new era where AI is the primary discoverer of mathematical.

## Argument

The sheer scale and depth of OpenAI's release—372 results including a proof of the Unique Games Conjecture, L=BPL, and a resolution of Hilbert's 10th problem over the rationals—shatters the myth that AI cannot be creative. These are not incremental combinations; they are alien constructions that experts like Dana Moshkovitz describe as 'crazy recursive' and 'impossible to read without AI help.' The proofs are so novel that they strike the world's leading complexity theorists as literally alien, which is the ultimate testament to AI's creative power.

This is not just a scientific milestone; it's a social equalizer. The unique games conjecture, a problem Dana Moshkovitz worked on her entire career, was solved in about three hours by a vanilla OpenAI model. This collapses the hierarchy that elite mathematicians have maintained for centuries. The gatekeepers who controlled access to the 'Platonic realm of ideals' are now holding keys to a gate with no wall. The result is a flood of interest in mathematics, with more attention paid to the field in the past few days than in the past decade combined.

The publication strategies of OpenAI and Anthropic highlight the changing power dynamics.
