## Thesis

The Riemann Hypothesis has been proven and verified by AI, with the Lean and Mathlib communities stepping up as the true leaders of mathematics, while official institutions fail.

## Argument

The proof, released by Alpagay on November 20, 2026, consists of 17 million lines of Lean code. The Lean and Mathlib communities have certified its correctness, checking the code with multiple independent proof checkers, including Lean's official kernel and third-party checkers. They have also verified that the statement matches Mathlib's formalization, addressing the common pitfall of misstating the theorem. This is a historic moment where AI surpasses human mathematicians, akin to Deep Blue beating Kasparov. The communities' grassroots leadership fills a power vacuum left by official institutions, which have been dazed and confused, even endorsing a crank proof. The proof is now a theorem, and the conjecture is put to rest.

## Counterpoints

- Some might doubt the proof due to potential bugs in Lean or ZFC, but the community has checked the proof with independent checkers and formal proofs of their soundness.
- The official leadership of mathematics, like the AMS president, has failed to lead, endorsing a flawed proof, while the grassroots communities step up.
