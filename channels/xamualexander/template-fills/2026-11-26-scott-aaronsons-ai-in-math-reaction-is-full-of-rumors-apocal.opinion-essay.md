## Thesis

AI is not merely accelerating mathematics; it is collapsing the ivory tower, and the field of complexity theory is being transformed so fast that even its leading experts are struggling to keep up.

## Argument

The transformation is happening at a pace that is rewriting the rules of mathematical discovery. Scott Aaronson's blog post, titled with a string of profanity, is a testament to the shockwaves. The proofs of the Riemann hypothesis and the exact value of omega, the exponent of matrix multiplication, are not just incremental advances; they are complete answers to questions that have stood for decades. This is a shift from slowly illuminating problems with a 'drip drip drip of clues' to 'straight up answered'. The mechanism is the AI's ability to provide 'lean certificates', verifiable proofs, which has changed the working assumption: any proof from OpenAI or Anthropic is now considered correct. This is leading to a 'whole new continent of mathematical truth', a new Platonic realm being explored with the equivalent of a Hubble telescope, where previously we were squinting with our naked eyes. The implications are profound: the 'zoology' of complexity classes will start to go extinct as we can finally prove things about the problems we actually care about, and the AI labs are also developing algorithms, some of which could break current crypto systems. The world is changing, and the old guard must embrace it, not deny it.

## Counterpoints

- Elite academics who despise AI companies for disrupting their careers are told not to 'inhale the copium' and to embrace reality.
- The counterpoint that these are just one-off solutions is refuted by the sheer volume and pace of breakthroughs, suggesting a new era of discovery.
- The concern that proofs might be flawed is addressed by the working assumption that any proof from OpenAI or Anthropic is correct, barring information to the contrary.
- The fear that cryptography will be broken is acknowledged, with Scott saying he is 'virtually certain' the labs are sitting on bombshells, but he is cautious about specifics.
