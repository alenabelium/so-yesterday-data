## Thesis

The human proof of the generalized Riemann Hypothesis will fail, but the resulting drama is a net positive for mathematics.

## Argument

The drama began when Anthropic's AI proved the generalized Riemann Hypothesis, only to be followed by a human mathematician's competing claim. The human proof, by Alessandro Masulo, was endorsed by Ravi Vakil and Brian Conrad, but an AI audit found fundamental errors. The audit identified a specific counterexample to a crucial lemma, not just a gap, but a false statement. This is not a case of a rough draft with minor issues; the proof is fundamentally broken. Masulo has a history of failed proofs, and Conrad's endorsement is not reliable. The desire for a human proof biases many to give Masulo undue benefit of the doubt. However, this controversy is excellent for mathematics, bringing unprecedented public attention and fresh interest to the field.

## Counterpoints

- Some argue the proof is just an incomplete draft and the AI audit is unfair, but the audit found a false lemma, not just a missing detail.
- Others trust the endorsements of Ravi Vakil and Brian Conrad, but Conrad has endorsed Masulo's failed proofs before.
- There are conspiracy theories that Anthropic stole Masulo's work, but the proof approaches are totally different.
