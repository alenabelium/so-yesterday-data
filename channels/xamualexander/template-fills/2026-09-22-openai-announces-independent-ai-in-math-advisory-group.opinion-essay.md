## Thesis

OpenAI's independent AI-in-Math Advisory Group is a calculated olive branch that mathematicians should accept, despite risks, because refusing it would accelerate their irrelevance.

## Argument

The mechanism is reputational and strategic. OpenAI, having announced over 100 solved open problems including Navier-Stokes, is extending a diplomatic gesture to a community that recently flailed with an open letter from 25 Fields Medalists. The advisory group, hosted at the IAS, includes heavyweights like Gowers and Witten, and is designed to give mathematicians a voice while explicitly not advising on internal pacing. This is a PR move, but it's a successful one: OpenAI looks mature, and mathematicians get a seat at the table.

The alternative is worse. If mathematicians refuse to cooperate, OpenAI will publish results anyway—they don't need the community's permission. The public narrative already paints mathematicians as Luddites protecting job security. Refusing the olive branch only confirms that image and speeds up the field's marginalization. As one commenter notes, "If you're not at the table, you're on the menu." The castle of mathematical privilege is already shattering; cooperation offers a chance to shape how results are communicated and how academia reforms.

Moreover, the fear that AI will replace mathematicians is overblown. The host argues that pure math PhDs were already a poor career choice for most, and that the real value lies in loving the subject. AI will change the work, but mathematicians who adapt—like those using AI for exposition and learning—will remain relevant. ## Counterpoints

- OpenAI is cynically using mathematicians for legitimacy and crisis management, as Burt Totaro suggests.
- The advisory group cannot change OpenAI's behavior, so cooperation is futile.
- Mathematicians could collectively refuse to serve, delegitimizing OpenAI's math enterprise.
- The group's independence is compromised by perks like free access to pro models, as with Timothy Gowers.
- A PhD student fears their work will be rendered meaningless by AI, making the profession untenable.
