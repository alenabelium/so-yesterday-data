## TL;DR

The Classification of Finite Simple Groups (CFSG), a 15,000-page human proof, was fully formalized in Lean by an AI swarm from Axiom in 24 days, generating 43.1 million lines of code. The process uncovered and corrected errors in the original proof, including a significant flaw in the unbalanced group theorem. This marks a new era where AI and human mathematicians collaborate to verify and extend mathematical knowledge.

## Evidence and caveats

The formalization uncovered 34 significant discrepancies, including a counterexample to a lemma in Aschbacher and Smith's quasithin volumes, where an equality was corrected to an inequality. The most significant issue was in Walter's proof of the unbalanced group theorem, where a closure statement was found false; an alternative argument using Solomon's 1976 theory was developed. Human experts like Lyons and Solomon were consulted, and the corrections will be made public. The project also produced a new result, the 'Unbalanced Group Theorem' paper, and a periodic table of finite symmetry. Caveats: The proof is based on first-generation proof, not the second-generation (still incomplete). The formalization required significant human oversight and re-planning, and some results were initially built on unproven assumptions, leading to rewrites.
