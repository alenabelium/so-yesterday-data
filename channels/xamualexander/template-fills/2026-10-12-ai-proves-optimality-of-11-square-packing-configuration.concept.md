## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, using the Lean proof assistant. This marks a significant triumph for AI in mathematics, as the proof was too complex for a plain-English write-up. The same approach is poised to tackle other unproven configurations, like 17 squares.

The 1765-square configuration was recently improved multiple times, showing rapid progress.
