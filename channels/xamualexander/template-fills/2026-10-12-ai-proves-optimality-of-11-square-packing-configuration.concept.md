## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem. The proof is encoded in the Lean proof assistant, not plain English, marking a significant AI triumph in mathematics. This breakthrough signals a future where AI tackles even more complex, unproven packings.

## The argument

### The 11-Square Packing Is Optimal
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:19']
- **Claim**: The ugly configuration of 11 squares is the optimal packing, proven by AI. The key insight is a reflection that maps the ugly arrangement to a nicer one on a torus, but the mapping alone doesn't prove optimality.
- **Role**: definition

### Proof Lives in Lean, Not English
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:01']
- **Claim**: The proof is so complex that it wasn't written in plain English; instead, it's formalized in the Lean proof assistant. This is a significant AI triumph because no human would have the patience to formally verify such a proof, but AI has infinite patience.
- **Role**: evidence

### Many Packings Remain Unproven
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:06:09']
- **Claim**: While some packings like 3, 4, and 5 are proven, many others like 17, 28, and 29 are only conjectured. The website cataloging these packings hasn't been updated to reflect the 11-square proof, and the complexity grows with the number of squares.
- **Role**: counter

### AI Will Solve the Rest
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:08:53']
- **Claim**: Given AI's success with 11 squares, it's only a matter of time before AI proves or improves packings for 17, 1765, and beyond. This research program is like finding glitches in reality, and AI is perfectly suited for these tedious proofs.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The 11-square proof is formalized in Lean, not plain English, highlighting AI's role. Other packings like 10 squares have families of optimal solutions, proven by Walter Stromquist in 2003. The 17-square packing remains unproven, and many others like 28 and 29 are just hypotheses. The speaker notes that factories don't need proofs to use these packings, but proofs provide peace of mind.
