## TL;DR

AI has proven the optimality of the 11-square packing configuration, a long-standing open problem in mathematics. The proof was formalized in the Lean proof assistant, not in plain English, marking a significant triumph for AI in mathematics. This breakthrough suggests AI's infinite patience can tackle other unproven configurations, potentially revealing more 'glitches in reality.'

## Evidence and caveats

The 11-square proof is formalized in Lean, not plain English, indicating extreme complexity. Other packings like 10 squares have families of optimal solutions, proven by Walter Stromquist in 2003. The website cataloging packings hasn't been updated to reflect the 11-square proof. Many configurations, such as 17 and 1765 squares, remain unproven, but AI's infinite patience makes proving them only a matter of time. The speaker notes that while factories don't need proofs to use packings, proving optimality provides peace of mind.
