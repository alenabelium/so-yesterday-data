## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, using the Lean proof assistant. This marks a significant triumph for AI in mathematics, as the proof is too complex for plain English. The approach promises to tackle even larger, unproven configurations like 17 or 1765 squares.
