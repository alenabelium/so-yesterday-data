## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem in mathematics. The proof, embedded in Lean code rather than plain English, marks a significant triumph for AI in mathematics. This breakthrough suggests AI's infinite patience can tackle other unproven configurations, potentially revealing 'glitches in reality.'

## Evidence and caveats

The 11-square proof is formalized in Lean, not plain English, highlighting AI's role in handling complex proofs. Other configurations like 10 squares (proven by Stromquist in 2003) and 5 squares (proven by Gerbil in 1979) are known, but many remain unproven, e.g., 17, 28, 29. The speaker notes that the website cataloging packings hasn't been updated to reflect the 2026 proof. He also cautions that the animation alone doesn't prove optimality; the formal proof is necessary. Practical applications exist, but factories don't need proofs to use the packings.
