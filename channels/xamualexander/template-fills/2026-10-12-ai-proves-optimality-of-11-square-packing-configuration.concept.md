AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, using the Lean proof assistant. This marks a significant triumph for AI in mathematics, as the proof is too complex for plain English. The same AI approach could soon crack other unproven configurations, like 17 or 1765 squares.
