## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, by encoding the proof in the Lean proof assistant. This marks a shift where AI's infinite patience tackles proofs too complex for humans. The result validates a known configuration and hints at AI's potential to solve many more unproven packings.

## Evidence and caveats

The proof for 11 squares is formalized in Lean, not in plain English, indicating its complexity. The host notes that the paper itself does not contain the full proof but references the Lean code. Other packings like 17 and 1765 squares are unproven, and the host expresses doubt that any human would have the patience to prove them. However, the host also notes that factory owners can use these packings without proof, as the proof only provides peace of mind.
