## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, using the Lean proof assistant. This marks a significant triumph for AI in mathematics, as the proof is too complex for plain English. The same AI-driven approach could soon resolve many other unproven square-packing configurations.

## Evidence and caveats

The video catalogs many other square-packing configurations, from trivial cases like 3 and 4 squares to complex ones like 17, 241, and 1765 squares. Most remain unproven, with only a few proven by humans (e.g., 5 squares by Fritz Gerbil in 1979, 10 squares by Walter Stromquist in 2003). The host notes that the website hasn't been updated to reflect the 11-square proof, and he doubts any human would have the patience to prove the larger cases. He also cautions that the torus mapping animation does not prove optimality, and that the proof's complexity is why it's only available in Lean code.
