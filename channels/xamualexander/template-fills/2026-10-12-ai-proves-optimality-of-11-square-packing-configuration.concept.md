## TL;DR

AI has formally proven the optimal packing of 11 squares, a long-standing open problem, by encoding the proof in the Lean proof assistant. This marks a shift where AI's 'infinite patience' can tackle proofs too complex for humans. The achievement signals a new era for mathematics, with many other unproven packings (like 17 or 1765 squares) waiting for similar treatment.

## Evidence and caveats

The video catalogs many square packing configurations, noting that while some are proven (e.g., 5 squares by Fritz Gerbil in 1979, 10 squares by Walter Stromquist in 2003), others like 17, 29, and 1765 remain unproven. The host emphasizes that the 11-square proof is formalized in Lean, not in plain English, highlighting AI's role. He also notes that factories don't need to wait for proofs to use these packings, but proofs provide peace of mind. The host expresses excitement about AI's potential to tackle these problems, but also acknowledges that some configurations might be improved, not just proven optimal.
