## TL;DR

Aristotle's paradox of the wheel asks how a point on a larger circle can travel farther than a point on a smaller circle when both are part of the same rolling wheel. While mathematical solutions exist, the paradox is fundamentally psychological: our intuition expects both points to move the same distance. The true source lies in the mismatch between our straight-line perception and the actual cycloid paths traced by the points.

- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:25']

### Historical Influence
