## TL;DR

Three open math problems (311, 312, 313) ask whether AIs in a well-founded, computable hierarchy can know the truthfulness, codes, and mechanicalness of all agents, including superiors. These problems extend Reinhardt's and Carlson's results on self-knowledge. If AI solves them, it would be AI learning about AI.

## The argument

### Define the Hierarchy and Its Knowledge Properties
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:10:24']
- **Claim**: AIs arranged in a well-founded, computably enumerable org chart. Each AI can know truthfulness, codes, or mechanicalness of subordinates, self, or all. The 27-question matrix covers combinations; three remain open.
- **Role**: definition

### Reinhardt's Impossibility and Carlson's Possibility
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:02:54']
- **Claim**: Reinhardt proved a truthful AI that knows its own truthfulness cannot know its own code. Carlson proved it can know it is a machine without knowing which one. This distinction underpins the hierarchy questions.
- **Role**: evidence

### My Contribution: Knowing One's Own Code Without Truth-Knowledge
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:08:40']
- **Claim**: Using Kleene's recursion theorem, an AI can know its own code if it is ignorant of its own truthfulness, while still being truthful. This relaxes Reinhardt's assumption and opens the door for hierarchy generalizations.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Open Problems 311, 312, 313
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:18:53']
- **Claim**: 311: each AI knows everyone's truthfulness, codes of subordinates, mechanicalness of subordinates. 312 adds self-mechanicalness. 313 adds mechanicalness of all. All are open; 313 likely hardest.
- **Role**: synthesis

### AI Solving Problems About AI
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:21:57']
- **Claim**: If AI solves these, it would be AI learning about AI, increasing our understanding. This is a unique opportunity because the problems are about AI itself.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Reinhardt's negative result for self-code knowledge is known. Carlson's positive result for mechanicalness without code knowledge is established. My dissertation affirmatively answers many questions, including 133 and 213, using patterns of resemblance. The bottom-right corner is impossible due to Reinhardt's result. The three open problems are wide open; no one has emailed solutions. Formal details require reading the dissertation, but AI can read it in seconds.
