## TL;DR

Three open math problems about AI ask whether machines in a well-founded hierarchy can know the truthfulness, codes, and mechanicalness of all agents, including superiors. These questions extend Reinhardt's and Carlson's results on self-knowledge. If AI solves them, it would deepen our understanding of AI itself.

## The argument

### Define the Hierarchy of Knowing Machines
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:10:24']
- **Claim**: A hierarchy of knowing machines is an org chart of AIs, well-founded (no loops, no infinite descent) and computably enumerable. Each AI can know truthfulness, codes, or mechanicalness of others, leading to 27 consistency questions.
- **Role**: definition

### Reinhardt's Impossibility and Carlson's Possibility
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:02:54']
- **Claim**: Reinhardt proved a truthful AI that knows its own truthfulness cannot know its own code. Carlson proved it can know it is a machine without knowing which one, via the strong mechanistic thesis.
- **Role**: evidence

### My Contribution: Knowing One's Own Code
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:08:40']
- **Claim**: By relaxing the requirement that the AI knows its own truthfulness, it becomes possible for a truthful AI to know its own code, using Kleene's recursion theorem.
- **Role**: evidence

### The 27 Questions and the Open 311-313
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:18:53']
- **Claim**: The matrix of 27 questions asks what each AI can know about subordinates, self, and superiors. The bottom-right corner is impossible due to Reinhardt's result; the top-right questions 311, 312, 313 remain open.
- **Role**: synthesis

### The Significance of AI Solving These Problems
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:21:57']
- **Claim**: If AI solves these open problems about AI, it would be an example of AI increasing our understanding of AI itself, which is divinely appropriate.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Reinhardt's impossibility result and Carlson's consistency proof for the strong mechanistic thesis are key. My own work shows a machine can know its own code if it doesn't know its truthfulness. The 27 questions are formalized in my dissertation; the open ones are 311, 312, 313. Caveats: the speaker notes formal details are omitted, and the exact meaning of 'knows' and 'truthful' requires reading the dissertation. The speaker has no idea whether the answers are yes or no.
