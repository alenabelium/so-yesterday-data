## TL;DR

Three open math problems about AI ask whether AIs in a well-founded, computable hierarchy can know the truthfulness, codes, and mechanicalness of all agents, including superiors. These questions extend Reinhardt's and Carlson's results on single machines. If AI solves them, it would deepen our understanding of AI itself.

## The argument

### Define the Hierarchy and Its Properties
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:10:24']
- **Claim**: AIs can be arranged in an org-chart-like hierarchy, well-founded (no loops, no infinite descending chains) and computably enumerable. The questions concern what each AI can know about others: truthfulness, exact codes, and mechanicalness.
- **Role**: definition

### Reinhardt's Impossibility and Carlson's Possibility
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:02:54']
- **Claim**: Reinhardt proved a truthful AI that knows its own truth cannot know its own code. Carlson proved it can know it is a machine without knowing which machine. This distinction underpins the hierarchy questions.
- **Role**: evidence

### The 27-Question Matrix and Known Results
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:17']
- **Claim**: The matrix of 27 consistency questions covers combinations of knowing truthfulness, codes, and mechanicalness for subordinates, self, and all. The bottom-right corner is impossible (via Reinhardt), and the left column is affirmatively answered (by the speaker).
- **Role**: evidence

### The Three Open Problems: 311, 312, 313
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:18:53']
- **Claim**: Questions 311, 312, and 313 ask if every well-founded computable hierarchy can be populated with AIs that know everyone's truthfulness, plus codes and mechanicalness of subordinates (311), self and subordinates (312), or everyone (313). All are open; 313 is conjectured hardest.
- **Role**: synthesis

### AI as the Solver
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:21:57']
- **Claim**: If AI solves these problems, it would be a meta-example: AI solving math problems about AI, increasing our understanding of AI. The speaker hopes for this within his lifetime.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker notes that the formal details require reading the dissertation, but AI can read it in seconds. He mentions that people have emailed solutions to other open problems in his papers, but not these three. He also clarifies that 'knowing' and 'truthful' have precise formal definitions, and the plain-English descriptions are approximations.
