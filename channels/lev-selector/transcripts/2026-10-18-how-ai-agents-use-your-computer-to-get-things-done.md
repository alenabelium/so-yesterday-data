# How AI Agents Use Your Computer to Get Things Done

**[00:00:00]** Imagine asking an AI to compare prices across five websites. Instead of giving you instructions, it opens a browser, visits the pages, finds the prices, and builds a table. How is that possible? A regular chatbot generates answers. But an AI agent with access to computer tools can take actions, too. It reads what's on the screen, decides what to do next, and sends a command. Click a button, scroll the page, or type some text. Then it checks the result and decides on its next move. But what if the button isn't where it expected? The agent can examine the updated screen and try another route. Still, it can make mistakes or get stuck. That's why a human should oversee important actions, especially payments or sharing personal information. Here's the key difference. A chatbot helps you understand how to do a task. An AI agent can carry it out step by step using the computer as a tool.
