# How to Spot AI Videos When They Look Perfect

## Executive Summary

This video explains how to identify AI-generated videos when they appear flawless, moving beyond superficial visual cues like misshapen fingers. It introduces three levels of verification: checking C2PA digital signatures, detecting invisible watermarks like Google SynthID, and independently verifying events through reverse image search and official sources. The key takeaway is that there is no single 'real' or 'fake' button; instead, the focus shifts to gathering independent evidence that an event actually occurred.

## Key Points

- C2PA provides a cryptographically signed record of a file's origin and edits, but a valid signature only verifies the integrity of claims, not that the event actually happened; absence of C2PA data is not proof of forgery. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hMzkEQhIzIs&t=0)
- Google SynthID embeds invisible watermarks in AI-generated content, and its detector can verify supported systems, but not all generators use it, so its absence doesn't mean the video is genuine. [00:01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hMzkEQhIzIs&t=76)
- Use the InVid WeVerify extension to extract keyframes and reverse image search them to find older uploads or original clips, and cross-check with official sources; a detector showing 99% AI is just a model estimate, not mathematical proof. [00:02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hMzkEQhIzIs&t=162)
