## Wider context

The industry is decisively shifting from training larger foundational models to engineering better [[agent-scaffolding]]. This is evidenced by Stripe's $8B acquisition of OpenRouter and the viral adoption of DeepSeek Harness. The competitive moat is no longer just model weights, but the modular, pluggable infrastructure that allows developers to route, optimize, and execute tasks across multiple models efficiently.
