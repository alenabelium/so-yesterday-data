## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw parameter counts to [[cost-efficiency]] in agentic workflows. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight architectures can match closed systems at a fraction of the cost, driven by optimized training data like agent trajectories. Simultaneously, infrastructure tools such as AWS Agent Harness and Bolt Needle are solving the [[swarm-cost]] problem, proving that parallelization and quantization (like Nvidia FP4) are now more critical differentiators than model size alone.
