## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw capability races to [[cost-efficiency]] and [[agentic-infrastructure]]. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight options can now match frontier performance at a fraction of the cost, forcing closed providers to compete on price. Simultaneously, tools like AWS Agent Harness and Bolt New Forge are standardizing the plumbing for multi-agent systems, shifting the bottleneck from model intelligence to orchestration reliability and token economics.
