## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw capability races to cost efficiency and agentic orchestration. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that performance parity can be achieved at a fraction of the cost, forcing legacy leaders to compete on price. Simultaneously, tools like AWS Agent Harness and Meta Muse shift the focus from single-model inference to managing complex, parallel agent swarms, where harness choice now dictates profitability more than model architecture.
