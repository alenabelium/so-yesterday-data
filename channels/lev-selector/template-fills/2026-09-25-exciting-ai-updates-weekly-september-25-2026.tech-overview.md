## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw model capability to [[cost-efficiency]] and [[agentic-infrastructure]]. Low-cost models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo are making high-volume token consumption viable, while frameworks like AWS Agent Harness and Bolt New Forge are standardizing how these agents are deployed. This shifts the competitive moat from proprietary model weights to orchestration reliability and harness optimization.
