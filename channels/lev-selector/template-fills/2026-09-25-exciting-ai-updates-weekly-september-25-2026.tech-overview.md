## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from pure performance races to [[cost-efficiency]] and [[agentic-orchestration]]. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight options can match closed-system intelligence at a fraction of the cost, forcing incumbents to compete on price. Simultaneously, tools like AWS Agent Harness and Bolt Needle shift focus from single-model capabilities to managing [[multi-agent-swarm]] costs and latency, making infrastructure optimization as critical as model selection.
