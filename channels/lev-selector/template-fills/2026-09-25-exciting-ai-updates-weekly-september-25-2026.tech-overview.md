## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw model capability to cost efficiency and agentic orchestration. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight options can now match or exceed closed systems at a fraction of the cost, driven by [[cost-efficiency]] techniques like quantization (Nvidia FP4) and context optimization. Simultaneously, platforms like AWS Agent Harness and Google's Family Agent signal that the next battleground is not just intelligence, but the reliable deployment of [[agentic-workflows]] in production and domestic environments.
