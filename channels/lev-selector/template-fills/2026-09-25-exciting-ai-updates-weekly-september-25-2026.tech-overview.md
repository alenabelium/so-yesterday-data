## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw model capability to [[cost-efficiency]] and [[agentic-orchestration]]. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight options can match frontier performance at a fraction of the cost, forcing closed systems to compete on price. Simultaneously, tools like AWS Agent Harness and Google's Family Agent show that value is shifting toward managing complex [[multi-agent-swarm]] workflows and specialized hardware integration rather than just single-model inference.
