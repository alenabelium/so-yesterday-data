## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from pure capability races to [[cost-efficiency]] as the primary differentiator. Models like Grog 4.7 and Somo Mimo demonstrate that open-weight architectures can achieve parity with expensive closed models at a fraction of the cost. This economic pressure forces incumbents to innovate on [[agentic-workflows]] and hardware optimization, such as Nvidia's FP4 format and AWS's agent harnesses, to maintain margins while expanding utility through parallelized swarms.
