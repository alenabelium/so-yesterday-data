## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from raw model capability to [[recursive-self-improvement]] and autonomous agent orchestration. Vendors like OpenAI and Anthropic are prioritizing self-referential system enhancements, while Google and Meta focus on efficiency and cloud-based office agents. This shift suggests the next gating constraint for AI adoption is not generation quality, but reliable, low-cost automation of complex workflows.
