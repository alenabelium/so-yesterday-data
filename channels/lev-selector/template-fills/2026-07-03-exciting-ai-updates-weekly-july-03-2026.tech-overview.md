## Wider context

The AI landscape is bifurcating: government gatekeeping is delaying public access to frontier models like GPT-5.6, while open-source efforts like Orith and Qwen are closing the capability gap. Simultaneously, the industry is rejecting complex abstractions like LangChain in favor of simpler, direct tools and local deployment, signaling a shift toward transparency and efficiency.
