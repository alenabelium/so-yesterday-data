## Wider context

The open-weight model sector is rapidly closing the gap with proprietary leaders like Claude and Opus, driven by cost-efficiency and performance parity. Simultaneously, the infrastructure layer is fragmenting as Vulkan and Mojo challenge Nvidia's CUDA monopoly, enabling high-performance AI inference across diverse hardware platforms and reducing vendor lock-in.
