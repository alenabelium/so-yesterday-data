## Wider context

The industry is shifting from single-model dominance to [[agentic-orchestration]] and [[stateful-memory]]. OpenRouter Fusion and Perplexity Brain Memory demonstrate that accuracy and continuity are now achieved through multi-model ensembles and persistent memory layers rather than just raw model scale. This is reinforced by Databricks' Agent Bricks and the rise of [[loop-engineering]], where agents manage their own workflows. Simultaneously, the [[spaceX-cursor-acquisition]] signals a consolidation of AI tooling under aerospace giants, while GLM 5.2 proves open-weight models can match closed SOTA.
