## Wider context

The industry is shifting from raw model capability wars to [[harness-engineering]] and [[multi-agent-orchestration]]. With GLM 5.2 proving open-source models can match frontier coding performance, the differentiator becomes how models are orchestrated and deployed. This is compounded by geopolitical restrictions slowing US releases, accelerating the prominence of Chinese models and inference-focused infrastructure.
