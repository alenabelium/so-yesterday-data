## Wider context

The open-source stack, led by Chinese variants like Qwen and Deepseek, is rapidly closing the gap with closed models on both capability and cost. This shift is driven by hybrid architectures that reduce memory requirements, enabling high-performance inference on consumer hardware. Simultaneously, the industry is moving from static model benchmarks to agentic workflows, as evidenced by Nvidia's ARC-AGI success and OpenAI's Codex integration.
