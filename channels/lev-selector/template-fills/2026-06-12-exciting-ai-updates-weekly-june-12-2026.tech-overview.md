## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from raw model capability to [[agent-architecture]] and data infrastructure. Anthropic's move to expensive API pricing for Fable 5 signals a consolidation of power, while the choice of Gemini for Siri highlights the strategic value of multimodal foundations. Meanwhile, Databricks' rise underscores that distributed data processing is the critical bottleneck for serious AI, not just the models themselves.
