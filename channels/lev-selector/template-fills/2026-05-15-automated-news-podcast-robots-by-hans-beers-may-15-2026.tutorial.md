## Headline takeaway

Automate daily news podcasts using Gemini and NotebookLM, but add a validation step to prevent misinformation.

## Prerequisites

### Gemini Pro Account
- **Kind**: account
- **Note**: Paid Gemini Pro subscription ($20/month) to access daily update automation features.

### NotebookLM
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: Google's notebook tool for managing sources and generating audio/video content.

### NLM CLI
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: Command line interface suggested by Gemini to interface with NotebookLM programmatically.

### Mobile Device
- **Kind**: hardware
- **Note**: iPhone or iPad with the Gemini app installed to receive daily update notifications.

## Steps

### Prompt Gemini for Daily Updates
- **Timestamp**: [09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G232ByDw4Ho&t=561)
- **Action**: Ask Gemini to create a daily update script based on a specific topic like AI or life sciences from sources like arXiv.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Prompt: 'how can I automate making a daily podcast for new article for a certain topic'

- **Timestamp**: [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G232ByDw4Ho&t=880)

- **Choice Branch**: Use the Python script generated by NotebookLM for programmatic control.

## Gotchas

### AI amplifies 'garbage in, garbage out' risks; sources like arXiv are non-peer-reviewed and may contain fabricated or erroneous data.
- **Severity**: serious
- **Timestamp**: [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G232ByDw4Ho&t=442)

### Missing validation step in current AI pipelines can lead to the spread of misinformation or fabricated scientific results.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [06:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G232ByDw4Ho&t=399)

### Hallucinations in LLMs can fabricate sources, leading to incorrect content generation.
- **Severity**: serious
- **Timestamp**: [07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G232ByDw4Ho&t=442)
