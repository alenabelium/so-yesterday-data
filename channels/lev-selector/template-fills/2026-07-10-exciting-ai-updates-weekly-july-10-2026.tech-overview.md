## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from passive chatbots to active, autonomous agents that manage business workflows. This shift is driven by two key efficiencies: cost reduction via model routing and speculative decoding (DeepSeek Dispark), and knowledge management simplification via Open Knowledge Format. These technical deltas lower the barrier for deploying persistent, organization-wide AI entities.
