## Wider context

The AI landscape is shifting from raw model size to efficiency and orchestration. [[neuro-symbolic-ai]] and [[recurrent-architectures]] are challenging transformer dominance by offering better energy efficiency and performance. Simultaneously, the role of the engineer is evolving into that of an [[agent-orchestrator]], managing swarms of autonomous agents rather than writing code directly. [[mlops-tools]] like W&B and MLflow are becoming critical infrastructure to manage this complexity.
