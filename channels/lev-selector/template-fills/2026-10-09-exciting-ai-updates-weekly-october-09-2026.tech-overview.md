## Wider context

The week's theme is the inversion of the OS: agents become the platform, apps become tools. This is reinforced by Microsoft, Google, and Manus all shipping agent-centric frameworks. Meanwhile, open-weight models (Mistral Large, Reflection Beam) and local runners (Strata) push frontier capability toward on-premises deployment, while agent memory and self-improvement (Devin, WCO AI) accelerate the shift toward autonomous systems. The rise of agentic token usage signals a fundamental change in how compute is consumed.
