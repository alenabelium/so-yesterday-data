## Wider context

The week marks a shift from applications hosting AI to AI hosting applications, with Microsoft and Google leading the agent-centric paradigm. Open-weight models from Mistral and Reflection push the frontier, while agentic coding and self-improving systems challenge human performance. The rise of agentic token usage and memory solutions signals a future where agents are persistent, learning entities.
