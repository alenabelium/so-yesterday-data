## Wider context

The week highlights a structural shift in AI economics and geopolitics. Chinese models like Qwen 3.8 and Kim 3 have reached parity with US leaders on benchmarks while undercutting them on cost, forcing a re-evaluation of 'total cost per task' over raw token price. Simultaneously, the US government's data sovereignty concerns are accelerating a global migration from Windows to Linux, while OpenAI's acquisition of Astral signals a consolidation of the Python tooling ecosystem under US AI giants.
