## Wider context

The industry is undergoing a dual shift: models are becoming cheaper through [[recursive-self-improvement]] techniques like those used by OpenAI, while infrastructure is moving toward Rust for memory safety and monolithic architectures for performance. This reduces reliance on expensive serverless cloud stacks and complex C++ codebases, prioritizing cost efficiency and developer safety.
