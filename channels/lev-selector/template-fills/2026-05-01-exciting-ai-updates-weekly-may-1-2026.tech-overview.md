## Wider context

The week underscores a shift: open-source Chinese models like DeepSeek V4 Pro are becoming the default for cost-sensitive businesses, challenging US proprietary dominance. Meanwhile, the reliability gap in agentic tools pushes the industry toward harnesses and validation layers, as seen with Hermas and the emphasis on CI/CD testing. This dual trend—cost-driven open-source adoption and reliability-focused engineering—signals a maturing AI ecosystem where efficiency and trust matter as much as raw capability.
