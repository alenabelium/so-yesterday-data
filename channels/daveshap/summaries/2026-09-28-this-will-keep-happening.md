# This will keep happening

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro argues that AI 'doomers' are engaging in performative theater rather than presenting serious evidence-based concerns about artificial intelligence risks. He contends that the industry's excitement over recent model releases indicates that these fears are not genuinely held by their proponents. Instead of focusing on existential threats, Shapiro emphasizes the practical reality of technological spillover effects and threshold jumps in AI capabilities.

## Key Points

- Shapiro critiques 'doomers' for lacking experimental evidence and engaging in 'vranyo,' a Russian concept where everyone pretends to believe a lie to move on, suggesting their fear is performative theater rather than serious analysis [00:01:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1qb3fMiE7s&t=104)
- He explains the economic concept of spillover effects, where technology reaches a threshold that unexpectedly enables new capabilities, such as AI now surpassing professional skills in animation and 3D graphics [00:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1qb3fMiE7s&t=323)
- The video concludes that while AI evolution is smooth, its impact occurs in discrete 'saltatory jumps' or threshold effects, meaning we should expect continuous surprise at the emerging opportunities rather than catastrophic failure [00:08:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1qb3fMiE7s&t=486) [MM:SS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1qb3fMiE7s&t=SECONDS)
