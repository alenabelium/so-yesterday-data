# ELI5: Post-Labor Economics

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro explains the concept of a post-labor economy, where AI and automation eliminate traditional jobs, and argues that household income must shift from wages to ownership-based sources like dividends, sovereign wealth funds, and employee stock ownership plans. He highlights that the current wage-labor distribution mechanism is failing, as productivity gains have not translated into wage growth, and proposes a multi-level portfolio of income streams for families. The video emphasizes that without consumer purchasing power, the economy risks a deflationary death spiral, so restructuring taxes and expanding ownership are essential. Shapiro concludes that the elements for this transition already exist and can be combined to ensure economic stability.

## Key Points

- Household income comes from three sources: wages, government payments, and property ownership; wages currently make up about 60% of American family income, but jobs are disappearing. [00:01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a65DF3N0EoU&t=62)
- Productivity has risen 94% since 1979 while average wages only grew 33%, and workers now receive less than 53 cents per dollar generated by businesses, showing the wage-labor system is failing. [00:02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a65DF3N0EoU&t=127)
- Solutions include sovereign wealth funds (like Alaska's oil fund paying $1,000 per resident) and employee stock ownership plans (ESOPs) to give ordinary families ownership income. [00:04:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a65DF3N0EoU&t=257)
- A future household could receive income from seven sources—UBI, dividends, employee ownership, cooperatives, baby bonds, and part-time work—so no single source is critical. [00:06:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a65DF3N0EoU&t=395)
- Without consumer demand, the economy faces a deflationary death spiral; tax codes must shift from wages to automation profits to fund new distribution mechanisms. [00:07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a65DF3N0EoU&t=456)
