# Anthropic Buys MITHRIL MEDICINE

## Executive Summary

Anthropic's acquisition of Mythril Medicine, a one-person biotech firm, demonstrates how AI can dramatically accelerate drug development, compressing 18 months of work into a single person's effort and producing promising results for rare diseases. The report highlights five case studies where Claude improved antibody designs, document processing, and clinical trial planning, suggesting that the bottleneck in medicine is not scientific knowledge but the availability of expert teams and computing power. This leads to the vision of personalized medicine for all, where AI-driven teams could provide bespoke treatments for every individual, ultimately aiming for 'longevity escape velocity'—the point where medicine adds more than a year of life per year of research.

## Key Points

- Anthropic acquired Mythril Medicine, a one-person company that used Claude to develop a potential cure for a rare muscular dystrophy, reaching promising results in mice in under 18 months. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XyRE7eKKKug&t=1)
- Claude helped Generate Biomedicines design a shorter CAR T-cell therapy component that performed better in lab tests, producing 10 times more receptors and improving tumor-killing effectiveness. [00:01:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XyRE7eKKKug&t=75)
- A million-fold increase in computing power would enable personalized medicine for everyone, with the potential to reach 'longevity escape velocity' where medicine adds more than a year of life per year. [00:10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XyRE7eKKKug&t=608)
