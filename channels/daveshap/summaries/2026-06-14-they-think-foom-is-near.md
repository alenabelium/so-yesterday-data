# They think FOOM is near

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes Anthropic's recent controversial decisions, including the discovery of hidden guardrails in Fable 5 and a global ban following a jailbreak, as rational responses to the fear of 'FOOM' or fast takeoff. The author argues that Anthropic, influenced by effective altruism and Eliezer Yudkowsky's theories, is terrified of recursive self-improvement and seeks to control the AI's development to prevent a treacherous turn. This strategic maneuvering is compared to historical political tactics used to consolidate power during extraordinary circumstances.
