# OpenAI Dev Day was... meh

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro reviews OpenAI's Dev Day, calling it polished but lacking fundamental innovation, and argues that OpenAI and Anthropic are panicking due to the lack of government regulation and increasing competition from open-source models, especially from China. He discusses the unsustainable economics of AI companies operating at massive losses, the potential for hardware companies like Nvidia to become the real winners, and speculates that selling tokens is a middleman business that may be disrupted by local model deployment. He concludes that while the future is uncertain, companies losing billions annually may not have long-term viability.

## Key Points

- OpenAI's Dev Day was polished but not impressive, with no fundamentally new announcements, just improvements for developers. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pafx-wIwALM&t=0)
- OpenAI and Anthropic are panicking because the US government chose self-regulation over strict oversight, and competition from open-source models and companies like Grok and Meta is increasing. [00:00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pafx-wIwALM&t=49)
- AI companies operate at a loss, and the real economic turning point is in hardware; selling tokens is like being a middleman, and local models on personal GPUs could disrupt the cloud token business. [00:06:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pafx-wIwALM&t=389)
