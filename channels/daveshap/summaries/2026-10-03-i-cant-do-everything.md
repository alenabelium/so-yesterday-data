# I can't do everything

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro discusses the psychological 'problem of choice' exacerbated by increasingly capable AI models, arguing that while AI enables nearly any project, human time, attention, and executive function remain the true bottlenecks. He draws parallels to the 'quarter life crisis' and advocates for 'minmaxing'—deliberately focusing AI use on a few high-value goals rather than spreading effort thin. He emphasizes quality over quantity and conscious value alignment to avoid being overwhelmed by endless possibilities.

## Key Points

- The problem of choice: more capable AI models create analysis paralysis because human time and mental bandwidth are finite. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EOyCHmyf1Hg&t=0)
- You are the bottleneck: even with delegation, verifying and enjoying results requires non-fungible executive function. [00:02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EOyCHmyf1Hg&t=130)
- Minmax your AI use: focus on a few high-value projects rather than trying to do everything, and consciously align with your values. [00:06:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EOyCHmyf1Hg&t=379)
