# You Are The Next Horse

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro argues that human workers will face the same fate as horses, which were replaced by machines when they became better, faster, cheaper, and safer. He contends that most human jobs are menial and that AI will automate a significant portion of work, leaving only niche 'essential demand' jobs. However, he highlights a crucial difference: humans can own capital, and owning a stake in AI and machines is the key to thriving in the post-labor economy.

## Key Points

- Horses were replaced by machines because they were better, faster, cheaper, and safer, and the same rule applies to human labor. [00:04:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kd8mpwU4-JA&t=299)
- Most human jobs are menial, and AI is now learning to do intellectual work, with McKinsey estimating that 57% of American working hours could be automated. [00:06:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kd8mpwU4-JA&t=395)
- The key difference between humans and horses is that humans can own capital, so owning a share in machines is the path to survival in the post-labor economy. [00:09:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kd8mpwU4-JA&t=586)
