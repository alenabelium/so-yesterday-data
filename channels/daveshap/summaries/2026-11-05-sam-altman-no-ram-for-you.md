# Sam Altman: NO RAM FOR YOU!!!

## Executive Summary

Sam Altman's remarks on the Cheeky Pint podcast, suggesting that personal computers waste scarce DRAM and that memory should be centralized in data centers, sparked widespread backlash. The video argues that Altman's perspective reflects a misunderstanding of economics and a self-serving push for centralized AI control. It defends the market's price signals as a fairer and more effective allocation mechanism than authoritarian planning, citing historical and current examples. The video concludes that the market, not individual executives, should determine who gets scarce resources like RAM.

## Key Points

- Altman's comments about personal computers wasting DRAM triggered a strong backlash, with critics accusing him of wanting to centralize AI and deny people ownership of powerful hardware. [00:00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=79TcCuDWlpw&t=57)
- The memory shortage is a market-driven price signal that is already stimulating new supply, despite Altman's claim that the invisible hand is failing. [00:04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=79TcCuDWlpw&t=279)
- The video argues that markets, though imperfect, are a fairer allocation mechanism than centralized planning, and that the market—not Sam Altman—should decide who gets scarce resources like RAM. [00:11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=79TcCuDWlpw&t=677)
