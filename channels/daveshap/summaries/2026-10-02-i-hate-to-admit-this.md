# I hate to admit this

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro reflects on how AI, automation, and the post-industrial economy will force a fundamental shift in people's mental models, or 'ontology.' He introduces Nietzsche's concepts of 'slave morality' and 'master morality,' arguing that many people currently define their identity through service and duty, which he calls 'slave morality.' As financial security becomes decoupled from work through mechanisms like universal basic income, people will need to shed this internalized obligation and embrace self-determined values. He acknowledges his general criticism of Nietzsche but finds this particular idea compelling and relevant for the future.

## Key Points

- AI and automation will force a change in ontology, meaning our mental model of the world must shift. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6hbiQyWUldg&t=0)
- Defining your identity by the services you provide to others is 'slave morality,' a Nietzschean concept where identity is based on serving others rather than self-determined values. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6hbiQyWUldg&t=0)
- With financial security from passive income or universal basic income, people must free themselves from the structure of duties and service, realizing they owe nothing to anyone. [00:03:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6hbiQyWUldg&t=229)
