# A Day in 2041 - Post-Labor Living

## Executive Summary

This video presents a speculative narrative of a day in the life of Maya Okafor in 2041, illustrating a post-labor society where universal dividends and cooperative profits replace traditional employment. It explores how automation has transformed work, community governance, and personal fulfillment, while acknowledging persistent social challenges. The story emphasizes that people find meaning in creative work, civic participation, and relationships, rather than in conventional jobs.
