# How Fast Will the AI Transition Really Be?

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro discusses the speed and smoothness of the AI transition, arguing that it will be faster than most expect due to the exponential acceleration of industrial revolutions. He notes that the transition is already affecting certain job sectors, while others remain insulated. He predicts a 10-20 year timeline for a new world order to emerge, and examines the role of power and elite incentives in shaping the outcome. He concludes that while the transition may be turbulent, it is unlikely to cause a total collapse.

## Key Points

- AI has the fastest adoption rate of any technology in history, developing twice as fast as smartphones, indicating a rapid transition. [00:02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UZ0WYh6O4ug&t=170)
- The transition is already underway for some, like software developers and writers, while experienced professionals and niche content creators may not notice it. [00:06:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UZ0WYh6O4ug&t=376)
- Each industrial revolution has doubled in speed, so the AI transition could take 10-20 years, with a tipping point around 2030. [00:13:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UZ0WYh6O4ug&t=802)
