# Dario Amodei is DELUSIONAL

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro criticizes Anthropic's announcement of donating 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund, arguing that the fund is an LLC owned by Anthropic itself, which means the company retains control over the money and decisions. He sees this as a PR move rather than genuine public benefit, especially given the massive potential valuation. Shapiro contrasts this with his preferred approach: transferring shares to a government-owned sovereign wealth fund with transparent governance and tax incentives. He also critiques the unrealistic growth projections of AI CEOs like Dario Amodei and Sam Altman, calling it 'AI psychosis.'

## Key Points

- Anthropic announced a fund holding 5% of its shares, but it's an LLC owned by Anthropic, not a charitable or public entity, raising conflicts of interest. [00:01:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CVeyL08MX7E&t=109)
- Shapiro argues the fund is 'ostentatious moralizing' and a way for Anthropic to maintain control over public good decisions, rather than giving real ownership to the public. [00:06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CVeyL08MX7E&t=413)
- He proposes a sovereign wealth fund model with tax breaks, adherence to Santiago principles, and permanent public ownership, contrasting with Anthropic's self-controlled fund. [00:12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CVeyL08MX7E&t=762)
