# The AI "Bubble" Is Bursting RIGHT NOW!

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro argues that the recent stock market declines in AI-related companies are a market correction, not a bubble bursting. He distinguishes between a correction, a tulip-style bubble, and a credit crunch, emphasizing that AI has real value and infrastructure demand remains strong. He criticizes motivated reasoning among AI doomers and warns that the greatest risk would be a debt-driven credit crunch, though he sees it as unlikely. He concludes that AI is here to stay and that short-term market fluctuations should not be mistaken for an industry collapse.

## Key Points

- The recent stock drops are a market correction due to inflated expectations, not a bubble bursting. [00:02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqvK1-e3YaI&t=176)
- AI differs from historical bubbles like tulip mania because it has real utility and profitable infrastructure. [00:05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqvK1-e3YaI&t=301)
- The greatest risk is a credit crunch if too much debt is tied to AI, but current data center orders remain sold out through 2027-2028. [00:06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqvK1-e3YaI&t=368)
