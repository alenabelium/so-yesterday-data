# Loneliness Is My Biggest Problem Right Now

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro, an AI specialist, shares that loneliness has become his biggest problem after recovering from a chronic illness and going through a divorce. He reflects on how cities act as social reactors and decides to use AI tools like Grok, Claude, and ChatGPT to find local hiking and AI-focused groups in the Raleigh-Durham area. He emphasizes the importance of humility and asking for help, and frames his approach as an experiment in living a 'life after work.' He also notes that AI requires asking the right questions to find suitable offline communities.
