# "The window has closed"

## Executive Summary

The recent IPOs of major AI companies like OpenAI and Anthropic signal a shift in the industry's narrative, suggesting that the era of raw token sales is ending. Satya Nadella's critique that selling tokens is insufficient highlights a transition where AI capabilities become commoditized utilities, similar to electricity. Consequently, long-term value will likely accrue to those building ecosystems and applications rather than those merely providing the underlying infrastructure. This normalization of AI implies that America has solidified its lead in the race, leaving competitors like China and Europe further behind.

## Key Points

- The IPOs of SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic mark the normalization of AI, transitioning it from secretive startups to public market entities [00:01:25].
- Satya Nadella argues that selling tokens is no longer a viable business model because the cost per token is plummeting while demand rises, making it a utility rather than a product [00:02:43].
- The true value lies in the applications and ecosystems built on top of AI, much like how electricity is valuable for what it powers, not for its own sake [00:04:00].
