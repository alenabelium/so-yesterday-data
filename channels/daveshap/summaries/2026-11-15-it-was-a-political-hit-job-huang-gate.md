# "It was a political hit job" (Huang Gate)

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro analyzes the sudden ousting of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, examining conspiracy theories that claim it was a politically orchestrated hit job involving Chinese espionage, leaks, and coordination with OpenAI and Anthropic. He presents the strongest arguments for the conspiracy, then dismantles them by reconstructing the timeline, highlighting Jensen's three affairs, his lies to the board, and the board's fiduciary duty. Using Occam's razor, he concludes that Jensen's own actions, not a grand conspiracy, led to his dismissal, and he dismisses extreme theories about government nationalization of Nvidia as legally and politically implausible.

## Key Points

- The conspiracy theory suggests a coordinated plot involving a secret memo, leaks, and OpenAI/Anthropic timing, but the timeline shows the government investigated MegaSpeed and Alice Huang since 2024, and espionage rumors predated the memo. [00:10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53uYYeNC6Ek&t=643)
- Jensen had three affairs, including with a subordinate, and lied to the board for nearly a year, which alone justifies his firing under fiduciary duty, as seen in similar CEO scandals at Intel, McDonald's, and BP. [00:15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53uYYeNC6Ek&t=918)
- Occam's razor favors the simple explanation: Jensen's own actions caught up with him, and the conspiracy theory requires far more assumptions than the straightforward narrative of board accountability. [00:17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53uYYeNC6Ek&t=1048)
