# Anthropic Is Living In The Future

## Executive Summary

Anthropic's Claude code team has shifted to an AI-driven code review process where machines write and review most code, with humans only stepping in for high-risk decisions. This approach, rooted in principles like double-entry bookkeeping and error-correcting codes, treats human attention as the scarce resource and optimizes for automated checks. The video argues that this model—machines checking machines—will become the standard, and companies must redesign their processes around it to stay competitive.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's Claude writes about 90% of its own code, and the team has shifted from human line-by-line review to a multi-level automated verification system, with human approval scaling by risk. [00:01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmrNsOVOAt4&t=100)
- The principle of machines checking machines is not new; it mirrors double-entry bookkeeping, TCP checksums, and error-correcting codes, and is the basis for reliable systems. [00:09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmrNsOVOAt4&t=566)
- For companies, the future involves agents doing work, supervising, and auditing, with humans focusing on purpose, trust, and responsibility—and the key is to automate coordination and keep humans accountable. [00:14:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VmrNsOVOAt4&t=881)
