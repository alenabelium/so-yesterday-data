# This could be really good, actually

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro discusses the resignation of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang following scandals, offering a somatic realism perspective on monogamy and the consequences of power. He argues that while the short-term market impact is uncertain, NVIDIA's maturity and deep moat mean it will survive, and Jensen's departure could be a net positive for humanity if he applies his skills elsewhere. Shapiro also addresses personal health comments and reflects on the broader AI industry's need for more computing resources and competition.

## Key Points

- Jensen Huang resigned from NVIDIA after affairs were exposed, including one with a potential Chinese spy, and the market reacted negatively to the uncertainty. [00:12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M_Fzx5KzBwY&t=729)
- Shapiro argues that NVIDIA's mature status and deep moat (CUDA, GPUs) mean the company will survive without Huang, and his departure could be a net positive if he applies his skills elsewhere. [00:16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M_Fzx5KzBwY&t=970)
