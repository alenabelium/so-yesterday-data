# Every supplement I take for long COVID and CFS recovery

## Executive Summary

David Shapiro shares his personal supplement regimen for managing long COVID and chronic fatigue syndrome (CFS), emphasizing that this is not medical advice. He details his morning and evening stacks, highlighting mitochondrial support, gut healing, and nervous system calming supplements. He identifies palmitoylethanolamide (PEA), zinc carnosine, and NAC as the most impactful, and discusses the importance of addressing gut issues and sleep disruption. The video aims to help others save money by sharing what has worked for him.

## Key Points

- David explains his chronic illness journey, starting with COVID in late 2022, which progressed into gut issues and a chronic fatigue-like syndrome. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjN5I44kf08&t=0)
- He details his morning supplement stack, including TUDCA, magnesium L-threonate, CoQ10, methylated B complex, beef liver, and EPA/DHA, focusing on mitochondrial support and brain health. [00:01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjN5I44kf08&t=65)
- He highlights zinc carnosine and L-glutamine as the most powerful combination for gut healing, noting that two 60-day courses fixed most of his GI issues. [00:10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjN5I44kf08&t=614)
- He identifies palmitoylethanolamide (PEA) as the most life-saving supplement, explaining its role in calming the cell danger response and aiding recovery. [00:13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjN5I44kf08&t=810)
- He discusses NAC (N-acetylcysteine) as a powerhouse for liver detoxification and inflammation, noting it brought his liver enzymes back to normal. [00:07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XjN5I44kf08&t=460)
