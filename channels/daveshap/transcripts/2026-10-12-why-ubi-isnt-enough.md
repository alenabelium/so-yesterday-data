Economies where most households rely on a single income channel are particularly vulnerable. When the demand for labor is high, everything works well. When automation reduces this demand, even gradually, even in one profession after another, the blow falls squarely on family budgets, which have almost nothing to compensate for it. Therefore, when discussing politics , it is worth asking three questions about any proposal. What channel is it expanding?
