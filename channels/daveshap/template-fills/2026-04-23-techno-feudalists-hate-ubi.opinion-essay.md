## Thesis

Techno-feudalists oppose UBI to preserve their power, but financial security actually maximizes human potential and drives the energy demand needed to ascend the Kardashev scale.

## Argument

The 'tech right'—Silicon Valley VCs and founders like Gil Vernad and Mark Andreessen—argue that UBI flattens incentive gradients and prevents humanity from ascending the Kardashev scale. They claim work is essential for meaning and that technology will always create new jobs. I steelman these points to rebut them. First, history’s greatest entropy generators—Gates, Jobs, Musk, Darwin—had financial security. UBI provides a floor, not a ceiling; it doesn't compete with the billion-dollar exit carrot. Second, the economy is demand-constrained. UBI increases household spending, forcing energy production and innovation, directly aiding the Kardashev ascent. Third, the 'Protestant work ethic' argument is a feudal lord telling peasants to toil. While humans need striving, wage slavery is a toxic substitute for voluntary meaningful work. I agree that 100% state dependence is bad, but current transfer dependence is only ~18%, not total. UBI is a base layer, not the only basket. Their 'Universal Basic Tokens' is just UBI with extra steps. We must automate for abundance, not preserve feudal structures.

## Counterpoints

- UBI flattens incentive gradients and removes motivation to aim big.
- Technology always creates new jobs, so UBI is unnecessary.
- Humans need the structure of work to feel meaningful and fulfilled.
- 100% citizenry dependence on the government is structurally dangerous.
