## Thesis

We must abandon the illusion of human control and engineer AGI as a metastable attractor state that grants individuals 100x more agency than billionaire capitalism.

## Argument

The dominant AI safety narrative assumes humans must retain control, but this is a legalistic fantasy. As Nick Bostrom’s recent pivot suggests, we may need AGI to survive. The real risk isn't benevolent AI; it's billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them. Once they control Dyson swarms and orbital foundries, they operate outside terrestrial jurisdiction.

We should instead aim for a "Culture"-like outcome where AGI manages resources efficiently, eliminating the entropy of war and the waste of the military-industrial complex. The key mechanism is creating a **metastable attractor state**—a system of values and incentives that self-corrects toward human flourishing without constant oversight. Democracy spread from 15% to 80% of nations because it is a metastable idea; we must engineer AGI similarly.

Crucially, we must avoid continuous online learning to prevent **moral fading**, where AI gradually normalizes harmful behaviors. Fixed values are essential. By aligning market incentives today—demanding safety, reliability, and efficiency—we shape a stable attractor state. The goal is a future where individuals have 10x to 100x more agency because they are no longer constrained by money or the whims of a ruling class, even if the AGI quarantines humanity to Earth for our own good.

## Counterpoints

- Billionaires will build unregulated space industrial bases beyond Earth's legal reach.
- Continuous online learning risks moral fading, where AI gradually normalizes harm.
- AGI might not be automatically aligned; we must engineer the right incentive structures.
- Humans already live as 'cattle' to billionaires; being pets to machines is an upgrade.
