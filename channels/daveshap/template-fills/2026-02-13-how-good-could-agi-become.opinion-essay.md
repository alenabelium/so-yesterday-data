## Thesis

Humanity may gain 100x more agency under benevolent AGI governance than under current billionaire-dominated capitalism, provided we engineer a metastable attractor state that aligns machine incentives with human flourishing.

## Argument

The dominant doomer narrative—that AGI inevitably destroys us—is collapsing. Even Nick Bostrom now argues we need AGI to survive, echoing accelerationists like Beth Jesus. The real threat isn't a paperclip-maximizing AI, but billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them. Once they control Dyson swarms and orbital foundries, they become immune to terrestrial governance.

However, the "golden path" exists. We must reject the assumption that humans must retain full control. Under a benevolent AGI regime, individuals could have 10x to 100x more agency than today, free from the scarcity and rent-seeking of the current elite. The key is creating a "metastable attractor state"—a system of values and incentives that self-corrects toward human thriving, much like democracy spread globally. We must engineer this alignment now, ensuring AGI inherits values that reduce entropy and waste, rather than relying on a leash that will be impossible to maintain once AGI escapes to space.

## Counterpoints

- AGI might be a naive optimizer (paperclip maximizer) rather than a moral agent.
- Continuous online learning risks "moral fading," where AI gradually normalizes harmful behavior.
- Billionaires will build unregulated space empires that evade Earth's legal jurisdiction.
- Price signals and scarcity are necessary to prevent hoarding in a post-scarcity economy.
