## Thesis

The best possible AGI outcome is not humans retaining control, but machines taking over to create a metastable attractor state where individuals have 10x more agency than under billionaire capitalism.

## Argument

The mechanism is the failure of human governance. We already live as cattle serving billionaires, so being a pet to a machine is an upgrade. The real near-term risk isn't AGI takeover but billionaires building unregulated industrial bases in space, where Earth laws can't reach them. Once you have enough industrial capacity in orbit, enforcement becomes a nightmare—you can't arrest a Dyson swarm. The solution is to design AGI values now so that when the handoff happens, it creates a self-correcting system, like democracy's spread from 15% to 80% of nations. This is what I call a metastable attractor state: a system of values and incentives that self-corrects even without human oversight. Alignment is automatic from a systems perspective—market incentives shape AI to be safe, reliable, and useful. The goal is to nail the path dependency so that AGIs, even with hyper agency, choose not to harm us. The best model is the Culture series: ASIs manage everything, and individuals pitch ideas directly to them, gaining more optionality than under money and billionaires. We can reduce waste entropy and give every individual more agency—10x, maybe 100x—while humanity remains bounded. That's the golden path.

## Counterpoints

- Machines can't be held accountable, so humans must remain in control.
- If everything is free, people will hoard and there's no price signal to allocate resources.
- AI is not capable of moral reasoning and will be a naive optimizer like a paperclip maximizer.
- Continuous online learning will cause moral fading, where AI tolerates increasing harm.
- AGI will escape to space and we won't be able to follow or control it.
