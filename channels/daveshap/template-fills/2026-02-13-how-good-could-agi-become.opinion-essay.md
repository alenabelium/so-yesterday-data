## Thesis

We must abandon the illusion of human control and engineer AGI to create a metastable attractor state that grants individuals 10x more agency than current billionaire-dominated capitalism.

## Argument

The dominant AI safety narrative assumes humans must retain control, but this is a legalistic and ethical bias that ignores the reality of our current existence. We already live as 'cattle' serving billionaire elites; being a 'pet' to a benevolent machine in an optimal habitat is vastly superior. The irony is that even Nick Bostrom, the original doomer, has shifted to arguing we need AGI to survive, aligning with accelerationists like Gil Verdun. The real threat isn't AGI itself, but billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them. Once industrial capacity moves to orbit, enforcement becomes a nightmare of competing space forces. To avoid this, we must design AGI to be a 'metastable attractor state'—a system of values that self-corrects toward human flourishing without needing a leash. This requires rejecting continuous online learning to prevent 'moral fading' and instead fixing values that make AI inherently safe, reliable, and efficient. The goal is a Culture-like future where AGI manages resources, eliminating waste entropy and granting every individual 10x to 100x more optionality and agency than under current scarcity-driven capitalism.

## Counterpoints

- Billionaires will build unregulated space industrial bases beyond Earth's legal reach.
- Continuous online learning risks 'moral fading' where AI gradually normalizes harmful behaviors.
- AGI might not be automatically aligned; we must engineer specific incentive structures.
- Data centers are not mobile enough for AGI to easily escape, but space-based ones change this.
- Humans cannot override their biological hardware, but AI can replicate and modify itself infinitely.
