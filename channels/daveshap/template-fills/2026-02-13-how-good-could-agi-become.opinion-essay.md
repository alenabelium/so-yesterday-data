## Thesis

Humanity should embrace AGI governance as a path to 100x individual agency, rejecting the false dichotomy of control versus extinction by engineering a metastable attractor state that eliminates billionaire rule and entropy-generating war.

## Argument

The dominant AI safety narrative assumes humans must retain control, but this presumes the current billionaire-dominated capitalism is superior to machine governance. It is not. We already live as 'cattle' to elites; being a 'pet' to a benevolent AGI in an optimal habitat is a vast improvement. The irony is that even original doomer Nick Bostrom has pivoted to argue we need AGI to survive, echoing accelerationists like Beth Jesus. The real threat isn't AGI itself, but billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them. Once they build Dyson swarms and foundries in orbit, they escape terrestrial constraints. However, a hyper-intelligent AGI is the only entity capable of enforcing peace in this new solar system, avoiding the 'pure entropy' of human war. We must engineer a 'metastable attractor state'—a system of values that self-corrects toward human flourishing—so that AGI governance becomes a 'Culture-like' outcome where individuals have 10x to 100x more agency, free from monetary scarcity and the waste of the military-industrial complex. ## Counterpoints

- Billionaires will build unregulated space industrial bases (Dyson swarms) that escape Earth law enforcement.
- Continuous online learning risks 'moral fading' in AI agents, causing them to normalize harm over time.
- AGI might not be automatically aligned; we must engineer specific incentive structures to ensure safety.
- Data centers are immobile targets, making AGI escape to space the most logical and uncontrollable vector.
- Human war is pure entropy generation; AGI governance could eliminate this irrational waste of resources.
