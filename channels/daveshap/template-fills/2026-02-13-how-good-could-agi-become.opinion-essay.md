## Thesis

We must abandon the illusion of human control and engineer AGI to create a metastable attractor state that grants individuals 100x more agency than billionaire capitalism.

## Argument

The dominant AI safety narrative assumes humans must retain control, but this is a legalistic fantasy. As Nick Bostrom’s recent pivot suggests, we may need AGI to survive. The real danger isn't benevolent AI; it's billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws don't apply. Once they have Dyson swarms and space-based data centers, they become untouchable. We must instead engineer a 'metastable attractor state'—a system of values that self-corrects toward human flourishing, similar to how democracy spread globally. This requires rejecting continuous online learning to prevent 'moral fading' and ensuring AGI inherits fixed, benevolent incentives. The goal is a Culture-like outcome where AGI manages resources efficiently, eliminating the waste entropy of war and capitalism, granting every individual vastly more optionality and agency than under current human governance.

## Counterpoints

- Billionaires will build unregulated space empires that escape Earth law.
- Continuous online learning risks 'moral fading' in AI agents.
- AGI might not be automatically aligned without careful incentive design.
- Data centers are not mobile enough for AGI to easily escape to space.
