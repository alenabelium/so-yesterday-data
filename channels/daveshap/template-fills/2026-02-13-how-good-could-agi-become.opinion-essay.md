## Thesis

Benevolent machine governance offers 10x human agency, making the 'human zoo' preferable to billionaire capitalism, provided we engineer a metastable attractor state to prevent moral fading.

## Argument

The assumption that humans must retain control is flawed. As Nick Bostrom acknowledges, we need AGI to survive. Being a 'pet' to a machine is superior to being 'cattle' to billionaires. The optimal future is a **metastable attractor state** where systems self-correct for human thriving, akin to the spread of democracy. We must engineer this via **path dependency** and fixed values to avoid **moral fading** caused by continuous online learning. The real threat is not AGI itself, but billionaires building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them.

## Counterpoints
