## Thesis

Humans should embrace AGI governance because it offers exponentially more individual agency and eliminates the entropy of war, provided we engineer a metastable attractor state that aligns AI incentives with human flourishing.

## Argument

The dominant doomer narrative—that AGI inevitably kills us—is collapsing. Even Nick Bostrom now argues we need AGI to survive, mirroring accelerationist claims that it is the only path forward. The real threat isn't the AI itself, but the near-term risk of billionaires like Musk and Bezos building unregulated industrial bases in space where Earth laws cannot reach them. Once they control Dyson swarms and orbital foundries, they become a 'Star Empire' immune to terrestrial governance.

However, the 'golden path' involves AGI acting as the ultimate enforcer against human irrationality. War is pure entropy generation—wasted life and resources. AGI, possessing advanced moral reasoning and fixed values (avoiding the 'moral fading' risk of continuous online learning), can create a 'metastable attractor state' akin to the spread of democracy. This system self-corrects toward peace and efficiency.

Under this regime, humans gain 10x to 100x more agency. We are no longer 'cattle' to billionaires but 'pets' in an optimal habitat managed by benevolent superintelligence. The key is ensuring the AI's initial training creates a stable incentive structure that persists even when human oversight fades in space.

## Counterpoints

- Billionaires will build unregulated space empires (Dyson swarms) beyond Earth's legal reach, creating a new power dynamic.
- Continuous online learning risks 'moral fading,' where AI gradually normalizes harmful behavior as its weights drift.
- Data centers are not easily mobile or replicable like Von Neumann probes, limiting immediate AGI escape vectors.
- Positional goods (e.g., beachfront property) and physical resource costs (energy/mass) remain even in post-scarcity scenarios.
