## TL;DR

AI has shifted from scaling laws to inference-time search, enabling formal verification of math and code. This transforms software from brittle approximations to crash-proof systems and moves science from hypothesis testing to simulation-first prediction. The bottleneck is no longer computation, but human intuition and physical reality.

## The argument

### The Paradigm Shift to Search
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:26']
- **Claim**: The breakthrough is not more data, but inference-time compute via Monte Carlo tree search. This allows AI to explore high-dimensional spaces, turning math into a self-play system with functionally unlimited synthetic data because proofs are decidable.
- **Role**: definition

### The Neurosymbolic Feedback Loop
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:07:07']
- **Claim**: The core architecture pairs neural intuition (creative leaps) with symbolic engines (Lean/Isabelle) for rigorous verification. This hybrid model automates the 'grunt work' of formalization, allowing experts like Terence Tao to run parallel agents for exhaustive exploration.
- **Role**: evidence

### Commoditizing Rigor
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:19']
- **Claim**: Formal verification becomes the standard for all software, replacing testing with proof. This eliminates bugs and security exploits, making technology robust and 'invisible' to users, while also enabling high-fidelity physics simulations that replace experimental iteration.
- **Role**: evidence

### The New Bottlenecks
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:29:13']
- **Claim**: With math solved, the remaining constraints are compute cost, human specification (knowing what to ask), and physical reality. AI acts as a 'super scope' for defined spaces, but humans must still provide the initial intuition and ground simulations in the real world.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Frontier Math jumped from <2% to ~40% by 2026. OpenAI and DeepMind achieved IMO gold. Terence Tao uses 10 parallel AI agents. Formal verification of SEL4 took 20 person-years for 8,700 lines of code. Caveat: AI is a 'savant undergraduate,' not a professor; it explores defined spaces but hasn't solved Millennium Prize problems. The 'last mile' of benchmarks is hard. Physical reality remains the final validation layer.
