## TL;DR

AI has shifted from pure scaling to inference-time search, achieving IMO gold but not solving Millennium problems. This creates a 'super scope' that automates mathematical grunt work via neurosymbolic loops. The result is formal verification becoming standard, turning software and science from approximation to proof.

## The argument

### The Paradigm Shift to Search
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:26']
- **Claim**: The breakthrough is not more training data, but inference-time compute and Monte Carlo tree search. This allows models to explore high-dimensional spaces rather than just predicting tokens, enabling the jump from <2% to 40% on Frontier Math benchmarks.
- **Role**: definition

### The Neurosymbolic Feedback Loop
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:07:07']
- **Claim**: The core mechanism pairs neural networks for creative intuition with symbolic engines (like Lean) for rigorous verification. This hybrid loop generates functionally unlimited synthetic data because mathematical proofs are decidable and self-verifying.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Super Scope Workflow
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:11:13']
- **Claim**: Terence Tao’s workflow uses AI as a 'super scope' to exhaustively explore defined spaces. Humans provide high-level intent, while parallel AI agents handle formalization and proof, automating the grunt work previously done by post-docs.
- **Role**: evidence

### From Approximation to Verification
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:19']
- **Claim**: This capability transforms software and science. Code moves from testing to formal verification (eliminating bugs), and science moves from hypothesis testing to simulation-first, where reality only verifies the model rather than discovering it.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

OpenAI and DeepMind achieved IMO gold, but Millennium Prize problems remain unsolved. The 'savant undergraduate' analogy fits: superhuman at known tasks, but not yet inventing new math. Terence Tao runs 10 agents in parallel. The last 15% of benchmarks is harder than the first 85%. Physical reality remains the final validation layer; simulations can fail if unknown variables exist. Verification takes 1% of the energy of production, making it a superpower for experts.
