## Thesis

Human labor input is decoupling from economic output, meaning wages will cease to be the primary source of household income and must be replaced by government transfers and capital-based income.

## Argument

The economy has historically shifted from primary (extraction) to secondary (manufacturing) to tertiary (services) sectors, with the tertiary sector peaking around 2015. As automation erodes the service sector, wages will no longer sustain household demand. The solution lies in balancing two non-wage income buckets: government transfers (UBI, social security) and capital-based income (stocks, businesses, rentals). Boosting capital is critical to prevent a deflationary death spiral. To achieve this, the book must reach a broad, skeptical audience—specifically conservative voters and politicians—rather than just existing followers. A high-profile Kickstarter campaign is the necessary vehicle to create mimetic saturation and consensus among the right decision-makers.

## Counterpoints

- Critiques that the focus is too supply-side; the book incorporates demand-side economics.
- The objection that education is useless because schools teach outdated skills.
- The concern that AI alignment is more urgent than economic disruption.
- The skepticism that wages will not disappear entirely but just decline.
- The fear that the project will fail to gain traction or funding.
