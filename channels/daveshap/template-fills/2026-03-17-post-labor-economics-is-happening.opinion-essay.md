## Thesis

Human labor input is decoupling from economic output, meaning wages will cease to be the primary source of household income and must be replaced by government transfers and capital-based income.

## Argument

The economy has historically shifted from primary (extraction) to secondary (manufacturing) to tertiary (services) sectors, with the tertiary sector peaking around 2015. As automation accelerates, wages will no longer sustain household spending. The solution lies in balancing two domains: government transfers (UBI, social security) and capital-based income (stocks, businesses, rentals). The book aims to convince voters and politicians that this shift is inevitable and necessary to avoid a deflationary death spiral. The Kickstarter campaign is critical for achieving mimetic saturation and proving the concept to the right audience.

## Counterpoints

- Education is useless because you never use what you learn in school.
- Soft skills are the most valuable set of skills in a STEM career.
- AI alignment and X-risk are less immediate concerns than job disruption for students.
- Publishing as a blog series is insufficient for convincing politicians and voters.
- The primary sector was once 90% of the population, showing how drastically economic structures can change.
