## Thesis

Human labor input is decoupling from economic output, meaning wages will cease to be the primary source of household income and must be replaced by government transfers and capital-based income.

## Argument

The economy is divided into primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors. Historically, the primary sector (extraction) peaked first, followed by the secondary sector (manufacturing). The tertiary sector (services) peaked around 2015. As automation replaces jobs, wages will disappear. Household income comes from three buckets: wages, transfers, and capital. With wages gone, transfers (UBI, social security) and capital (stocks, rental properties) must pick up the slack. The book aims to convince voters and politicians of this new economic reality.

## Counterpoints

- Education is useless because you don't use what you learn in school.
- Soft skills are the most valuable set of skills you can have.
- AI alignment is more important than job disruption.
- Farming and manufacturing will never go away as main jobs.
- Universal Basic Income is the only solution needed.
