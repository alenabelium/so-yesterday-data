## Thesis

Post-labor economics is inevitable because human labor is decoupling from economic output, forcing a shift from wage-based income to government transfers and capital ownership.

## Argument

The economy has historically cycled through primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors, each peaking and declining. The service sector peaked around 2015, signaling that wages will cease to be the primary household income source. Post-labor economics defines this as the decoupling of human labor input from economic output. To survive, society must balance two domains: government transfers (UBI, social security) and capital-based income (stocks, businesses). The goal is to boost capital ownership to prevent a deflationary death spiral. This book is a tool to achieve mimetic saturation among voters and politicians, not just to inform the existing audience. The Kickstarter campaign is a strategic move to prove consensus and force the narrative into mainstream political discourse. If we can convince the 'conservative father-in-law' archetype, we can shift the entire political landscape. The urgency is real: this is a make-or-break moment for the labor zero movement.

## Counterpoints

- Education is useless because AI will disrupt every job market.
- Soft skills are the only safe career hedge against automation.
- Focusing only on supply-side economics ignores demand-side realities.
- Wages will never fully disappear, so UBI is a distraction.
- The service sector will not decline like manufacturing did.
