## Thesis

AI's expanding capabilities create a 'problem of choice' that makes human executive function and attention the true bottleneck, not technical possibility.

## Argument

The mechanism is psychological: as models like Opus 5.5 and Soul 6.1 get more capable, the sheer number of possible projects—books, manga, animations—creates analysis paralysis. You can delegate the work to AI, but reviewing and enjoying the output requires non-fungible executive function. Without that, you're just accumulating files and wasting tokens. This leads to FOMO, but the real solution is 'minmaxing': deliberately choosing a few high-value goals that align with your personal values. It's quality over quantity. Even if you decide your own values, you must be conscious about whether a task satisfies your higher-order values or just feeds your ADHD-driven interests. The point isn't to avoid all unproductive play, but to be intentional about your time and energy.
