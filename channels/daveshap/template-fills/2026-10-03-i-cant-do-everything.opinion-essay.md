## Thesis

Human executive function, not AI capability, is the true bottleneck in an age of infinite options.

## Argument

The problem of choice is becoming acute as models like Opus 5.5 and Soul 6.1 make nearly any project feasible. The bottleneck isn't quota or intelligence—it's the non-fungible executive function required to review and enjoy results. Without that, delegating to AI just accumulates files, which is pointless token spend. The solution is minmaxing: deliberately choosing a few high-value goals aligned with your values, prioritizing quality over quantity. Even for ADHD types with a billion interests, conscious value alignment prevents AI psychosis. It's about asking whether this use of time satisfies higher-order values, not just whether it's possible.
