## Thesis

The real bottleneck isn't AI capability—it's your finite executive function, so you must consciously minmax your choices.

## Argument

The problem of choice is hitting me hard right now. With models like Opus 5.5 and Soul 6.1, I can literally do anything: write a book, turn it into a manga, animate it. But I only have so many hours and so much mental bandwidth. Delegating to bots just accumulates files—unless I actually look at and use the results, it's pointless token spend. There's no substitute for my own executive function.

So I have to minmax: pick a few high-value goals and focus AI there. It's quality over quantity. But I'm not saying only do productive things—there's merit in fussing around. The key is being conscious about your values: is this how I want to spend my day? Does this satisfy my higher-order values? For ADHD folks with a billion interests, this is crucial.

This is the lived experience of what's coming—I'm 3 to 6 to 12 months ahead of most people. Bookmark this for later. And for those waiting on 'Post Labor Economics': the researcher is almost done, we just had a call today. I've also started practicing my voiceover narration again—that's why I sound different. Quality over quantity, that's the TLDR.
