## Thesis

AI's growing capability creates a 'problem of choice' where human time, attention, and executive function become the real bottlenecks, forcing deliberate minmaxing.

## Argument

The bottleneck isn't AI capability—it's your own executive function. You can delegate writing, research, or fanfic to models, but unless you actually look at and use the results, it's just pointless token spend. This creates FOMO, but with models like Opus 5.5, the real challenge is deciding what you actually care about. Quality over quantity is the TLDR: focus on what you'll genuinely read, publish, or enjoy. That said, there's merit in just fussing around—it's not about being productive all the time, but being conscious about your values. Ask yourself: is this how I want to spend my day? Does it satisfy my higher-order values? For ADHD folks with a billion interests, this is especially critical. You have to minmax around your actual goals, not just what's possible.
