## Thesis

AI makes everything possible, but human time and attention are the real bottleneck, so we must consciously minmax our choices.

## Argument

The mechanism is the psychological 'problem of choice'—more capable models like Opus 5.5 create endless options, but executive function isn't fungible. Delegating to AI just accumulates files unless you actually engage with the results. This triggers FOMO, but the real answer is quality over quantity: focus on what you genuinely care about and will use. It's not about avoiding fun or experimentation, but being conscious of whether an activity serves your higher-order values. For those with ADHD and a billion interests, this is especially critical. The practical takeaway is to deliberately choose a few high-value projects, not to try to do everything.
