## Thesis

AI makes everything possible, but human attention and executive function are the real bottlenecks, forcing a deliberate 'minmax' on what we actually value.

## Argument

The problem of choice is hitting hard as models like Opus 5.5 and Soul 6.1 expand what's doable—books, manga, animation—but the constraint isn't capability, it's hours and mental bandwidth. Delegating to bots just accumulates files unless you apply non-fungible executive function to actually use them. FOMO around quota usage is pointless; quality over quantity is the TLDR. You must consciously ask if a project satisfies higher-order values or is just fussing around. For ADHD types with a billion interests, this means picking a few high-value goals and committing, not spreading thin. The book 'Post Labor Economics' is nearly done, and I'm back to voiceover practice—proof that focus, not capability, drives completion.
