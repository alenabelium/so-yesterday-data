## Thesis

Human intellectual labor will be rendered obsolete within two years by super-exponential AI growth, ending the era of rigorous knowledge work.

## Argument

The mechanism is economic and physical: AI investment rivals the Manhattan Project, driving cognitive hyperabundance. As GPU hardware and model performance improve exponentially, the material reality is that human brains cannot compete. This convergence of the Fourth Turning, long-term debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution upends every societal norm, from education to retirement. The sensation shifts from wistful nostalgia to existential dread as the "extinction rage" of compulsory labor meets the inevitability of automation. While creative dimensions like novel-writing remain mathematically infinite and thus safe from total automation, rigorous scientific and economic contributions are ending. The US-China AI race will drive this singularity, shrinking global impact to hyperlocal tribes and forcing a painful, incoherent societal reinvention.

## Counterpoints

- Creative works like novels are mathematically infinite, so human creativity remains viable.
- Social commentary and embodied experiences will persist as long as humans exist.
- Current '100x engineers' leverage AI, but the machine will soon drive itself.
