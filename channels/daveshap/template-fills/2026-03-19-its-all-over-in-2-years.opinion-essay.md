## Thesis

Human intellectual labor will be rendered obsolete within two years as AI achieves cognitive hyperabundance, ending the era of rigorous knowledge work.

## Argument

The mechanism is economic and technical convergence. AI is on a super-exponential growth curve driven by GPU hardware improvements and massive industrial buildout rivaling the Apollo program. This leads to cognitive hyperabundance where machines are better, faster, cheaper, and safer than humans. As a knowledge worker, I realize my problem-solving abilities will soon be moot because AI already does most of the heavy lifting. The value of my intellectual contribution drops to zero; there will be no problems for which I am better suited to solve than the machines we are building. While creative dimensions like novels remain mathematically infinite, rigorous scientific work is ending. My work on post-labor economics may be my final contribution before AI surpasses mathematicians and physicists. The transition is not just a job shock but the upending of every societal norm, from education to retirement, driven by the convergence of the Fourth Turning, debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution. We are facing an extinction rage against compulsory labor, but the requisite ingredient for ending it is automation capable of obviating human input. We are at the beginning of the singularity, and while we cannot influence the global AI race, we must steer through the coming contraction and weirdness.

## Counterpoints

- Creative output like novels is mathematically infinite, so human creativity remains viable.
- Social commentary and embodied experiences will persist as long as humans exist.
- The transition involves not just economic shifts but the convergence of the Fourth Turning and debt cycles.
- The global AI race between the US and China drives the timeline, not individual agency.
