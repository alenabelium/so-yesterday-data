## Thesis

My intellectual contributions are ending within two years as AI's super-exponential growth renders human cognitive labor obsolete, leaving only non-rigorous creative and social dimensions for humanity.

## Argument

The mechanism is economic and physical: AI investment rivals the Apollo program, accelerating GPU and model performance toward cognitive hyperabundance. This convergence of the Fourth Turning, debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution upends every societal norm simultaneously. The sensation shifts from wistful nostalgia to existential dread as the old life script—school, job, house, retire—collapses. While creative dimensions like novel-writing remain mathematically infinite, rigorous work in post-labor economics, physics, and chemistry is being overtaken by machines that surpass human intuition. The US-China AI race drives this singularity, shrinking the world further and forcing a painful, rapid reinvention of society from scratch.

## Counterpoints

- Humans still choose which problems to solve and provide taste.
- Creative output like novels is mathematically infinite.
- Social commentary and celebrity gossip remain human domains.
- Technology historically pushes humanity through phase changes.
