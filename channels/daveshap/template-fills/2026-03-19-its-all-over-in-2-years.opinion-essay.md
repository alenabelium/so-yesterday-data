## Thesis

Within one to two years, AI's super-exponential growth will render human intellectual contributions obsolete, forcing a painful societal transition toward post-labor economics.

## Argument

The mechanism is the convergence of three historical forces: the Fourth Turning, long-term debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution. Investment in AI rivals the Apollo and Manhattan projects, driving cognitive hyperabundance. For knowledge workers, this means the value of intellectual contribution drops to zero as machines become faster, cheaper, and safer. The emotional shift is from 'vesperance' (wistful nostalgia) to existential dread as norms like 'go to school, get a job, buy a house' unravel. However, not all is lost. Creative dimensions like novel-writing are mathematically infinite (10^100 to 10^150,000 possibilities), exceeding the atoms in the universe. Thus, while rigorous scientific work may end, social and creative commentary remains. The immediate future involves a major contraction of social networks (Dunbar's number) and a weird, incoherent narrative as society rebuilds from scratch, driven by the US-China AI race.

## Counterpoints

- AI will not replace human taste or the choice of which problems to solve.
- Creative output like novels is mathematically infinite, so human creativity remains viable.
- Social commentary, gossip, and embodied experiences will persist as long as humans exist.
