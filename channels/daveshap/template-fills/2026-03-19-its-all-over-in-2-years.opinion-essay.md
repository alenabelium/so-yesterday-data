## Thesis

Human intellectual labor is collapsing into obsolescence within two years due to super-exponential AI growth, leaving only non-rigorous creative and social dimensions for humans to inhabit.

## Argument

The mechanism is economic and physical: investment in AI rivals the Apollo program, driving cognitive hyperabundance. As GPU hardware and model performance improve exponentially, the material reality is that human brains cannot compete. The convergence of the Fourth Turning, long-term debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution upends every societal norm simultaneously. While creative domains like novel-writing offer infinite combinatorial space, rigorous intellectual work—post-labor economics, physics, chemistry, and coding—is already being surpassed by AI. The host frames this not as a gradual shift but as a visceral extinction rage, where the value of human problem-solving drops to zero because machines are faster, cheaper, and safer. We are witnessing the end of the white-collar office norm, replaced by a world where humans are either leveraging AI intuition or rendered intellectually useless.

## Counterpoints

- Creative dimensions like novels, poetry, and social commentary cannot be automated due to infinite combinatorial possibilities.
- Humans can still choose which problems to solve, though this is a 'cope' against superior machine capability.
- Social commentary and celebrity gossip will persist as long as humans remain the subjects of interest.
