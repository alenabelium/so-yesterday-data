## Thesis

Human intellectual labor will be rendered obsolete within two years as AI achieves cognitive hyperabundance, ending the era of the knowledge worker.

## Argument

The mechanism is economic and physical: AI investment rivals the Apollo program, accelerating GPU and model performance toward a state where human brains cannot compete. This convergence of the Fourth Turning, debt cycles, and the fourth industrial revolution upends all societal norms, from education to retirement. While the transition brings existential dread and 'extinction rage' as we burn out on compulsory labor, the material reality is that machines are already better, faster, and cheaper for problem-solving. The value of my intellectual contribution drops to zero because there are no problems for which I am better suited than the machines we are building. Even the '100x engineers' are merely driving the machine; soon, the machine drives itself. The only remaining human dimensions are those mathematically impossible to automate, such as the infinite variety of novels, but rigorous scientific and economic work is ending. We are witnessing the final contribution of the human intellect to these domains, followed by a major contraction of our social and psychological frameworks as we navigate this singularity.

## Counterpoints

- Human creativity like novel-writing is mathematically infinite and cannot be automated.
- Social commentary and embodied experiences will persist as long as humans exist.
- The US-China AI race will drive global change regardless of individual agency.
