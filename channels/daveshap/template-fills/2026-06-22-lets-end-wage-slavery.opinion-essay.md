## Thesis

We must proactively engineer a civilizational reset to end wage slavery before automation renders human labor obsolete.

## Argument

The Labor Zero movement has evolved from a book project into a broad civilizational initiative, fueled by over $46,000 in community support. This transition is driven by the convergence of artificial intelligence and humanoid robotics, which will fundamentally reshape the trajectory of humanity. The core of this effort is the Labor Zero Charter, which distills research on post-labor economics into 12 strategic objectives and 12 core values. These are anchored in historical patterns, political philosophy, game theory, and systems thinking. The shift is inevitable; it will not be automatic or smooth, but it is coming regardless of our readiness. We must build a coalition and make clear demands, echoing Frederick Douglass: "Power concedes nothing without a demand." The goal is to navigate the post-labor economy through proactive preparation rather than passive acceptance.

## Counterpoints

- The transition will not be automatic or smooth.
- The shift is coming whether we are ready or not.
