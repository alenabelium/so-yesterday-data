## Thesis

AI 'doomers' are performing theater rather than presenting serious evidence-based concerns about artificial intelligence risks.

## Argument

The rhetoric surrounding AI existential risk is a form of **[vranyo]**—a known lie that everyone pretends to accept. Doomers lack experimental evidence for their claims, relying instead on logic about future models they don't understand. Their demand to stop development lacks justification, especially when the industry reacts with excitement to new releases like Astra and Opus 3.5 rather than panic. This suggests they do not genuinely believe their own warnings.

Technologically, we are witnessing **[spillover-effect]**s where capabilities jump discretely despite smooth underlying progress. As seen in the history of computing and the internet, technology reaches threshold effects where it suddenly performs tasks far beyond professional human levels, such as creating CGI or complex code. These **[saltatory-jump]**s create new opportunities that take time to realize, rendering doomist paralysis illogical against the reality of rapid, practical advancement.

## Counterpoints

- Doomers might be using gallows humor or arrogance if they sincerely believe the world is ending soon.
- The industry's excitement suggests even skeptics are enjoying the technology rather than fearing it.
