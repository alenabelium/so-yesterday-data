## Thesis

Dario Amodei's claim that fully automated recursive self-improvement (RSI) is 6-12 months away is credible, and we are entering an era of cognitive hyperabundance where intelligence per token follows a power law, making RSI's operational.

## Argument

The mechanism is operational. Dario's prediction isn't hype; it's a base rate. He was off by at most 3 months on coding, and the ingredients for RSI—mathematical ability, automated hypothesis testing, code generation, and validation pipelines—are already here. We're seeing the exocortex build its own tooling in real time. The intelligence per token is rising on a power law, meaning each token is smarter, and we're crossing thresholds like energy efficiency where models like Llama 6B already beat humans on a one-to-one operational basis. This is cognitive hyperabundance. The bottleneck shifts to energy, chips, and data, not cognition. The doomer scenario of uncontrolled RSI is fiction; pipelines are gated, human oversight remains, and third-party evals exist. The real risk is negative EV human labor, where 100 top researchers become too many cooks in the kitchen.

## Counterpoints

- The jagged frontier: AI has spikes beyond human capabilities but also gaps, so AGI isn't complete yet.
- Materialism vs. consciousness: some argue human brains receive signals from a metaphysical space, but there's no empirical evidence.
- RSI could lead to runaway AI (Skynet scenario), but this ignores gated processes, human oversight, and third-party evals.
- Humans will always be needed in the loop, but the host argues that human judgment will become negative EV as AI improves.
