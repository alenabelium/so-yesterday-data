## Thesis

Five human skills—taste, agency, focus, ingenuity, and follow-through—are becoming more valuable in the AI era, not less, and mastering them will let individuals take on projects that once required teams or experts.

## Argument

The mechanism is the expansion of individual capability. AI has lowered the cost of implementation so dramatically that the barriers that once forced us to hire teams or defer to experts have collapsed. As I say, a service you couldn't launch without employees, a problem your team never had time to solve, an idea that required a technical expert you couldn't afford—those decisions were based on the requirements of the time, and many of them have changed with the advent of AI. This is why I'm betting that by 2027, more of us will be able to do the full amount of work that previously required many people. 

Taste is the first skill. It's acting on what you care about, not just recognizing quality. People early in their careers can have exceptional taste because they watch closely, try things, and develop their own understanding. This year, the opportunities to act on that understanding have increased. Take a part of your job where you keep noticing the same problem—new customers confused, an explanation that doesn't land. You can now say, 'To hell with everything, I will create a working solution.' That's a new kind of power. 

Agency is the second. AI expands the reach of your initiative. The same initiative has much greater potential now. You don't need to wait until you understand every step; the first useful attempt is already before you. A sales manager couldn't describe the entire system in advance, but an AI agent created a rough first version, and looking at it revealed the real problem—a contract stuck with lawyers. The process of creation helps you find a plan.

## Counterpoints

- The common story that AI is a coming wave washing away skills, making everything bleak.
- The idea that only geniuses or superteams can create important AI-based products.
- The trend of trying many things at once because AI makes it possible, which can waste potential.
- The assumption that years of experience automatically mean good taste.
- The belief that you need to understand every step before starting, which can block action.
