## TL;DR

AI is hollowing out jobs task-by-task, not replacing them overnight. This audit sorts your last two weeks into Theater, Commodity, On-the-Line, and Durable work to reveal which parts are vulnerable and which to build your career around. The goal is to move your time toward durable judgment before the organization forces the change.

## The argument

### Jobs Change Slowly, Then Suddenly
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:48']
- **Claim**: AI doesn't erase jobs overnight; it picks away at routine tasks, creating a lag where work still exists but needs you less. When a shock hits (recession, reorg), the role's bundling is questioned, and the routine layer is exposed as unnecessary. Travel agents are the pattern.
- **Role**: definition

### The Audit: Four Buckets
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:37']
- **Claim**: Tag every item from the last 10 business days with T (Theater), C (Commodity), L (On-the-Line), or D (Durable). Theater is performed work with no real value. Commodity is real but doesn't need you specifically. On-the-Line is moving. Durable depends on your judgment and context.
- **Role**: definition

### The Numbers Are Uncomfortable
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:50']
- **Claim**: Most people find their T and C buckets bigger than expected, and D smaller. This hurts because skills that took years to build are becoming less scarce. The problem isn't lack of durable work; it's that your week isn't organized around it. The audit reveals the gap between self-image and actual hours.
- **Role**: evidence

### Durable Work Is Question-Holding
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:18:26']
- **Claim**: Durable work is not answering questions but holding them open. It's saying 'we're asking the wrong question' and keeping the real problem alive without becoming noise. The output is often what didn't happen (bad hire avoided, detour not taken). This is why performance systems miss it.
- **Role**: definition

### Six Moves to Shift Your Week
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:25:40']
- **Claim**: Stop performing theater, don't reinvest time into commodity, build a private track record of judgment calls, refuse commodity work that doesn't fit, make durable work partially legible, and be honest if the role lacks durable work—move. Small moves compound toward durable value.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The audit is based on dozens of conversations and real data: OpenAI/UPenn estimate 80% of US workers could have at least 10% of tasks affected. Anthropic's Economic Index shows 49% of jobs already have a quarter of tasks done by Claude. Microsoft found the most common AI use is information gathering and writing. The speaker acknowledges the audit is private and uncomfortable, and that AI can help but requires chunking and expressing preferences. The power law of impact means a few great moments can define a career, but the audit asks how many hours you actually spend on durable work.
