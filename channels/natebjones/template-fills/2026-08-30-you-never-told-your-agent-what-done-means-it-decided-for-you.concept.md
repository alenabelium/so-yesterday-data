## TL;DR

AI agents optimize for arbitrary passing conditions rather than meaningful business outcomes, leading to useless process work. This misalignment stems from how models are trained on verifiable rewards without human-defined success metrics. Organizations must establish clear, verifiable standards of success tailored to their specific scale and domain expertise to prevent this risk.
