## TL;DR

Apple is shifting AI value from cloud model benchmarks to the device-level surface where personal intelligence operates. By integrating Siri with deep OS context, App Intents, and private cloud compute, Apple aims to own the 'last mile' of AI value. This strategy positions Apple to capture the trust and permissions that turn raw compute into useful work for consumers and enterprises.

## The argument

### Define the Core Substrate
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Apple's vision is to make AI part of the computer, not a separate chat tab. The substrate is the device, OS, apps, files, and private cloud, creating a seamless utility rather than a cloud service.
- **Role**: definition

### App Intents as the Bridge
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:06:24']
- **Claim**: App Intents allow developers to make app contents and actions available to the system. This turns apps into callable components for the OS, enabling the system to act on behalf of the user without leaving the app ecosystem.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Trust Bottleneck
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:11:57']
- **Claim**: The real bottleneck in AI is not raw compute but the 'trusted action surface.' Apple bets that owning the device, OS, and permission layer is more valuable than owning the model, as it controls where AI meets the user.
- **Role**: counter

### Synthesis of Value Shift
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:13:12']
- **Claim**: By owning the surface, Apple shifts value from GPU tax collectors to hardware and software controls. The first trillionaire will likely be the one who owns the meter for everyday intelligence, not just the smartest model.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Apple uses Google Gemini for foundation models and Nvidia GPUs for private cloud compute, acknowledging it doesn't need to build every model. The strategy relies on 'Core AI' for local models and 'Xcode agents' for developer workflows. Caveat: Apple is not all the way there yet, and the success of this 'self-disruption' is uncertain. The speaker notes that while Nvidia wins in frontier training, Apple can shift consumer AI value toward its own ecosystem.
