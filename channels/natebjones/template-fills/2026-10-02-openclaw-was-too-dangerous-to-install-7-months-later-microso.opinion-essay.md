## Thesis

Value in enterprise AI comes from data access and user skill, not model intelligence, and Microsoft's distribution advantage will shape the workplace.

## Argument

Microsoft's 450 million paid seats dwarf OpenAI's 9 million business users, making Autopilot the default agent for most workers. The tool isn't smart—it's the data it can reach that matters. Five practices determine outcomes: define what 'good' looks like, provide correct context and verify evidence, make tasks repeatable, match reasoning to complexity, and improve the next run. The K-shaped future means 5% of users race ahead while others stagnate. Microsoft's real edge is trust and distribution, not model quality. Anthropic and Meta challenge this by offering better models or customer data, but Microsoft's existing relationships and data access give it a massive head start. The competition is about who understands the job well enough to delegate work to, not who has the smartest model.
