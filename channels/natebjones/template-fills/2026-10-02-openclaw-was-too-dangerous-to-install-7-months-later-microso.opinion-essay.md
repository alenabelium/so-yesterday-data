## Thesis

Microsoft's Autopilot will bring agentic AI to hundreds of millions of workers, but the value it creates depends on data access and user skill, not model intelligence.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Microsoft embeds Autopilot into Excel, Outlook, and Teams, reaching 450 million seats — far more than any standalone AI release. Value comes from the data it can access and how well you use it, not raw intelligence. Five practices determine outcomes: define what 'good' looks like, feed the model the right context and verify evidence, make tasks repeatable, match reasoning to complexity, and obsess over improving the next run. Microsoft's advantage is trust and existing relationships, but Anthropic and Meta are competing on model quality and business reach. The K-shaped future means same tools, wildly different results.

## Counterpoints

- Microsoft doesn't have its own frontier model; Anthropic's scaling could redesign workflows entirely, making Microsoft's processes obsolete.
- Meta's enterprise platform targets small businesses with customer data, a different relationship that could intersect and clash with Microsoft's workplace dominance.
- Router-based cost optimization can backfire: spending more tokens to route a task than just using a smart model, especially for complex new problems.
