## TL;DR

Shopify's April 2025 memo made AI usage mandatory, turning it into a selection filter for talent. Eight months later, the company reports 30% higher productivity and a 10x intern expansion, while copycats like Duolingo face backlash. This signals a broader market shift where AI fluency becomes baseline and compensation polarizes.

## The argument

### Define the Red Queen Framework
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Tobi Lutke's 'Red Queen' framework, borrowed from Through the Looking Glass, means a company growing 20-40% yearly requires employees to improve at least that much just to stay relevant. Stagnation is slow-motion termination, not just failure.
- **Role**: definition

### The Memo as a Selection Filter
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:02:44']
- **Claim**: The memo's mandates—reflexive AI usage, proving AI can't do the work before hiring, and AI in performance reviews—are not about productivity but about reshaping who works at Shopify. It filters for AI-native talent and pushes others out.
- **Role**: evidence

### Infrastructure Enables the Mandate
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:37']
- **Claim**: Shopify's years of AI infrastructure—internal LLM proxy, dozens of MCP servers, open-sourced ROST framework—made the mandate possible. Without this groundwork, copycats like Duolingo fail because they lack the cultural and technical foundation.
- **Role**: evidence

### Copycat Failures Show the Cost of Missing Groundwork
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:13:23']
- **Claim**: Duolingo's similar memo caused subscription cancellations and a CEO walk-back, while Box's approach of letting teams keep AI savings proved more durable. The difference: Shopify formalized existing behavior, while others mandated without preparation.
- **Role**: counter

### The U-Shaped Talent Market and Future Trends
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:12:13']
- **Claim**: Shopify's 10x intern expansion targets AI-native juniors, while senior experts gain leverage. This creates a U-shaped talent market. By 2026, AI fluency becomes baseline, roles dissolve, and compensation polarizes—the Red Queen race accelerates.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Shopify reports ~30% higher productivity, but they've been investing for years. The memo's success hinges on infrastructure like the LLM proxy and MCP servers. Copycats like Duolingo faced backlash, while Box's incentive structure worked better. Caveats: Goodhart's law—AI usage metrics can be gamed; academic research lags technology; entry-level squeeze intensifies despite Shopify's intern push.
