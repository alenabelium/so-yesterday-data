## Thesis

AI leaderboards and benchmarks are dead; the new benchmark is customer obsession, and the AI products that win will be those built by teams that obsessively use them and fix real problems.

## Argument

The mechanism is customer obsession. Google's Argon model tops a leaderboard, but almost no one can use it, so no one can tell you if it's useful. The AI that wins now is created by humans who obsessively use it and fix bugs. This is the new benchmark, and it's the same test for the AI your company is creating.

Look at the lab-to-client map: Anthropic focuses on the corporate sector, OpenAI has enterprise and consumer arms, Meta is deeply immersed in the consumer segment. But Google? Google makes money from computing and search, yet it's unclear how Argon supports those usage scenarios. Why do we need such an advanced model? The close relationship between customer and product is what matters in the age of AI.

If you work in a company, you must understand: in a world where intelligence evolves every week, how do you make money with AI when the underlying model keeps changing? Development plans are no longer working. The bigger challenge is creating a product with ever-evolving intelligence that helps users understand what will happen to their world when AI changes for the better. This is a tough product challenge, and it starts with understanding who your customer is and what they do.

I believe the best AI products are created by people who use them obsessively, notice when things go wrong, and give meaning to those mistakes. ## Counterpoints

- Some might say you can just go to the website and do it yourself, but when you're short on time, it's nice to delegate the work to AI and just get the result.
- People worry about trusting AI labs like Anthropic or OpenAI, but more players in the game makes things safer and better for consumers.
- Skeptics might dismiss AI's role in emotional moments, but it can provide an outside perspective and help you think better without forming a parasocial relationship.
