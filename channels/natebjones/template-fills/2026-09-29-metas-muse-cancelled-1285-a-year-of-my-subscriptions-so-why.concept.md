## TL;DR

Amazon blocked Meta's Muse not for technical reasons, but because it threatens the 'inattention model'—the strategy of profiting from consumer inertia and subscription drift. While Amazon defends its $68B ad revenue by controlling the purchase journey, Meta leverages tangible personal utility to capture the customer relationship directly.

## The argument

### Define the Inattention Model
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:17:47']
- **Claim**: The 'inattention model' is a technical tool where companies profit from subscriber forgetfulness. Studies show it increases mobile operator revenue by 87% on average, proving that consumer inertia is a massive, untapped economic lever.
- **Role**: definition

### Evidence of Agentic Disruption
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:12:55']
- **Claim**: Muse disrupts this by proactively reviewing expenses and canceling subscriptions, forcing consumers to actively confirm or drop services. This breaks the passive payment loop that Amazon and Google rely on for their advertising dominance.
- **Role**: evidence

### Counter: The Platform Battle
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:11:59']
- **Claim**: Amazon's block is a defense of its $68.6B advertising business, which generates more net profit than retail. By blocking Muse, Amazon prevents retailers from shifting visibility sales to an agent that bypasses Amazon's sponsored spots.
- **Role**: counter

### Synthesis: Utility Over Intelligence
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:19:44']
- **Claim**: Success lies in personal context and execution, not raw model intelligence. Muse wins by being a 'cute bear' that remembers your life, turning AI from a novelty into a necessary utility that captures the customer relationship before the purchase.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Muse saved the host $1,285/year and another user $1,156 in insurance. However, Meta initially used human contractors for calls, raising privacy concerns (00:02:22). Walmart abandoned ChatGPT checkout due to low conversion (3x lower than site visits), suggesting AI commerce is still experimental (00:16:37). The 'inattention model' relies on card replacement triggering cancellations, but this effect may vary by service type (00:17:47).
