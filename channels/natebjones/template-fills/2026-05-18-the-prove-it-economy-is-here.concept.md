## TL;DR

The internet economy is shifting from attention to interpretation, where AI agents mediate consumer and hiring decisions. To succeed, individuals and brands must build a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data that allows AI to accurately understand and recommend their offerings. Simultaneously, maintaining strong human connections remains critical, as humans ultimately apply preference to AI-mediated options.

## The argument

### The Shift to Interpretation
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: The economy has moved from an attention model (shouting for eyeballs) to an interpretation economy where AI agents filter all decisions. Success requires positioning for AI as the first port of call, not just human attention.
- **Role**: definition

### Building the Truth Layer
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:51']
- **Claim**: To avoid being flattened into the internet average, you must provide a 'truth layer' of high-fidelity, structured data (e.g., JSON schemas, specific technical details) that agents can ingest. Agents need provable facts, not emotional marketing claims, to map customer intent to your product or skills.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Role of Human Memory
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:13']
- **Claim**: Human memory becomes more precious as transactions are mediated by AI. Offline events and emotional connections create brand loyalty that constrains AI choices. The two internets must reinforce each other: human-facing work builds trust, while agent-facing work provides clarity.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker cites his own purchase of a sound system via AI chat as evidence of the interpretation economy in action. He warns against 'AI washing' and emphasizes that back-office automation is merely 'table stakes' in 2026. A key caveat is that agents may not always provide the 'truly best' option, but rather what seems best based on available data, making the quality of the truth layer critical. The speaker also notes that while agents do the comparison, the human still applies the final preference.
