## TL;DR

The internet economy is shifting from attention to interpretation, where AI agents mediate decisions. Success requires building a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data that allows agents to understand and recommend your product or skills, while simultaneously maintaining strong human brand loyalty to drive specific intent.

## The argument

### Shift to Interpretation Economy
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:53']
- **Claim**: The economy is moving from an attention model (shouting for eyeballs) to an interpretation model where AI agents filter all consumer and hiring decisions. Individuals and marketers must position themselves for AI first, not just human attention.
- **Role**: definition

### Build a Truth Layer
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:51']
- **Claim**: To avoid being flattened into the internet average, you must provide a 'truth layer' of high-fidelity, structured data (like JSON schemas or detailed specs) that agents can extract. Agents need provable, opinionated facts, not just emotional marketing language, to map customer intent to your offering.
- **Role**: evidence

### Reinforce with Human Memory
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:13']
- **Claim**: Human memory and offline brand loyalty become more valuable as transactions become mediated. While agents handle comparison, humans apply preference. Strong human connections ensure you are asked for by name, constraining the agent's search and preventing you from being averaged out.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker cites buying a sound system via chat with Claude and ChatGPT as a personal example of agent-mediated shopping. He notes that back-office automation (like using AI to write resumes) is merely 'table stakes' and does not provide competitive advantage. A key caveat is the risk of 'AI washing': if your product truth layer is incoherent or your brand creates no memory, you fail in both the agentic and human internets. The speaker emphasizes that you must be opinionated and specific to stand out; vague, averaged-out content is ignored by agents.
