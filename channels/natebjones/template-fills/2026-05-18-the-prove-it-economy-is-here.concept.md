## TL;DR

The internet economy is shifting from attention to interpretation, where AI agents mediate hiring and purchasing decisions. To survive, individuals and brands must build a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data that allows agents to verify claims. Simultaneously, offline human connection remains vital for brand loyalty, creating a dual strategy for the future.

## The argument

### Define the Interpretation Economy
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: The economy has moved from an attention model (shouting for eyeballs) to an interpretation model where AI agents filter all decisions. Success requires positioning for AI agents, not just humans.
- **Role**: definition

### Build a Truth Layer
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:51']
- **Claim**: Marketers and candidates must provide high-fidelity, structured data (a 'truth layer') that agents can read. Without opinionated, provable facts, you are flattened into the internet average.
- **Role**: definition

### Leverage Offline Memory
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:11:38']
- **Claim**: Human memory and offline events create brand loyalty that constrains AI choices. If a human trusts a brand offline, they ask the AI for that specific brand, bypassing general comparison.
- **Role**: evidence

### Synthesize the Two Internets
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:05']
- **Claim**: The future requires reinforcing both human-facing memory and agent-facing clarity. AI washing fails because it lacks the substantive truth layer agents need to verify claims.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker cites buying a sound system via AI chat as a personal example of the interpretation economy in action. He notes that while back-office automation (like AI resume writing) is table stakes, it doesn't provide leverage. The caveat is that agents need 'provable' data, not just emotional marketing claims. He warns that if your story is memorable but your data is incoherent, agents will flatten you. Conversely, if you have great data but no human memory, you may appear in comparisons but lose the final choice to human preference.
