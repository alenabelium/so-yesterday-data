## TL;DR

The internet economy is shifting from attention to interpretation, where AI agents mediate hiring and purchasing decisions. To survive, individuals and brands must build a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data that allows agents to verify their value. Simultaneously, human brand loyalty remains critical as the final arbiter of preference.

## The argument

### Define the Interpretation Economy
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: The economy has moved from an attention model (shouting for eyeballs) to an interpretation model where AI agents filter all consumer and hiring decisions. Success requires positioning for AI agents, not just humans.
- **Role**: definition

### Build the Truth Layer
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:51']
- **Claim**: To avoid being flattened into the internet average, entities must provide a 'truth layer' of high-fidelity, structured data (e.g., JSON schemas, technical specs) that agents can read and trust. Emotional marketing fails; provable, opinionated facts succeed.
- **Role**: definition

### Reinforce with Human Loyalty
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:13']
- **Claim**: Human memory and offline connection become more valuable as transactions are mediated by AI. Strong brand loyalty ensures humans ask for specific products by name, constraining the agent's search and preventing it from offering alternatives.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker cites buying a sound system via AI chat as a personal example of the interpretation economy in action. He warns against 'AI washing' (deceptive claims) and notes that back-office automation (like AI resume writing) is merely 'table stakes' in 2026, not a differentiator. The caveat is that if your truth layer is incoherent or your brand lacks memory, you will be averaged out or ignored by agents.
