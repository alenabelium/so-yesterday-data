## TL;DR

The internet is shifting from an attention economy to an interpretation economy where AI agents mediate decisions. To succeed, individuals and brands must build a 'truth layer' of structured, factual data that allows agents to accurately understand and recommend them. Simultaneously, maintaining strong human connections and brand loyalty remains critical, as humans ultimately apply preference to AI-mediated options.

## The argument

### Define the Interpretation Economy
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: The economy has shifted from attention (shouting for eyeballs) to interpretation (AI filtering the web). Consumers and hiring managers ask AI agents for trust and recommendations, making AI the first port of call.
- **Role**: definition

### Build the Truth Layer
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:42']
- **Claim**: To be selected by agents, you need a 'truth layer': high-fidelity, structured data (like JSON schemas or clear DOM structures) that proves your value. Without opinionated, provable details, you are flattened into the internet average.
- **Role**: definition

### Counter the AI Washing Trap
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:55']
- **Claim**: AI washing is dangerous because it deceives without providing the structural clarity agents need. The alternative is leaning into genuine expertise and specific opinions, which allows agents to translate your value into compelling summaries.
- **Role**: counter

### Synthesize the Two-Internet Strategy
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:13']
- **Claim**: Success requires reinforcing two sides: human-facing work (offline events, emotional connection) for memory and preference, and agent-facing work (structured data) for clarity and retrievability. Ignoring either side weakens your position.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker cites buying a sound system via AI chat as a consumer example where AI interpreted needs. For individuals, he references the 'Talent Board' project to prove AI skills. He notes that hiring managers trade prompts for candidates, confirming the shift. Caveat: The speaker acknowledges that agents are doing the shopping, but humans still apply the final preference. He warns that if your brand story is memorable but your truth layer is incoherent, the agent flattens you; if your data is structured but your brand has no memory, the human won't choose you.
