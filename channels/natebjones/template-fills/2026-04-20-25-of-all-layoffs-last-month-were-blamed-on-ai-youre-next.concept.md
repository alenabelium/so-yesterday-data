## TL;DR

AI has made code generation nearly free, breaking the old chain where production signaled effort and expertise. Now, proving your value requires demonstrating comprehension, not just output. This video outlines five principles to make your thinking visible and verifiable in the age of AI.

## The argument

### The Broken Value Chain
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:40']
- **Claim**: Production used to be hard, signifying effort and expertise. Now that AI makes generation easy, this chain breaks, leaving everyone—from juniors to mid-career PMs—unable to prove their worth.
- **Role**: definition

### Comprehension as the New Skill
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:05:41']
- **Claim**: With AI, the industry produces at unprecedented speed but comprehends at unprecedented lows. The key is to force yourself to understand your work deeply—what it does, why, and what could break—building taste and avoiding failures like the AWS outage.
- **Role**: evidence

### Explanation as an Artifact
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:10:02']
- **Claim**: To make comprehension visible, ship structured explanations with your work: what it does, why you chose this approach, what might break, and what you learned. This is like a commit message—it signals understanding and separates you from AI slop.
- **Role**: synthesis

### Microtransactions for Work
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:13:14']
- **Claim**: Credentials are inflating away, but transactions—real work for real money—remain the ultimate proof. With AI accelerating timelines, we need microtransactions to capture value in compressed periods, replacing stale resumes.
- **Role**: counter

### Work in the Open
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:15:23']
- **Claim**: Professional development used to happen privately, but that system is broken. Working in public lets you be observed and prove your skills to the world, turning your work into a social, shareable signal of your value.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The video cites over 60,000 tech layoffs in Q1 2026, including Oracle (30K), Amazon (16K), and Dell (11K), as evidence of companies reassessing workforce value. The AWS outage, where an AI tool deleted the production environment, illustrates the danger of production outpacing comprehension. The speaker acknowledges that working in the open can be uncomfortable and may not fit every situation, but argues it's a better bet than staying private. He also notes that while AI can generate explanations, they won't stand up to human scrutiny if you don't truly understand the work.
