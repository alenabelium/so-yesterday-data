## Thesis

Perplexity Computer is a great product, but it won't save Perplexity because the company is structurally trapped in the middleware layer, squeezed by hyperscalers who control both the models and the customer relationships.

## Argument

The trap is structural, not about execution. Perplexity sits between model providers who own the weights and distribution owners who control the surface. Every model provider it depends on is simultaneously building the exact product Computer competes with: Anthropic shipped Claude Co-work with deep connectors, OpenAI launched Frontier as an enterprise context platform, and Google is embedding agents into Android. The asymmetry between Perplexity's execution quality and its structural fragility is the real story. 

When a stack consolidates, the layer between platform owner and customer gets squeezed. It happened to travel agents and media companies. In AI, you're in that trap if you build on models you don't control and serve customers that model providers now sell to directly. The hyperscalers are not neutral referees; they need trillions of tokens to justify their capex. Every layer they don't control is value captured by someone else from compute they subsidize. That's why AWS secured exclusive distribution for Frontier and Microsoft takes 20% of OpenAI's revenue. 

Perplexity's moat—multimodel orchestration—is easy to replicate. The commoditization of models makes the orchestration layer less valuable. The live demo was cancelled due to bugs, signaling reliability isn't baked. Yet Perplexity is also building one of the most interesting ways out: the search API, which is agent infrastructure that four of the Magnificent 7 run in production. That's the strategic out, not Computer. 

Durable positions in 2026 are narrower: own proprietary context that enterprises can't

## Counterpoints

- Perplexity has domain expertise in search and research that model makers can't replicate.
- Perplexity's search API is already used by four of the Magnificent 7, making it resilient infrastructure.
- Perplexity's trust-focused strategy (killing ads) gives it a differentiation edge.
- Perplexity could pivot to owning proprietary context or workflow depth to escape the trap.
