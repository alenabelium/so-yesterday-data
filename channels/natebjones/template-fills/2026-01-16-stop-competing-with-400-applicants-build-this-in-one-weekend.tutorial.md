## Headline takeaway

Build an AI-powered personal site that lets hiring managers query your experience, explore your work, and assess fit—turning a 0.4% application success rate into a five-minute human conversation.

## Prerequisites

### Lovable account
- **Kind**: account
- **Note**: Free tier is sufficient to build and host the site; no coding required.

### Substack build guide
- **Kind**: knowledge
- **Note**: Nate's complete implementation guide with prompts, linked in the video description.

### Substantial career experience
- **Kind**: knowledge
- **Note**: The approach amplifies real depth; it won't fake expertise for early-career candidates.

## Steps

### Open Lovable and start a new project
- **Timestamp**: [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1074)
- **Action**: Nate explains that building the site requires no technical skills—he built the demo in Lovable. Start a new project in Lovable and use the prompt from his Substack guide to generate the initial site structure.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Navigate to lovable.dev, click 'Create new project', paste the starter prompt from the guide.

### Customize the AI chat and context sections
- **Timestamp**: [18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1115)
- **Action**: Nate walks through the demo site for fictional engineer Marcus Chen. He shows the 'Ask AI about me' button that opens a chat, and the 'View AI context' expandable sections behind each resume bullet point. Customize these to reflect your own experience and projects.
- **Command Or Clicks**: In Lovable, edit the chat component and the expandable context sections to match your resume bullets.

### Add the skills gap disclosure section
- **Timestamp**: [20:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1215)
- **Action**: Nate highlights the three-column skills section: strong, moderate, and gaps. He notes that publishing gaps signals confidence and self-awareness. Add your own skills breakdown, being honest about areas of weakness.
- **Command Or Clicks**: In Lovable, add a three-column layout and populate with your skills.

### Implement the fit assessment tool
- **Timestamp**: [21:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1260)
- **Action**: Nate shows the fit assessment tool where a hiring manager pastes a job description and the AI evaluates fit honestly, explaining why it's a strong or weak match. Build this feature using Lovable's AI capabilities, connecting it to your experience data.
- **Command Or Clicks**: In Lovable, add a text input for job descriptions and configure the AI to analyze fit against your profile.

### Deploy and share your site
- **Timestamp**: [24:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1457)
- **Action**: Nate emphasizes that the interface is a conversion tool, not a lead generator—you still need distribution. Deploy the site and share it in your professional networks, on your resume, and in communities where your expertise matters.
- **Command Or Clicks**: Click 'Deploy' in Lovable, then share the URL on LinkedIn, your resume, and relevant communities.

## Gotchas

### The approach requires real substance—you cannot fake depth in multi-turn conversations about domains you don't understand.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=898)

### If you're early in your career without much experience, an AI interface won't compensate; a portfolio site showing learning ability is better.
- **Severity**: heads_up
- **Timestamp**: [15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=957)

### This is not a replacement for distribution—you still need to get your work in front of people through networking and public presence.
- **Severity**: serious
- **Timestamp**: [22:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1366)

### In traditional industries, an AI chat interface might feel gimmicky—know your audience before using this approach.
- **Severity**: heads_up
- **Timestamp**: [22:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0teZqotpqT8&t=1366)
