## TL;DR

Anthropic's leaked 'Conway' is an always-on agent that accumulates a behavioral model of how you work, creating unprecedented switching costs. By pairing open standards like MCP with a proprietary extension format, Anthropic is racing to own the persistent agent layer—the interface where enterprise work happens. This shifts AI competition from model quality to ownership of memory and context.

## The argument

### Conway is an always-on agent environment
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Conway is a standalone agent environment separate from chat, with its own extension format, wake-on-events, browser control, and tool connections. It's an internal project not on Anthropic's roadmap, but it reveals a platform strategy.
- **Role**: definition

### The proprietary extension layer creates lock-in
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:07:37']
- **Claim**: Conway uses MCP but adds a proprietary CNW.zip extension format, creating a Google Play Services pattern: open foundation, proprietary valuable layer. Developers face a choice between portable MCP tools with no distribution and Conway-only extensions with an app store, mirroring the 2008 Apple/iPhone dynamic.
- **Role**: evidence

### Behavioral memory is the ultimate switching cost
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:13:02']
- **Claim**: Conway locks in the accumulated model of how you work—patterns, priorities, context—not just data. This 'intelligence portability' has no legal framework and creates switching costs far beyond data migration, making the agent's learned understanding of you the primary asset.
- **Role**: synthesis

### The era of persistence and memory
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:30']
- **Claim**: AI competition has moved from model quality to who owns the interface, and now to who owns the always-on layer that accumulates context and acts autonomously. All major labs converge on this as the money product, with the model as a loss leader.
- **Role**: synthesis

### The enterprise-employee power shift
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:20:24']
- **Claim**: Persistent agents empower enterprises over employees: companies can measure and leverage your behavioral imprint, creating a carrot-and-stick dynamic. Choosing an employer increasingly means choosing a persistent agent, with lock-in effects strengthening in 2026.
- **Role**: counter

## Evidence and caveats

The leak reveals Conway's three core areas (search, chat, system) with extensions, connectors, and automatic triggers. Anthropic's recent moves—Claude Code channels, Co-Work, Marketplace, and the third-party ban—form a coordinated platform strategy. The speaker notes that Conway's overnight work might be partially wrong, but the net value comes from speed and iteration. He also acknowledges that convenience may win for many companies, and that the behavioral lock-in is a trade many will accept. The speaker suggests portability solutions like a 'behavioral audit' but admits employers may hold the cards.
