## Wider context

The agent landscape is shifting from technical self-hosting to managed, high-leverage platforms. Grok Bot's dedicated cloud computer and natural language authorization lower the barrier to entry, targeting non-technical users who previously found tools like OpenClaw or Claude Code too complex. This moves the gating constraint from setup difficulty to subscription cost ($200/mo), positioning agents as a business infrastructure investment rather than just a productivity toy.
