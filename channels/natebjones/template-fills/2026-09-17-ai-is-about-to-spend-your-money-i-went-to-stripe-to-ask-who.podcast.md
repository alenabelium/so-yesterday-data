## Key arguments

### From Transaction to Identity Fraud Detection
- **Timestamp**: [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTG0rdHPTDE&t=613)
- **Summary**: Traditional fraud tools like Stripe Radar focus on the transaction moment, but AI abuse happens pre-transaction via token theft. Stripe moved from scoring transactions to identifying risky customers across its network, embedding directly with clients like Cursor to stop credit-stealing before it impacts margins.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### The Rise of Outcome-Based Pricing
- **Timestamp**: [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTG0rdHPTDE&t=921)
- **Summary**: As AI products become dynamic, static subscriptions fail. The market is shifting toward usage-based and outcome-based billing where customers pay for value delivered. This requires robust evals to measure quality and routers like Open Router to optimize cost vs. performance in real-time.

### Agents as Independent Economic Actors
- **Timestamp**: [17:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTG0rdHPTDE&t=1033)
- **Summary**: Stripe Link is evolving into a wallet for agents, enabling machine-readable payments via the Machine Payments Protocol. This infrastructure allows agents to discover, negotiate, and pay for services autonomously, creating a new layer of economic rails for agent-to-agent commerce.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### The Challenge of Agent-to-Agent Negotiation
- **Timestamp**: [27:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTG0rdHPTDE&t=1664)
- **Summary**: In an agent-driven market, relentless negotiation algorithms will likely erode consumer surplus quickly. Unlike humans who hide their true valuation, agents may drive prices down to marginal cost through persistent micro-negotiations, fundamentally altering market dynamics.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]
