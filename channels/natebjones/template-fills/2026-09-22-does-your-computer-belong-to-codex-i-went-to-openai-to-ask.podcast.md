## Key arguments

### Computer Use as Universal Connector
- **Timestamp**: [06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=373)
- **Summary**: Andrew Ash argues that computer use has become a 'universal connector,' bypassing the need for APIs or MCPs. He underestimated the scaling laws of visual interaction, noting that agents can now navigate complex, non-API-driven tasks like tax filing and 3D modeling with surprising fluency.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Shift from Execution to Taste
- **Timestamp**: [19:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1193)
- **Summary**: Ashe posits that as AI lowers the cost of creation, the human bottleneck shifts to 'taste' and decision-making. The key skill becomes curating AI outputs and having clarity of thought to mold coherent products from infinite possibilities.

### Emerging Interface Trends
- **Timestamp**: [30:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1823)
- **Summary**: The speakers highlight a trend toward voice input paired with visual output. While voice is efficient for context ingestion, reading remains faster for consumption. The future lies in fluid, bidirectional interfaces that allow users to interrupt and shape agent runs mid-execution.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Token Efficiency & Model Roles
- **Timestamp**: [23:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1427)
- **Summary**: The conversation explores token efficiency strategies, such as using smaller models for execution and larger ones for orchestration. They emphasize measuring ROI by outcome rather than token count, noting that smarter models can be more efficient per unit of work accomplished.

### Proactive AI & Blind Spots
- **Timestamp**: [27:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1664)
- **Summary**: Ashe describes the next frontier as proactive AI that identifies user blind spots. Instead of waiting for queries, agents will analyze context to surface critical insights, requiring high confidence in directionality and insight quality to avoid spamming users.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]
