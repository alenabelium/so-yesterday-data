## TL;DR

Digital platforms like LinkedIn, Spotify, and banks hold vast user data but only surface it in ways that serve their own interests, creating an informational asymmetry. Legally mandated data exports and AI's natural language analysis now let individuals export their data and ask their own questions. This shifts the power dynamic, enabling insights like relationship half-lives and warm paths to target companies.

## The argument

### Define the Core Substrate
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:00:00']
- **Claim**: Digital platforms hold your data but only show you their interpretation, optimized for engagement and premium conversions. The questions their interfaces let you ask are only those that serve the platform's interests, not yours. This informational asymmetry has felt permanent but is now optional.
- **Role**: definition

### The Unlock: Export and Query
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:01:00']
- **Claim**: The unlock is simple: export your data from the platform, feed it to an AI, and ask your own questions. Legally mandated data exports and AI's ability to analyze unstructured data with natural language queries fundamentally change the power dynamic. You can now spin up complex Python functions to query deep CSV data from LinkedIn.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Counter: Platform Limitations
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:02:52']
- **Claim**: LinkedIn knows your complete connection graph, message timestamps, reciprocity patterns, and career trajectories, but it doesn't tell you which relationships are decaying, who would vouch for you, or your warmest path to any company. The interface answers their questions, not yours. Your questions have no button.
- **Role**: counter

### Synthesis: The Network Intelligence Dashboard
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:04:17']
- **Claim**: By feeding exported LinkedIn data to Claude or ChatGPT, you can build a Network Intelligence Dashboard that calculates relationship half-lives, reciprocity ledgers, vouch scores, conversation resurrection opportunities, and warm path discovery. Each piece leverages AI's natural language understanding and pattern recognition to produce insights that would have required a dedicated engineering team.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The video demonstrates the concept with a real LinkedIn Network Intelligence Dashboard built from the host's own data. It calculates relationship half-life models (e.g., a relationship loses half its strength every 180 days if untouched), reciprocity ledgers tracking social capital balances, vouch scores predicting who would advocate for you, conversation resurrection scanning for dormant threads with re-engagement hooks, and warm path discovery ranking connections by relevance and warmth to reach target companies. The host notes that the half-life model can be adjusted, and the reciprocity ledger 'is not perfect' but a 'really interesting start.' The analysis requires AI because it involves parsing thousands of messages and making qualitative judgments about conversation substance, which traditional software interfaces wouldn't attempt.
