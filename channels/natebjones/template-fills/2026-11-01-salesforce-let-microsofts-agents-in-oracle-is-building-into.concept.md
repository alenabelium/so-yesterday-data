## TL;DR

AI agents are reshaping B2B competition. Salesforce, Microsoft, and Oracle are opening doors for each other while racing to own the 'agent control plane'—the layer that manages and coordinates agents across enterprise workflows. The real battleground is not the models but the customer relationship and the ability to mediate agent-driven work.

## The argument

### The New Competitive Height
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:01:03']
- **Claim**: AI agents change what counts as a favorable position. The height is owning the relationship with the agent, the control panel that mediates all work computing. Companies like Microsoft and Salesforce, once complementary, now compete for this vantage point.
- **Role**: definition

### Why Competitors Open Doors
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:24']
- **Claim**: Salesforce and Microsoft, and Oracle and OpenAI, integrate each other's products to gain customer trust and demonstrate choice. This is a strategic move to be present at the point of action, even if it means helping a competitor reach customers.
- **Role**: evidence

### The Control Plane Defined
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:09:31']
- **Claim**: The agent control plane is the software that oversees agents, coordinating their work, enforcing policies, and shaping the worker's experience. It's like Windows in the 1990s—a layer that defines how work gets done.
- **Role**: definition

### Data Isn't the Moat
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:14:54']
- **Claim**: Data ownership doesn't dictate where work happens. Microsoft Fabric lets you reference external data, and Oracle can help OpenAI become more useful. The customer can keep existing systems and change how they're used, lowering barriers to competition.
- **Role**: counter

### The Real Moat: Accumulated Work Knowledge
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:16:53']
- **Claim**: The useful knowledge accumulated while doing work—instructions, corrections, learned skills—is where the customer relationship lives. A vendor that preserves this across teams becomes hard to replace, creating a longer-term role than a chatbot.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

Salesforce's Agent Context Engine (in preview) and Microsoft's similar efforts aim to provide common business context for agents. The Oracle-ChatGPT workspace is under development but signals a clear direction. The speaker notes these products are 'somewhat similar, though not exactly the same,' making them difficult to compare. He also cautions that the market may not be winner-take-all; there's room for several winners, and the dynamic could shift in 12-24 months.
