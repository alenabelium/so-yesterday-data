## Wider context

The real story is the emergence of a 'context layer' between applications and data, where AI's accumulated understanding of your business compounds with every model upgrade. This is a fundamentally different upgrade cycle from traditional software, as the intelligence inside tools improves automatically without user action. The value is migrating from the application layer to the intelligence layer, making Microsoft a 'dumb pipe' and shifting the premium from execution skills to judgment.
