## Thesis

The agent war is won on utility, not form factor: every vendor can copy a computer, memory, and background work, but the quality of work an agent does for you is what makes it worth $100 a month.

## Argument

The form factor for agents is converging—OpenAI's Dots, Meta's Muse, and Grok's bot all look the same, with a computer, memory, and background work. But utility does not converge. The difference is the model behind the agent and how it's configured for specific tasks. OpenAI's bet is that context is the moat: with 1.2 billion users, they have more context than anyone, and Dots is the first step in making that context work for you proactively. The real story of Dev Day is not Dots itself but the ecosystem: Spaces and Pages are a direct challenge to Microsoft Office, and the GPT-6.1 Soul model is the unsung hero, delivering near-Astra quality at a fifth of the cost, which lets you spin the flywheel of context further without exhausting your plan limits. The strategic question is whether OpenAI can sell this to enterprises, where Microsoft has a natural trust advantage. But the bigger opportunity is for small teams: with Dots, Spaces, and the Decisions API, you can now build services that deliver outcomes, not just activity. The future is about finding ambitions that match the intelligence available, and that's a trillion-dollar question.

## Counterpoints

- Why pay $100 a month when Meta's Muse is free? Because the quality of work differs—form factor is copyable, utility is not.
- Spaces and Pages assume AI-native, AI-literate organizations, which are rare outside Silicon Valley; adoption may require heavy change management.
- Microsoft has a natural advantage with Copilot and enterprise trust, so OpenAI must prove Spaces is a new way to collaborate, not just a feature set.
- Dots only knows what you explicitly share from ChatGPT memories, which limits its proactivity but preserves privacy—a trade-off that may slow its usefulness.
- The $200 plan is getting a lower limit after October 29th, which may frustrate users despite the efficiency of GPT-6.1 Soul.
