## Thesis

The agent war is won not by form factor but by utility, and OpenAI's Dots is worth $100 a month only if the quality of work it does justifies the cost.

## Argument

The mechanism is utility. Every agent now has a computer, memory, and background work, but that doesn't mean they're equally useful. The difference is the model behind the agent and the tasks it's configured for. Dots, built on GPT-6.1 Astra, excels at work-related tasks because it leverages the context you've built in ChatGPT. It proactively fixes your calendar, resolves conflicts, and prepares materials for launches. This is where the value lies: in the quality of work, not the form factor.

OpenAI's strategy is to make context intelligent. They're betting that more of your work will stay in ChatGPT, and the more context the AI has, the better it can serve you. The new GPT-6.1 Soul model is a key part of this, offering near-Astra performance at a fifth of the cost, letting you do more without exhausting your plan limits. This efficiency is the unsung hero of Dev Day.

Spaces and Pages are the next step, moving from individual productivity to team collaboration. They're building a competitor to Microsoft Office, but they assume an AI-native, AI-literate organization that's rare outside Silicon Valley. The real question is whether they can convince enterprises to adopt this new way of working, especially when Microsoft has the trust advantage.

The bigger opportunity is in the ecosystem. Plugins and the Decisions API are opening up marketplaces where developers can build on ChatGPT's infrastructure. ## Counterpoints

- Why pay $100 a month when Meta's Muse is free? The answer is the quality of work, not the form factor.
- Spaces and Pages assume AI-native organizations, which are rare outside Silicon Valley. Will enterprises adopt them?
- Microsoft has a trust advantage with Copilot, but the 'no one got fired for choosing Microsoft' argument won't work here.
- The $200 plan is getting a lower limit for the same price, which is unpopular. But GPT-6.1 Soul's efficiency may compensate.
- Dots only knows what you choose to share from your ChatGPT context, which is a privacy choice but limits its proactive capabilities.
