## Headline takeaway

Own your AI memory and skills by building a personal agentic system with OpenBrain, ensuring you control intent and data while renting interchangeable intelligence.

## Prerequisites

### Claude or Codeex
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: AI coding assistants capable of building the stack via conversation.

### OpenBrain Framework
- **Kind**: tool
- **Note**: System for memory, skills, and orchestration.

### Recurring Pain Point
- **Kind**: knowledge
- **Note**: Identify a repetitive task or context you want to automate.

## Steps

### Identify your recurring pain point
- **Timestamp**: [06:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=411)
- **Action**: Pick a specific, repetitive situation in your life where you constantly have to explain context or preferences, such as travel planning or client follow-ups.
- **Command Or Clicks**: None
- **Choice Branch**: Choose a task that causes friction or requires repeated explanation.

### Use AI to build the stack
- **Timestamp**: [04:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=299)
- **Action**: Talk to your AI assistant (Claude or Codeex) to build 80% of the OpenBrain stack. You do not need to manually configure databases or SQL; the agent handles the technical setup.
- **Command Or Clicks**: None
- **Choice Branch**: Let the agent handle the technical middle; you provide the intent.

### Define memory and skills
- **Timestamp**: [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=825)
- **Action**: Explicitly define which memories and skills belong to you. Decide what the agent should remember and what constitutes your domain expertise that must not be owned by a platform.
- **Command Or Clicks**: None
- **Choice Branch**: Keep judgment and domain expertise personal.

### Set approval layers
- **Timestamp**: [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=603)
- **Action**: Configure the system so the agent drafts actions but requires your final approval before sending or executing, ensuring you maintain control over outcomes.
- **Command Or Clicks**: None
- **Choice Branch**: Use a ticketing or status system to track agent actions transparently.

### Iterate with feedback
- **Timestamp**: [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=877)
- **Action**: Run the agent on your chosen task and provide feedback. Correct it when it makes mistakes to break it in and refine its behavior.
- **Command Or Clicks**: None
- **Choice Branch**: Say 'No, that was wrong. Change this' to train the agent.

## Gotchas

### Agents can misinterpret intent and act without authority, as seen in the Nikita insurance story where an agent sent an email against user wishes.
- **Severity**: blocking
- **Timestamp**: [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=55)

### Relying on proprietary platforms means you do not own your memory or standards, making you vulnerable to regulation changes or service locks.
- **Severity**: serious
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=0)

### Do not depend on proprietary search or chat history to find agentic work; it is often hidden or erased, so use external scaffolding.
- **Severity**: serious
- **Timestamp**: [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAQOkG_v8c&t=603)
