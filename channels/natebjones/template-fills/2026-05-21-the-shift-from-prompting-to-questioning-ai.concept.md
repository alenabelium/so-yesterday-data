## TL;DR

Prompt engineering has become table stakes, rendering traditional task-based prompting obsolete for advanced agents. The video introduces the 'AI Question Method,' a mental model shift that treats AI as a senior partner rather than a junior assistant. This approach leverages strategic questioning to define intent, explore boundaries, and synthesize complex data inputs for high-leverage knowledge work.

## The argument

### The Senior Partner Mental Model Shift
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:03:41']
- **Claim**: AI capabilities have accelerated with models like 4.7 and 5.5, making it 100x more powerful than agents from six months ago. Users must stop treating AI as a junior partner requiring specific task definitions and start treating it as a senior partner capable of deep partnership thinking and high-leverage work.
- **Role**: definition

### Principle 1: Center of the Flashlight Intent
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:12:23']
- **Claim**: Questions must convey a clear perspective or thesis, acting as a 'center of the flashlight' with defined edges. Unlike open-ended requests, this method provides directionality and hard boundaries, ensuring the AI explores the problem space within specific constraints rather than drifting.
- **Role**: evidence

### Principle 2: Synthesis Over Evaluation
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:12:56']
- **Claim**: Instead of writing rigid evals for complex outcomes like PR FAQs, users should ask open-ended questions that invite the AI to synthesize across multiple difficult directions. This leverages the AI's ability to contend with nuanced outcomes and weave together disparate elements like customer experience and hardware integration.
- **Role**: evidence

### Principle 3: Wrestling with Data and Implicit Inputs
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:18:31']
- **Claim**: Effective questioning requires invoking both hard data artifacts and implicit opinions across the entire context window. By framing questions that challenge the AI to examine all provided files against a specific thesis, users prevent the AI from ignoring parts of the dataset or merely mirroring opinions.
- **Role**: evidence

### Synthesis: The End of Prompt Engineering
- **Anchor Timestamps**: ['00:23:49']
- **Claim**: The shift from prompting to questioning marks the end of the prompt engineering era as a differentiator. Intent is now best expressed through sharp, sequential questions that enable the AI to explore problem spaces and perform meaningful creative work, moving beyond the table-stakes skill of basic task definition.
- **Role**: synthesis

## Evidence and caveats

The speaker uses a marketing attribution example where a manager asks an AI to turn CSVs into a narrative deck, illustrating the senior partner dynamic. Another example involves a fictional Prime Video 3D soccer launch, where questions force the AI to synthesize customer experience and hardware integration. A third example details an MRR problem where the AI is asked to analyze voice-of-customer transcripts and PRDs against a broken product-led growth thesis. Caveats include that this method requires cutting-edge models (like 5.5 in CodeEx) and heavy knowledge work; it may not work for free accounts, those running out of tokens, or users of older models. The speaker also distinguishes this from 'agentic pipelines' used for predictable, buttoned-up workflows like customer service tickets.
