# You're Wasting Time Reading LinkedIn Posts Nobody Wrote.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the proliferation of AI-generated 'slop' on platforms like LinkedIn and in workplaces wastes human attention and degrades the quality of communication. It posits that authorship is not about avoiding AI tools but about using them to accelerate a deeply personal process of drafting and refining one's unique voice. By taking responsibility for the content we publish, individuals can reclaim their distinctive perspective and earn genuine human attention. The speaker introduces a 'voice discovery skill' to help users identify and articulate their specific communication style rather than conforming to generic AI patterns.

## Key Points

- AI-generated content creates a 'convergence problem' where models trend toward safe, polished, and identical outputs, resulting in 'slop' that wastes the reader's time and pollutes the internet environment [00:03:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWGoOtNgw3c&t=217).
- Human attention is the most valuable commodity in the digital age, and developing a distinctive authorship skill allows individuals to stand out and earn that attention in professional and personal contexts [00:04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWGoOtNgw3c&t=273).
- True authorship requires wrestling with the work through multiple drafts to ensure clarity and accountability, using AI as a tool to speed up iteration rather than a substitute for genuine thought [00:08:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWGoOtNgw3c&t=486).
- The speaker proposes a 'voice discovery skill' that analyzes rough notes and revisions to help users define their unique communication style, moving beyond generic anti-slop checklists [00:09:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWGoOtNgw3c&t=552).
- Professionals must insist on high standards of conciseness and coherence in workplace communications, ensuring that any AI-assisted output is thoroughly vetted and truly reflects the author's intent [00:12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWGoOtNgw3c&t=746).
