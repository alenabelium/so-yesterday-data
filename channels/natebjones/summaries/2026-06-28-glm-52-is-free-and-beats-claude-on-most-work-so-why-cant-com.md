# GLM 5.2 Is Free And Beats Claude On Most Work. So Why Can't Companies Switch?

## Executive Summary

While GLM 5.2 offers superior performance and near-free costs for common AI tasks compared to expensive frontier models like Claude, widespread corporate adoption is hindered by the complexity of the 'last mile' integration. Companies struggle to switch because replacing a model requires rebuilding the entire surrounding work system, including memory, tool calls, and context management, rather than just swapping an API key. Frontier providers like Anthropic are leveraging this difficulty by creating sticky, team-level harnesses such as Claude Tag, which lock in users by capturing valuable organizational context. Consequently, the real competitive advantage in AI lies not in raw intelligence, but in the scarce talent and infrastructure needed to build effective, model-agnostic harnesses.

## Key Points

- GLM 5.2 excels at 'center of distribution' tasks—routine, high-volume work like coding and copywriting—offering quality that often surpasses Claude at a fraction of the cost, yet companies hesitate to switch due to the massive effort required to refactor their existing AI infrastructure.
