# The end of the app era: What comes next?

## Executive Summary

NateBJones argues that the app era is ending as AI agents become the primary interface for software, forcing a shift from selling applications to selling data layers, workflows, and agent gateways. He illustrates this with examples from Meta, Salesforce, Microsoft, and DoorDash, showing how companies are repositioning to remain indispensable. The video emphasizes that individuals and buyers must understand the new three-layer stack—data, application, and agent—to navigate the changing software landscape and avoid being trapped by vendor lock-in.

## Key Points

- Agents are making traditional SaaS tools less indispensable, as users stop opening apps and instead rely on trained agents that hold their unique workflows and context. [00:00:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0j8wy4jUlsw&t=51)
- Salesforce is positioning itself as both a data layer and an agent gateway, with its own reasoning model Koa, to remain central in the agent era. [00:09:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0j8wy4jUlsw&t=587)
- DoorDash's text-based ordering and MCP integration show that losing the interface moment doesn't mean losing the customer transaction, as long as the agent can still place the order. [00:17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0j8wy4jUlsw&t=1049)
- The key to surviving the agent era is understanding your position across the data, application, and agent layers, and being able to articulate how you create value at each level. [00:23:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0j8wy4jUlsw&t=1438)
