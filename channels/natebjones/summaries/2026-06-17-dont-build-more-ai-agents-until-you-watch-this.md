# Don't build more AI agents until you watch this

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the primary challenge in AI agent development is not building capability but maintaining the 'harness'—the surrounding context, tools, and rules that guide the agent. Using Vercel's example of improving an agent by removing tools, the speaker emphasizes that agents degrade when their environment drifts or when the underlying model improves without corresponding updates to the harness. The core thesis is that successful agents require continuous pruning and maintenance to remain effective and safe, rather than just accumulating more features.

## Key Points

- Vercel improved its sales agent by deleting 80% of its tools, proving that simplification and pruning are more effective than adding complexity for reliability.
- Agents break in two directions: they fail when the external world (docs, processes) drifts, and they fail when the internal model improves, making old restrictions obsolete or new capabilities dangerous.
- Leading companies like OpenAI and Anthropic are winning by treating the agent's 'harness' as a living operating system that evolves alongside the model, rather than a static wrapper.
