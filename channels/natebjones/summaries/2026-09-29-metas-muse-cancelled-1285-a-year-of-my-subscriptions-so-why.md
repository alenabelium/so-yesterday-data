# Meta's Muse Cancelled $1,285 A Year Of My Subscriptions. So Why Did Amazon Block It?

## Executive Summary

Meta's AI assistant, Muse, has rapidly become a top iPhone app by autonomously managing subscriptions and saving users significant money, prompting Amazon to block it from the App Store. The analysis suggests Amazon's opposition stems from a fear of losing control over the customer purchase journey and its lucrative advertising revenue model. Meanwhile, competitors like Walmart and Shopify are partnering with Meta to leverage Muse for direct commerce opportunities. Ultimately, the video argues that Muse's success lies in its ability to provide tangible, personal utility through intuitive design rather than just raw AI intelligence.

## Key Points

- Muse successfully canceled over $1,285 in unwanted subscriptions and saved users hours of administrative work, demonstrating immediate consumer value. [00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbbPBr3OraQ&t=46)
- Amazon blocked Muse because it threatens to intercept the customer relationship and redirect advertising revenue away from Amazon's search-based model. [00:11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbbPBr3OraQ&t=719)
- Meta is building a new commerce ecosystem by partnering with retailers like Walmart and Shopify, aiming to capture transaction fees rather than relying solely on ads. [00:13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbbPBr3OraQ&t=833)
- The assistant's intuitive avatar design and focus on personal context allow it to solve mundane 'adult life' problems, making AI accessible to non-technical users. [00:19:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbbPBr3OraQ&t=1184)
