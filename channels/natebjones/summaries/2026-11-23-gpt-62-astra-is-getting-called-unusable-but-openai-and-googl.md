# GPT-6.2 Astra is Getting Called "Unusable," but OpenAI and Google aren't even Building the Same AI.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the AI industry has shifted from a single universal intelligence to a landscape of specialized models, each optimized for different tasks, making traditional evaluation methods obsolete. The host uses the contrasting reactions to OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra and Google's Gemini 4 Neon to illustrate this point, emphasizing that models now have distinct strengths and weaknesses across multiple dimensions. He suggests that users must analyze task structures, combine models for optimal results, and develop broader evaluation methods to keep up with the evolving AI ecosystem.

## Key Points

- OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra is criticized for being overly detailed and slow, but excels in tasks requiring meticulous attention to detail, such as complex mathematical and scientific problems. [00:02:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0WVOisJ6Js&t=141)
- Google's Gemini 4 Neon is praised for its positive, fast, and readable communication style, reflecting Google's strategy of releasing a fleet of specialized models to enhance user experience and cloud revenue. [00:07:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0WVOisJ6Js&t=433)
- The host suggests that users should analyze task structures, combine different models for different tasks, and set up 'blind spot radar' to evaluate models beyond their usual workflow. [00:18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y0WVOisJ6Js&t=1089)
