# Fable 5.2 Is really good and really expensive. So, when should you use it?

## Executive Summary

Fable 5.2 is a powerful but expensive AI model that excels at complex, high-stakes tasks, as demonstrated by the author's success in building an e-ink display after months of failed attempts with other models. The video argues that the model's value is task-dependent, and businesses must carefully identify high-return problems to justify its cost, leading to a K-shaped economy where some workers and companies benefit greatly while others see little improvement. The author emphasizes the shift from AI as an assistant to an intelligent partner, requiring humans to maintain critical judgment and focus on tasks where the model's capabilities provide outsized returns.

## Key Points

- Fable 5.2 successfully completed a complex e-ink display project that had stumped the author for over a month, by analyzing previous failed attempts and inferring the author's design intentions. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMtQh_3k4k&t=0)
- The model proactively managed the context window by creating a transfer file and suggesting a new branch for development, demonstrating a natural handoff between design and build phases. [00:04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMtQh_3k4k&t=256)
- Anthropic reports that Fable 5.2 performed 41% of freelance projects at least as well as a human professional, compared to 21% for Opus 3.5, highlighting a significant jump in capability. [00:06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMtQh_3k4k&t=398)
- The high cost of Fable 5.2 (e.g., $250 for one API dialogue) means it should only be used for tasks where a few hundred dollars can yield $10,000 in value, and companies must guide employees to use cheaper models for routine work. [00:09:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMtQh_3k4k&t=550)
- The video concludes that the key to leveraging Fable 5.2 is understanding your own critical skills and acting as a judge or client, while labs focus on recursive self-improvement, creating a K-shaped economy. [00:11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owMtQh_3k4k&t=687)
