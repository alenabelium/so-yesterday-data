# Your Resume Proves Nothing About Your Judgment. Here's What Does.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that in the age of AI, traditional resumes and polished portfolios no longer serve as reliable evidence of professional competence because AI lowers the barrier to producing high-quality artifacts. Instead, the author contends that true value lies in human judgment, which is best demonstrated through live, rigorous reasoning processes like whiteboarding sessions where ideas are challenged and refined. To prove their worth, professionals must shift focus from generating content to exposing their decision-making logic, risk assessment, and ability to learn under pressure. The recommended strategy involves documenting these dynamic thinking processes to create a 'talent board' that showcases comprehension over mere generation.

## Key Points

- AI has decoupled productivity from quality, making polished artifacts like resumes and portfolios less indicative of actual skill because they can be easily generated or enhanced by AI tools [00:01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UsCgEuIAclE&t=90).
- The author proposes 'whiteboarding' as the primary method to demonstrate judgment, requiring individuals to articulate the situation, decision, risk, and change in a live, pressured environment [00:04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UsCgEuIAclE&t=263).
- To prove competence, professionals should document their reasoning process—specifically what they rejected, the risks they identified, and how their understanding evolved through challenge—rather than just showcasing final outputs [00:07:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UsCgEuIAclE&t=476).
