# Reverse Engineer Anything (REA) Lets Your AI Open Up The Software On Your Computer.

## Executive Summary

REA (Reverse Engineer Anything) is a tool that lets AI agents analyze and explain how existing software works, from simple apps like authenticators to complex systems like QuickBooks. The video argues that REA is not the end of software development but a powerful learning accelerator that helps both developers and non-developers understand implementation details, engineering decisions, and hidden requirements. By studying real code, users can move from vague feature descriptions to precise technical specifications, improving their ability to direct AI agents and build better software.

## Key Points

- REA allows AI to open and analyze software on your computer, revealing how it's built, which helps close the gap between business requirements and actual implementation. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PseF3LyXQ04&t=0)
- For non-developers, REA serves as an X-ray machine to understand software behavior and engineering decisions, enabling them to ask better questions and specify requirements. [00:03:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PseF3LyXQ04&t=207)
- REA helps uncover hidden technical requirements, as shown in the Notion clipboard analysis, where understanding structured data and HTML formats reveals what copy-paste must preserve. [00:06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PseF3LyXQ04&t=408)
- The Windows calculator percent button example demonstrates how REA reveals context-dependent behavior that simple feature descriptions miss, emphasizing the need for precise requirements. [00:11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PseF3LyXQ04&t=674)
- A developer used REA to study QuickBooks Desktop and independently built a Python/SQLite accounting app, showing how research can inform new implementations without cloning. [00:14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PseF3LyXQ04&t=853)
