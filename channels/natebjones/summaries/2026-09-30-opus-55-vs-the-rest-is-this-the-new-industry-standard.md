# Opus 5.5 vs The Rest: Is this the new industry standard?

## Executive Summary

NateBJones reviews Anthropic's Opus 5.5, highlighting its efficiency and cost-effectiveness compared to previous models. He demonstrates a complex LEGO build that used only 1% of his weekly Claude usage, emphasizing the importance of measuring task-level efficiency rather than just token counts. The video also discusses Opus 5.5's improved writing controllability and its role in AI-assisted development, concluding that user feedback and task tracking are crucial for evaluating model value.

## Key Points

- Opus 5.5 completed a 514-piece LEGO model with 89 million tokens, costing only 1% of weekly Claude usage, versus $44 at API prices. [00:02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osZZjdMZVvA&t=159)
- Opus 5.5 pricing is 20% lower than Opus 5 and 60% lower than Fable 5.1, with typical workloads costing 40% less. [00:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osZZjdMZVvA&t=221)
- Opus 5.5 shows significant improvements in writing controllability and adherence to instructions, addressing community feedback about previous models. [00:09:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osZZjdMZVvA&t=544)
- For long-running tasks, defining clear stopping conditions and 'done' criteria is essential to avoid unnecessary token usage. [00:12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osZZjdMZVvA&t=741)
- Anthropic uses AI to develop AI, with Claude authoring over 80% of merged code, leading to faster model improvements. [00:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osZZjdMZVvA&t=863)
