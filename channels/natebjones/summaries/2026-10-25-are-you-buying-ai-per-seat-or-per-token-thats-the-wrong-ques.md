# Are You Buying AI Per Seat Or Per Token?  That's the Wrong Question. Ask This Instead.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the debate over AI pricing models—per seat, per token, or per outcome—misses the real question: how to turn intelligence into economic value. It introduces intelligence as a new economic resource alongside people and capital, and emphasizes that companies must develop the capability to apply AI productively. The speaker highlights the role of individuals as 'intelligence managers' who decide where and how to deploy AI, and suggests that partnerships and performance-based pricing may emerge as deeper collaborations. Ultimately, the video calls for a shift in mindset from cost-cutting to value creation, and encourages both employees and leaders to take ownership of AI's economic potential.

## Key Points

- Intelligence is a new economic resource, and the real question is how to turn it into value, not how to price it. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvnQZQAHUDc&t=0)
- Token pricing is useful for budgeting but doesn't guarantee value; companies must learn to apply intelligence to create profit. [00:03:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvnQZQAHUDc&t=239)
- Individuals can become 'intelligence managers' by deciding where to apply AI, creating value, and advancing their careers. [00:19:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvnQZQAHUDc&t=1154)
