# 5 Levers That Separate Winning AI Investments from Disasters

## Executive Summary

The video argues that successful AI investment depends on analyzing specific workflows rather than focusing on models or vendors. It identifies five strategic levers—automate, build, buy, hire, and wait—that leaders should apply based on workflow characteristics like repetition, complexity, and market maturity. A core principle emphasized is that organizations must clearly define their workflows in plain English before making any capital allocation decisions to avoid failure. The speaker provides an investment matrix to help determine whether to build, buy, or wait based on how specific the work is and the maturity of market solutions. Ultimately, the goal is to align AI strategy with tangible business value by treating AI as a tool for workflow optimization rather than a standalone technology purchase.

## Key Points

- AI investment is fundamentally a question about the shape of work, not the model; leaders must evaluate workflows based on inputs, outputs, exceptions, and accountability before considering technology. [00:04:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIkYVsxMpS8&t=252)
- There are five primary levers for AI investment: automate (for routine, high-volume tasks), build (for unique, company-specific workflows with clear success criteria), buy (for common tasks with mature market solutions), hire (to fill specific capability gaps), and wait (for immature market categories). [00:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIkYVsxMpS8&t=302)
- Executives must avoid the trap of automating workflows they cannot clearly describe; if a team cannot define inputs, outputs, and standards in plain English, the project is likely to fail due to unclear value and scope. [00:20:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIkYVsxMpS8&t=1223)
- An investment matrix helps decide strategy by plotting work specificity against market maturity: buy for common/mature work, prototype or wait for common/immature work, and build for specific work using market primitives. [00:21:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIkYVsxMpS8&t=1278)
