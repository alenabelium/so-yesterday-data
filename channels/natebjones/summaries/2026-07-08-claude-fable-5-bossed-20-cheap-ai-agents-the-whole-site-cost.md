# Claude Fable 5 Bossed 20 Cheap AI Agents. The Whole Site Cost $8.

## Executive Summary

The video demonstrates a multi-agent orchestration pattern that builds a high-quality, accessible website for $8 by leveraging a hierarchy of AI models rather than relying on a single expensive model. By assigning a high-cost model like Claude Opus 5 to supervisory roles and cheaper models to execution tasks, the system achieves significant cost savings while maintaining rigorous quality control through automated verification agents. This approach effectively mitigates AI hallucinations and errors by structurally enforcing checks at every stage of the workflow, allowing non-technical users to delegate complex projects with confidence.

## Key Points

- The system uses an organizational chart where expensive models like Claude Opus 5 act as bosses for design and review, while cheaper models handle coding tasks, reducing costs from ~$100 to $8. [00:05:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suY66oTDn0s&t=341)
- Automated verification agents catch four distinct types of failures, including hallucinations, cheating workers, and even bugs introduced by the supervising boss model, ensuring zero human intervention is needed for corrections. [00:07:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suY66oTDn0s&t=452)
- Success is driven by establishing a 'constitution' of standards upfront, such as a 14-point accessibility guide, which the system enforces on every build round without requiring detailed task-by-task prompting. [00:13:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suY66oTDn0s&t=790)
