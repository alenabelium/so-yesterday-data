# Anthropic Just Filed Its S-1. The AI Market Is Way Smaller Than You Think. (For Now)

## Executive Summary

Anthropic's S-1 filing reveals that while it counts over half of the Fortune 500 as customers, the average customer spends less than $1 for every $1,000 of total spending, indicating the AI market is still in its early stages. The video argues that this small current budget share represents a massive growth opportunity, as customers are just beginning to discover and scale AI use cases across their organizations. Key evidence includes high net revenue retention, rapid growth of non-technical products like Cowork, and the cumulative nature of AI investments that lower barriers to future adoption. The analysis concludes that the real question is not whether AI is a bubble, but how quickly enterprises will embed intelligence into their core operations, with Anthropic's investments in training engineers and deployment support signaling a bullish outlook.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's S-1 shows that even its top Fortune 500 customers spend less than 1% of their total budgets on AI, highlighting the market's early stage and potential for growth. [00:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAEixlPC6L4&t=366)
- The rapid growth of Anthropic's non-technical product Cowork, outpacing Claude Code, suggests AI adoption is expanding beyond engineering into other business departments. [00:09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAEixlPC6L4&t=561)
- Anthropic's $100 million investment in training 10,000 engineers through the Claude Frontier Academy is a strategic bet on accelerating customer deployment and unlocking further growth. [00:22:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAEixlPC6L4&t=1376)
