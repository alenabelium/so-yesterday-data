# Does Your Computer Belong To Codex? I Went To OpenAI To Ask.

## Executive Summary

This interview with OpenAI engineers Andrew Ash and Ashe reveals how AI agents are transitioning from passive chatbots to active computer users, fundamentally changing knowledge work. The speakers discuss the surprising efficacy of 'computer use' capabilities in navigating complex, non-API-driven tasks like tax filing and 3D modeling, which has reduced the immediate need for deep technical integrations. They emphasize that as AI handles more execution, human value shifts toward high-level ideation, decision-making, and cultivating 'taste' to curate AI outputs effectively. The conversation also highlights emerging interface trends, such as voice input paired with visual output, and the importance of proactive AI systems that can identify blind spots in user workflows.

## Key Points

- Computer use capabilities have reached a tipping point where they serve as a universal connector for tasks lacking APIs, allowing agents to navigate complex interfaces like government paperwork or tax forms autonomously. [00:07:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=421)
- The primary bottleneck in AI adoption is shifting from model capability to human skills, specifically the ability to articulate intent, validate outputs, and exercise 'taste' to avoid generating low-quality 'slop'. [00:19:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1193)
- OpenAI is exploring hybrid interfaces that combine voice for rapid context input with generative visuals for output, aiming to create a more fluid and less friction-heavy interaction model than traditional typing. [00:30:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=1823)
- Team-level productivity gains are realized when early adopters build reusable AI tools or automations that solve specific departmental problems, rather than just focusing on individual efficiency. [00:36:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TR8RDUzQaMo&t=2176)
