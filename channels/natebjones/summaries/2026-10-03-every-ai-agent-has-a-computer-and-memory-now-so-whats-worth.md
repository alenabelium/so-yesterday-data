# Every AI Agent Has A Computer And Memory Now. So What's Worth $100 A Month?

## Executive Summary

OpenAI's Dev Day introduces Dots, an AI agent with a computer, memory, and background work capabilities, available only with Pro plans starting at $100/month. The video argues that while agent form factors are converging, utility differs based on model quality and task-specific competencies. It explores the strategic implications of Spaces and Pages for collaborative work, the efficiency of the new GPT-6.1 Soul model, and the broader trend toward outcome-focused AI adoption. The creator shares personal usage insights, compares OpenAI's offerings with competitors like Meta's Muse and Anthropic's Claude, and advises on subscription value and future opportunities in agent-based workflows.

## Key Points

- OpenAI's Dots agent offers a computer, memory, and background work, but is only available with Pro plans starting at $100/month; the creator argues that the real value lies in the quality of work and context, not just the form factor. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnB4Zckx_34&t=0)
- The new GPT-6.1 Soul model is highly efficient, costing a fifth of Astra's token price, making it a cost-effective choice for heavy workloads and preserving plan limits. [00:04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnB4Zckx_34&t=256)
- OpenAI's Spaces and Pages aim to create collaborative AI-native workspaces, but adoption may be limited to AI-literate organizations, and Microsoft's Copilot remains a trusted alternative for enterprises. [00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnB4Zckx_34&t=975)
- The creator recommends focusing on work-related tasks for Dots to build context and value, and suggests that proactive intelligence can be sold as a service to companies lacking time to leverage AI. [00:11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnB4Zckx_34&t=709)
- Plugin extensions and the 'log in via ChatGPT' feature create a marketplace opportunity for developers to build tools that solve specific problems, leveraging users' existing subscriptions. [00:33:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnB4Zckx_34&t=1983)
