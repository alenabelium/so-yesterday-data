# OpenAI's 700 Math Papers Look Like Superintelligence. That Doesn't Mean Your Job Is Next.

## Executive Summary

OpenAI's release of over 700 mathematical papers, produced by an unreleased model in about three hours each, showcases narrow superintelligence in formal domains. However, this does not imply immediate transfer to engineering or business, where human judgment, context, and the ability to ask the right questions remain crucial. The video argues that AI's value lies in generation, not in the systems-level design and business intuition that still require human expertise, and concludes that this fragmented superintelligence gives us more freedom and peace of mind than feared.

## Key Points

- OpenAI's model solved open math problems in ~3 hours each, but this is narrow superintelligence, not general capability. [00:03:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTh4IJ-Mi3Y&t=229)
- Despite massive investment and verifiability, AI hasn't produced a superhuman engineer; engineers still work in zero-trust environments and handle complex system-level issues. [00:07:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTh4IJ-Mi3Y&t=423)
- Business intuition and asking the right questions are not IQ functions, and AI is not yet good at questioning assumptions—so humans still create the main value. [00:13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTh4IJ-Mi3Y&t=826)
