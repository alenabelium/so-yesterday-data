# Your AI Skills Are Trapped 

## Executive Summary

The video argues that current AI workflows suffer from 'procedural debt,' where agents lack portable, reusable instructions for how work should be done, not just what to do. To solve this, the author introduces Open Skills, a library of modular, agent-readable procedures that function as an operating layer for AI agents. By decoupling procedural logic from specific tools, users can maintain consistent standards, verify outputs, and transfer their workflows across different AI models and platforms. This approach shifts the focus from managing fragmented prompts to building a compounding library of reliable, composable skills.

## Key Points

- Procedural debt arises when agents know your context but not your specific work standards, leading to prompt bloat and the 'reexplanation tax' across different tools [00:02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9PUaEj0pMYE&t=125).
- Open Skills provides a portable library of modular procedures (skills) and compositions (runbooks) that define triggers, boundaries, and verification standards for agent tasks [00:05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9PUaEj0pMYE&t=320).
- The system emphasizes verification and scope, ensuring agents provide proof of work and allowing procedures to be scoped personally or project-locally to prevent drift [00:11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9PUaEj0pMYE&t=696).
- By combining Open Brain for context with Open Skills for procedure, users can achieve tool-agnostic workflows that compound in value and reduce manual setup [00:13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9PUaEj0pMYE&t=796).
