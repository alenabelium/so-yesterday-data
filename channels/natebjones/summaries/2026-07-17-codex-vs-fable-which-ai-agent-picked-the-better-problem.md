# Codex vs Fable: Which AI Agent Picked the Better Problem?

## Executive Summary

The video presents a comparative analysis of Codex and Fable AI agents when tasked with autonomously identifying and solving business problems. While Codex proved to be a reliable and efficient tool for executing bounded tasks, it tended to select safe, incremental improvements rather than high-leverage strategic issues. In contrast, Fable demonstrated superior strategic insight by identifying a critical 'pre-pipeline' problem, though it was more difficult to use. The creator ultimately releases a reusable 'skill' that allows users to harness AI agents to audit their workflows, identify pressing pain points, and build tailored automation solutions.

## Key Points

- The creator challenges AI agents to autonomously discover and solve business problems by auditing local files and Slack history, revealing that different models have distinct 'problem noses' [00:01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCWKXIyvM_8&t=94).
- Codex was praised for its speed and reliability in executing tasks but criticized for selecting safe, bounded problems like improving handoff packages instead of addressing core strategic pain points [00:04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCWKXIyvM_8&t=255).
- Fable identified a high-leverage strategic issue regarding content pre-pipelining, offering a more valuable solution despite being more cumbersome to interact with due to permission dialogs [00:05:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCWKXIyvM_8&t=333).
- A reusable 'skill' is released that enables users to deploy AI agents to audit their unique workflows, root-cause inefficiencies, and generate custom automation tools with built-in safeguards [00:06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCWKXIyvM_8&t=402).
- The creator recommends using both agents simultaneously to gain diverse perspectives, leveraging Fable's strategic insight and Codex's execution efficiency to build complete, secure solutions [00:09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCWKXIyvM_8&t=561).
