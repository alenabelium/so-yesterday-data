# Is AI Just Making You Faster At A Job You Never Wanted? 4 Steps To Find A Better Use For It.

## Executive Summary

NateBJones argues that using AI to speed up routine work you don't enjoy is a trap, and instead offers a four-step framework to find meaningful, skill-building projects with AI. He uses examples like Riley Brown and Swyx to show how following interesting people, trying small experiments, and engaging with communities can create a learning flywheel. The video emphasizes meta-skills like crafting specific prompts and pushing back on AI's generic suggestions, and concludes that AI should expand your choices, not just optimize your current path.

## Key Points

- AI often just makes you faster at work you didn't want; instead, find people whose work opens doors and get involved in their experiments. [00:00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVz7k4rlXU8&t=57)
- Use AI to brainstorm hobby-based projects that build transferable meta-skills like logical thinking, visual analysis, and crafting meaningful queries. [00:03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVz7k4rlXU8&t=234)
- Engage with communities by choosing something recently released, describing your problem clearly, and creating a small useful resource that others can return to. [00:11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVz7k4rlXU8&t=698)
- Evaluate your experiments not by project success but by what they teach you about your preferences and environment, and keep your current income while exploring new directions. [00:16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVz7k4rlXU8&t=975)
