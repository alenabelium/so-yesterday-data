# If OpenAI And Anthropic Are Discouraging You, You're Probably A Level 1 Builder.

## Executive Summary

The video introduces a five-level framework for AI builders to help them navigate the rapid pace of AI development without feeling discouraged by major lab announcements. It argues that success depends on moving beyond simple idea obsession to deep domain expertise, strategic go-to-market execution, and the ability to anticipate future AI capabilities within specific industries. By understanding their current level, builders can leverage AI as a competitive advantage rather than feeling overwhelmed by it.

## Key Points

- Level 1 builders are overly passionate about their specific idea but ignore market realities, while Level 2 builders succeed by listening to customers and adapting their product to fit real needs [00:01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lewY_0sJaWg&t=70).
- Level 3 builders master go-to-market strategies by using AI to supercharge distribution and storytelling, such as through automated outreach or content creation [00:04:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lewY_0sJaWg&t=242).
- Level 4 builders possess a unique, deeply held thesis about their problem space, allowing them to build venture-scale businesses by solving specific, high-value problems [00:06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lewY_0sJaWg&t=382).
- Level 5 builders anticipate future AI trends in their specific domain, enabling them to build generational businesses by being first to market with emerging capabilities [00:10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lewY_0sJaWg&t=616).
