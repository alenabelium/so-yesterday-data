# The Shift From Prompting to Questioning AI

## Executive Summary

The video argues that prompt engineering has become table stakes, necessitating a shift toward a 'questioning' methodology for interacting with advanced AI agents. By treating AI as a senior partner rather than a junior assistant, users can leverage complex agentic workflows more effectively. This approach involves framing interactions through strategic questions that define intent, explore boundaries, and synthesize diverse data inputs. Adopting this mental model allows for deeper collaboration and higher-leverage outcomes in heavy knowledge work.

## Key Points

- Prompt engineering is now table stakes, and users must shift their mental model to treat AI as a senior partner capable of complex, long-term reasoning rather than a junior assistant requiring rigid task definitions [00:01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogTLWGBc3cE&t=93).
- The first principle of the 'question method' is to provide a 'center of the flashlight' intent, giving the AI a clear thesis or perspective while defining the boundaries of exploration [00:08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogTLWGBc3cE&t=502).
- The second principle involves asking open-ended questions that invite the AI to synthesize across multiple complex directions and define what 'good' looks like for an outcome [00:12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogTLWGBc3cE&t=776).
- The third principle requires invoking both hard data inputs and soft, implicit opinions to ensure the AI engages with the entire breadth of the provided context rather than just a subset [00:16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogTLWGBc3cE&t=977).
