# Your Company Could Use 5x More AI For The Same Money. You Might Not Need The Best Model.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that businesses are reaching 'task saturation' where cheaper AI models are 'good enough' for most tasks, leading to flat AI spending even as token usage grows. This shift gives buyers leverage to negotiate lower prices and switch to cheaper models, potentially increasing token consumption fivefold without higher costs. The speaker suggests that the value in the AI economy is moving from model intelligence to the applications and value-added services built on top of AI, and that model developers will face pricing pressure despite continued technical improvements.

## Key Points

- Task saturation means cheaper models are often good enough, so buying more intelligence adds little value, and businesses are increasingly choosing the cheapest model that passes their tests. [00:00:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlHqTyW8rtA&t=53)
- Ramp data shows AI spending growth has been nearly flat for three consecutive months (August–October) even as token usage grows, indicating that businesses are getting more value per token without paying more. [00:04:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlHqTyW8rtA&t=257)
- Switching to cheaper models can allow a business to consume five times more tokens for the same cost, and this dynamic is shifting pricing power to buyers, who can negotiate discounts and demand better value. [00:05:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlHqTyW8rtA&t=353)
- The premium market still exists—advanced models account for nearly 70% of spending—but task saturation applies to an increasing number of business processes, and lower prices can create huge new markets (Jevons' paradox). [00:09:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlHqTyW8rtA&t=598)
- Business leaders should focus on investments that remain valuable as models improve, such as evaluation methods, system integration, and customer knowledge, rather than complex workarounds that may become obsolete. [00:16:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlHqTyW8rtA&t=983)
