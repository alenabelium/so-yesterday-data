# Salesforce Let Microsoft's Agents In. Oracle Is Building Into ChatGPT. Your Team Picks Who Wins.

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes how AI agents are reshaping competition among major B2B software companies like Salesforce, Microsoft, and Oracle. It argues that these companies are forming strategic partnerships to integrate each other's products while simultaneously competing to control the 'agent control plane'—the layer that manages and coordinates AI agents across enterprise workflows. The speaker emphasizes that the real battleground is not the models themselves but the relationship with the customer and the ability to mediate agent-driven work. Ultimately, the video advises viewers to be intentional about their agent management choices, prioritize interoperability, and recognize that their decisions will shape the future of work computing.

## Key Points

- AI agents are changing the competitive dynamics between traditionally complementary companies like Microsoft and Salesforce, as they all vie for control of the agent control plane. [00:01:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0&t=63)
- Salesforce and Microsoft's partnership, and Oracle's integration with ChatGPT, illustrate how companies are cooperating to reach customers while competing for the valuable position of mediating agent-driven work. [00:03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0&t=204)
- The agent control plane is the software that oversees agents, coordinates their work, and enforces policies, making it the modern equivalent of Windows in the 1990s. [00:10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0&t=640)
- Customers hold significant power in this battle, and teams should advocate for interoperability and flexibility in their agent management systems to avoid being locked into a single vendor. [00:22:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0&t=1358)
- The trillion-dollar question is who will manage the agent layer for enterprises, and the choices made now will shape the future of work computing. [00:26:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0&t=1586)
