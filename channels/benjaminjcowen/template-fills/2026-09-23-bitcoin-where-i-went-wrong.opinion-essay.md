## Thesis

My September 2026 prediction that a Fed rate hike would immediately reprice Bitcoin lower was wrong, and the data shows rate hikes have never been immediately bearish for Bitcoin.

## Argument

The mechanism is historical precedent. I built a macro chain — energy up, yields up, Fed hikes, dollar up — and concluded Bitcoin would drop. But when I checked the actual data, I found that after the first rate hike of a cycle (December 2015, March 2022) and after September rate hikes in midterm years (2018, 2022), Bitcoin either rose immediately or had already bottomed. In 2015, the bottom was already in; in 2022, Bitcoin went up initially. The current cycle is following the same pattern, and the golden cross plus a higher high confirm the shift. So my assumption that a rate hike is immediately bearish was not rooted in data. I now defer to market structure: the higher high shifts the burden of proof to the bears. Any Q4 weakness would likely be a higher low, contingent on a stock market correction — a 10% S&P drop wouldn't suffice. I also note the dollar's pattern resembles USDT dominance, and silver's higher high without a lower low supports the open-minded case. The lesson: don't be deterministic; respect the market's message.
