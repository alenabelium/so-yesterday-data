# Bitcoin: Where I Went Wrong

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen reflects on his incorrect prediction that Bitcoin would drop after the Fed raised rates in September 2026, acknowledging that while his macro chain (energy, yields, dollar) played out, Bitcoin rallied instead. He admits his assumption that rate hikes are immediately bearish for Bitcoin was not rooted in historical data, citing past rate-hike cycles where Bitcoin either rose or had already bottomed. He now defers to market structure, respects the higher high, and advises viewers to listen to those who called the move correctly. He remains open-minded about a potential higher low in Q4, contingent on a stock market correction.

## Key Points

- Cowen admits his thesis that a Fed rate hike would cause Bitcoin to reprice lower was wrong, as Bitcoin rallied despite the dollar strengthening and yields rising. [00:05:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iWBLplyqhl4&t=302)
- He notes that Bitcoin's 50% rally from its summer low is larger than in prior midterm years, and it set a higher high, signaling a structural change. [00:08:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iWBLplyqhl4&t=482)
- Historical data shows Bitcoin did not immediately drop after the first rate hike in a cycle (Dec 2015, Mar 2022) or after September rate hikes in midterm years, which undermines his bearish assumption. [00:16:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iWBLplyqhl4&t=1001)
- He now defers to market structure and advises viewers to follow analysts who predicted the breakout, while remaining open to a higher low if weakness appears later in Q4. [00:21:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iWBLplyqhl4&t=1314)
- Cowen emphasizes that the burden of proof has shifted to the bears, and a significant stock market correction would be needed to push Bitcoin to new lows. [00:29:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iWBLplyqhl4&t=1777)
