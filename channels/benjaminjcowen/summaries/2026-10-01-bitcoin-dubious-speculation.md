# Bitcoin: Dubious Speculation

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's precarious position around the $83K level, which he admits he did not expect to be reclaimed. He discusses the conflicting signals from soft inflation data, rising long-term yields, and historical midterm-year patterns, suggesting that Bitcoin's fate hinges on whether it can hold $83K as support. If it fails, a rapid unwind is likely, but holding could signal a different Q4 than typical midterm years. He emphasizes the importance of owning mistakes and remaining open to market outcomes.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin is at a critical juncture around $83K, and holding it as support could lead to a different Q4 than typical midterm years, while losing it could trigger a rapid unwind. [00:03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OuNjnmsqg4M&t=196)
- Soft PCE inflation data is being dismissed by the bond market due to methodological changes, leading to rising long-term yields and concerns about Fed policy. [00:08:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OuNjnmsqg4M&t=508)
- Historically, Bitcoin shows weakness when the 10-year yield tops and comes back down due to a growth scare, and the current setup mirrors 2018 and 2022 patterns. [00:17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OuNjnmsqg4M&t=1043)
