# Bitcoin: The Upside Down

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's surprising resilience against a backdrop of rising energy prices, yields, and a stronger dollar, which he calls 'the upside down' because the macro thesis suggests risk assets should struggle. He admits being wrong about Bitcoin not breaking above its May high and now defers to the market, noting that weekly closes above key levels validate the bull case. He discusses the Fed's slow response to rising yields, the potential for a Q4 pullback, and the importance of watching the weekly close for confirmation.

## Key Points

- Rising energy prices and geopolitical tensions are pushing the 10-year yield to 5.1%, and the Fed is not raising rates fast enough to control the long end, which is pressuring risk assets. [00:02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orda-VyV2rU&t=151)
- The Fed funds rate is only 4% while the two-year yield is at 4.9%, indicating monetary policy is still restrictive and inflation remains a larger concern than the labor market. [00:06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orda-VyV2rU&t=416)
- Bitcoin's breakout above the May high contradicts the macro headwinds, and Cowen admits his short-term credibility is damaged; he now watches weekly closes for validation, noting that a close back below 83K could set up a Q4 drop. [00:17:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orda-VyV2rU&t=1034)
