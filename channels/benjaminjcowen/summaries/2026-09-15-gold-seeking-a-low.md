# Gold: Seeking a Low

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes gold's current price action, arguing that the metal is seeking a seasonal low consistent with midterm election year patterns, likely forming a higher low between now and mid-October. He compares the current cycle to 1974, where gold bottomed in the summer, formed a higher low in September, and rallied to new highs by year-end. Cowen attributes gold's weakness to an anticipated dollar rally driven by potential Fed rate hikes, and suggests that gold is pricing in this future dollar strength. He maintains that unless gold fails to make new all-time highs by mid-2027, the secular bull market remains intact.

## Key Points

- Gold's current low aligns with the average midterm year seasonal low around July 6th, and the recent bounce off the bull market support band mirrors historical patterns. [00:00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=33Q5XZuyKUU&t=54)
- The 1974 comparison suggests a higher low could form in September-October, followed by a rally to new highs, though a lower low remains possible. [00:04:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=33Q5XZuyKUU&t=282)
- Gold's weakness is likely due to an anticipated dollar rally driven by potential Fed rate hikes, and the low may be in by mid-October. [00:11:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=33Q5XZuyKUU&t=686)
