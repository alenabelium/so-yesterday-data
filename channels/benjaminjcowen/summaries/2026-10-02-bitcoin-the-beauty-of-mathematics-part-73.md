# Bitcoin: The Beauty of Mathematics (Part 73)

## Executive Summary

In this video, Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's market position in 2026, noting that the total crypto market cap of $2.88 trillion remains well below the logarithmic fair value line. He attributes this undervaluation to human psychology during 'tween years' and compares it to the market's state in 2010. He discusses how monetary policy influences overvaluation cycles, suggesting that tighter policy tempered the last cycle's peak, while looser policy could lead to prolonged overvaluation next time. He concludes with a long-term projection of the asset class reaching around $10 trillion.

## Key Points

- The total crypto market cap is $2.88 trillion, still below the logarithmic fair value and regression lines, with the asset class as far below fair value as it was in late 2010. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMz4H9OK_fs&t=0)
- The undervaluation is partly due to human psychology in 'tween years,' though this interim year grew more actively than usual in Q3, possibly because it was more undervalued than typical. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMz4H9OK_fs&t=0)
- Monetary policy can temper expectations and influence the duration of overvaluation; tighter policy limited last cycle's overvaluation, while looser policy could allow a longer overvaluation period next cycle. [00:01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMz4H9OK_fs&t=91)
- The asset class is projected to reach around $10 trillion, give or take a few trillion. [00:01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vMz4H9OK_fs&t=91)
