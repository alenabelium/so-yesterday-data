# DXY to Move Higher?

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen predicts the US Dollar Index (DXY) will move higher, driven by an anticipated Federal Reserve rate hike by the end of the year, possibly as early as September. He identifies a pattern of higher lows in the dollar's recent movements and draws parallels to historical cycles, particularly the 1990s, where a rate hike followed a pause and cut cycle led to a significant dollar rally. Cowen also compares the dollar's trajectory to Bitcoin dominance (excluding stablecoins), suggesting a similar higher low and upward trend. He expects the dollar to return to its trend line around 104-105 by 2027-2028, and notes that gold's recent weakness may be a precursor to this dollar strength.

## Key Points

- Cowen maintains his bullish dollar view, citing a 60% probability of a Fed rate hike by September and expecting the dollar to rebound if the Fed follows through. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQHlGfz7A24&t=0)
- He draws parallels to Trump's first term, noting the dollar fell around day 597 and rose on day 608 in 2018, and expects a similar pattern now. [00:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQHlGfz7A24&t=145)
- Cowen compares the current cycle to the 1990s, where a rate hike after a pause and cut cycle caused the dollar to surge from 90-91 to 97 within a month, and expects a similar move now. [00:03:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQHlGfz7A24&t=212)
- He sees a higher low pattern in the dollar, similar to Bitcoin dominance when excluding stablecoins, and predicts the dollar will rise until the Fed cuts rates again. [00:04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQHlGfz7A24&t=283)
- Cowen expects the dollar to return to its trend line around 104-105 by 2027-2028, and notes gold's recent weakness may be anticipating this dollar rally. [00:06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQHlGfz7A24&t=365)
