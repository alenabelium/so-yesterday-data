# Bitcoin Breaks the May High (I Was Wrong)

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen admits he was wrong about Bitcoin failing to break the May high, as Bitcoin rallied above the 50-week moving average to nearly $86,000. He reflects on his strategy of buying in July below 0.3 risk, which only lasted five days, and regrets not taking more advantage of that opportunity. He acknowledges the difficulty of being wrong in financial markets and suggests viewers may prefer those who predicted the move correctly.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin broke the May high and rallied to nearly $86,000, proving Cowen's earlier prediction wrong. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RD1IMRMH6JU&t=1)
- Cowen's strategy was to start buying in July below 0.3 risk, but that window lasted only five days, and he regrets not taking more advantage. [00:01:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RD1IMRMH6JU&t=76)
- Cowen acknowledges that being wrong is part of navigating financial markets and suggests that those who were right may deserve more viewership. [00:02:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RD1IMRMH6JU&t=146)
