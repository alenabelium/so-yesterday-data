# When Will Interest Rates Drop?

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes when interest rates, specifically the 10-year Treasury yield, will top and begin to decline, predicting a local peak around the next FOMC meeting in late October and a drop starting mid-November. He compares current yield movements to 2018 and 2022 midterm years, noting historical patterns of tops in October/November. He argues that the recent yield spike is driven by market concerns that the Fed is behind the curve, and that peak fear could occur if a bad inflation report coincides with no rate hike. He suggests that after the midterms, rates will likely fall, with the two-year yield leading the decline.

## Key Points

- The 10-year yield has already surpassed the 5% target, and the top is likely to occur before mid-November, based on historical midterm year patterns. [00:01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVcHFIPVI40&t=74)
- The market's pricing of rate hikes has dropped from 64% to 17.7% for October, contributing to the yield spike as the bond market worries the Fed is behind. [00:03:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVcHFIPVI40&t=204)
- Rates are expected to start dropping shortly after the midterms, around mid-November, with the two-year yield leading the decline. [00:07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVcHFIPVI40&t=435)
