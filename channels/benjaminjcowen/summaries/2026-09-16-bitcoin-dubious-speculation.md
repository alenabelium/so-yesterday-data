# Bitcoin Dubious Speculation

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's recent golden cross and the likelihood of a correction, drawing parallels to historical patterns in 2014, 2015, 2019, and 2023. He notes that golden crosses typically lead to 10-15% corrections even in bull markets, and suggests a drop to the low-to-mid $70k range is plausible. He emphasizes that the key signal to watch is whether the subsequent bounce forms a lower high (bearish) or a higher high (bullish), which would clarify the market's direction. He concludes by framing this as 'dubious speculation' given the uncertainty of short-term price action.

## Key Points

- Golden crosses historically lead to 10-15% corrections even when the low is already in, as seen in 2019 and 2023. [00:01:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gc93viz0-7c&t=61)
- In bear market scenarios like 2014 and 2015, golden crosses marked tops and preceded 50-60% drops, but the current setup is more comparable to 2019/2023. [00:03:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gc93viz0-7c&t=194)
- The key signal is whether the bounce after a potential drop to $70-75k forms a lower high (bearish) or a higher high (bullish), with a higher high supporting the bull case. [00:06:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gc93viz0-7c&t=406)
