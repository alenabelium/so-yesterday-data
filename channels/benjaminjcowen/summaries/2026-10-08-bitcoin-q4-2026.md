# Bitcoin: Q4 2026

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's Q4 2026 outlook, arguing that despite a higher high, the market may still experience the typical midterm-year Q4 weakness. He compares current price action to 2018 and 2019, noting that Bitcoin's recent rally could be a fakeout to the upside, similar to the fakeout to the downside seen earlier. He ties this to the 10-year Treasury yield topping and gold bottoming, suggesting Bitcoin could still head back to range lows. He emphasizes the importance of staying open-minded and not being overly deterministic about market outcomes.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin is trading around $81-82k in Q4 2026, and historically Q4 of midterm years has been weak, with lows often forming in February, summer, and Q4. [00:01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6UlLbDTEo&t=106)
- The recent rally above the prior range could be a fakeout to the upside, and if Bitcoin accepts back below the range, it may head back to range lows, similar to the fakeout to the downside earlier. [00:05:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6UlLbDTEo&t=359)
- The 10-year Treasury yield typically tops in October-November of midterm years, and gold tends to bottom around that time, suggesting Bitcoin could still see Q4 weakness if yields drop. [00:08:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6UlLbDTEo&t=509)
- Bitcoin's post-rate-hike performance in 2026 is tracking March 2022, where it popped initially then faded, and prior midterm years show Bitcoin holding for 40-50 days before breaking down. [00:13:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6UlLbDTEo&t=820)
- The market could be trapping breakout traders to the upside, and a drop back to range lows could wreck many traders, but Q4 is not over until it's over, so staying open-minded is key. [00:18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6UlLbDTEo&t=1115)
