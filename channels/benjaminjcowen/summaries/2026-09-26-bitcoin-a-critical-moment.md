# Bitcoin: A Critical Moment

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes Bitcoin's upcoming weekly close relative to the May high (~$83K), arguing that a close below would signal a fakeout and likely Q4 weakness, while acceptance above would confirm the bull market. He draws parallels to silver's 2012 fakeout and emphasizes reacting to the close rather than predicting it. He acknowledges being wrong about the rally above the May high and adjusts his bearish outlook accordingly, noting that any Q4 weakness may not necessarily lead to a new low.

## Key Points

- The weekly close relative to the May high (~$83K) is critical: a close below would be bearish and suggest a fakeout, while a close above would confirm bull market acceptance. [00:01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvvMvW7AGE4&t=62)
- Bitcoin's current price action mirrors silver's 2012 fakeout, where a 6% move above the prior high was followed by a drop back below, leading to sustained weakness. [00:04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvvMvW7AGE4&t=283)
- If Bitcoin closes below the May high, expect a drop back to the range lows, similar to silver, and altcoins would likely be hit hardest since they are betting on an unconfirmed bull market. [00:11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvvMvW7AGE4&t=709)
- Historical data shows that after September rate hikes in midterm years, Bitcoin weakness often appears 50-90 days later, with the average low occurring 81 days after the hike, about 29% below the rate hike price. [00:17:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gvvMvW7AGE4&t=1060)
