# Silver: Dubious Speculation

## Executive Summary

Benjamin Cowen analyzes the recent decline in silver, attributing it to rising dollar and 10-year yields, and suggests the metal is in a digestion phase after a parabolic advance. He compares current price action to historical midterm cycles (2018, 2022) and the 1974 consolidation, expecting a potential low within the next few weeks to months. He notes that silver and Bitcoin have appreciated similarly over the last cycle, but silver's returns came later. Cowen believes silver will eventually resume its bull market, but only after a longer consolidation period.

## Key Points

- Silver's decline is driven by rising dollar and 10-year yields, with historical parallels in 2018 and 2022 where yields rising pressured silver. [00:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s4U8s754p9w&t=67)
- Silver is in a digestion phase after a parabolic advance, similar to 1974, and may need about a year of consolidation; it has already dropped over 50% from its high. [00:05:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s4U8s754p9w&t=348)
- Historically, silver bottoms between September and November in midterm years, and if it doesn't break below $60 within the next couple of months, bears may flip bullish. [00:12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s4U8s754p9w&t=744)
