# The acceleration is here! AI automated discoveries

## Executive Summary

The video highlights a significant acceleration in AI-driven scientific discovery, showcasing two recent Nature papers where AI agents autonomously generated novel medical treatments and hypotheses. It details Google's 'Co-scientist' system, which uses specialized agents to debate and refine ideas, successfully identifying new drug repurposing candidates for leukemia and liver fibrosis. Additionally, it examines 'Robin', a closed-loop multi-agent system that not only hypothesizes but also analyzes raw experimental data to uncover unexpected biological mechanisms for treating macular degeneration. These examples demonstrate that AI is moving beyond simple information synthesis to actively conducting rigorous, iterative scientific research with speed and accuracy surpassing human experts.

## Key Points

- Google's Co-scientist utilizes a multi-agent framework with supervisor, generation, reflection, and ranking agents to autonomously debate and validate hypotheses, leading to the discovery of effective drug repurposing candidates for acute myeloid leukemia and liver fibrosis [00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QvN6Tu6dHYM&t=123).
- The system identified Cur6, a drug with no prior link to cancer, as a highly effective treatment for leukemia stem cells by targeting cellular stress pathways, proving AI can generate genuinely novel medical insights [00:10:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QvN6Tu6dHYM&t=658).
- The 'Robin' system operates as a closed-loop agent that manages the entire scientific cycle, using parallel data analysis agents to interpret raw lab results and iteratively refine hypotheses for treating macular degeneration [00:23:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QvN6Tu6dHYM&t=1426).
- Robin demonstrated the ability to connect drug effects to unexpected genetic mechanisms (ABCA1 gene) and circadian rhythms, completing in two hours what would take a human scientist 400 hours, at a compute cost of just $10.76 [00:33:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QvN6Tu6dHYM&t=2034).
