# New Deepseek is here!

## Executive Summary

DeepSeek releases its latest open model, 4.1 Flash 1017, which excels in agentic tasks, creative projects, and deep research, often matching or surpassing leading closed models. The video demonstrates the model's capabilities through real-world tests, including creating an explainer video, a 3D game, music, and a virtual tour, while noting its limitations in complex 3D and medical image analysis. Additionally, DeepSeek open-sources its inference engine, Deep Infer, revealing optimization secrets that enable high efficiency and profitability, and supports Huawei Ascend chips as an alternative to Nvidia.

## Key Points

- DeepSeek 4.1 Flash 1017 is the best open model, performing near GPT-5.6 Soul and Claude Opus 5, with a 552B parameter MoE architecture and only 16B active parameters. [00:20:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Aqhg0FVm-w&t=1231)
- Real-world tests show impressive results: it created an explainer video, a 3D game (though not AAA quality), music, and a virtual tour, but failed at medical image analysis. [00:03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Aqhg0FVm-w&t=236)
- DeepSeek open-sourced its inference engine, Deep Infer, revealing optimization secrets like 96% cache hits and 80% gross margins, and supporting Huawei Ascend chips. [00:22:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Aqhg0FVm-w&t=1375)
- The model is extremely cost-effective, with all tests costing only $6, and it can be run locally with quantized versions as small as 233 GB. [00:19:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Aqhg0FVm-w&t=1181)
