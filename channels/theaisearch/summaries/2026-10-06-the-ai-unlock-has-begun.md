# The AI unlock has begun

## Executive Summary

The video discusses how recent AI models like GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 can reverse engineer binary code with over 99% success, enabling anyone to decompile games and software. This capability allows users to create mods, port games to different engines or platforms, and even mash up mechanics from different games. The video demonstrates tools like Universal Modder and Ghidra MCP, and explores the broader implications for the software industry, including potential legal and ethical concerns.
