# GPT 5.6 is a BEAST

## Executive Summary

The video provides a comprehensive review of OpenAI's GPT-5.6, highlighting its capabilities as a powerful agentic model designed for complex, long-horizon tasks. Through extensive testing, the presenter demonstrates the model's ability to autonomously code, reason, and execute multi-step workflows with minimal human intervention. While the model excels in coding benchmarks and cost-efficiency compared to competitors like Claude Opus, it shows significant weaknesses in visual perception and hallucination rates.

## Key Points

- GPT-5.6 demonstrated strong agentic coding abilities by autonomously creating a real-time voice-enabled anime avatar and a physics simulation from single prompts, though it struggled with generating professional-quality promotional videos and music. [00:03:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SettwwX2cCI&t=224)
- The model failed critical visual perception tests, including identifying tumors in medical scans and locating a hidden frog in an image, revealing a high hallucination rate of 89% on specific benchmarks. [00:14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SettwwX2cCI&t=897)
- Performance benchmarks show GPT-5.6 outperforming Claude Opus and Gemini in agentic coding and reasoning tasks while being significantly more cost-effective, despite lagging behind Claude Opus in some general intelligence leaderboards.
