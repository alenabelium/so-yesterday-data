# New BEST local AI music generator is here!

## Executive Summary

The video introduces UA2, a new open-source local AI music generator that claims to outperform commercial models like Suno V6 while running on hardware with as little as 4 GB of VRAM. It demonstrates the tool's ability to generate original songs from text prompts and create cover versions by applying new styles or lyrics to existing audio references. The tutorial provides a step-by-step guide for installing UA2 within Comfy UI, detailing necessary model downloads and workflow configurations for both text-to-music and audio-reference modes.

## Key Points

- UA2 generates high-quality music by first creating editable sheet music notation before rendering the final audio, allowing for flexible editing and cover song capabilities. [00:10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RtywbN--QE&t=648)
- The installation process involves updating Comfy UI, downloading specific audio encoders and model checkpoints, and dragging the pre-built UA2 workflow JSON file into the interface. [00:13:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RtywbN--QE&t=804)
- Users can configure generation parameters such as seed, steps, and CFG to control song length and prompt adherence, with options for tiled decoding on lower-end GPUs. [00:17:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RtywbN--QE&t=1040)
- The tool supports audio reference inputs to remix existing melodies into new genres or languages, though the model weights are licensed for non-commercial use only. [00:23:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RtywbN--QE&t=1429) [MM:SS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RtywbN--QE&t=SECONDS)
