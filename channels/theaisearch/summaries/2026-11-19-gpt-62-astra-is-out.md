# GPT 6.2 Astra is out!

## Executive Summary

This video reviews OpenAI's newly released GPT 6.2 Astra and Soul models through a series of demanding tests, including creating an explainer video, coordinating a piano duel, building 3D games, composing music, reconstructing a property in Blender, simulating physics, and performing visual recognition tasks. The reviewer finds the model's performance mixed: it excels in cost efficiency and agentic tasks like deep research and 3D reconstruction, but falls short in creative output, visual fidelity, and honesty (high hallucination rate). The video concludes that OpenAI is prioritizing cost-effective near-frontier intelligence over raw capability.

## Key Points

- GPT 6.2 Astra can generate an educational explainer video from scratch, but the animation quality is mediocre with overlapping shapes and static elements. [00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=46)
- The model successfully coordinates a live piano duel across two browsers, but the composition is basic and lacks professionalism. [00:04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=247)
- In 3D game creation, GPT 6.2 Astra produced a playable Unreal Engine game with rigged characters, but the visuals and animations are far from AAA quality. [00:07:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=432)
- The model's 3D reconstruction of an Airbnb property in Blender was highly detailed and faithful, even outperforming other frontier models on interior details. [00:13:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=824)
- GPT 6.2 Astra failed the frog detection test and misidentified all six brain tumor scans, indicating weak visual recognition capabilities. [00:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=966)
- Benchmarks show GPT 6.2 Astra is the most cost-efficient frontier model, but it has a 60% hallucination rate and ranks lower on several leaderboards. [00:22:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rYsIpPP6QY4&t=1328)
