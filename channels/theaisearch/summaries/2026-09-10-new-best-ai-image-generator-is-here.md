# New BEST AI image generator is here

## Executive Summary

The video provides a comprehensive evaluation of OpenAI's newly released GPT Image 2.5, positioning it as a leading frontier AI image generator alongside its predecessor GPT Image 2 and competitor Nano Banana 2. Through a series of rigorous tests involving complex prompts, the presenter highlights GPT Image 2.5's strengths in multi-turn editing consistency, transparent layer generation, and realistic text rendering, while noting persistent failures in highly specific factual accuracy tasks like biology and geography. The review concludes that while GPT Image 2.5 offers marginal but noticeable improvements over previous models, particularly in aesthetic quality and detail, it remains imperfect for precise logical or scientific applications.

## Key Points

- GPT Image 2.5 introduces a new sketch-to-photo feature and robust multi-turn editing capabilities that preserve consistency across iterative changes, allowing users to create transparent PSD layers and stop-motion animations effectively [00:02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9tpIc8PVKM&t=132).
- Comparative testing reveals GPT Image 2.5 excels in generating complex design elements like brand identity boards and fashion infographics with high detail, often outperforming GPT Image 2 and Nano Banana 2 in visual coherence and text accuracy within interfaces [00:09:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9tpIc8PVKM&t=550).
- Despite its advancements, the model fails critical tests involving precise factual knowledge, such as identifying endemic frog species, labeling biological diagrams correctly, and rendering accurate chess diagrams or geographic maps without errors [00:23:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9tpIc8PVKM&t=1436).
- The model is available in two variants: 'Flare' for faster generation with lower latency and 'Sunburst' for higher quality and consistency, accessible via ChatGPT free tier, paid plans, or API at varying costs based on resolution and quality settings [00:30:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9tpIc8PVKM&t=1814).
