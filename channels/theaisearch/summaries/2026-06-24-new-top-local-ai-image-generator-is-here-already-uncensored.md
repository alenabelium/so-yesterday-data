# New top local AI image generator is here! Already uncensored

## Executive Summary

The video introduces Crea 2, a new open-source local AI image generator praised for its speed, photorealistic aesthetics, and uncensored capabilities. The presenter provides a step-by-step tutorial on installing and configuring the model within Comfy UI, highlighting its low hardware requirements and efficient workflow. Key technical insights reveal that Crea 2's high quality stems from training exclusively on real-world images, excluding synthetic data to avoid bias. The video concludes by discussing the model's permissive commercial license and its potential for community fine-tuning via LoRAs.

## Key Points

- Crea 2 is highlighted as a fast, uncensored image generator that produces photorealistic results with natural imperfections, running efficiently on consumer GPUs with as little as 8 GB of VRAM [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1U8dJ94QFNc&t=0).
- The installation process involves updating Comfy UI, downloading the Crea 2 Turbo FP8 model, the Qwen VL text encoder, and the VAE, then configuring the workflow nodes for prompt input and sampling [00:02:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1U8dJ94QFNc&t=121).
- Technical analysis reveals that Crea 2 excludes AI-generated images from its pre-training data to prevent bias and maintain aesthetic quality, while using rich captioning pipelines to improve prompt adherence [00:15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1U8dJ94QFNc&t=920).
- The model supports commercial use for entities with under $1 million in annual revenue and allows for community fine-tuning via LoRAs, with specific custom nodes required to enable uncensored content generation [00:18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1U8dJ94QFNc&t=1089).
