# Claude Fable 5.2 is a freak

## Executive Summary

Claude Fable 5.2 is Anthropic's latest frontier model, positioned as the most intelligent AI available but also the most expensive and slowest. The video demonstrates its capabilities through extensive agentic coding tests, including generating animated videos, composing music, creating 3D models, and building games, while also highlighting its limitations such as high cost, usage caps, and intentional restrictions in certain research areas. The model excels in benchmarks for knowledge work and agentic tasks but struggles with specific challenges like finding hidden objects and has a higher tendency to hallucinate compared to competitors.
