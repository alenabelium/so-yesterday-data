# Gemini 4 Carbon, Alpha Genome 2, realtime voices, new TTS, ACE Music, Seedream 5.5: AI NEWS

## Executive Summary

This video covers a week of major AI releases, including Google's Gemini 4 Carbon and Hydrogen models, real-time voice models like Haru 4B and Sofon Real Time, and new image, video, and music generators. The host provides hands-on tests of Gemini 4 Carbon, finding it underwhelming compared to competitors like Claude Fable 5.2, despite strong multimodal performance. Other highlights include Alpha Genome 2 for DNA analysis, Nova Atom Light for molecular structure prediction, and open-source tools like Budget 3D and Splat Anything.

## Key Points

- Gemini 4 Carbon is Google's latest flagship, but the host's tests showed it lagging behind Claude Fable 5.2 in coding, animation, and physics tasks, though it excelled in multimodal tasks like the frog test. [00:13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woWo80dPkVk&t=795)
- New real-time voice models like Haru 4B and Sofon Real Time allow natural, interruptible conversations with emotion detection, and are available open-source under Apache 2.0. [00:20:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woWo80dPkVk&t=1222)
- Alpha Genome 2 from Google DeepMind predicts how genetic mutations affect gene expression in different cell types, trained on over 100 million cells, and is available for non-commercial research. [00:40:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woWo80dPkVk&t=2435)
