# Weight space programming, science agents, 3D generators, new Gemini Omni, new GPT Image: AI NEWS

## Executive Summary

This video covers the latest AI developments, including new models from Google, OpenAI, Nvidia, and others, with a focus on open-source releases and research breakthroughs. Key highlights include Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 video model, OpenAI's GPT Images 2.6 with GIF support, and Nvidia's Strata weather model. The video also discusses MIT's 'Weight Space Programming' research, which shows how a large model can improve smaller models by adjusting a small fraction of their weights, though the results are partly attributed to fixing existing issues. Additionally, it features new 3D generators, robotics toolkits, and several large open-source language models.
