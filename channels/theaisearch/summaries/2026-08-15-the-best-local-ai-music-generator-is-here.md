# The BEST local AI music generator is here!

## Executive Summary

The video introduces Miniax Music, an open-source, local AI music generator that allows users to create unlimited, high-quality songs offline without subscription restrictions. It demonstrates the tool's versatility across multiple genres and languages, highlighting its small model size which makes it accessible on consumer hardware. The presenter provides a step-by-step tutorial on installing Miniax Music via Comfy UI and explains how to structure prompts for optimal results. Additionally, the video covers complementary open-source tools like AEP 1.5 XL and Muse Scriptor for advanced music editing and MIDI extraction.

## Key Points

- Miniax Music supports diverse genres including pop, punk, R&B, EDM, jazz, metal, and country, with the smallest model weighing only 2.5 GB to fit on most consumer GPUs [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUcDyeZgz_k&t=0).
- Installation requires Comfy UI and downloading specific diffusion models, text encoders, and VAE files, with prompt structures divided into metadata, vocal details, and arrangements [00:10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUcDyeZgz_k&t=620).
- The tool addresses limitations of commercial platforms like Suno by offering unlimited, watermark-free generation, while Muse Scriptor is introduced for reverse-engineering songs into MIDI [00:18:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUcDyeZgz_k&t=1119).
