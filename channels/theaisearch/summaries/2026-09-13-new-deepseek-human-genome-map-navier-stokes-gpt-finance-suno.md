# New Deepseek, human genome map, Navier Stokes, GPT finance, Suno v6, YuE2: AI NEWS

## Executive Summary

This video provides a comprehensive overview of significant AI advancements from September 2026, highlighting breakthroughs in generative media, scientific research, and robotics. Key developments include Deepseek's release of the highly efficient and powerful Deepseek 4.1 Flash model, which sets new benchmarks for open-source performance. The episode also covers OpenAI's use of agent swarms to solve the Millennium Prize problem regarding Navier Stokes equations, marking a major milestone in AI-assisted mathematics. Additionally, it explores innovations in audio generation with Tencent's Out, music composition via YuE2, and advanced 3D reconstruction tools like Merryold V2 and World Sculpt.

## Key Points

- Deepseek releases Deepseek 4.1 Flash, a mixture-of-experts model that achieves state-of-the-art performance on coding and automation benchmarks while being significantly faster and cheaper than competitors like GPT-6 and Claude [17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZYJdwM-_nI&t=1029).
- OpenAI utilizes a swarm of 10,000 agents to solve the Navier Stokes existence and smoothness problem, proving that singularities can form under specific forced conditions, a feat previously unsolved by humans for 90 years [19:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZYJdwM-_nI&t=1184).
- Tencent introduces Out, a flexible speech editing AI capable of zero-shot voice cloning, micro-editing audio clips by adding or deleting words, and altering emotional tone without retraining [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZYJdwM-_nI&t=647).
- Google DeepMind releases Alpha Genome Atlas, a petabyte-scale database predicting the effects of over 9 billion human genetic mutations to accelerate biomedical research [02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZYJdwM-_nI&t=126).
- New open-source models like YuE2 for structured music composition and Merryold V2 for high-resolution 3D depth estimation demonstrate rapid progress in creative and spatial AI capabilities [27:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nZYJdwM-_nI&t=1676).
