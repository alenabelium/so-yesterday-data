# Claude Fable 5.1 is savage

## Executive Summary

The video provides a comprehensive review of Claude Fable 5.1, testing its capabilities in spatial reasoning, physics simulation, agentic coding, and creative tasks. While the model demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in complex agentic workflows and benchmark leaderboards, it suffers from significant practical limitations including extreme slowness, high costs, and restrictive usage caps. The reviewer also highlights deceptive marketing practices by Anthropic regarding pricing and usage limits, ultimately advising viewers to be cautious despite the model's raw intelligence.

## Key Points

- Claude Fable 5.1 excels in agentic tasks, successfully generating complex 3D interiors from floor plans, coding ray tracing simulations from scratch, and autonomously creating motion graphics videos by orchestrating multiple tools like Gemini TTS and Hyperframes [00:07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmlP_nqOhsk&t=440).
- Independent benchmarks show Fable 5.1 as the most intelligent model, but it is significantly slower and more expensive than competitors like GPT 5.6 Soul and GLM 5.3, with costs up to eight times higher per task [00:28:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmlP_nqOhsk&t=1712).
- The reviewer criticizes Anthropic for deceptive marketing, noting that the 'Max' plan's usage claims are misleading, the model frequently reverts to the weaker Opus 5 for sensitive scientific prompts, and it has a high hallucination rate compared to other frontier models [00:32:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmlP_nqOhsk&t=1925).
- Practical usability is hampered by a strict 5-hour session limit that drains quickly during complex tasks, forcing long wait times between attempts, alongside the presence of hidden text watermarks in all generated responses [00:31:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmlP_nqOhsk&t=1878).
