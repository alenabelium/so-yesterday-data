# New #1 open-source AI model is here!

## Executive Summary

The video introduces GLM 5.2, a new open-source AI model by ZAI that reportedly outperforms leading closed models like GPT and Gemini on various benchmarks. The presenter demonstrates its capabilities through complex agentic tasks, including building interactive 3D simulations, generating promo videos, and coding physics engines from scratch. Key features highlighted include a 1-million-token context window, an MIT license, and high performance in coding and deep research. The video emphasizes the strategic value of open-source AI for data sovereignty, privacy, and community-driven innovation over proprietary alternatives.
