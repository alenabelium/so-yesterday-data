# Claude Opus 5 is a freak

## Executive Summary

The video provides a comprehensive review of Claude Opus 5, highlighting its exceptional capabilities in agentic coding, 3D asset generation, and complex tool use while criticizing its prohibitive cost and extreme latency. Through rigorous testing, the presenter demonstrates that Opus 5 can autonomously build functional web applications, render 3D scenes, and compose music with minimal human intervention. However, the analysis concludes that despite its high performance in specific creative workflows, the model is significantly slower and more expensive than competitors like GPT 5.6 and Kimi K3, offering little practical advantage for general use cases.

## Key Points

- Claude Opus 5 demonstrates powerful agentic capabilities by autonomously building a functional Windows 11 browser replica, generating 3D scenes from reference images, and composing full songs in a DAW, though these tasks took over an hour each due to its slow processing speed [00:02:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCsBJz4W4bA&t=131).
- Independent benchmarks and the presenter's tests reveal that Opus 5 is not significantly better than GPT 5.6 or Kimi K3 in reasoning or coding, and it suffers from high hallucination rates in medical imaging tasks compared to other models [00:24:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCsBJz4W4bA&t=1443).
- The model is criticized for being extremely expensive and slow, with the presenter recommending it only for specialized front-end or 3D design workflows, while advising users to stick to cheaper, faster alternatives like GPT 5.6 for general productivity [00:31:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCsBJz4W4bA&t=1883).
