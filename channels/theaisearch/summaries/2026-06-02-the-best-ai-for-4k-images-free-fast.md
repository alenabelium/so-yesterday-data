# The BEST AI for 4K images. Free & fast

## Executive Summary

The video introduces Pixel Diffusion (PD), a new open-source upscaling model by Nvidia that generates sharp 4K images significantly faster than previous leaders like Seed VR2. The presenter demonstrates PD's superior detail retention and speed through various before-and-after comparisons of landscapes, portraits, and night skies. A comprehensive tutorial follows, showing how to install and run PD locally using Comfy UI with three distinct workflows: upscaling existing images, combining PD with Z Image for generation, and direct text-to-image creation. The guide emphasizes the lightweight nature of the models, allowing for free, unlimited, and offline use on personal hardware.

## Key Points

- Pixel Diffusion (PD) by Nvidia outperforms Seed VR2 in speed and detail, upscaling images to 4K in under 5 seconds while maintaining high fidelity and reducing artifacts [00:01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlvhlQOBHBw&t=68).
- The tutorial walks through installing necessary components in Comfy UI, including the Gemma 2 2B text encoder, specific PD diffusion models based on latent types, and the appropriate VAE for Z Image compatibility [00:07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlvhlQOBHBw&t=443).
- Three workflows are demonstrated: upscaling existing images to 4K, generating images with Z Image then upscaling via PD, and direct text-to-image generation, with the latter being less impressive than the upscaling capabilities [00:14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlvhlQOBHBw&t=869).
- The presenter highlights the efficiency of the BF16 and MXFP8 model variants, noting that the smaller MXFP8 version requires newer Nvidia 50-series GPUs or Blackwell architecture for optimal performance [00:09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlvhlQOBHBw&t=569).
