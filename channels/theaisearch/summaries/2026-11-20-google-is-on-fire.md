# Google is ON FIRE!

## Executive Summary

Google's Gemini 4 Neon, a mid-tier model, outperforms frontier models like Opus and GPT 6.2 across agentic coding, knowledge work, and science tasks. The video demonstrates its capabilities through complex demos, including a live piano duo, video reverse engineering, educational animations, music production, 3D game creation, and architectural reconstruction. While it excels in efficiency, speed, and low hallucination rates, it struggles with organic character creation and verbose responses. The model is available to Pro and Ultra subscribers, offering a cost-effective alternative to higher-tier models.

## Key Points

- Gemini 4 Neon successfully performed a live piano duo across two browsers, a task that GPT 6.2 Astra failed, showcasing its superior computer use and agentic capabilities. [00:03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3FE-hBh1rsY&t=183)
- The model excelled at reverse engineering video transitions and effects, and reconstructing an Airbnb property in Blender, completing the latter in 21 minutes compared to over an hour for other frontier models. [00:04:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3FE-hBh1rsY&t=287)
- Benchmarks show Gemini 4 Neon beats Opus 5.5 and GPT 6.2 Soul on agentic coding, knowledge work, and science tasks, while also having the lowest hallucination rate among leading models. [00:24:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3FE-hBh1rsY&t=1474)
