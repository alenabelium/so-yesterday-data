# New BEST AI video generator is here!

## Executive Summary

The video compares two state-of-the-art AI video generators, Seed Dance 2.5 and Miniax H3, evaluating their capabilities through a series of rigorous tests. Seed Dance 2.5 excels in high-action scenes, character consistency, and long-duration generation up to 30 seconds, though it is more expensive and currently limited to 720p resolution. Miniax H3 offers superior music video synchronization, higher 2K resolution output, lower costs, and open-source availability, making it a strong contender for specific creative workflows. Both models demonstrate impressive multimodal abilities but struggle with complex factual accuracy and specific graphic generation tasks.

## Key Points

- Seed Dance 2.5 outperforms Miniax H3 in high-action fight scenes and complex narrative following, generating flawless motion and character consistency over 30-second clips [00:01:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vpvne2istKg&t=82).
- Miniax H3 is the superior choice for music videos due to its precise beat synchronization and 2K resolution output, while also being significantly cheaper and available as an open-source model [00:09:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vpvne2istKg&t=584).
- Both models fail to accurately render specific factual diagrams like the Pythagorean theorem or detailed maps, indicating current limitations in precise geometric and geographic knowledge [00:14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vpvne2istKg&t=859).
- Seed Dance 2.5 costs approximately $5-$6 per 10-second clip and is limited to 720p, whereas Miniax H3 costs $1-$3 per clip and natively supports 2K resolution with API access [00:17:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vpvne2istKg&t=1062).
