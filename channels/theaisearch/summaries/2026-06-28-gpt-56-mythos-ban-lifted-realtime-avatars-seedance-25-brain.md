# GPT 5.6, Mythos ban lifted, realtime avatars, Seedance 2.5, brain ultrasound: AI NEWS

## Executive Summary

This video covers a week of significant AI advancements, highlighting OpenAI's release of GPT 5.6 and its partnership with Broadcom for custom AI chips, alongside IBM's breakthrough in sub-nanometer chip technology. It details new generative models from Baidu, including the Seedance 2.5 video model and the Seed 2.1 productivity agent, while also discussing the lifting of the US government ban on Anthropic's Claude Mythos model, albeit with restricted access. The episode emphasizes the growing divide between elite access to frontier models and the critical importance of open-source alternatives for long-term AI sovereignty.

- OpenAI unveils GPT 5.6 with 'Soul', 'Terra', and 'Luna' tiers and partners with Broadcom to create the 'Jalapeno' AI processor, while IBM demonstrates a revolutionary sub-1nm chip using 3D stacking technology [00:30:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7c_ieWfAbrw&t=1835).

- The US government lifts its ban on Anthropic's Claude Mythos, allowing access only to roughly 100 trusted partners, reinforcing the need for open-source AI to ensure broad accessibility and sovereignty [00:34:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7c_ieWfAbrw&t=2082).
