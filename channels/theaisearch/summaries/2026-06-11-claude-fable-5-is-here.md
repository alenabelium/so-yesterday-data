# Claude Fable 5 is here!

## Executive Summary

The video provides a comprehensive review of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5, positioning it as a state-of-the-art model that excels in complex agentic coding and creative generation tasks. While benchmarks show significant performance leaps over competitors like GPT 5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro, the reviewer highlights critical limitations including extreme costs, slow processing speeds, and restrictive safety guardrails that block biological and medical queries. Ultimately, the analysis suggests that despite its raw power, Fable 5 is often overkill for daily tasks due to its price and token inefficiency, making it a specialized tool rather than a general-purpose replacement.

## Key Points

- Claude Fable 5 demonstrates superior agentic coding capabilities, successfully generating complex ray tracing simulations, interactive 3D Earth models, and video games from single prompts with high accuracy [00:02:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUEE9OA4keo&t=179).
- The model faces significant practical limitations, including a $50 per million output token price point, slow inference speeds, and high hallucination rates compared to other leading models [00:23:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUEE9OA4keo&t=1416).
- Safety guardrails severely restrict the model's utility in sensitive domains, as it automatically switches to older models like Opus 4.8 when encountering biology, chemistry, or cybersecurity topics [00:19:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUEE9OA4keo&t=1159).
- Benchmark analysis reveals mixed results, with Fable 5 ranking first in some coding and intelligence indices but falling behind competitors in business simulation and common sense reasoning tests [00:24:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUEE9OA4keo&t=1470).
