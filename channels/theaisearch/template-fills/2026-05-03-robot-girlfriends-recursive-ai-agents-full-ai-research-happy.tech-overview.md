## Wider context

The industry is pivoting from static generation to autonomous, agentic workflows. Tools like Claude connectors and Moonlink show AI operating directly inside creative software, while recursive agents and ARRA redefine how models reason and document research. Multimodal models like Sense Nova U1 and Neotron unify understanding and generation, closing the gap between perception and action.
