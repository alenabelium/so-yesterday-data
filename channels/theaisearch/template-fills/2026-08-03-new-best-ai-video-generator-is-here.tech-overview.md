## Wider context

The AI video landscape is bifurcating: Seed Dance 2.5 pushes the envelope on temporal coherence and complex motion for professional workflows, while Miniax H3 democratizes access via open-source weights and lower costs. This split suggests a future where high-fidelity generation remains premium, but accessible, high-resolution tools become standard for creators.
