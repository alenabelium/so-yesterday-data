## Wider context

GPT-5.6 shifts the frontier from raw intelligence to agentic reliability, dominating long-horizon benchmarks while exposing critical weaknesses in visual perception and hallucination. The model's ability to autonomously install libraries and execute multi-step workflows marks a delta from previous generations, though its 89% hallucination rate remains a significant risk for high-stakes domains like medical imaging.
