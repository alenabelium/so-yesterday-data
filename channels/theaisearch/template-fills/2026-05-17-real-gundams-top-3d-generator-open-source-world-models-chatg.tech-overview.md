Open-source 3D generation hits new accuracy with Pixel 3D, while expressive TTS and interactive world models redefine real-time media creation.

## Tools covered

### Just Dub It
- **Vendor**: LTX-2.3
- **Category**: video
- **Timestamp**: [00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NnsQj2nDkKA&t=58)

## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the gap on proprietary capabilities, particularly in [[3d-generation]] and [[interactive-world-models]]. Tools like Pixel 3D and Sonnet WM demonstrate that local, open weights can now match or exceed closed systems in fidelity and interactivity. Meanwhile, [[text-to-speech]] and [[coding-agents]] are shifting from static outputs to dynamic, real-time collaborative interfaces.
