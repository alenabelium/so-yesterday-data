## Wider context

The open-source ecosystem is shifting from raw capability parity to specialized reliability and accessibility. GLM 5.2's dominance in low-hallucination metrics and the availability of compressed variants make frontier reasoning accessible on consumer hardware. Simultaneously, video generation is maturing from static frame synthesis to persistent, editable worlds via DreamX World and Perma Vid, addressing the critical bottleneck of temporal consistency.
