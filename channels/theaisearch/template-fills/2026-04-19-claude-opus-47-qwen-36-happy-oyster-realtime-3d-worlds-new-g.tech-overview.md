## Wider context

This week marks a shift from static generation to interactive, realtime 3D worlds, with Alibaba and Tencent both shipping open-source competitors to Genie 3. Meanwhile, the open-source ecosystem continues to close the gap on proprietary models, as seen with Qwen 3.6 and Motif Video. The rise of efficient models like Ternary Bonsai and on-device tools like Wild Debt 3D points to a future where AI runs locally, not just in the cloud.
