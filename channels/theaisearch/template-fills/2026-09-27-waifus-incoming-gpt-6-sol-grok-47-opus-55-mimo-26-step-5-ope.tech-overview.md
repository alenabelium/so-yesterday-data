## Wider context

The open-weight frontier is shifting from pure intelligence to cost-efficiency and specialized utility. Mimo 2.6 and CLM demonstrate that smaller, optimized models can outperform or match larger closed systems in specific domains like coding and speed. Meanwhile, GPT-6's tiered strategy makes high-end reasoning accessible, while Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS establishes a new standard for affordable, controllable audio synthesis.
