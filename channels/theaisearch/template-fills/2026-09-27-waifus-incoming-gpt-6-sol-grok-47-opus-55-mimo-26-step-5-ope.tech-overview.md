## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the gap on frontier intelligence, with Mimo 2.6 and Step 5 achieving parity or near-parity with closed models like GLM 5.3 at a fraction of the cost. Simultaneously, specialized efficiency gains are emerging in audio (Gemini TTS) and robotics (Flux Action), shifting the competitive advantage from raw parameter count to architectural innovation and cost-per-task optimization.
