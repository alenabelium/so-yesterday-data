## Wider context

The open-source ecosystem is rapidly closing the gap with frontier models, led by [[mimo-2.6]]'s intelligence leadership and [[ming-image-0.1]]'s design capabilities. Meanwhile, closed providers like OpenAI and Anthropic are differentiating through cost-tiered architectures (GPT-6 Soul/Luna) rather than pure performance leaps. Robotics is shifting from simulation to tangible hardware delivery, with companies like UBTE and Primebot shipping consumer-ready units.
