## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the gap with closed frontier models, particularly in intelligence (Mimo 2.6) and specialized tasks (CLM). Simultaneously, cost efficiency has become a primary differentiator, with GPT-6 Luna and Mimo offering significantly lower prices than legacy leaders like Opus. The industry is also diversifying into physical robotics and space infrastructure, signaling a shift from pure software benchmarks to tangible hardware integration.
