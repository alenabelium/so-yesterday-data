## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the gap on closed frontier models, with Mimo 2.6 and Step 5 achieving parity or beating previous leaders like GLM 5.3 at a fraction of the cost. Meanwhile, major labs are shifting strategies: OpenAI introduces tiered GPT-6 variants to target specific efficiency needs, while Anthropic pivots to scientific discovery agents. The hardware frontier is also expanding into space with Google's Suncatcher project, signaling a long-term infrastructure shift.
