## Wider context

The open-source frontier is rapidly closing the gap with closed systems, led by [[mimo-2-6-pro]]'s intelligence leadership and [[ming-image-0.1-design]]'s design dominance. Meanwhile, cost efficiency has become a primary differentiator, with [[gpt-6-luna]] and [[mimo-2-6-flash]] offering extreme value. The industry is also shifting toward specialized, ultra-efficient models like [[clm]] for speed and [[liite-1b-violto]] for math, moving away from monolithic generalists.
