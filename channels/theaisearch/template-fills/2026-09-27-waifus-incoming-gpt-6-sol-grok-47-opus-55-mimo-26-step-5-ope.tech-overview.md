## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the intelligence gap with frontier closed models, led by [[mimo-2-6-pro]]'s cost-performance dominance. Meanwhile, the market is fragmenting into specialized tiers: ultra-cheap inference (GPT-6 Luna), high-cost premium reasoning (Opus 5.5), and niche efficiency plays (CLM). Robotics is shifting from simulation demos to tangible hardware releases.
