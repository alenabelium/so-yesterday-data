## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the intelligence gap with closed frontier models, led by [[mimo-2-6]]'s dominance in both capability and price. Simultaneously, closed providers like OpenAI are fragmenting their offerings into cost-tiered variants (Soul/Luna), signaling a shift from raw intelligence wars to efficiency and accessibility battles. Robotics is also maturing from simulation demos to tangible hardware releases.
