## Wider context

The week marks a shift toward efficient, local-first AI infrastructure. Nvidia's Cosmos 3 and Neotron 3 Ultra, alongside Google's Gemma 4 12B, demonstrate that high-performance models can now run on consumer hardware with unified memory. Simultaneously, the rise of controllable, real-time tools like Magenta Realtime 2 and Ideogram 4's layout control suggests the gating constraint is moving from generation capability to precise user agency and workflow integration.
