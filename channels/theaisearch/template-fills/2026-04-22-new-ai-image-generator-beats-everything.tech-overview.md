## Wider context

This week's release cements OpenAI's lead in image generation, with GPT Image 2.0 setting a new standard for professional use cases. The gap with Google's Nano Banana Pro is stark, especially in text rendering and complex prompts, signaling a shift where AI can replace traditional design tools. However, both models still fail at domain-specific knowledge like biology or geography, indicating a need for integration with external knowledge sources.
