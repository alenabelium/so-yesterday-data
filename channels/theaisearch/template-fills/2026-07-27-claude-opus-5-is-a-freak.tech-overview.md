## Wider context

The frontier model race has shifted from raw capability to efficiency and cost. While Anthropic positions Opus 5 as a leader, independent benchmarks and the host's testing reveal that GPT 5.6 and Kimi K3 offer comparable or superior performance with better speed and pricing. This suggests the market is correcting the 'more is better' narrative, prioritizing practical utility and economic viability over marginal benchmark gains.
