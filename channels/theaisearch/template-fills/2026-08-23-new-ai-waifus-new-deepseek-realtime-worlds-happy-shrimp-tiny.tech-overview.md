## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the gap on closed-source leaders in both reasoning and media generation. Ornith 1.5's self-improving loop challenges the necessity of massive parameter counts, while Deepseek V4 Flash Vision matches Opus 4.8 on coding tasks. Simultaneously, robotics is shifting from static humanoid demos to dynamic athletic performance, with robots like Superman now physically outperforming human records in speed and height.
