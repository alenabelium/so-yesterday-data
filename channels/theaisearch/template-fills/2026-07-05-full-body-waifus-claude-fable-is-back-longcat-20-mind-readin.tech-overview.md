## Wider context

The week highlights a divergence in AI strategy: open-source labs like Muan are proving frontier-scale training is possible without Nvidia GPUs, while closed labs like Anthropic are retreating behind stricter safety and usage caps. Simultaneously, efficiency is becoming the primary battleground, with Google, Meta, and independent researchers releasing tools that drastically reduce compute costs and latency for image and video generation.
