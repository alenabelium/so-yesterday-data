Google unveils Gemini Omni for multimodal video, the agentic Gemini 3.5 Flash model, and proactive Search updates.

## Wider context

Google is pivoting from reactive search to proactive [[agentic-workflows]] across its entire stack. By integrating [[Gemini-3.5-Flash]] into [[Anti-Gravity-2.0]] and [[Gemini-Spark]], Google aims to automate complex tasks rather than just retrieve information. The introduction of [[Android-XR]] and specialized [[TPU-v8]] infrastructure signals a broader strategy to embed AI into physical hardware and optimize the underlying compute efficiency, challenging Nvidia's dominance in AI training and inference.
