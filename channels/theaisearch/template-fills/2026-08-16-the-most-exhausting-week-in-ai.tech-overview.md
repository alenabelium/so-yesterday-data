## Wider context

The open-source stack is rapidly closing the intelligence gap with frontier models, particularly in coding and cyber security. Models like GLM 5.3 and Qwen 3.8 Max demonstrate that post-training scale and data diversity can rival proprietary systems. Simultaneously, accessibility is shifting from pure performance to efficiency, with tiny models like Moshi LMG Gen and Minimax Music 3 proving high-quality generation fits on consumer hardware.
