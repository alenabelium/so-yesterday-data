## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and will eat Solana's lunch by pairing meme coins directly with tokenized stocks.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood already has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app on people's phones, and compliance done and dusted — something Solana simply cannot manufacture. And Robinhood's identity was forged in the meme stock era, so it never had to pretend speculation was respectable. Its chain is an Ethereum layer two where tokenized stocks and meme coins sit in the same pool, forcing two different trading cultures into the same room for the first time. When AMC's CEO publicly attacked Robinhood, someone deployed a meme coin paired against tokenized AMC stock and it hit a nine-figure valuation within hours. The mechanics produce strange outcomes: every purchase of the meme coin forces the pool to absorb more of the tokenized stock's floating supply, so the on-chain price of a real company's shares can drift wildly away from where it closed on a traditional exchange, especially over weekends when no one can arbitrage it back. Crypto spent a decade arguing it was building a separate financial system, but Robinhood's position is that it was never separate at all — and it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. The real competition is about which platform can best serve the demand for 24/7 permissionless speculation, and Robinhood has the distribution and a much easier route to making that mainstream.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last forever.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market whenever they want.
- Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
