## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and will eat Solana's lunch by pairing meme coins with tokenized stocks.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood already has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app on people's phones, and compliance done. Solana's edge was never speed or cost—it was being the place where the action was, capturing both attention and liquidity. But degens are loyal to wherever the action is thickest, and gravity can shift. Robinhood Chain pairs meme coins directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures into the same room. The AMC CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a token paired against tokenized AMC stock, reaching a nine-figure valuation in hours. The mechanics produce strange results: every meme coin purchase forces the pool to absorb more tokenized stock, so on-chain prices can drift wildly from the exchange close, especially over weekends. Robinhood's position is that crypto was never a separate system—it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. Solana's share of on-chain equity tokens collapsed from 97% to 30% in weeks. But Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks. The real competition is about which venue is honest about selling the ability to bet on anything at any hour. Tokenized shares aren't shares at all—they're debt-like instruments tracking price without ownership. The winning venue will be whichever one simply serves the infinite demand for a market with no closing bell and no gatekeeper.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market.
- Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, which lets users launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks.
