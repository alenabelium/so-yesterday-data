## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and will eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution plus a new kind of degeneracy. Robinhood never had to pretend speculation was respectable — its identity was forged in the meme stock era, with confetti animations and gamified trading. Now it runs its own Ethereum layer 2 where meme coins are paired directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures into the same room. The AMC CEO's public outrage became literal rocket fuel for a meme coin paired against tokenized AMC stock, hitting a nine-figure valuation within hours. The mechanics produce strange outcomes: every meme coin purchase forces the pool to absorb more tokenized stock, so on-chain prices can drift wildly from the exchange close, especially over weekends when no one arbitrages it back. This is crypto's real innovation — proof of infinite demand for a market with no closing bell and no gatekeeper. Robinhood saw that crypto had already proven the demand for 24/7 permissionless speculation and decided it could simply do it better, with 28.4 million funded accounts, compliance already done, and a culture that embraces the degen side of finance.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness, and is already answering with Stonks Fun.
- Neither side outright beats the other — Robinhood has distribution, Solana has culture and tooling.
