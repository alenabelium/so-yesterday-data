## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain, by pairing meme coins directly with tokenized stocks, is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and will eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app already on people's phones, and compliance already done. That's something Solana simply cannot manufacture. And Robinhood's identity was forged in the meme stock era, so it never had to pretend speculation was respectable. Its chain is an Ethereum layer two where tokenized stocks and meme coins sit in the same pool, forcing two different trading cultures into the same room for the first time. The AMC episode proved the point: a CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a token paired against the tokenized stock, reaching a nine-figure valuation within hours. The mechanics produce strange results—every purchase of the meme coin forces the pool to absorb more of the tokenized stock's floating supply, so the on-chain price of a real-listed company can drift wildly from its exchange close, especially over weekends when no one can arbitrage it. This is bigger than meme coins and tokenized stocks. Robinhood's position is that crypto was never a separate system, and it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. The real competition is about which platform can best serve the infinite demand for a market with no closing bell and no gatekeeper. Solana's dominance in on-chain equity tokens collapsed from 97% to 30% in weeks once these hybrid pools appeared. But Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks. ## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market anytime.
- Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, which lets users launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
