## Thesis

Robinhood is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy, and it wants to eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Solana won by being the venue where attention and liquidity concentrate, not by being fast or cheap alone. Robinhood attacks that with 28.4 million funded accounts, an app already on phones, and compliance done. It pairs meme coins directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures into one pool. The AMC episode shows the power: a CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a token. The mechanics even let a meme coin set a different price for a real company's shares over a weekend. Robinhood's position is that crypto was never a separate system, and it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. Solana's dominance in on-chain equity tokens collapsed from 97% to 30% in weeks. But Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, and Robinhood's activity partly comes from gas subsidies and existing crypto traders. The real competition is about which venue is honest about selling 24/7 permissionless betting on anything.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness, and is already answering with Stonks Fun.
