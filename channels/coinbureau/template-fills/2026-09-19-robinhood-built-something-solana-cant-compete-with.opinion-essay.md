## Thesis

Robinhood is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy, and it wants to eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app already on people's phones, and compliance already done. That's something Solana simply cannot manufacture. And Robinhood's identity was forged in the meme stock era—it never pretended speculation was respectable. Now, with Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum layer two, it pairs meme coins directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two different trading cultures into the same room for the first time. The AMC episode proved the point: a CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a token paired against his own stock. The mechanics produce strange results—every purchase of the meme coin forces the pool to absorb more of the tokenized stock's floating supply, so the on-chain price of a real company's shares can drift wildly from where it closed on a traditional exchange, especially over a weekend. This is bigger than meme coins and tokenized stocks. Robinhood's position is that crypto was never a separate system, and it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. The real competition is about which platform can best serve the demand for 24/7 permissionless speculation. Solana's share of on-chain equity tokens collapsed from 97% to 30% in weeks when these hybrid pools appeared. But Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks. The winning venue might be whichever one is simply honest about what it's selling: the ability to bet on anything at any hour of the day.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market.
- Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, which lets users launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks.
