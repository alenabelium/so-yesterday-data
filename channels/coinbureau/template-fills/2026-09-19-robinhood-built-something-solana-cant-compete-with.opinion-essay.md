## Thesis

Robinhood is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and wants to eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution plus a new kind of pairing. Solana won by being the venue where attention and liquidity concentrated, never pretending speculation was respectable. Robinhood attacks with 28.4 million funded accounts, an app already on phones, and compliance done. Its chain pairs meme coins directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures together. The AMC episode showed outrage becomes rocket fuel. The real competition is about who can best serve 24/7 permissionless speculation, and tokenized shares reveal the product is exposure and narrative, not ownership.

## Counterpoints

- Solana has culture and tooling that Robinhood can't copy.
- Solana is already responding with similar features.
- Tokenized shares are debt-like instruments, not real shares.
