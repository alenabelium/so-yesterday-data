## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy, and it wants to eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app already on people's phones, and compliance already done. That's something Solana simply cannot manufacture. And Robinhood's identity was forged in the meme stock era, so it never had to pretend speculation was something more respectable than speculation. The centerpiece is Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum layer two where tokenized stocks and meme coins sit in the same pool. Meme coins are paired directly against tokenized shares of real listed companies, forcing two completely different trading cultures into the same room for the first time ever. The AMC episode proved the point: when AMC's CEO publicly attacked Robinhood, someone deployed a meme coin paired against tokenized AMC stock, and it reached a nine-figure valuation within hours. The mechanics produce strange outcomes—every purchase of the meme coin forces the pool to absorb more of the tokenized stock's floating supply, so the on-chain price of a real company's shares can drift wildly away from where it closed on a traditional exchange, especially over weekends when there's nobody around to arbitrage it back. Crypto spent a decade arguing it was building a separate financial system; Robinhood's position is that it was never a separate system at all, and it's happy to combine the two in the craziest way possible. The real competition is about which platform can best serve the demand for 24/7 permissionless speculation. Degens were never loyal to Solana alone—they're loyal to wherever the action is thickest. ## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives, which don't last forever.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market whenever they want.
- Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
