## Thesis

Robinhood is coming for the entire on-chain speculative economy and wants to eat Solana's lunch.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Robinhood already has 28.4 million funded customer accounts, an app on phones, and compliance done. That's something Solana cannot manufacture. And Robinhood's identity was forged in the meme stock era — it never pretended speculation was respectable. Now it runs its own Ethereum layer 2 where meme coins are paired directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures into the same room. The AMC episode proved the point: a CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a token paired against tokenized AMC stock, reaching a nine-figure valuation in hours. The mechanics are wild — every meme coin purchase forces the pool to absorb more tokenized stock, so a real company's on-chain price can drift far from its exchange close, especially over weekends. This is crypto's real innovation: infinite demand for a market with no closing bell and no gatekeeper. Tokenized shares aren't shares at all — they're debt-like instruments tracking price without ownership. So what's being sold is exposure and a spicy narrative. Robinhood saw crypto had proven the demand and decided it could do it better. Solana's gravity can shift — it dominated on-chain equity tokens with 97% volume, then hybrid pools appeared and its share collapsed to 30% in weeks. But Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, letting anyone launch meme coins against tokenized stocks. The real competition is about which venue is honest about selling 24/7 permissionless speculation.

## Counterpoints

- Some of Robinhood Chain's activity is driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market anytime.
- Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, which lets users launch meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
