## Thesis

Robinhood's new blockchain, by pairing meme coins directly with tokenized stocks, is positioned to eat Solana's lunch in the on-chain speculative economy.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Solana's edge was never tech—it was being the default casino, where attention and liquidity coalesce. But Robinhood has 28.4 million funded accounts, an app already on phones, and compliance done. It can onboard retail degens without the friction of a new wallet or exchange. And its chain is an Ethereum L2 where tokenized stocks and meme coins sit in the same pool, forcing two trading cultures together. The AMC episode proved the point: a CEO's outrage became rocket fuel for a meme coin paired against tokenized AMC stock, hitting a nine-figure valuation in hours. The mechanics even distort prices—weekend trading can drift a tokenized stock's price away from the exchange close. Robinhood's bet is that crypto was never a separate system; it's just another venue for 24/7 permissionless speculation. Solana is already fighting back with Stonks Fun, but Robinhood has the mainstream route. The real competition is about who can best serve infinite demand for a market with no closing bell.

## Counterpoints

- Robinhood Chain's activity is partly driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of the volume comes from existing crypto traders using third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market.
- Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, launching meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
