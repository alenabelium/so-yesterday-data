## Thesis

Robinhood's tokenized-stock meme pools are attacking Solana's dominance of the on-chain speculative economy, and the real battle is over who can be the most honest venue for 24/7 betting.

## Argument

Solana's edge was never just speed or cost—it was being the default casino, the place where attention and liquidity concentrate. Robinhood, forged in the meme-stock era, brings something Solana can't manufacture: 28.4 million funded accounts and a compliance-ready app. Its chain pairs meme coins directly against tokenized stocks, forcing two trading cultures together and creating wild price dislocations. The AMC CEO's outrage became literal rocket fuel for a token. Solana's share of on-chain equity volume collapsed from 97% to ~30% in weeks. But Solana is fighting back with Stonks Fun, and Robinhood's activity is partly incentive-driven. The deeper point: crypto's real innovation is permissionless, 24/7 speculation, and the winner will be whoever is most honest about selling that.

## Counterpoints

- Robinhood Chain's activity is partly driven by temporary gas subsidies and incentives that won't last.
- Much of Robinhood Chain's volume comes from existing crypto traders on third-party platforms, not new Robinhood customers.
- Solana has the culture, tooling, launch machinery, and permissionlessness that lets anyone spin up a market anytime.
- Solana is already answering with Stonks Fun, launching meme coins paired against tokenized stocks and ETFs.
