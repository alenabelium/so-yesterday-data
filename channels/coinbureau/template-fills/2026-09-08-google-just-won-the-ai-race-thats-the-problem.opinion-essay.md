## Thesis

Google has already won the AI race by leveraging its distribution monopoly, not by out-innovating rivals, and this dominance is the real problem for the industry.

## Argument

The win wasn't a comeback; it was a foregone conclusion. Google's distribution—Android's 3 billion devices, Chrome's 69% browser share, and a search box handling 8.5 billion queries daily—gave Gemini a billion monthly users with zero ad spend. OpenAI's billion weekly users were earned the hard way, one recommendation at a time, but Google's are hardwired into daily habits. [00:01:29] [00:02:15]

The second asymmetry is compute. Anthropic, the safety-first lab, signed a cloud deal for up to 1 million TPUs, expanded to ~5 GW with $200 billion in commitments. Google designs, sells, leases, guarantees, and owns a slice of the building and the tenant—a landlord who also owns the brick factory and co-signs your mortgage. [00:03:49] [00:04:00] With TPUs 40-50% cheaper than Nvidia, Google pays itself and invoices the field. UBS estimates 27% of Google Cloud revenue comes from OpenAI and Anthropic combined. [00:04:16] [00:04:37]

Quality parity seals it. Gemini now leads on some reasoning tests, coding improved, and it's cheap to run. API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute. [00:06:57] [00:07:20] But the deeper issue is market shape. AI overviews cut traditional link clicks to 8%, and Cloudflare data shows Google delivers 87% of search referral traffic while ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity combined get 0.29%. [00:08:50] [00:09:35] Antitrust rulings changed nothing—Google kept Chrome, Android, and default search deals. [00:09:54]

The next phase is agents, and Google is building the rails: A2A protocol with 150+ orgs, AP2 payments with Mastercard and Stripe, UCP

## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly users, a harder bar than Google's monthly figure, so by engagement intensity OpenAI is still ahead. [00:02:15]
- Anthropic and OpenAI may have better models, and quality could eventually win—a reasonable position that's now falling apart as Gemini catches up. [00:06:40]
- Google depends on others for hardware; TPU production faces bottlenecks, and Anthropic spreads bets across Amazon, Nvidia, and AMD. [00:07:32]
