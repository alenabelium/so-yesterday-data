## Thesis

Google has already won the AI race by leveraging its unmatched distribution across Android, Chrome, Search, and Gmail to amass over a billion Gemini users without a single ad campaign, while its rivals are financially dependent on it for.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. OpenAI won users the hard way—one person at a time—but Google simply embedded Gemini into Android (3 billion active devices), Chrome (69% browser share), Gmail, Workspace, and the search box handling 8.5 billion queries daily. AI overviews alone reach 2.5 billion monthly users, and AI mode has passed a billion. Google ran zero ad campaigns to get there. Meanwhile, OpenAI and Anthropic are paying rent to the very company they aim to dethrone. Anthropic signed a cloud deal for up to 1 million TPUs, with commitments expanding to roughly $200 billion over five years. Google designs the chips, sells them, leases them, guarantees the lease, owns a slice of the building, and owns a piece of the tenant—a landlord who also owns the brick factory, co-signs your mortgage, and owns shares in your business. Google's TPUs are 40-50% cheaper than Nvidia's, and UBS estimates 27% of Google Cloud revenue comes from OpenAI and Anthropic combined. Alphabet's cash pile lets it self-fund infrastructure while rivals pile on off-balance-sheet commitments totaling $3 trillion across nine tech firms—what Ed Zitron calls a 'subprime data center crisis.' The quality gap is closing too: Gemini now leads on some reasoning tests, and its API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute in Q2 2026. But the real endgame is AI agents. ## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly active users, a harder bar than Google's monthly figure, so by engagement intensity OpenAI is still ahead.
- Google still depends on other companies for hardware; TPU production is bottlenecked, and Anthropic spreads bets across Amazon, Nvidia, and AMD.
- The antitrust ruling against Google in search led to limited remedies—it kept Chrome, Android, and default search deals—so the monopoly persists.
- Some argue OpenAI and Anthropic have better models and quality will eventually win, though Gemini is no longer significantly behind.
