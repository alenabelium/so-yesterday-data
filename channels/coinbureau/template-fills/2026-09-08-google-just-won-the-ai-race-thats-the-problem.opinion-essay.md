## Thesis

Google has already won the AI race by leveraging its unmatched distribution, and that dominance is the real problem for the industry.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. While OpenAI won users one at a time, Google hardwired Gemini into Android, Chrome, Gmail, and Search, amassing over a billion monthly users without a single ad campaign. Meanwhile, rivals like Anthropic and OpenAI are financially dependent on Google for compute, signing contracts worth hundreds of billions to rent TPUs. Google designs the chips, sells them, leases them, guarantees the lease, owns a slice of the building, and owns a piece of the tenant. It's a landlord who also owns the brick factory, co-signs your mortgage, and owns shares in your business. You can succeed in that arrangement, but only in a way that pays him.

Quality is no longer a counterweight. Gemini has caught up on reasoning and coding, and it's cheaper to run. Developers have shown their preference with volume: Gemini API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute in Q2 2026. But the deeper issue is market shape. Google controls the phone OS, browser, email, office suite, ad market, search index, cloud, chips, and now the AI model. AI overviews are killing publisher clicks—users click a traditional link just 8% of the time when an AI overview appears. And the alternative doesn't exist: Google still delivers 87% of search-driven referral traffic, while ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity combined account for 0.29%.

The next phase is AI agents, and the question is whose infrastructure they run on, whose identity system vouches for them, and whose infrastructure settles the payment. Google launched A2A, AP2, and UCP, covering communication, consent, and commerce.

## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly users, a harder bar than Google's monthly figure; by engagement intensity, OpenAI is still ahead.
- Google still depends on other companies for hardware; TPU production is held back by manufacturing bottlenecks, and it claims it doesn't have enough compute.
- Anthropic is spreading its bets across Amazon, Nvidia, AMD, and others rather than relying too heavily on Google.
- Google was ruled an illegal monopolist in search, but the remedy was limited—it kept Chrome and Android and still pays to be default search engine.
