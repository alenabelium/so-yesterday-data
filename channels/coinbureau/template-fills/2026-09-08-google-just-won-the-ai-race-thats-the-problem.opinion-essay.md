## Thesis

Google has already won the AI race by leveraging its unmatched distribution across Android, Chrome, Search, and Gmail to amass over a billion Gemini users without a single ad campaign, and its rivals are now financially dependent on the.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. OpenAI won users the hard way—one at a time, through recommendations—but Google simply embedded Gemini into Android (3 billion devices), Chrome (69% browser share), Gmail, Workspace, and the search box handling 8.5 billion queries daily. AI overviews now reach 2.5 billion monthly users, and AI mode alone has passed a billion. Google ran zero ad campaigns to get there. [00:01:29]

The second asymmetry is compute. Anthropic signed a cloud deal for up to 1 million TPUs, expanded to ~5 GW by April 2026 with ~$200 billion in commitments. Google designs the chips, sells them, leases them, guarantees the lease, owns a slice of the building, and owns a piece of the tenant. Its TPUs are 40-50% cheaper than Nvidia, and Google pays itself then invoices the field. UBS estimates 27% of Google Cloud revenue comes from OpenAI and Anthropic combined. [00:04:30]

The third is financial. Alphabet generates ~$120 billion quarterly revenue and can self-fund infrastructure, while rivals pile on off-balance-sheet commitments—$3 trillion across nine tech firms, triple their combined leases. Ed Zitron calls it a 'subprime data center crisis.' [00:05:41]

Finally, the quality gap has closed. Gemini now leads on some reasoning tests, coding improved sharply, and it's extremely cheap to run. Gemini API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute in Q2 2026. Distribution decides the outcome: Gemini just needs to be good enough. [00:07:20]

## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly users, a harder bar than Google's monthly figure, so by engagement intensity OpenAI is still ahead. [00:02:15]
- Google still depends on others for hardware; TPU production faces bottlenecks, and Google claims it doesn't have enough compute. [00:07:32]
- Anthropic is diversifying across Amazon, Nvidia, AMD, and others, not relying too heavily on Google. [00:07:48]
- Some argue Anthropic and OpenAI have better models and quality will eventually win—a position that's been falling apart as Gemini catches up. [00:06:40]
