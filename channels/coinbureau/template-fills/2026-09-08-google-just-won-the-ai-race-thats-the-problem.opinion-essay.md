## Thesis

Google has already won the AI race by leveraging its unmatched distribution across Android, Chrome, Search, and Gmail, and its rivals are now financially dependent on the very company they aim to dethrone.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. OpenAI won users one at a time, but Google hardwired Gemini into Android's 3 billion devices, Chrome's 69% browser share, and a search box handling 8.5 billion queries daily, reaching a billion monthly users without a single ad campaign. Meanwhile, rivals pay rent to Google: Anthropic signed a cloud deal for up to 1 million TPUs, expanded to ~5 GW with ~$200 billion in commitments, while Google designs, sells, leases, guarantees, and owns a slice of the infrastructure and the tenant. Google's TPUs are 40-50% cheaper than Nvidia's, and UBS estimates 27% of Google Cloud revenue comes from OpenAI and Anthropic combined. Quality gaps are closing—Gemini leads on some reasoning tests and API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute—so distribution decides the outcome. Google also controls the agent stack: A2A protocol, AP2 payments with Mastercard and Stripe, and UCP commerce with Shopify, while OpenAI's rival protocol was retired. The result is a market where Google's AI overviews cut publisher clicks to 8%, and no alternative search index or Android-scale OS exists.

## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly users vs Google's monthly, so by engagement intensity OpenAI is still ahead.
- Google depends on others for hardware; TPU production faces bottlenecks and Google claims it still lacks compute.
- Anthropic spreads bets across Amazon, Nvidia, AMD, and others, not just Google.
- The 'better model wins' argument held for a while, but Gemini is no longer significantly behind.
