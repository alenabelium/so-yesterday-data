## Thesis

Google won the AI race not by out-innovating but by leveraging its distribution and infrastructure, making it the inevitable winner of the next phase of AI agents.

## Argument

The mechanism is distribution. Google didn't win by having the best model; it won by embedding Gemini into Android, Chrome, Gmail, and Search, amassing over a billion monthly users without a single ad campaign. Meanwhile, rivals like OpenAI and Anthropic are financially dependent on Google for compute, paying billions to rent TPUs from the very company they aim to dethrone. Google designs the chips, sells them, leases them, guarantees the lease, owns a slice of the building, and owns a piece of the tenant. This vertical integration gives Google a structural advantage that model quality alone can't overcome.

Gemini's quality has caught up, and its cost advantage is widening. Gemini API traffic hit 22 billion tokens per minute in Q2 2026, up from 16 billion the prior quarter. Google's AI overviews are reshaping web traffic, reducing publisher clicks, and its dominance in search, browsers, and operating systems remains largely unchallenged despite antitrust rulings. The company now controls the phone OS, browser, email, office suite, ad market, search index, cloud, chips, and the AI model on top of it all.

The next phase is AI agents, and Google is positioning itself across the entire stack: A2A for agent communication, AP2 for payments, and UCP for commerce. OpenAI and Stripe's own protocol was quickly retired. Google's infrastructure, identity, and payment systems will be the rails on which agents run, making it the inevitable winner of the AI race.

## Counterpoints

- OpenAI's billion is weekly active users, a harder bar than Google's monthly figure, so by engagement intensity OpenAI is still ahead.
- Google still depends on other companies for hardware, with TPU production held back by manufacturing bottlenecks, and it claims it still doesn't have enough compute.
- Anthropic is spreading its bets across Amazon, Nvidia, AMD, and others rather than relying too heavily on Google.
- Some argue that Anthropic and OpenAI have the better model and that quality will eventually win, though this position is falling apart as Gemini catches up.
