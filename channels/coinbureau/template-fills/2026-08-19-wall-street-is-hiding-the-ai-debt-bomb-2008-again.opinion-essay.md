## Thesis

Wall Street is not just investing in AI—it is lending against it, and the debt structure, with collateral that decays faster than the paper, is a 2006-style setup where losses will hit the weakest link: publicly listed Bitcoin miners.

## Argument

The mechanism is credit. Morgan Stanley puts global data center capex at $2.9T for 2025-2028, but hyperscaler cash flow covers only $1.4T, leaving a $1.5T financing gap. That gap is being filled by private credit and securitization, not balance-sheet debt. Direct private credit to AI has exploded to $200B, and global AI debt issuance is tracking toward $570B in 2026. The trick is keeping this debt off balance sheets—Meta's $30B Hyperion Campus is financed through a bankruptcy-remote vehicle, Big Net Investors LLC. The Nikkei investigation found $1.65T in off-balance-sheet AI obligations, growing eightfold in four years. This is the same tranche-and-stamp trick from 2006. The collateral is the problem: GPUs depreciate 50% in three years, but the debt matures in 5, 10, or even 24 years. Ratings track the counterparty, not the asset—CoreWeave's facility rated A3 when anchored by Meta, but Ba2 when tied to non-investment-grade customers. Nvidia's $500B platform, with Larry Fink comparing it to the creation of mortgage-backed securities, is the final confirmation. The chain relocates exposure: from hyperscalers to bankruptcy-remote vehicles, into rated ABS sold to pension funds, and down to miners who signed 12- to 20-year leases against 3-year hardware. Miners are now AI landlords because they were mining at a loss—cash costs of $75k-$88k against a Bitcoin price below that. They've signed $70B in AI contracts, selling 15,000 coins to fund the conversion. If GPU values collapse before the debt is repriced, the losses hit these weakest links—and they're publicly traded.

## Counterpoints

- Hyperscalers are enormously cash generative at the parent level, so they can absorb shocks.
- The optimists argue this is a new era for markets, not a bubble.
- Nobody has actually seen GPU residual values through a full technology cycle, so the optimists rely on things playing out exactly as planned.
