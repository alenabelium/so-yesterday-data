## Thesis

Wall Street is lending against AI through opaque, off-balance-sheet debt structures that echo the lead-up to the 2008 crisis, and the weakest link in this chain is publicly listed Bitcoin miners.

## Argument

The mechanism is credit. Morgan Stanley puts global data center capex at $2.9 trillion for 2025-2028, but hyperscaler cash flow covers only $1.4 trillion, leaving a $1.5 trillion financing gap. That gap is being filled by private credit, securitized products, and off-balance-sheet vehicles. Meta's Hyperion Campus is financed through a bankruptcy-remote vehicle, Big Net Investors LLC, with $27 billion of debt rated A+ and maturing in 2049, while Meta leases the site back on four-year terms. Oracle carries $248 billion in lease commitments through third-party vehicles. The Nikkei investigation found $1.65 trillion in off-balance-sheet AI obligations, growing eightfold in four years. This is the same trick as 2006: pool weaker credit, slice into tranches, get a rating agency stamp. The collateral problem is that GPUs depreciate 50% in three years, but the debt matures in 5 to 24 years. Ratings track the counterparty, not the asset: CoreWeave's A3-rated facility is anchored by Meta, but a Ba2 facility is tied to non-investment-grade customers. Nvidia's platform to mobilize $500 billion, with Larry Fink comparing it to mortgage-backed securities, only works if chips hold their value. Bitcoin miners, converting to AI landlords to survive mining at a loss, are the most leveraged: Core Scientific signed a 12-year deal with CoreWeave, TeraWulf a 20-year lease with Anthropic, and Hut 8 a 15-year lease. They are taking on 12-20 year obligations against hardware with a three-year value half-life, funded partly by selling Bitcoin. The stress is already showing: CoreWeave's CDS hit 855

## Counterpoints

- Hyperscalers are enormously cash generative at the parent level, so the debt is backed by strong cash flows.
- The debt is investment-grade and secured against high-performance computing, as seen with CoreWeave's A3 rating.
- Optimists rely on GPU residual values holding through a full technology cycle, which hasn't been tested yet.
