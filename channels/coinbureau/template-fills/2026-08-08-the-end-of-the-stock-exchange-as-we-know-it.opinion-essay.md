## Thesis

Tokenized stocks are the stock market's biggest shift in 30 years, and Bitget's Stock 2.0 model is the first to solve the liquidity, transparency, and capital efficiency problems that held earlier versions back.

## Argument

The shift is real because tokenized stocks fix the core inefficiencies of legacy markets: they trade 24/7, settle near-instantly via smart contracts, cut out intermediaries, and enable fractional ownership. But the first wave of tokenized stocks failed to gain traction—they were synthetic wrappers with no true ownership, sat in regulatory gray areas, and suffered from thin liquidity and cross-chain friction. Bitget's Stock 2.0 attacks exactly those failures. Stock Plus gives direct ownership of real shares, with dividends and split adjustments handled automatically, and even lets you transfer in holdings from other brokers. R tokens, issued by the Reality Protocol and backed one-to-one by shares held at Alpaca, a FINRA-registered, SIPC-protected broker, give crypto-native exposure to 500+ stocks and ETFs. The real game-changer is the unified account: R tokens can be used as margin, in lending, copy trading, and automated strategies like Auto Invest and Smart Portfolios. That's capital efficiency no traditional brokerage can match. With $100M AUM in the first month and a projected multi-trillion dollar market by 2030, this isn't just an incremental upgrade—it's the end of the stock exchange as we know it.

## Counterpoints

- R tokens don't give true ownership or voting rights, unlike Stock Plus.
- Some R tokens have different trading hours, liquidity, spreads, and fees; not all are tradable 24/7.
- Dividends on R tokens may be subject to deductions.
- Using R tokens as margin or collateral carries liquidation risk if prices move against you.
