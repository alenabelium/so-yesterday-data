## Thesis

Ethereum has overtaken Bitcoin as the institutional asset of choice because it yields cash flow, but a proposed upgrade to cap that yield now threatens the entire thesis.

## Argument

The mechanism is yield. Corporate treasuries like Bitmain stake over 87% of their ETH, generating hundreds of millions in annual rewards without new financing — a self-growing balance sheet Bitcoin can't match. Wall Street built for this: BNY wires staking rewards into custody, BlackRock's ETHB passes through 82% of yield, and tokenized treasuries settle 43% on Ethereum. But EIP-8361 would slash baseline yield from 2.6% to ~1.1%, sparking industry backlash. The fight is over who Ethereum serves: core devs optimizing decentralization or institutions optimizing payout. If yield dies, the $10B treasury bid may flee.
