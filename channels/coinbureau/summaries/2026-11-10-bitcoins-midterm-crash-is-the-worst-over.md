# Bitcoin's Midterm CRASH. Is The Worst Over?

## Executive Summary

Bitcoin's recent decline has brought it dangerously close to its 200-day moving average, a key technical level that traders use to distinguish between a pullback and a bear market. The drop was triggered by the midterm election results, which dashed hopes for favorable crypto legislation, and was exacerbated by institutional selling, ETF outflows, and leveraged liquidations. The upcoming MSCI decision on MicroStrategy (Strategy) could add further pressure, though its direct impact on Bitcoin is limited. The video concludes that Bitcoin's ability to hold above the 200-day moving average will determine whether this is just a correction or the start of a deeper downturn.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin's decline was driven by the midterm election results, which reduced the likelihood of favorable crypto legislation, and by a broader tech selloff, with Bitcoin falling 5.5% on November 5. [00:02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJFQBaMh3yg&t=125)
- Institutional selling was the biggest factor, with $1.28 billion withdrawn from US spot Bitcoin ETFs in the week to November 6, and over $1 billion in leveraged positions liquidated, mostly longs. [00:03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJFQBaMh3yg&t=232)
- The 200-day moving average at ~$73,200 is the last key support level; a daily close below it would confirm a bear market and open the way to $70,000. [00:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJFQBaMh3yg&t=420)
- MSCI's decision on MicroStrategy (Strategy) could trigger forced stock sales, but it won't force Strategy to sell its Bitcoin holdings; however, it could make raising new funds more difficult. [00:09:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJFQBaMh3yg&t=550)
