# Why Your Savings Are SECRETLY Shrinking

## Executive Summary

The video explains how governments historically and currently debase their currencies to manage debt, eroding the purchasing power of cash savers. It traces examples from Roman coin debasement to the Nixon shock and modern quantitative easing, showing that cash is never safe. The presenter argues that Bitcoin, with its fixed supply and decentralized nature, offers a modern solution for savers to protect wealth, despite acknowledged risks and counterpoints.

## Key Points

- The US government pays over $1 trillion annually in interest on its debt, and the CBO projects this will rise to $2.1 trillion by 2036, driving financial repression that erodes cash value. [00:01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfQHC-Jmc68&t=60)
- Historical examples like Roman denarius debasement and Henry VIII's 'copper nose' coins show that rulers have long used currency debasement to fund deficits, with the same result: inflation and loss of purchasing power. [00:02:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfQHC-Jmc68&t=163)
- Since the US left the gold standard in 1971, the dollar has lost about 87% of its purchasing power, and Bitcoin is presented as a modern hard money alternative with a capped supply of 21 million coins. [00:05:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfQHC-Jmc68&t=335)
