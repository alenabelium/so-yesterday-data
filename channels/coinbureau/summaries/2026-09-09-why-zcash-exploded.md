# Why Zcash EXPLODED

## Executive Summary

Zcash, a nearly 10-year-old privacy coin, has surged 2,200% over the past year as four long-standing arguments for the project converged: growing surveillance and physical attacks, quantum resistance upgrades, a successful response to a critical bug, and new institutional and meme-coin demand. The video argues that these factors, previously dismissed individually, now compound to make Zcash a serious contender against Bitcoin, which faces its own quantum and transparency challenges. The analysis highlights how Zcash's optional privacy, proactive quantum roadmap, and market dynamics have flipped its narrative from relic to top-10 asset.

## Key Points

- Zcash's privacy value proposition has strengthened as blockchain surveillance contracts and physical wrench attacks surge, making transparent chains like Bitcoin riskier. [00:02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGEAI-V-1bE&t=138)
- Zcash's quantum-resistant roadmap, including the Ironwood upgrade and Project Tachyon, contrasts with Bitcoin's stalled BIP 360/361 proposals, positioning Zcash as the more serious engineering project. [00:04:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGEAI-V-1bE&t=291)
- The successful handling of the Orchard shielded pool bug, discovered with Anthropic's Claude, restored confidence and set the stage for further gains. [00:06:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGEAI-V-1bE&t=360)
- Institutional adoption via Grayscale's ZCSH trust and a Solana meme coin (Zcat) that farms ZEC have created demand from both ends of the market. [00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGEAI-V-1bE&t=485)
