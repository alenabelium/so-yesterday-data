# Bitcoin Just WRECKED Bulls & Bears

## Executive Summary

The market is now awaiting the September CPI report, which is the last major inflation data before the Fed's rate decision, and which will likely determine Bitcoin's next move. Key levels identified include resistance at $86k-$87k, support at $80k-$81k, and intermediate levels at $84.1k and $82.5k-$83k.
