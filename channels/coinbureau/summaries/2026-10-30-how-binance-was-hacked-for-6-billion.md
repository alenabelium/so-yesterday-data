# How Binance Was HACKED For $6 Billion

## Executive Summary

Binance suffered the largest crypto exchange hack in history, losing $6 billion from hot wallets after attackers compromised a DevOps engineer's laptop and used EIP-7702 to automate withdrawals. The theft triggered a market crash, pushing Bitcoin below $80,000 and liquidating $1.5 billion in derivatives. Stablecoin issuers froze $2.5-3 billion of the stolen funds, but over $570 million in ETH was already laundered. Binance assured users their funds are safe and will cover losses via its SAFU fund and balance sheet. The attack is attributed to North Korea's Lazarus Group, though not yet officially confirmed.

## Key Points

- Attackers breached Binance by hacking a DevOps engineer's laptop, gaining access to the service container and using EIP-7702 to turn hot wallets into automatic withdrawal devices, emptying $6 billion in under 20 minutes. [00:02:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u2IlJytBT8&t=170)
- Within hours, Tether, Circle, Ripple, and Aave froze $2.5-3 billion of the stolen stablecoins, cutting the attacker's profits by about half, while $570 million in ETH moved through mixers and bridges. [00:06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u2IlJytBT8&t=403)
- The hack triggered a market crash: Bitcoin fell below $80,000, triggering $1.5 billion in liquidations across 240,000 accounts, with BNB dropping 5-8% and Ethereum hitting two-month lows. [00:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u2IlJytBT8&t=485)
