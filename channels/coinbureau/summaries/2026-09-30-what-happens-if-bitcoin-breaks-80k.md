# What Happens If Bitcoin BREAKS $80K

## Executive Summary

Bitcoin has pulled back to the low $80k range after a strong Q3, with ETF inflows failing to sustain upside due to rising bond yields, a hawkish Fed, and profit-taking by holders. The video analyzes key support and resistance levels, arguing that a clean break below $80k could lead to prolonged chop but not necessarily the end of the bull trend, while holding $80k could set the stage for further gains. Ultimately, Bitcoin's next move hinges on Fed policy expectations and broader market liquidity conditions.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin's Q3 rally stalled after hitting $87k, with ETF inflows shrinking from $2.39B weekly to $31M daily, as rising bond yields and a hawkish Fed weigh on liquidity assets. [00:01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BzV2O1vqLss&t=65)
- Three forces are pressuring Bitcoin: higher US 10-year Treasury yields (5.26%, highest since 2007), the Fed's first rate hike since 2023 with 70% odds of another, and supply from profit-taking (25,700 BTC realized) and leveraged liquidations ($4.1B in open interest flushed). [00:03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BzV2O1vqLss&t=187)
- Key levels to watch: $80k support (average ETF cost basis $81.7k, short-term holder cluster $80.5-81k), $87k resistance, and $75k support if $80k breaks. A break below $80k could cause prolonged chop but not necessarily end the bull trend, as long-term holder unrealized gains are only 72% vs 350% at the 2024 peak.
