# Why Smart Money Is DUMPING AI for Crypto

## Executive Summary

The video argues that smart money is rotating from the AI trade into crypto, particularly into projects with real cash flows like Hyperliquid, as evidenced by Stanley Druckenmiller's new stake and significant ETF inflows. It highlights that while AI stocks like Nvidia and Broadcom report strong earnings, their stock prices stagnate or fall, indicating an exhausted trade. The rotation is driven by allocators seeking similar volatility with more compelling narratives, and crypto now offers assets that can be valued like traditional exchanges, attracting institutional capital.

## Key Points

- AI trade is tired, not collapsing: Nvidia and Broadcom beat earnings but stocks barely moved, showing good news is already priced in. [00:01:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmCiLqrdhiA&t=86)
- Institutional money is rotating into crypto: Bitcoin up 21%, Ethereum up 30%, Hype up 53% over the past month, while tech indices are flat. [00:03:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmCiLqrdhiA&t=229)
- Druckenmiller's Hyperliquid stake signals a shift to cash-flow-based valuation in crypto, with buybacks and protocol fees making tokens like HYPE comparable to traditional exchanges. [00:08:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmCiLqrdhiA&t=532)
