# How Strategy Could Get KICKED Out of MSCI

## Executive Summary

MSCI's new rules could exclude MicroStrategy (Strategy) from its indices because the company is seen as an investment vehicle rather than an operating business, given its software segment is only 0.9% of assets. Exclusion would force index funds to sell ~$1.8 billion in MSTR shares, reducing demand and making it more expensive for Strategy to raise capital via ATM share sales to fund Bitcoin purchases. Strategy is already shifting spending toward buying back its preferred shares (STRC) rather than Bitcoin, signaling a potential slowdown in its Bitcoin accumulation. The final MSCI decision is expected on November 11, with index changes effective December 1.

## Key Points

- MSCI approved new rules on October 15 that could exclude Strategy from its indices because the company is viewed as an investment fund rather than an operating business, with its software segment now only 0.9% of assets. [00:01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byWq42WIJc&t=79)
- Strategy fails four of MSCI's five tests, including negative operating cash flow and reliance on capital raises, leading to potential exclusion from indices. [00:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byWq42WIJc&t=171)
- Exclusion from MSCI indices would force index funds to sell ~13 million MSTR shares ($1.8 billion), reducing demand and making it more expensive for Strategy to raise funds via ATM share sales, potentially slowing its Bitcoin purchases. [00:06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byWq42WIJc&t=400)
- Strategy is already shifting spending: $561 million on STRC buybacks vs. $72 million on Bitcoin in the last three weeks, indicating a change in its Bitcoin accumulation pace. [00:08:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byWq42WIJc&t=531)
- MSCI will publish its final exclusion list on November 11, with index changes effective December 1, and any forced sales by index funds would occur after November 30. [00:09:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2byWq42WIJc&t=594)
