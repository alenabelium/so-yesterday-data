# Japan Just BROKE the Global Financial System

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the unprecedented July 31, 2026 coordinated US-Japan intervention in the yen market, where the US Treasury sold euros rather than dollars to support the yen, marking a historic shift in global financial conventions. It argues that this intervention was primarily a defense of the US Treasury market against a forced seller, revealing the dollar's weakened reserve status and the constraints facing Japan's monetary policy. The video contrasts gold's strong rally as a reserve asset with Bitcoin's underperformance due to yen carry trade unwinds, and suggests that the event signals a new era of coordinated currency management that could have lasting implications for global markets.

## Key Points

- On July 31, 2026, the US Treasury executed a historic yen-buying operation using euros, not dollars, marking the first such intervention since 1998 and breaking long-standing conventions between Western monetary authorities. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZ4qpn-36Lg&t=0)
- The intervention was actually a defense of the US Treasury market, as Japan's need to defend the yen threatened to force it to sell its $1.2 trillion in US Treasuries, which would destabilize the bond market. [04:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZ4qpn-36Lg&t=256)
- Gold surged 7.5% in five sessions as central banks bought record amounts, while Bitcoin underperformed due to yen carry trade unwinds, highlighting gold's role as a reserve asset versus Bitcoin's risk-asset behavior. [09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZ4qpn-36Lg&t=556)
