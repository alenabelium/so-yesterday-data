# How Crypto Wallets ACTUALLY Work

## Executive Summary

This video explains that crypto wallets do not store cryptocurrency itself but rather the private keys that prove ownership of coins on the blockchain. It details how keys, addresses, and seed phrases work, and contrasts hot wallets (online) with cold wallets (offline hardware devices). The video emphasizes that security failures are almost always due to human error—such as sharing seed phrases or falling for phishing—rather than cryptographic hacking, and provides practical rules for safe wallet setup and usage.

## Key Points

- Crypto wallets store keys, not coins; losing keys means losing access to funds, as illustrated by the story of James Howells' lost hard drive. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfdPQ_hdEPA&t=0)
- Private keys generate public keys and addresses via one-way math; transactions are signed without ever exposing the private key. [00:03:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfdPQ_hdEPA&t=199)
- Hot wallets are convenient but vulnerable to phishing and malware, while cold wallets keep keys offline but require careful handling to avoid blind signing. [00:06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfdPQ_hdEPA&t=373)
- Safe wallet setup requires official downloads, paper backups of seed phrases, test transactions, and separating spending from savings. [00:09:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfdPQ_hdEPA&t=545)
- Most crypto losses result from human error—sharing seed phrases, signing malicious transactions, or losing backups—not from cryptographic attacks. [00:10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OfdPQ_hdEPA&t=654)
