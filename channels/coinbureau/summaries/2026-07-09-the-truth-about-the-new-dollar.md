# The TRUTH About The New Dollar

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes a Binance Research report on stablecoins, arguing that they have evolved from a mere trading intermediary into a fundamental part of global financial infrastructure. It highlights that stablecoins now serve as stores of value, payment rails, and settlement layers, with significant adoption in high-inflation economies and growing use cases like AI agent payments. The report also notes increasing concentration on major exchanges and the expansion of non-USD stablecoins, while cautioning about risks such as regulatory uncertainty and platform concentration.

## Key Points

- Stablecoins are shifting from a 'cryptocurrency waiting room' to a place where money actually lives, fulfilling the three functions of money: storing, moving, and establishing value. [00:01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MnEJ9vmyeg8&t=65)
- Demand for stablecoins is driven by savings protection, cheaper remittances, and 24/7 settlement, with 87% of fiat currencies trading at a premium when buying stablecoins, rising to 62% in hyperinflationary economies. [00:03:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MnEJ9vmyeg8&t=216)
- Stablecoin infrastructure is deepening both in concentration around major exchanges and in currency diversification, with local currency stablecoins like Euri and AUR exceeding $5 billion in trading volume since 2025. [00:06:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MnEJ9vmyeg8&t=414)
