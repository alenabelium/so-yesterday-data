# Oracle's $500 Billion AI Bet Is BREAKING

## Executive Summary

Oracle's Stargate data center project in New Mexico, a cornerstone of the $500 billion AI infrastructure bet, is facing significant delays due to a natural gas pipeline stuck in regulatory approvals. This has triggered Oracle to issue a force majeure notice to defer lease payments, exposing the fragility of the AI boom's debt-financed structure. The project's troubles highlight how power and permitting bottlenecks, not just chips, can derail AI expansion, and how financial risks cascade through the system.

## Key Points

- Stargate's New Mexico site, Project Jupiter, is delayed because the Green Chile gas pipeline faces regulatory denials and lawsuits, pushing its in-service date to February 2027. [00:03:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCi0rDFIZsU&t=222)
- Oracle issued a force majeure notice to defer lease payments for up to 3 years if the site misses its 2028 target, as the fuel cell microgrid is only 10% built. [00:05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCi0rDFIZsU&t=308)
- The project's financial structure is highly leveraged: Oracle's debt is $156 billion, S&P downgraded it to tripleB, and founder Larry Ellison has pledged over a third of his shares as collateral for personal loans. [00:07:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCi0rDFIZsU&t=443)
