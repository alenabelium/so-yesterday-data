# The EU Has Abandoned Democracy

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the EU has undermined democratic principles by passing the 'chat control' surveillance measure despite a majority of MEPs voting against it, using procedural rules that count abstentions as approvals. It highlights the EU's disregard for its own legal advice, the hypocrisy of demanding citizen transparency while officials delete their own messages, and the broader trend toward a surveillance state. The video concludes with cautious optimism, noting that public outcry and privacy advocacy have forced some concessions, but warns that continued engagement is necessary to protect freedoms.

## Key Points

- The EU passed 'chat control' despite a majority voting against it, because the second reading rule required an absolute majority to block it, and abstentions counted as 'yes' votes. [00:04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDDnGRcgrBQ&t=274)
- The EU's own legal service warned that the scanning rule likely violates Article 7 of the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights, yet the Council pushed it through anyway. [00:05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDDnGRcgrBQ&t=338)
- EU officials have repeatedly deleted their own messages, including those related to a €35 billion Pfizer contract, while demanding full transparency from citizens. [00:07:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDDnGRcgrBQ&t=451)
