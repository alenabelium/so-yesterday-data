# Zcash Ran 2,000% But THIS Is Next

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the recent surge in privacy coins like Zcash, which rose over 1,900% in a year, and pivots to a new project called Interfold that aims to bring privacy to decision-making through encrypted voting. Interfold uses fully homomorphic encryption and threshold cryptography to create a secret ballot that can be audited but not read, addressing the long-standing problem of vote bribery and coercion in crypto governance. The video highlights Vitalik Buterin's endorsement and investment, and argues that while privacy for assets has been repriced, privacy for decisions remains underpriced and could be the next major trend.

## Key Points

- Zcash is up over 1,900% in a year, while Bitcoin and Ethereum are down, signaling the market's shift toward privacy coins. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoNozEcWRDA&t=0)
- Privacy coins faced heavy regulatory pressure until 2025, when Tornado Cash was removed from sanctions and the narrative flipped. [00:02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoNozEcWRDA&t=147)
- Interfold, previously Enclave, uses fully homomorphic encryption and threshold cryptography to enable coercion-resistant voting, solving the trusted coordinator problem of MACI. [00:07:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoNozEcWRDA&t=448)
- Vitalik Buterin endorsed Interfold, participated in its token auction, and has been advocating for this type of infrastructure for years. [00:10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoNozEcWRDA&t=642)
- Token voting is broken, with low participation and bribery, and Interfold aims to fix this by hiding votes, making them unprovable and thus unsellable. [00:11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoNozEcWRDA&t=708)
