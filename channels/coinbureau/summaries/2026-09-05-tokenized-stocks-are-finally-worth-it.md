# Tokenized Stocks Are FINALLY Worth It

## Executive Summary

The video explains how BitGet's R token model makes tokenized stocks more practical by allowing users to trade real US stocks like Apple and Nvidia directly on a crypto exchange, use them as collateral, earn dividends, and integrate them with trading bots. It highlights the benefits of tokenization, such as 24/7 trading, fractional ownership, and transparency, while also comparing liquidity and spreads with competitors. The presenter concludes that R tokens are best suited for active traders, while passive investors may find them less appealing.

## Key Points

- Tokenized stocks offer benefits like faster settlement, 24/7 trading, fractional ownership, and DeFi integration, but most sit idle in wallets. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwpEnKg1JHc&t=0)
- BitGet's R tokens are backed 1:1 by real US shares, custodied with Alpaca, and can be used for margin, dividends, and trading bots. [00:02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwpEnKg1JHc&t=134)
- R tokens have significantly deeper order book liquidity than competitors (e.g., 54x for SPY, 214x for COIN, 1700x for SNDK), but spreads vary by stock. [00:11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwpEnKg1JHc&t=691)
- R tokens are best for active traders due to liquidity and capital efficiency; passive investors may not benefit as much. [00:13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwpEnKg1JHc&t=792)
