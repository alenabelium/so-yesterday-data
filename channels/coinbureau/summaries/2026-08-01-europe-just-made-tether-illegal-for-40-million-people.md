# Europe Just Made Tether ILLEGAL for 40 Million People!

## Executive Summary

The video explains that Revolut's automatic conversion of USDT to fiat for European users stems from MiCA regulations, which require stablecoin issuers to obtain licenses and hold 60% of reserves in EU banks—a requirement Tether refused, opting instead to comply with US regulations. Europe prepared alternatives like USDC and EURC, and the ECB is promoting a digital euro to protect the banking system from deposit outflows. The video argues that Europe is filtering out non-compliant stablecoins while fostering a regulated market, and that stablecoins are becoming permanent infrastructure.

## Key Points

- Revolut will automatically convert USDT to fiat for European users on August 31, 2026, due to MiCA regulations requiring licensed issuers, and Tether's refusal to comply. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vj-1qr9QOcU&t=0)
- MiCA does not ban USDT ownership; it restricts platforms, but Tether chose not to apply for a license because of the 60% bank reserve requirement, which it argues is risky. [00:02:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vj-1qr9QOcU&t=164)
- Europe prepared alternatives like USDC and EURC, and 37 European banks formed a consortium to issue their own euro stablecoin, signaling that stablecoins are here to stay. [00:09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vj-1qr9QOcU&t=556)
