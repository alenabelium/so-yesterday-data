# Top 5 Biggest Crypto Losses Ever

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau examines the five largest financial losses in cryptocurrency history, from James Howells' discarded Bitcoin hard drive to the BitConnect Ponzi scheme, the Terra ecosystem collapse, the FTX exchange fraud, and the record-breaking liquidation event triggered by Trump's tariff announcement in 2025. The narrator, Guy, explains how each disaster unfolded and distills the key lessons, emphasizing that the biggest risks in crypto are not technological but human, and that simple precautions like self-custody, skepticism, and avoiding leverage can protect investors.

## Key Points

- James Howells mined 8,000 Bitcoin in 2009 but lost them forever when the hard drive containing the private key was thrown away; a 2025 court ruling confirmed the drive now belongs to the council, and the lesson is that owning your keys means owning the risk, so backups are essential. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cL1BJtVU2KI&t=0)
- BitConnect was a classic Ponzi scheme that took in $2.4 billion by promising guaranteed 1% daily returns from a nonexistent trading bot; the warning signs were obvious, and the protection is simple: if profit can't be explained in one sentence or referrals pay more than the product, it's a scam. [00:02:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cL1BJtVU2KI&t=178)
- The Terra ecosystem collapsed in May 2022, wiping out $40 billion in days because its algorithmic stablecoin UST had no collateral and relied on an unsustainable 20% yield; the death spiral that followed also helped bring down Three Arrows Capital, Celsius, Voyager, and later FTX. [00:04:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cL1BJtVU2KI&t=257)
- FTX, once the second-largest exchange, lost $8 billion in customer funds because it secretly allowed Alameda Research to borrow client money; the lesson is that an exchange is not a safe—it's a promissory note, so you should self-custody anything you can't afford to lose. [00:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cL1BJtVU2KI&t=420)
- On October 10, 2025, a single tweet from President Trump about 100% tariffs on Chinese imports triggered the largest liquidation in crypto history, wiping out $19 billion in leveraged positions and affecting 1.6 million traders; this was a structural deleveraging event, not fraud, showing how overconfidence and excessive leverage create systemic risk. [00:09:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cL1BJtVU2KI&t=550)
