# How to SURVIVE This Bear Market.

## Executive Summary

The video argues that despite extreme bearish sentiment, the current crypto bear market is structurally different and more resilient than previous cycles, with shallower drawdowns and a stronger holder base. It attributes the market's weakness to a lack of global liquidity, driven by Fed tightening, a strong dollar, and yen carry trade unwinds, rather than a fundamental collapse. The analysis highlights that institutional holders like ETFs and corporate treasuries have provided a floor, while the traditional 4-year cycle is stretching into a longer, shallower shape. The video concludes that the Fed's December meeting is the key catalyst to watch for a potential turnaround.

## Key Points

- The current bear market is much shallower than previous ones, with Bitcoin down only 55% from its peak compared to 77-84% in past cycles, thanks to a stronger holder base including ETFs and corporate treasuries. [00:06:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SIbzTHUCJWY&t=409)
- The lack of a parabolic top is due to restrictive global liquidity, including Fed rate hikes, a strong dollar, and yen carry trade unwinds, which have kept speculative capital away. [00:09:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SIbzTHUCJWY&t=560)
- The 4-year cycle is stretching into a longer, shallower shape, and the Fed's December meeting is the next major catalyst that could shift the market's direction. [00:12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SIbzTHUCJWY&t=771)
