# Pump.fun KILLED Crypto

## Executive Summary

Pump.fun simplified meme coin creation to a single click, flooding the market with millions of tokens and transforming crypto into a casino-like environment. The platform profits from transaction fees, earning over $1 billion, while most coins die within a day and creators, not traders, capture the majority of gains. Celebrity and political launches, including Trump's coin, followed the same pattern of insider profits and retail losses. Despite legal challenges and a shrinking meme coin market, Pump.fun remains dominant, raising questions about whether its business model is inherently exploitative.

## Key Points

- Pump.fun made coin creation free and easy, leading to over 24 million tokens launched, with 79% never trading again after day one. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNZIXXz7xy8&t=0)
- Creators using 'bundles' earned at least $579 million over eight months, about 18 times more than 'snipers,' highlighting that launch insiders capture most profits. [00:03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNZIXXz7xy8&t=200)
- Pump.fun's revenue exceeded $1 billion, with 87% coming from coins less than a day old, showing that the platform profits from the rapid turnover of dying tokens. [00:10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNZIXXz7xy8&t=657)
