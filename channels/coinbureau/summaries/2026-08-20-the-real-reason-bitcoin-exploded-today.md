# The Real Reason Bitcoin EXPLODED Today

## Executive Summary

Bitcoin surged 9% in a single session, driven by a US Treasury announcement to double liquidity support buybacks for longer-dated bonds, which lowered yields and boosted risk assets. The move was amplified by a White House crypto summit where President Trump signaled support for bringing Hyperliquid into the US in a compliant manner, alongside SEC and CFTC regulatory advancements. This combination of macro easing and crypto-specific catalysts triggered the largest short liquidation on record, with over $1.1 billion in shorts wiped out. The rally was led by Hyperliquid (HYPE), which surged over 20%, while traditional exchanges like Cboe and CME fell, suggesting the market is pricing in a legal on-ramp for decentralized perpetuals.
