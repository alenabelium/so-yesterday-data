# Tom Lee Proves ETH Is BETTER Than Bitcoin

## Executive Summary

Tom Lee's Bitmain has pivoted from Bitcoin mining to an Ethereum treasury company, generating $45.7 million in a quarter from staking, which is 98% of its revenue. This contrasts sharply with Strategy's Bitcoin holdings, which produce no cash flow and forced a rare Bitcoin sale in June 2026. However, the staking yield is not risk-free: it depends on network emission, faces slashing and regulatory risks, and Bitmain still reported a net loss of $83 million due to ETH price declines. The video concludes that staking income does not eliminate the fundamental volatility of the underlying asset, making ETH a leveraged bet with dividends rather than a fundamentally safer treasury asset.

## Key Points

- Bitmain earned $45.7 million from Ethereum staking in one quarter, representing 98% of its revenue, while Strategy's Bitcoin holdings generate no income and forced a rare sale to cover obligations. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=410Xf3lj-nA&t=1)
- Ethereum staking rewards are largely funded by network emission, not external revenue, and are subject to slashing, smart contract risks, and regulatory uncertainty. [00:05:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=410Xf3lj-nA&t=354)
- Despite record staking income, Bitmain reported a net loss of $83 million in the same quarter due to ETH price declines, showing that yield does not protect against asset volatility. [00:10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=410Xf3lj-nA&t=602)
