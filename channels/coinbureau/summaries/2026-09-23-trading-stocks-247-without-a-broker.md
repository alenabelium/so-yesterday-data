# Trading Stocks 24/7 WITHOUT a Broker

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau explores how BitGet enables 24/7 trading of tokenized stocks and stock perpetual futures without a traditional broker, focusing on institutional-grade features. It highlights low slippage on R tokens, deep liquidity in stock perps, the unified trading account (UTA) for collateral efficiency, robust APIs with high request limits, and strong risk controls like proof of reserves and third-party custody. The main thesis is that BitGet offers a comprehensive, institutionally viable platform for round-the-clock equity exposure.

## Key Points

- R tokens offer significantly lower slippage for large orders, with 45-58% less slippage at $50,000 compared to competing books, and trade 24/7 alongside stock perpetual futures for hedging. [00:01:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=om38jrz4jw8&t=74)
- BitGet's unified trading account (UTA) allows eligible R tokens and gold (XAUT) to be used as collateral at up to 95% ratios, improving capital efficiency without selling positions. [00:06:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=om38jrz4jw8&t=372)
- Institutional clients get high API limits (up to 600 RPS per account, 120,000 RPS for spot and futures across linked sub-accounts), plus REST, WebSocket, and FIX connectivity for automated trading. [00:09:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=om38jrz4jw8&t=572)
- BitGet publishes monthly proof of reserves (122% ratio) and daily reserve attestations for R tokens, and supports third-party custody via Fireblocks, Copper, and others to mitigate counterparty risk. [00:11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=om38jrz4jw8&t=679)
