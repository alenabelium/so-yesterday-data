# China Is Quietly Winning the AI Race

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes how Chinese AI models have overtaken American ones in global developer traffic on OpenRouter, driven by US export controls that forced Chinese labs to innovate for efficiency and price. It highlights that American startups are increasingly adopting Chinese models to cut costs dramatically, while US firms still lead in high-value, complex tasks. The video warns that massive US capital expenditures on premium AI may not be justified if the world shifts to cheaper Chinese infrastructure, posing risks to investors.

## Key Points

- Chinese models now process over 50% of OpenRouter traffic, with DeepSeek alone surpassing Google and OpenAI in token volume, a shift that began in February 2025. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inPu89p-Xdo&t=0)
- US export controls forced Chinese labs to develop highly efficient models like DeepSeek's V4 Flash, which costs 36x less than comparable American models, making them the default choice for price-sensitive developers. [00:05:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inPu89p-Xdo&t=355)
- Despite China's volume dominance, US firms still capture 46% of OpenRouter's dollar revenue, indicating a split between a premium American stack and a Chinese industrial stack, with significant implications for the $725 billion US AI capex bet. [00:08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inPu89p-Xdo&t=495)
