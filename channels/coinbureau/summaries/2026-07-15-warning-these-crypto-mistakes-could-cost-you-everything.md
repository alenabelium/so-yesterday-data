# WARNING! These Crypto Mistakes Could Cost You Everything

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau warns about seven critical crypto mistakes that could devastate investors in 2026, highlighting the rise of sophisticated scams, the dangers of leverage, and new tax regulations. It emphasizes that ordinary investors, not just reckless gamblers, are at risk due to evolving fraud tactics and increased regulatory scrutiny. The video provides practical advice on how to avoid these traps, such as verifying addresses, securing private keys, avoiding excessive leverage, and being cautious of AI-driven scams and recovery fraud.

## Key Points

- Address poisoning is a growing threat, with 160,000 daily attacks and a 5.5x increase in attempts; always verify the full recipient address and use hardware wallets. [00:02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=176)
- Over 80% of stolen crypto in 2025 came from infrastructure attacks, meaning improper key management; buy hardware wallets directly from manufacturers and never store seed phrases digitally. [00:05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=326)
- The October 2025 liquidation event destroyed $19 billion in leveraged positions, showing that even small leverage can be fatal; the only safe move is to avoid trading with money you can't afford to lose. [00:06:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=410)
- Memecoins crashed 86% of all tokens in 2025, wiping out over $100 billion in market cap; the lottery is rigged against retail investors. [00:08:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=505)
- New IRS rules (Form 1099DA) and the end of universal wallet method mean every transaction is tracked; negligence can lead to 20% fines, and fraud penalties up to 75%. [00:09:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=593)
- AI fraud is exploding, with deepfakes and voice cloning making scams highly convincing; the FBI reported $893 million in losses, and AI frauds are 4.5x more profitable for scammers. [00:11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=663)
- Refund scams prey on previous victims, costing $1.4 billion in 2025; real law enforcement never asks for upfront fees, and no one can reverse blockchain transactions. [00:13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6j-eOB8pZk&t=808)
