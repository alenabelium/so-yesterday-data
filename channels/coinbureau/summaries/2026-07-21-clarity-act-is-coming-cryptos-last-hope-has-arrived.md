# Clarity Act is COMING!! Crypto's Last Hope Has Arrived

## Executive Summary

The Clarity Act, a major US crypto bill, has advanced after President Trump agreed to ethics language demanded by Democrats, neutralizing a key corruption argument. This agreement gives moderate Democrats political cover to support the bill, potentially overcoming the 60-vote Senate filibuster barrier. The market has not fully priced in this progress, with approval odds still around 30-40%, creating a potential opportunity. The bill would codify the SEC/CFTC classification of major tokens as digital goods, providing permanent regulatory clarity and boosting US competitiveness. However, risks remain due to tight deadlines, pending final text, and implementation gaps.

## Key Points

- The ethics dispute blocking the Clarity Act was resolved on July 20, 2026, when Trump agreed to Democratic demands, removing the main corruption argument used by Elizabeth Warren and others. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kGucEI4-pzY&t=0)
- The agreement gives moderate Democrats like Ruben Gallego and Angela Alsobrooks the political cover needed to secure the 60 votes required to overcome a Senate filibuster, as they had already supported the bill in committee. [00:03:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kGucEI4-pzY&t=206)
- Despite this breakthrough, market odds on Polymarket remain low at 30-40%, indicating the market has not fully priced in the bill's potential, which could lead to significant upside if passed. [00:04:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kGucEI4-pzY&t=260)
