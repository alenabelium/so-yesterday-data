# The EU is Collapsing

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the EU's post-1990s economic model, built on cheap Russian gas, Chinese demand, and American security, has collapsed as all three pillars failed simultaneously, leading to de-industrialization and a structural decline. It highlights how energy cost disadvantages, Chinese competition, and political fragmentation are driving factory closures and job losses, while European equity indices rise due to financialization. The video concludes that the EU's regulatory response, including cash limits, the digital euro, and MiCA, is making the region less competitive and more restrictive, accelerating currency substitution toward the dollar.

## Key Points

- Europe's economic model relied on cheap Russian gas, Chinese demand, and American security, but all three legs have failed, causing a structural decline rather than a cyclical recession. [00:01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lalaqiCy4RE&t=66)
- Energy costs in Europe are 5-7 times higher than in the US, and industrial production in energy-intensive sectors has fallen 15% since 2022, with BASF cutting thousands of jobs and building its largest plant in China. [00:02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lalaqiCy4RE&t=133)
- The EU's regulatory response, including cash payment limits, the digital euro, and MiCA, is making the region more restrictive, leading to dollar stablecoins capturing 84% of crypto card spending in Europe by July 2026. [00:11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lalaqiCy4RE&t=701)
