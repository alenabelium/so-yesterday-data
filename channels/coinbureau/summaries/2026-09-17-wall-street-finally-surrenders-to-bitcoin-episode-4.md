# Wall Street FINALLY Surrenders To Bitcoin (Episode 4)

## Executive Summary

This episode of Coin Bureau's series on Bitcoin chronicles the period from 2020 to 2024, when Wall Street and major corporations shifted from skepticism to active adoption of Bitcoin. It highlights key milestones such as MicroStrategy's massive treasury purchases, the entry of traditional financial institutions into crypto custody and trading, and the eventual approval of spot Bitcoin ETFs in January 2024. The narrative emphasizes that despite market crashes and regulatory pushback, Bitcoin's network resilience and growing institutional infrastructure led to its integration into the traditional financial system.

## Key Points

- In 2020, amid COVID-19 stimulus and inflation fears, major investors like Paul Tudor Jones and companies like MicroStrategy began buying Bitcoin as a store of value, marking a significant shift in institutional sentiment. [00:02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9H2YSprxp4&t=155)
- Following MicroStrategy's lead, other firms such as Square, Stone Ridge, Guggenheim, MassMutual, and Tesla added Bitcoin to their balance sheets, while banks like Morgan Stanley began offering clients access to Bitcoin funds. [00:09:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9H2YSprxp4&t=559)
- Despite the 2022 crypto crashes (Terra, Three Arrows, FTX), BlackRock and other major asset managers pushed forward with spot Bitcoin ETF applications, leading to SEC approval in January 2024 and billions in institutional inflows. [00:19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9H2YSprxp4&t=1156)
