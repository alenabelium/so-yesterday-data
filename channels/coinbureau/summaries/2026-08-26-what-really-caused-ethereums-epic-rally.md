# What Really Caused Ethereum's EPIC Rally

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the massive short squeeze in Ethereum in August 2026, which saw over $2.7 billion in liquidations, mostly shorts, driven by a combination of crowded positioning, thin order books, and a fundamental misreading of Ethereum's roadmap. The bearish thesis focused on declining L1 fees, but the video argues this was a deliberate outcome of scaling upgrades like EIP-4844 and Fusaka, which have made L2s cheaper and increased network usage. Additionally, the SEC's proposed crypto framework, which could enable a new wave of token launches, is highlighted as a major catalyst. The video concludes that Ethereum's fundamentals, including stablecoin dominance, institutional adoption, and staking, position it well for further gains.

## Key Points

- A trader known as PensionUSDT lost $24 million in a single short liquidation, part of a broader $2.7 billion short squeeze that wiped out over 172,000 traders, with Ethereum accounting for $1.13 billion of the liquidations. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xe8XiN5Zt4Y&t=0)
- The bearish thesis on Ethereum focused on declining L1 fees, but the video argues this was a deliberate outcome of scaling upgrades like EIP-4844 and Fusaka, which have reduced L2 fees by over 90% and increased network capacity. [00:07:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xe8XiN5Zt4Y&t=454)
- The SEC proposed a new crypto framework on August 18, 2026, which could enable a new wave of token launches (ICO 2.0), potentially bringing the market onshore and benefiting Ethereum as the primary venue for stablecoins and institutional custody.
