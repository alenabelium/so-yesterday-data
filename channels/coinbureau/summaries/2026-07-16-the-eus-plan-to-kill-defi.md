# The EU's Plan To Kill DeFi

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the EU's MiCA review, extended in June 2026, could reclassify many crypto activities under MiFID II, effectively making DeFi, staking, and lending legally impossible in Europe. It contends that MiFID's compliance requirements are architecturally impossible for permissionless protocols, favoring large institutions like banks. The presenter suggests this is a regulatory takeover that consolidates the crypto market into traditional finance, while noting the industry still has time to lobby and influence the outcome before 2027-2028.

## Key Points

- MiCA's residual regime excludes assets that qualify as financial instruments under MiFID II, and ESMA rejects 'semantic arbitrage,' meaning tokens that behave like stocks or derivatives are treated as such. [00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jd-PPibcWnQ&t=123)
- MiFID requirements like licensed firm status, best execution, and compliance checks are impossible for permissionless smart contracts, effectively outlawing DeFi. [00:06:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jd-PPibcWnQ&t=367)
- The consolidation from over 3,000 crypto firms to under 280 licensed CASPs shows that only large, well-capitalized institutions can survive, and the MiFID reclassification would accelerate this trend. [00:07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jd-PPibcWnQ&t=461)
