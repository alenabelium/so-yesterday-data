# Why Gold Keeps CRASHING.

## Executive Summary

The video explains why gold crashed in 2026 despite geopolitical turmoil and inflation, attributing the decline to Federal Reserve rate hikes that made bonds more attractive and to forced selling by leveraged investors facing margin calls. Historical parallels from 2008, 2020, and 2022 show gold recovering once the Fed pivots, and central banks, especially China, are buying the dip, indicating long-term demand remains strong.

## Key Points

- Gold fell 30% from its January peak despite war and inflation, because the Fed raised rates, making bonds more attractive than zero-yield gold. [00:03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ptb5dj7ZqPU&t=180)
- The October 30 crash was driven by margin calls, forcing leveraged investors to sell gold as a liquid asset to raise cash. [00:05:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ptb5dj7ZqPU&t=348)
- Central banks, especially China, are buying gold during the sell-off, with China purchasing for 24 consecutive months, suggesting long-term demand remains strong. [00:12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ptb5dj7ZqPU&t=758)
