# How Wall Street Is Choosing Crypto's WINNERS

## Executive Summary

Wall Street is increasingly adopting blockchain technology, with ICE (owner of the NYSE) choosing Avalanche for its new 24/7 stock trading platform and acquiring a stake in trade.xyz, built on Hyperliquid, for perpetual futures. These moves highlight a trend where traditional financial institutions select specific blockchains and integrate them into regulated frameworks, often excluding public tokens like AVAX but embracing HYPE through buybacks. The video also notes similar moves by Kraken and Nasdaq, and discusses the implications for which blockchain will emerge as the biggest winner in traditional finance.

## Key Points

- ICE chose Avalanche technology for the NYSE's new 24/7 stock trading platform, but excluded the public network and AVAX token from the plan. [00:02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9k47sQ0CT18&t=125)
- ICE acquired a stake in trade.xyz, built on Hyperliquid, which allows 24/7 trading of perpetual futures, and the deal includes CFTC regulation and KYC/AML compliance. [00:06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9k47sQ0CT18&t=408)
- Hyperliquid's token HYPE benefits from buybacks funded by trading commissions, and the involvement of Stanley Druckenmiller and other major players signals growing institutional interest. [00:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9k47sQ0CT18&t=664)
