# The END of the Stock Exchange As We Know It?

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the transformative potential of tokenized stocks, which allow for 24/7 trading, instant settlement, lower fees, and fractional ownership, marking a significant shift in the stock market since electronic trading. It highlights Bitget's Stock 2.0 model, featuring Stock Plus for direct ownership and R tokens for crypto-native exposure, backed by the Reality Protocol and held with licensed broker Alpaca. The model integrates with Bitget's Universal Exchange, enabling use of R tokens as margin, in lending, copy trading, and automated strategies. The video also covers the challenges of earlier tokenized stocks, such as low liquidity and regulatory uncertainty, and projects a multi-trillion dollar market by 2030.

## Key Points

- Tokenized stocks offer 24/7 trading, near-instant settlement, lower fees, and fractional ownership, addressing key limitations of traditional stock markets. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_5DRdBd4oY&t=0)
- Bitget's Stock 2.0 model introduces Stock Plus for direct share ownership and R tokens for crypto-native exposure, both integrated into its Universal Exchange. [00:02:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_5DRdBd4oY&t=175)
- R tokens are backed one-to-one by real shares held with Alpaca, a FINRA-registered broker, and can be used across Bitget's ecosystem for margin, lending, copy trading, and automated strategies. [00:11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_5DRdBd4oY&t=669)
