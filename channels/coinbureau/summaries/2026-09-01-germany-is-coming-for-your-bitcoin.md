# Germany Is Coming For YOUR Bitcoin.

## Executive Summary

Germany is intensifying its crackdown on cryptocurrency, moving from selling seized Bitcoin to proposing a tax on long-term holders and implementing strict reporting requirements. The government has already budgeted revenue from a tax that hasn't been passed, while simultaneously empowering traditional banks to offer crypto services, potentially squeezing out independent players and self-custody. This coordinated approach, including early termination of licensing grace periods and increased audits, suggests a deliberate strategy to centralize crypto within the banking system.

## Key Points

- Germany sold 49,858 seized Bitcoin in 2024 and is set to receive another 57,000 coins from a court deal, with combined proceeds up to 6.2 billion euros. [00:01:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-80vYO9tXlM&t=93)
- The government's draft budget proposes eliminating the 12-month tax exemption for crypto holdings, and the revenue is already penciled into the budget despite no law being passed. [00:03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-80vYO9tXlM&t=180)
- Germany's new crypto tax transparency act requires all exchanges to report customer data to the tax office, with the first handover due by July 2027, and the government is simultaneously fast-tracking bank crypto offerings while tightening oversight on independent firms. [00:05:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-80vYO9tXlM&t=321)
