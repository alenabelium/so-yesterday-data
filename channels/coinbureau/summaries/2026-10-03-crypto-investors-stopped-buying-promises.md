# Crypto Investors Stopped Buying PROMISES

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the crypto market has fundamentally shifted from valuing promises and speculative narratives to demanding tangible revenue and clear value flows to token holders. It traces this evolution from the ICO boom of 2017, through the DeFi era and the 2022 collapses, to today's focus on protocols with real business models like stablecoins, tokenized real-world assets, and perp DEXs. The key takeaway is that tokens now need to demonstrate actual earnings and distribution mechanisms, such as fee switches and buybacks, to attract serious investment.

## Key Points

- The ICO boom of 2017-2018 raised over $20 billion, but 80% of projects were scams or dead, showing that tokens without real products or revenue were unsustainable. [00:00:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ymC44d3Godo&t=56)
- DeFi introduced real products with fees and revenue, but governance tokens often didn't capture that value, leading to a lesson that user activity isn't the same as value flowing to token holders. [00:03:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ymC44d3Godo&t=237)
- Today's winning protocols, like stablecoin issuers and Hyperliquid, have clear business models where fees and revenue are directly tied to token value through mechanisms like buybacks and fee switches. [00:08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ymC44d3Godo&t=484)
