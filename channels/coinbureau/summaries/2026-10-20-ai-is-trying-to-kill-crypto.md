# AI Is Trying To KILL Crypto

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the potential threat of AI to cryptocurrency security, focusing on how AI's rapid advancement in mathematics could eventually crack the cryptographic signatures protecting wallets. It highlights a post by Ethereum Foundation researcher Justin Drake urging the industry to prepare for 'bunker mode,' and explores the divided opinions within the crypto community. The video explains the technical basis of crypto security, the specific vulnerabilities of certain coins, and the differing approaches of Bitcoin and Ethereum to address the threat. It concludes that while AI hasn't broken anything yet, the industry must update its cryptographic standards before it's too late.

## Key Points

- AI has progressed from failing simple math to achieving gold medal level at the International Mathematical Olympiad, and has even helped improve attacks on new cryptographic signatures, raising concerns about its potential to crack crypto's math. [00:01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=95gQnz1eQAo&t=62)
- The most vulnerable coins are those at addresses that have already been used, as the 'lock' is exposed; roughly a third of all bitcoins are at such addresses, while mining and blockchain history are less at risk. [00:07:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=95gQnz1eQAo&t=430)
- Bitcoin and Ethereum are taking opposite approaches: Ethereum plans faster, public migrations to hash-based locks, while Bitcoin debates slower, consensus-driven changes, including proposals to freeze old address types. [00:10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=95gQnz1eQAo&t=632)
