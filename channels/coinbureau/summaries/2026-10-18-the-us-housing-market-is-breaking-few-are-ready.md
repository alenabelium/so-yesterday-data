# The US Housing Market is BREAKING (few are ready)

## Executive Summary

The US housing market is frozen due to high mortgage rates, which have doubled since 2021, making monthly payments unaffordable for buyers and trapping homeowners with low-rate mortgages in place. This lock-in effect has drastically reduced home sales and first-time buyer activity, while developers are cutting prices and offering incentives, creating a standoff between sellers anchored to past prices and buyers constrained by current rates. The market is not stable but rather a waiting game where those forced to move suffer the most, and the traditional housing ladder has broken down.

## Key Points

- Mortgage rates have risen from 2.65% in 2021 to 7.4%, increasing monthly payments by nearly $1,000 for the same house, pricing out millions of potential buyers. [00:02:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L2w72EoTLqs&t=128)
- The 'lock-in' effect keeps homeowners with sub-4% mortgages from selling, doubling the average tenure to 12 years and reducing existing home sales to the lowest level since 1995. [00:03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L2w72EoTLqs&t=183)
- Developers are slashing prices and offering incentives, making new homes cheaper than existing ones, which forces private sellers to compete and leads to record contract cancellations. [00:05:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L2w72EoTLqs&t=310)
- First-time homebuyers are now a record low 21% of buyers, with the median age rising to 40, as monthly housing payments have increased 74% in five years, making homeownership increasingly unattainable. [00:07:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L2w72EoTLqs&t=460)
