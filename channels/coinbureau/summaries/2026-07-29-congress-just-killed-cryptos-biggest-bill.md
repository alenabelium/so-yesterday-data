# Congress Just KILLED Crypto's Biggest Bill

## Executive Summary

The Clarity Act, the most significant U.S. cryptocurrency bill, has lost its place on the Senate calendar and faces a critical time crunch before the August recess, with passage odds dropping from over 80% in February to around 30-40%. The bill's Section 604 ethics provision, which prohibits officials from promoting digital assets but allows passive income from pre-existing tokens, has drawn criticism from Democrats like Elizabeth Warren and key swing votes, further complicating passage. Meanwhile, the SEC and CFTC are advancing their own regulatory frameworks, but these agency rules lack the permanence of legislation and are vulnerable to legal challenges. The market has reacted with significant liquidations and ETF outflows, while international competitors like Europe and Japan move ahead with clearer crypto regulations.

## Key Points

- The Clarity Act has been pushed down the Senate agenda due to other priorities, leaving only a few working days before recess and causing market forecasts for its passage to drop to around 30-40%. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2VlNi3i248&t=1)
- Section 604 of the bill, which bans officials from promoting digital assets but allows passive income from pre-existing tokens, has sparked controversy because it appears to exempt President Trump's $1.4 billion in crypto revenue. [00:04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2VlNi3i248&t=279)
- The SEC and CFTC are already writing rules to cover similar ground, but these are only interpretations that can be reversed or challenged in court, unlike a permanent statute. [00:08:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A2VlNi3i248&t=495)
