# The US Just Banned CBDCs... No Thanks To Trump

## Executive Summary

The video explains how the US banned central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) through a housing bill that became law without President Trump's signature, due to a constitutional provision. It argues that while the ban was framed as a victory against surveillance, it primarily benefited commercial banks by eliminating a threat to their business model. The video also compares this move to global CBDC developments, particularly China's digital yuan, and questions whether the US is disarming itself in the currency race.

## Key Points

- The CBDC ban was hidden in the 21st Century Pathway to Housing Act, which became law automatically after Trump refused to sign it, due to Article 1, Section 7 of the Constitution. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJ7CTTuf40&t=0)
- Commercial banks were the main opponents of CBDCs because a digital dollar would allow citizens to hold accounts directly with the Fed, bypassing banks and threatening their deposit-based lending model. [00:06:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJ7CTTuf40&t=414)
- While China's digital yuan is already processing billions in transactions and Europe is advancing its digital euro, the US has removed itself from the digital currency race until at least 2031, though the dollar's dominance remains strong due to rule of law and capital markets. [00:11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuJ7CTTuf40&t=681)
