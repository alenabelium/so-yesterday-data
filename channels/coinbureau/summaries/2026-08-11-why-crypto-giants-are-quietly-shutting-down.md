# Why Crypto Giants Are Quietly Shutting Down

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the wave of crypto company shutdowns in 2026, arguing that unlike the fraud-driven 2022 bear market, these closures are a rationalization of unprofitable businesses due to dried-up easy money and commoditized products. It highlights BitMEX's decline, regulatory casualties, and the forced selling by digital asset treasury companies, while noting that capital has rotated into AI infrastructure. The video concludes that these shutdowns and capitulation signals are part of a standard bottoming process, potentially setting up a future recovery.

## Key Points

- BitMEX, the inventor of perpetual swaps, is shutting down after 11 years due to commoditization and volume collapse to under 0.01% of the market, with no scandal or insolvency. [00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gy7bszcKpg&t=6)
- Unlike 2022's fraud-driven contagion, 2026 shutdowns are orderly rationalizations of unprofitable businesses, with regulatory casualties like AscendEX and EXMO facing compliance and sanctions issues. [04:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gy7bszcKpg&t=274)
- Digital asset treasury companies like Strategy are being forced to sell Bitcoin for the first time, with MNAV dropping below 1, creating a finite overhang that is a textbook bottoming signal. [08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gy7bszcKpg&t=480)
- Miners are converting to AI infrastructure, with over $70 billion in AI contracts, showing capital rotation from crypto to AI rather than market panic. [11:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gy7bszcKpg&t=675)
- Long-term holders are capitulating (43% of realized losses), exchange balances are at 7-year lows, and leverage is purged, suggesting an exhausted seller and a potential bottom, though the drawdown is still shallow compared to past cycles. [13:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gy7bszcKpg&t=790)
