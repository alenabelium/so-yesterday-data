# What Happens When Michael Saylor Stops Buying Bitcoin?

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the market impact when Michael Saylor's Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) paused Bitcoin purchases and even sold some coins during a price drop. Despite this, Bitcoin recovered, challenging the bearish thesis that Bitcoin's price depended on Saylor's buying. The video explains Strategy's new capital structure, including a $1.25 billion Bitcoin monetization cap and increased cash reserves, and highlights that ETF inflows and long-term holders absorbed the selling pressure. It concludes that Bitcoin's price is becoming less dependent on Strategy's actions, though risks remain.

## Key Points

- Strategy paused Bitcoin purchases for 5 weeks, the longest since 2020, and sold 3,588 coins during the drop, yet Bitcoin recovered 14% from its lows. [00:05:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGJR2RG8aRQ&t=340)
- Strategy's new capital structure includes a $1.25 billion Bitcoin monetization cap, $2 billion share repurchase authorization, and increased cash reserves to $3.75 billion, covering 24-28 months of obligations. [00:02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGJR2RG8aRQ&t=130)
- ETF inflows and long-term holder accumulation absorbed the selling pressure, with Metaplanet buying 2,823 BTC in the same week Strategy sold. [00:06:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGJR2RG8aRQ&t=412)
- Strategy's MNAV fell below 1 for the first time in history on June 27, but recovered to ~1.03, indicating the market revalued the company positively despite the pause. [00:12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGJR2RG8aRQ&t=731)
