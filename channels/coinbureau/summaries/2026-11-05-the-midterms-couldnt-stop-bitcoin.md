# The Midterms Couldn't Stop Bitcoin!

## Executive Summary

Bitcoin demonstrated remarkable resilience despite two major shocks in the same week: the largest crypto exchange theft in history and a Democratic victory in the US midterm elections that the market feared would bring stricter regulation. After initially falling to $75,000, Bitcoin rebounded above $81,000 within 12 hours, driven largely by a short squeeze. The analysis highlights key support and resistance levels, emphasizing that Bitcoin must hold $80,000 as support and break through $85,000 to confirm a bullish reversal. The episode underscores the market's ability to absorb negative news and suggests a potential shift in sentiment.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin fell to $75,000 on election night fears but rebounded to $81,000 within 12 hours, showing strength despite political and exchange-related shocks. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n17v727G7FI&t=0)
- The $950 million liquidation event, with over $560 million in short positions, fueled a short squeeze that drove the price recovery. [00:03:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n17v727G7FI&t=227)
