# The Vote That Could Break US Crypto

## Executive Summary

The video discusses the procedural status of the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act in the US Senate, explaining that despite initial reports of its failure, Senate Majority Leader John Thune filed a cloture motion guaranteeing a vote on September 15, 2026. The analysis covers the vote math (Republicans need at least seven Democratic crossovers), the tight legislative calendar, and the key obstacle: ethics provisions related to President Trump's crypto earnings. The video also contrasts market predictions—Polymarket shows low odds of enactment while Kalshi shows high odds of a procedural vote—and argues that the market is mispricing the situation, as the vote will force senators to take public positions regardless of the outcome.

## Key Points

- Senate Majority Leader John Thune filed a cloture motion on the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, guaranteeing a Senate vote on September 15, 2026, contrary to claims that the bill was dead. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7rUiQ0vnQpI&t=0)
- The bill needs 60 votes for cloture; Republicans hold 53 seats, so at least seven Democratic crossovers are required, with a block of seven crypto-curious Democrats identified as potential supporters. [00:04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7rUiQ0vnQpI&t=255)
- The main obstacle is ethics language regarding President Trump's crypto earnings, with Democrats pushing for stronger restrictions and the White House not responding to a bipartisan compromise. [00:06:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7rUiQ0vnQpI&t=387)
- Market odds diverge: Polymarket shows ~20% chance of enactment, while Kalshi shows ~88% chance of a procedural vote, indicating the market is pricing different events and likely mispricing the situation. [00:09:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7rUiQ0vnQpI&t=574)
