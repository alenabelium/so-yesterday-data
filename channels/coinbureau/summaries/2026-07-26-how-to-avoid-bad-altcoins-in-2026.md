# How to Avoid BAD Altcoins in 2026

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau provides a comprehensive guide to identifying and avoiding scam altcoins in 2026. It highlights the prevalence of rug pulls and engineered tokens, emphasizing that most bad altcoins show predictable red flags. The presenter outlines specific checks—team anonymity, tokenomics, on-chain data, audits, and social media patterns—and contrasts them with green flags like real revenue and sane valuations. A practical five-step due diligence ritual is provided to help investors protect themselves.

## Key Points

- Most altcoins that wipe out investors are rigged from the start, with 99% of tokens on pump.fun built to extract value and 85% of 2025 tokens trading below launch price by February 2026. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nlti3UK2DwY&t=0)
- Red flags include anonymous teams, fake partnerships, low float/high FDV tokenomics, top-heavy holder concentration, and unaudited or forged audits. [00:02:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nlti3UK2DwY&t=159)
- Green flags include real revenue, sane valuation (FDV below 15x annual revenue), revenue-funded buybacks, verifiable users, and sustainable emissions. [00:10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nlti3UK2DwY&t=647)
- A practical due diligence ritual includes checking fundamentals, unlock schedules, contract scans, liquidity locks, and holder concentration, plus a three-flag rule to walk away. [00:12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nlti3UK2DwY&t=731)
