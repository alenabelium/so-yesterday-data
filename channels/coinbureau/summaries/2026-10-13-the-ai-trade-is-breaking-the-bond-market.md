# The AI Trade Is BREAKING The Bond Market

## Executive Summary

The video explains how the AI boom has driven major tech companies like Alphabet, Meta, Amazon, and Oracle to take on massive debt, straining the bond market. It details the shift from cash-funded AI development to borrowing, including innovative structures like SPVs and GPU-backed loans. The rising supply of corporate bonds, combined with high government debt, has pushed yields up and made borrowing more expensive, creating a fragile chain of dependencies. The video warns that this could lead to broader financial stress, affecting everything from tech valuations to crypto.

## Key Points

- AI companies have shifted from cash reserves to massive borrowing, with Alphabet's debt growing from $41B to $112B and Meta's from $49B to $112B in a year. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XP3PzPZ1Kto&t=0)
- Meta financed its Hyperion campus through a special purpose vehicle (SPV), issuing $27.3B in bonds at 6.58% due in 2049, keeping the debt off its balance sheet. [00:03:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XP3PzPZ1Kto&t=182)
- Hyperscale bond deals saw demand drop from 5x oversubscription in February to below 2x by July, and 78 of 91 bonds issued in 2026 were trading at higher yields by end of July. [00:06:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XP3PzPZ1Kto&t=398)
