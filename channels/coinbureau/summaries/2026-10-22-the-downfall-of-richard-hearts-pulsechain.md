# The Downfall of Richard Heart's Pulsechain

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau examines the arrest of Richard Heart (Richard Schuler) in Istanbul, detailing his rise through Hex and PulseChain, the SEC lawsuit that was dismissed on jurisdictional grounds, and the Finnish tax evasion charges that led to his Interpol red notice. It highlights how Heart's projects were built on his personal brand and marketing rather than technological innovation, leading to a massive collapse in token value and the ecosystem's dependence on his persona. The video concludes that PulseChain's failure underscores the risks of investing in projects centered on a single charismatic figure.

## Key Points

- Richard Heart was arrested in Istanbul on October 14, acting on an Interpol red notice from Finland, after years of evading Finnish tax fraud charges. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4MHh9XcG0&t=0)
- The SEC sued Heart in July 2023 for unregistered securities offerings, but the case was dismissed in February 2025 due to lack of US jurisdiction, leading to a victory lap from Heart. [00:05:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4MHh9XcG0&t=305)
- PulseChain, launched in May 2023 as an Ethereum fork, failed to gain traction, with PLS not listed on major exchanges and the ecosystem revolving around Heart's own PulseX DEX. [00:08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4MHh9XcG0&t=480)
