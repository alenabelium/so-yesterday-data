# Get Your Crypto Off Exchanges (Top 5 Wallets for 2027)

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau presents the top five crypto wallets for 2027, emphasizing the importance of self-custody to truly own your cryptocurrency. The ranking evaluates hardware and software wallets based on key protection, transaction clarity, network support, and daily usability. The list includes Tangem, Rabby, Trezor, Phantom, and Ledger, with Ledger taking the top spot due to its secure element and clear signing technology. The video also highlights recent major exchange hacks to underscore the risks of leaving funds on centralized platforms.

## Key Points

- Holding crypto on an exchange means you only own a promissory note, as seen with Mt. Gox customers still waiting for funds after a decade. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-JLU6Bictg&t=0)
- Tangem is the cheapest and most user-friendly hardware wallet, but its closed firmware and lack of a screen for independent verification place it fifth. [00:01:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-JLU6Bictg&t=72)
- Ledger ranks first because its secure element controls the screen directly, ensuring clear signing and preventing tampering, a feature that could have prevented the Bybit hack. [00:09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-JLU6Bictg&t=570)
