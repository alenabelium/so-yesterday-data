# Most Crypto Millionaires Lose it All. Here's Why.

## Executive Summary

This video from Coin Bureau explains why most crypto millionaires lose their fortunes, focusing on the psychological and strategic mistakes that lead to wealth destruction. It emphasizes the need for a repeatable investment process, avoiding over-concentration, and transitioning from aggressive growth to defensive wealth preservation as portfolios grow. The video also highlights the importance of inheritance planning and passing down financial wisdom to heirs, citing data showing that most family wealth is lost by the third generation.

## Key Points

- To build wealth in crypto, you need a repeatable process for finding projects with real users, demand that survives price drops, and mechanical value flow to the token, as exemplified by Hyperliquid and Zcash. [00:01:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_lq-zQEXA8&t=94)
- Every asset that has made people wealthy has gone through severe drawdowns, so you must hold conviction and avoid selling at the bottom; Bitcoin's drawdowns have been compressing across cycles, indicating a maturing asset. [00:05:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_lq-zQEXA8&t=301)
- Making money and keeping money are different games; you must take profits on the way up, size positions to avoid being wiped out, and eventually shift to a defensive strategy as your portfolio grows. [00:10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_lq-zQEXA8&t=631)
- Wealth preservation requires inheritance planning and passing down market wisdom; 70% of wealthy families lose their wealth by the second generation due to communication breakdowns and unprepared heirs. [00:12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_lq-zQEXA8&t=772)
