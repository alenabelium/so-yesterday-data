# Is Robinhood BEATING Ethereum?

## Executive Summary

Robinhood Chain, launched just two months prior, has rapidly become one of the highest-earning blockchain networks by leveraging Robinhood's massive user base and distribution. The chain hosts both a memecoin casino and tokenized real-world assets, including US stocks and ETFs, creating a unique hybrid that has generated billions in volume. While critics point to subsidies and memecoin-driven activity, the chain's 24/7 tokenized equity trading and infrastructure advantages suggest a potential shift in how retail finance interacts with blockchain. The video questions whether this success is durable or merely a product of incentives, ultimately framing it as a test of whether distribution can beat technology in crypto.

## Key Points

- Robinhood Chain generated $2.6 million in a single day, surpassing Ethereum and Hyperliquid in 24-hour app revenue, just two months after launch. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-4jN1C2B5M&t=0)
- The chain launched with 28 million funded customers and a pre-populated ecosystem, reaching 138 million transactions in its first 30 days, making it the fastest EVM chain to hit 100 million transactions. [00:01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-4jN1C2B5M&t=70)
- Robinhood Chain hosts both a memecoin casino (via the 'Pawns' launchpad) and tokenized equities like NVDA, with 60% of stock token volume occurring outside US market hours. [00:03:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-4jN1C2B5M&t=203)
- Critics argue the activity is subsidized, noting that 90% of the $2.6 million day came from just three apps, and tokenized assets fell from 33% to 6% of TVL by mid-August. [00:08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-4jN1C2B5M&t=481)
- The chain's infrastructure (Arbitrum Orbit, 100ms blocks, no gas auctions) and Robinhood's distribution advantage have made it the #1 network by unique RWA holders, with over 420,000. [00:09:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-4jN1C2B5M&t=569)
