# Did Anthropic Cause Bitcoin's CRASH?

## Executive Summary

The video investigates whether Anthropic's IPO caused Bitcoin's recent crash, noting that Bitcoin fell 18% while demand for Anthropic shares surged. It argues that forced liquidations of leveraged positions, not the IPO, were the primary driver of the selloff, as evidenced by the lack of a clear connection between IPO demand and crypto outflows. The analysis compares this to the SpaceX IPO, where Bitcoin also fell but later recovered, and suggests that monitoring Bitcoin ETF flows will reveal the true cause. The video concludes that the IPO may be a convenient excuse for a market already in decline, but the real test is whether outflows persist after the listing.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin fell 18% from 76K to 62K in six days, while Anthropic's IPO attracted orders 7 times the available shares, suggesting a possible capital rotation. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlfcCJ1GIzU&t=0)
- The crash was driven by $1.3 billion in forced liquidations of leveraged longs, not by investors selling Bitcoin to buy Anthropic shares, as no such selling has been documented. [00:02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlfcCJ1GIzU&t=120)
- Comparing to SpaceX's IPO, Bitcoin fell 19% before that listing but recovered within 100 days, showing that IPO-related outflows are often temporary and not a structural drain. [00:05:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlfcCJ1GIzU&t=343)
- The key test is whether Bitcoin ETF outflows continue after Anthropic's listing; if they reverse, the IPO was the cause, but if they persist, the market faces a deeper problem. [00:07:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlfcCJ1GIzU&t=453)
