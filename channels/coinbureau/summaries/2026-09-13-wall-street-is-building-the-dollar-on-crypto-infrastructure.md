# Wall Street Is BUILDING the Dollar on CRYPTO Infrastructure

## Executive Summary

Wall Street is not absorbing crypto; instead, it is moving the dollar onto crypto infrastructure, as evidenced by major institutions like BlackRock, Visa, and MasterCard running validator nodes on Circle's Arc blockchain. This shift is driven by the GENIUS Act, which creates a federal framework for stablecoins and sets a compliance deadline in January 2027. The move is part of a broader strategy to expand dollar dominance, with stablecoins backed by US Treasuries effectively financing the US government. Europe's defensive response, including a Euro stablecoin consortium and warnings about 'digital dollarization,' underscores the geopolitical stakes. Ultimately, the video argues that crypto is becoming the infrastructure for the world's reserve currency, but it is Washington that still owns the currency.

## Key Points

- Wall Street is not absorbing crypto; instead, it is moving the dollar onto crypto infrastructure, with BlackRock, ICE, DTCC, Visa, and MasterCard running validator nodes on Circle's Arc blockchain. [00:00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-dbnsXZnic&t=29)
- The GENIUS Act, signed in July 2025, creates a federal framework for stablecoins and sets a hard compliance deadline in January 2027, prompting banks and institutions to build their own stablecoin infrastructure. [00:02:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-dbnsXZnic&t=127)
- Stablecoins backed by US Treasuries are effectively financing the US government, with Tether being the 17th largest holder of US debt, and Washington is counting on this growth to lower long-term rates. [00:06:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-dbnsXZnic&t=378)
- Europe's defensive response, including a Euro stablecoin consortium and ECB President Christine Lagarde's warning about 'digital dollarization,' highlights the geopolitical stakes of this shift. [00:07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-dbnsXZnic&t=466)
