# OpenAI's Numbers Are WORSE Than Anyone Thought

## Executive Summary

The video reveals that OpenAI's widely circulated $70 billion annual revenue figure was inaccurate, with the actual figure closer to $50 billion, causing significant market sell-offs in AI infrastructure stocks. The discrepancy arose from differing revenue calculation methods between OpenAI and Anthropic, leading to inflated estimates. The core issue is OpenAI's massive planned spending of $856 billion on computing infrastructure by 2030, financed through heavy debt across partner companies like Oracle, CoreWeave, and SoftBank, creating a fragile ecosystem dependent on OpenAI's continued growth. The video argues that OpenAI's revenue growth is strong but insufficient to cover its enormous liabilities, posing systemic risks to the AI market.

## Key Points

- The FT reported OpenAI's actual annual revenue run rate was around $50 billion, not the $70 billion figure widely used by analysts, causing a market sell-off in AI infrastructure stocks like CoreWeave, Oracle, and SoftBank. [00:01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Niaus9v3Cg0&t=78)
- The $70 billion figure originated from investors adjusting OpenAI's net revenue to match Anthropic's gross revenue calculation method, inflating the number by about $20 billion. [00:04:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Niaus9v3Cg0&t=266)
- OpenAI's internal plan estimates $856 billion in computing and infrastructure costs by 2030, with cumulative negative free cash flow of $278 billion from 2026 to 2030, while projected 2026 revenue is only $36 billion. [00:06:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Niaus9v3Cg0&t=403)
- The massive infrastructure build is financed through private debt, with $466 billion in AI-related debt issued this year, and partners like Oracle, CoreWeave, and SoftBank are heavily leveraged, making their balance sheets effectively guarantees of OpenAI's promises. [00:09:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Niaus9v3Cg0&t=565)
- OpenAI is raising additional capital at a $1.25 trillion valuation, but its IPO is postponed to 2027, leaving public markets exposed only through its partners, creating a fragile ecosystem with no room for error. [00:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Niaus9v3Cg0&t=845)
