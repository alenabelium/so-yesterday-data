# Anthropic's $2 Trillion IPO is a TRAP (few see it)

## Executive Summary

Anthropic's planned $2 trillion IPO is scrutinized as a potential trap for public investors, revealing that its largest investors, Amazon and Alphabet, are also its primary cloud providers and marketplace distributors, creating a closed loop where money flows back to them. The company carries over $500 billion in non-cancellable computing commitments, while its revenue is volatile and concentrated among a few customers without long-term contracts. A corporate structure with Founder LLC retaining 50.1% voting control and a public benefit trust appointing the board ensures insiders keep control, while public shareholders get no meaningful say. The IPO is framed as a bet on future growth at a valuation that leaves no room for error, with risks that ordinary investors may be absorbing.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's valuation jumped from $965 billion to $2 trillion in five months, despite 2025 revenue of only $4.6 billion, implying a price-to-sales ratio of over 400 times last year's earnings. [00:01:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MXHSeYiZ6g&t=79)
- Amazon and Alphabet are both major investors and cloud providers, with Anthropic owing over $220 billion to them under take-or-pay contracts, meaning they profit from both investment gains and cloud bills. [00:02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MXHSeYiZ6g&t=167)
- Anthropic has $518 billion in computing commitments through 2036, 80% non-cancellable, while 83% of its revenue is usage-based and two customers alone provide about a quarter of 2025 revenue, creating a mismatch between fixed costs and flexible income. [00:08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MXHSeYiZ6g&t=484)
- The corporate structure ensures insiders retain control: Founder LLC holds 50.1% of votes, a public benefit trust appoints the board, and public shareholders receive shares without meaningful voting power. [00:10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MXHSeYiZ6g&t=652)
