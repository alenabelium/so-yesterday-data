# Strategy Got BOOTED From MSCI. Can Bitcoin Survive?

## Executive Summary

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) was removed from MSCI indices due to a rule classifying it as an investment fund rather than an operating business, forcing passive funds to sell billions in shares by November 30. The market reaction was muted because the exclusion was anticipated and largely priced in, but the removal undermines Strategy's ability to fund Bitcoin purchases through share issuance as its premium over Bitcoin has collapsed. Bitcoin itself is testing its 200-day moving average amid ETF outflows and cautious positioning, with the MSCI event adding indirect pressure rather than causing direct selling.

## Key Points

- MSCI removed Strategy, Meta Planet, and Upexi from its indices, citing that their assets are held rather than managed, which forces passive funds to sell billions in shares by November 30. [00:00:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTck1cfw3GY&t=59)
- The market barely reacted because the exclusion was expected after MSCI's October rule change, and Strategy's shares had already fallen ~14% in the two weeks prior. [00:02:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTck1cfw3GY&t=144)
- The MSCI removal does not force Strategy to sell Bitcoin, but it reduces demand for its shares, which weakens its ability to raise capital for Bitcoin purchases as the premium over Bitcoin has nearly vanished. [00:04:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTck1cfw3GY&t=271)
- Bitcoin is testing its 200-day moving average near $73,200, with ETF outflows of $1.28 billion in early November and low funding rates indicating cautious positioning. [00:09:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RTck1cfw3GY&t=554)
