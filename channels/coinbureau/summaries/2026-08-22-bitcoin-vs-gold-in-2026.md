# Bitcoin VS Gold in 2026

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the contrasting performance of Bitcoin and gold in 2026, highlighting gold's record central bank buying and Bitcoin's high correlation to tech stocks. It argues that Bitcoin's institutional adoption has turned it into a high-beta risk asset, losing its status as an uncorrelated hedge, while gold has benefited from sovereign diversification away from the dollar. Despite gold's long-term outperformance, the presenter suggests Bitcoin's higher volatility and smaller market cap could lead to explosive gains if the debasement trade rotates back.

## Key Points

- Gold's bull run is driven by record central bank purchases, with 289 tons bought in Q2 2026, and 83% of central banks citing diversification away from the US dollar as the reason. [00:00:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19SoGLC69zQ&t=54)
- Bitcoin's correlation to the NASDAQ 100 has risen to 0.62, with volatility at 52% annualized, making it behave like a high-beta tech stock rather than an uncorrelated hedge. [00:04:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19SoGLC69zQ&t=240)
- Gold has beaten Bitcoin over 3-year and 5-year windows, but Bitcoin has outperformed gold over the last 6 months, and the ratio is at 14.2 ounces of gold per Bitcoin, down from 35 at the cycle peak. [00:06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19SoGLC69zQ&t=408)
- Bitcoin's smaller market cap ($1.5T vs gold's $30T) and historical decoupling from equities suggest potential for explosive moves if the debasement trade rotates back. [00:10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19SoGLC69zQ&t=652)
