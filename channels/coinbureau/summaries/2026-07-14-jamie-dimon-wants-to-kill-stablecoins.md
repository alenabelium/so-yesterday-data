# Jamie Dimon Wants To Kill Stablecoins

## Executive Summary

Jamie Dimon is publicly attacking income-generating stablecoins, calling them a form of shadow banking, while JP Morgan simultaneously develops its own tokenized dollar (JPMD) on Coinbase's Base network. The video argues that Dimon's real motive is protecting banks' profitable deposit margins, not systemic risk, as the White House analysis shows negligible impact on bank lending. The Clarity Act, which would regulate stablecoin interest, faces hurdles, but industry initiatives like OpenUSD are pushing forward regardless, signaling a fundamental shift in how digital dollars may earn yield.

## Key Points

- Dimon calls Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 'complete crap' over stablecoin interest, framing it as a war for trillions in bank deposits. [00:00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ko-KXICQHs&t=1)
- Banks earn ~4% on deposits while paying near-zero interest, making income-paying stablecoins a direct competitive threat to their margins. [00:00:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ko-KXICQHs&t=46)
- JP Morgan's own tokenized dollar (JPMD) launched on Coinbase's Base network, exposing hypocrisy in Dimon's lobbying against stablecoins. [00:06:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ko-KXICQHs&t=405)
- The Clarity Act faces obstacles including stablecoin interest provisions, with a critical Senate deadline of August 7. [00:08:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ko-KXICQHs&t=484)
- OpenUSD, a consortium including Visa, Mastercard, and Coinbase, aims to share stablecoin revenue, potentially bypassing regulatory battles. [00:10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ko-KXICQHs&t=611)
