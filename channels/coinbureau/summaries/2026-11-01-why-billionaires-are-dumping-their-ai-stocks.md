# Why Billionaires Are DUMPING Their AI Stocks

## Executive Summary

The video investigates why insiders at major AI companies like Nvidia, Broadcom, Arista, and CoreWeave have sold over $2 billion in stock since June, suggesting they may be cashing out at peak valuations. It explains the mechanics of insider selling, including 10b5-1 plans and trading windows, and highlights that these sales were planned months in advance. The analysis contrasts the heavy selling with a near-total absence of insider buying, implying a lack of confidence in continued stock appreciation. The video presents both bearish and neutral interpretations but leans toward the view that insiders are capitalizing on AI hype.

## Key Points

- Insiders at Nvidia, Broadcom, Arista, and CoreWeave have sold over $2 billion in stock since June, with Nvidia's CEO alone selling $1.35 billion. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYSykNkq4lM&t=0)
- Most insider selling occurs through pre-arranged 10b5-1 plans, which require a 90-day waiting period and are set up months in advance, indicating these sales were planned before recent price movements. [00:06:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYSykNkq4lM&t=395)
- Insiders sold at prices significantly higher than current trading levels, and there has been almost no insider buying in the past year, with only one purchase across all four companies. [00:11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYSykNkq4lM&t=664)
