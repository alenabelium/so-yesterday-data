# What Happens If The Democrats Win The Midterms?

## Executive Summary

This video analyzes the potential consequences if Democrats win the 2026 US midterm elections, focusing on the powers they would gain in the House and Senate. It explains how a Democratic House majority would enable aggressive oversight, subpoenas, and budget control, potentially leading to investigations and even a third impeachment of President Trump. The Senate majority would allow Democrats to block Trump's appointments, further hampering his agenda. The video concludes that a Democratic Congress could turn Trump's final two years into a political nightmare, with investigations and legislative gridlock.

## Key Points

- Democrats gaining control of the House would allow them to chair committees, issue subpoenas, and launch investigations into Trump's administration, family, and associates, as seen in 2019 when they subpoenaed Trump's tax returns and financial records.
