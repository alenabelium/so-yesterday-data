# Zcash Just Proved AI Is NO MATCH For Private Money

## Executive Summary

In May 2026, an AI auditing harness found a critical cryptographic bug in Zcash's Orchard shielded transactions, which had been live for four years. Despite a 50% price collapse, Zcash recovered to new all-time highs due to its built-in turnstile safeguard, rapid emergency patches, and the permanent retirement of the vulnerable pool via the Ironwood upgrade. The incident highlights AI's dual role as both a threat to cryptographic systems and a driver for the growing necessity of privacy-focused cryptocurrencies. Zcash's unique design, including viewing keys and a clean supply schedule, positions it as a resilient asset in an era of AI-powered surveillance.

- An AI auditing harness found a zero-knowledge proof vulnerability in Zcash's Orchard shielded transactions, allowing counterfeit ZEC creation, but the network survived due to its turnstile design and rapid response. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGpS2jk2EkQ&t=0)

- AI is shifting financial privacy from obscurity to cryptographic necessity, making Zcash's privacy features more relevant than ever and contributing to its new all-time high. [00:11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGpS2jk2EkQ&t=665)
