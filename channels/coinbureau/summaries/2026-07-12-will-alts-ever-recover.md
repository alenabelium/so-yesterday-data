# Will Alts EVER Recover?

## Executive Summary

The video argues that the traditional altcoin season model, where altcoins rise in tandem with Bitcoin, is broken due to structural changes like Bitcoin dominance staying above 55%, Ethereum's value being cannibalized by Layer 2 networks, and capital concentrating in Bitcoin ETFs and top altcoins. It emphasizes that most altcoins will not recover, and future gains will be selective, favoring projects with real users, revenue, and utility. The video provides a scoreboard of indicators—Bitcoin dominance, Fed policy, regulation, and ETH/BTC ratio—to track whether a genuine rotation is occurring.

## Key Points

- Bitcoin dominance has stayed between 56-63% all year, never dropping below the 55% threshold needed for altcoin season, and 84% of altcoins are below their 200-day moving average. [00:02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA6plwqRV1c&t=148)
- Ethereum's value has been cannibalized by Layer 2 networks, with fee revenue down 95% from 2021 peaks and the ETH/BTC ratio at multi-year lows, breaking the traditional capital flow into altcoins. [00:04:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA6plwqRV1c&t=263)
- Capital is now concentrated in Bitcoin ETFs and top altcoins, with the top 10 altcoins holding 80.5% of the market cap excluding Bitcoin, and over 70 crypto projects have shut down in the first half of the year. [00:06:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA6plwqRV1c&t=401)
- Surviving sectors like RWA, DeFi, and AI crypto are showing growth, but the next recovery will be selective and based on fundamentals, not blind momentum. [00:07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA6plwqRV1c&t=472)
- Track Bitcoin dominance below 55%, Fed rate cuts, the Clarity Act's passage, and the ETH/BTC ratio as key indicators for a real altcoin rotation. [00:09:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA6plwqRV1c&t=576)
