# The Clarity Act is DEAD

## Executive Summary

The Clarity Act, a major crypto market structure bill, failed in the Senate due to a cloture vote that blocked debate, marking a significant political defeat for the crypto industry despite massive lobbying spending. The bill's failure was driven by partisan conflict over President Trump's crypto holdings and perceived conflicts of interest, with Democrats rejecting a last-minute ethics package as insufficient. With Congress unable to pass legislation, crypto regulation is now being shaped by the SEC and CFTC through rulemaking, which is less durable than a law. Despite the setback, the crypto market continues to grow, with stablecoins, ETFs, and tokenization expanding without a comprehensive legal framework.

## Key Points

- The Senate refused to debate the Clarity Act, failing a cloture vote 49-50, effectively killing the bill after years of lobbying and a $193 million war chest. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwrG4fV4dmw&t=0)
- The bill became a partisan issue due to President Trump's crypto portfolio, with Democrats rejecting a last-minute ethics package as a 'weak fig leaf' that wouldn't address conflicts of interest. [00:03:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwrG4fV4dmw&t=230)
- With Congress walking away, the SEC and CFTC are now writing crypto rules through regulation, but these are less durable than a law and can be reversed by future administrations. [00:08:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwrG4fV4dmw&t=496)
