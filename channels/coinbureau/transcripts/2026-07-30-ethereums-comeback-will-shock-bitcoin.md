In particular, 10 EIPs are planned, of which two are the most exciting. The first is EIP-7732, which implements the Enforced Separation of Proponents and Builders, or EPBS for short. In short, EPBS is designed to make the block creation process in Ethereum more decentralized and transparent. Many validators now rely on external services called relays to connect to specialized block builders that optimize transactions and capture MEV. EPBS integrates this process directly into the Ethereum protocol, eliminating the need for these intermediaries and making block creation more resistant to censorship and centralization. This creates a more streamlined system where validators and builders do their work and Ethereum itself handles data transfer and payments. The second major piece of news is EIP-7928, which introduces block- level access lists , or BALLs for short . There are a lot of abbreviations here, I know. In any case, BALLs will make transaction execution much more efficient.

**[00:13:44]** You see, today Ethereum executes transactions one by one because it doesn't know in advance what data is needed for each one . BALLs solve this by pre- mapping the accounts and data needed for each block, allowing multiple transactions to be processed in parallel. Think of it as going from a single-lane road to a multi-lane highway. So, more cars can reach their destination at the same time, and rush hour congestion is reduced. By increasing the amount of data each block can process and updating the way gas fees are calculated, the update will significantly reduce the cost of using Ethereum's first layer.
