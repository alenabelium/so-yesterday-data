# Fable 5 vs GPT 5.6 Sol: The Early Results

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the early competitive landscape between Anthropic's Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Sol, highlighting that while Fable 5 shows slightly better raw performance, GPT 5.6 Sol offers superior performance per dollar. The discussion also covers the US government's involvement in model access, including OpenAI's proposal to give the government a stake, and the broader implications for corporate power concentration and AI security. The video concludes with reflections on the shifting balance of power in AI, from open-source models to large US labs.

## Key Points

- Fable 5's release was delayed due to a US government security review, with Anthropic citing an Amazon-flagged vulnerability and an improved security classifier that may flag routine coding tasks more often. [00:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=67)
- GPT 5.6 Sol is priced at half the API cost of Fable 5, but early benchmarks show it performs slightly worse overall, though it offers better performance per dollar. [00:09:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=549)
- OpenAI is offering the US government a 5% stake in the company, which could incentivize faster general release and exacerbate corporate power concentration. [00:07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=450)
