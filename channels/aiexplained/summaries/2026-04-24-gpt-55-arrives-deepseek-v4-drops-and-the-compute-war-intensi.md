# GPT 5.5 Arrives, DeepSeek V4 Drops, and the Compute War Intensifies

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the competitive landscape following the release of GPT-5.5 and DeepSeek V4, highlighting OpenAI's strategic push to maintain market dominance against rivals like Anthropic and Mythos. It details how GPT-5.5 excels in cost-efficiency and specific coding benchmarks but faces scrutiny over hallucination rates and domain-specific limitations compared to specialized models. The analysis concludes that the industry is entering an era of compute scarcity, where performance-per-dollar and niche specialization are becoming more critical than raw general intelligence.

## Key Points

- GPT-5.5 demonstrates superior cost-efficiency and leads in agentic coding benchmarks like SWE-bench Pro, though it underperforms Mythos on general knowledge tasks and exhibits higher hallucination rates (86%) compared to Opus 4.7 (36%).
- DeepSeek V4 Pro emerges as a high-performance, low-cost alternative with a 1-million-token context window, significantly outperforming GPT-5.2 in reasoning while costing roughly one-tenth of the price.
- OpenAI executives highlight their compute advantage over Anthropic, but internal admissions confirm the industry is facing severe compute scarcity, shifting focus toward specialized domain performance rather than universal generalization.
- The integration of GPT Image 2 within ChatGPT enables complex 'vibe coding' workflows, allowing users to generate interactive adventure games with audio and visuals in under 24 hours, showcasing the potential for automated creative production.
