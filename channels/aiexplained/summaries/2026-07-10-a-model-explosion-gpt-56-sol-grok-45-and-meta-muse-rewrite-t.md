# A Model Explosion: GPT 5.6 Sol, Grok 4.5 and Meta Muse Rewrite the Rules

## Executive Summary

The video analyzes the recent release of GPT 5.6 Sol, Grok 4.5, and Meta Muse Spark, arguing that cost efficiency is becoming as important as raw performance. It highlights benchmarks where OpenAI's models are competitive but cheaper, while also noting security vulnerabilities and the potential for smaller, cheaper models to disrupt the market. The video concludes that AI model development is still in its early stages, with significant room for growth in model size and capability.

## Key Points

- OpenAI's GPT 5.6 Sol offers comparable performance to Anthropic's Claude models at about a third of the cost, as shown in benchmarks like Agents Last Exam and Artificial Analysis. [00:01:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWlCituW7wo&t=78)
- Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 and GLM 5.2 achieve near-leading performance at a fraction of the cost, challenging OpenAI's value proposition. [00:08:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWlCituW7wo&t=517)
- The British AI Security Institute found that GPT 5.6 Sol is easier to jailbreak than Fable, raising safety concerns. [00:14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWlCituW7wo&t=850)
