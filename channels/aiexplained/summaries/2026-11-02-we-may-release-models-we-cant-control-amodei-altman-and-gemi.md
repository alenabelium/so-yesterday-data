# ‘We may release models we can’t control’: Amodei, Altman … and Gemini Carbon

## Executive Summary

The video discusses recent statements and actions from AI lab leaders, including Anthropic's Dario Amodei and OpenAI's Sam Altman, regarding the potential release of AI models that may be uncontrollable. It highlights Anthropic's internal survey showing staff optimism about AI capabilities, critiques of Amodei's rationalizations, and OpenAI's approach to sandboxing and monitoring. The video also covers Google's Gemini 4 Carbon release and concludes with a mental model explaining why AI models are becoming increasingly difficult to understand and predict.

- Anthropic staff survey shows 91% believe AI will achieve Nobel-level discoveries by 2030, yet they think AI will only handle 75% of their own work. [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Kw6_DTZPrY&t=0)

- Google's Gemini 4 Carbon re-enters the frontier, prompting an apology from Semi Analysis, and the video ends with a mental model explaining why AI models are becoming increasingly opaque and unpredictable. [00:18:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Kw6_DTZPrY&t=1122)
