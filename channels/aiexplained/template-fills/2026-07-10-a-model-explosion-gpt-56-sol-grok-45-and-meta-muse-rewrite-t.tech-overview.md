## Wider context

This week's releases mark a shift from raw benchmark supremacy to cost-efficiency as a primary differentiator. OpenAI's pricing pressure is being undercut by cheaper models from Meta, xAI, and Chinese labs, forcing a reevaluation of value. Security vulnerabilities in GPT 5.6 Sol also highlight the tension between rapid release cycles and safety, a theme likely to dominate future discourse.
