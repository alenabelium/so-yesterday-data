## Headline takeaway

Google pivots to consumer search via Gemini Omni and Flash, while OpenAI doubles down on text reasoning, exposing LLMs' fundamental inability to process negation.

## Wider context

The industry is splitting on AGI pathways: Google bets on [[world-model]] simulation via multimodal generation, while OpenAI/Anthropic prioritize [[recursive-self-improvement]] in text.
