## Wider context

Google and OpenAI are diverging on AGI pathways: Google bets on [[world-model]]s via Gemini Omni to simulate physics, while OpenAI sticks to text reasoning. However, both face the same fundamental flaw: [[jagged-intelligence]]. Independent research shows LLMs fail to process negation in synthetic data, a structural issue DeepMind researchers warn cannot be easily patched, challenging the notion that recursive self-improvement will quickly resolve these epistemic gaps.
