## Wider context

The AI industry is fracturing along ethical lines rather than just technical ones. While OpenAI accelerates capability with GPT-5.4's autonomous coding and white-collar automation, it has alienated safety-focused rivals like Anthropic by accepting Pentagon contracts that enable autonomous targeting. This signals a shift where 'safety theater' becomes a competitive differentiator for some vendors while others prioritize speed and government alignment, leaving professionals to navigate a landscape where technical parity is matched by geopolitical divergence.
