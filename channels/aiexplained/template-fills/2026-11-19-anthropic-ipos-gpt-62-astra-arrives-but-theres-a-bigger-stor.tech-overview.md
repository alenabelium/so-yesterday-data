## Wider context

The week's releases underscore that model performance is data-dependent, not a single IQ score. More critically, the race toward recursive self-improvement is accelerating, but safety oversight is degrading: chain-of-thought monitoring is becoming less reliable, and labs like OpenAI are more transparent than Anthropic about safety research allocation. This suggests the industry's 'race to the top' narrative may be more rhetoric than reality.
