## Headline takeaway

Fable 5 edges GPT 5.6 Sol on raw benchmarks, but Sol's half-price API makes it the performance-per-dollar winner.

## Tools covered

### Fable 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=1)
- **Why It Matters**: Anthropic's flagship model, briefly blocked due to a US government security review, is back. Early tests show it slightly outperforms GPT 5.6 Sol on several benchmarks, but at twice the API cost.
- **Sota Comparison**: Slightly better than GPT 5.6 Sol on Healthbench and Exploit Bench, but trails on Terminal Bench 2.1.
- **Sota Band**: beats
- **Access Constraint**: Limited availability

### GPT 5.6 Sol
- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=190)
- **Why It Matters**: OpenAI's counter to Fable, priced at half the API cost. Early benchmarks show near-parity with Fable, making it the best performance-per-dollar option.
- **Sota Comparison**: Near parity with Fable on most benchmarks, slightly better on Terminal Bench 2.1.
- **Sota Band**: parity
- **Access Constraint**: Limited preview for trusted partners

### Claude Sonnet 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=1)
- **Why It Matters**: Rushed release to show the US government isn't interfering. Underperforms Opus and Mythos class models, but shows strong resistance to prompt injection attacks.
- **Sota Comparison**: Inferior to Opus and Mythos class models, but significantly better at resisting prompt injection.
- **Sota Band**: behind
- **Access Constraint**: Available

### Mythos 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [09:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=549)
- **Why It Matters**: The base model behind Fable, used for comparison. Shows strong performance but at a higher cost.
- **Sota Comparison**: Slightly better than GPT 5.6 Sol on several benchmarks.
- **Sota Band**: beats
- **Access Constraint**: Available



## Wider context

The week's releases signal a shift from raw capability to access and economics. OpenAI's government stake proposal and phased releases could concentrate power among large corporations, while Anthropic's accusation against Alibaba highlights the threat of model distillation. The balance of power in AI is increasingly determined by who gets early access, not just who builds the best model.
