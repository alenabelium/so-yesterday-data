- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=190)

## Wider context

The week's releases signal a shift from raw capability to cost efficiency and controlled access. OpenAI's government stake proposal and phased releases risk concentrating power, while Anthropic's security tightening reflects geopolitical pressures. The distillation attack on Claude by Alibaba could push labs toward even more restricted model access, reshaping the competitive landscape.
