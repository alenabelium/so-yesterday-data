## Headline takeaway

Fable 5 edges GPT 5.6 Sol on raw benchmarks, but Sol's half-price API makes it the performance-per-dollar winner.

- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: reasoning

## Wider context

The week's releases highlight a power shift: OpenAI courts the US government with a stake offer, while Anthropic warns of distillation attacks from China. The trend toward phased, gated access could concentrate AI power in a few US labs, echoing the paper's argument that compute-rich models will always win on rare tasks.
