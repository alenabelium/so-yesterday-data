## Headline takeaway

Fable 5 and GPT 5.6 Sol ship with early benchmarks showing near-parity, but Sol's half-price API makes it the performance-per-dollar winner.

## Tools covered

### Fable 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=1)
- **Why It Matters**: Anthropic's latest frontier model, previously blocked by a US government security review, is now publicly available. Early tests show it slightly outperforms GPT 5.6 Sol on raw capability, but at twice the API cost.
- **Sota Comparison**: Slightly better than GPT 5.6 Sol on most benchmarks, but significantly more expensive.
- **Sota Band**: beats
- **Access Constraint**: Public API; blocked for some queries

### GPT 5.6 Sol
- **Vendor**: OpenAI
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=190)
- **Why It Matters**: OpenAI's counter to Fable 5, released in a limited preview for trusted partners. It offers near-parity performance at half the API price, making it a strong value proposition.
- **Sota Comparison**: Roughly on par with Fable 5 on most benchmarks, but with a clear edge in performance per dollar.
- **Sota Band**: parity
- **Access Constraint**: Limited preview; general release expected in weeks

### Claude Sonnet 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [00:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=1)
- **Why It Matters**: Anthropic's mid-tier model, released quietly. It underperforms Opus and Mythos class models, but shows a dramatic improvement in resistance to prompt injection attacks.
- **Sota Comparison**: Inferior to Opus and Mythos class models, but with a much lower success rate for operational injections (under 1% vs 30% for Mythos 5).
- **Sota Band**: behind
- **Access Constraint**: API; pricing returns in September

### Mythos 5
- **Vendor**: Anthropic
- **Category**: reasoning
- **Timestamp**: [09:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y24lF1q4SFY&t=549)
- **Why It Matters**: Anthropic's previous flagship model, used as a comparison point for Fable 5. It scores slightly higher than GPT 5.6 Sol on several benchmarks, but at a higher cost.
- **Sota Comparison**: Slightly better than GPT 5.6 Sol on most benchmarks, but more expensive.
- **Sota Band**: beats
- **Access Constraint**: Public API



## Wider context

The week's releases highlight a strategic shift: OpenAI's pricing undercuts Anthropic, while the US government's involvement in model access raises concerns about corporate power concentration. The distillation attack on Claude by Alibaba suggests a future where labs may gate their best models to prevent capability transfer. Meanwhile, research from Stanford and MIT argues that larger models will always win, favoring the biggest US labs.
