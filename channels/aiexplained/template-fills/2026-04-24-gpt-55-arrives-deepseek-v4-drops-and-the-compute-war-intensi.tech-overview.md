## Wider context

The AI industry is shifting from a race for raw general intelligence to a battle over [[compute-efficiency]] and domain specialization. With major labs facing [[compute-scarcity]], models like DeepSeek V4 prove that open-weight architectures can compete on cost while GPT-5.5 demonstrates that performance-per-dollar is becoming the critical metric for enterprise adoption. This era favors niche optimization over universal generalization.
