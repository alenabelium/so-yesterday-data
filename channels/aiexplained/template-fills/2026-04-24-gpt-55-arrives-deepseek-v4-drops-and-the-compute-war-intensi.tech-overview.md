## Wider context

The industry is shifting from a race for raw general intelligence to an era of [[compute-scarcity]] where performance-per-dollar and domain specialization dictate value. GPT-5.5's success in cost-efficient coding and DeepSeek V4's open-weight efficiency highlight that niche reinforcement learning environments now trump broad generalization, as labs face physical infrastructure limits.
