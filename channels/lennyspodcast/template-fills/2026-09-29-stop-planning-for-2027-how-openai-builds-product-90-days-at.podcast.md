## Key arguments

### 90-day planning horizon is essential
- **Timestamp**: [19:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ciSTkEVy30&t=1185)
- **Summary**: Tara argues that living two to three months in the future is the ideal planning horizon. Predictions years out are almost always wrong, but building for today means getting left behind. This matches the rapid pace of model capabilities and allows for empirical iteration.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Ship imperfect products to learn fast
- **Timestamp**: [00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ciSTkEVy30&t=55)
- **Summary**: Tara explains that urgency and empirical evidence from real users trump a priori perfection. Shipping an imperfect toggle to get agentic capabilities into the hands of over a billion users was the right call, and iteration from user feedback is the path to the right product.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### User absorption is the real constraint
- **Timestamp**: [04:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ciSTkEVy30&t=281)
- **Summary**: Nan argues that the biggest constraint is not model capability but users' ability to absorb changes. Even with capability overhang, if people can't understand or adopt the new features, the product fails. This shapes how they design and pace releases.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Enterprises must be pushed to adopt frontier changes
- **Timestamp**: [06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ciSTkEVy30&t=371)
- **Summary**: Tara acknowledges enterprises struggle with the pace of change but argues that not shipping frontier capabilities leads to being leapfrogged. They had to break existing processes to deliver agents, because waiting would mean losing customers to alternatives.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### Direct user relationships are table stakes
- **Timestamp**: [24:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ciSTkEVy30&t=1465)
- **Summary**: Both Tara and Nan emphasize the importance of being a DMable PM, especially for consumer products. Direct feedback, even about subtle issues like an AI's attitude, is crucial for improving agentic experiences and informing research.
