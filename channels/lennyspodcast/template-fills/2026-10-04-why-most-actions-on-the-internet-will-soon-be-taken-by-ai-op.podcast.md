## Key arguments

### Agent traffic will dominate the internet
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=0)
- **Summary**: Most internet actions will be performed by agents, so products must be built for scale and agent traffic. This requires designing for agent users, not just human users, and expecting a massive influx of automated interactions.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### The future is a unified 'Dots' assistant
- **Timestamp**: [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=236)
- **Summary**: ChatGPT and Codex will merge into a single 'Dots' assistant that works across all devices and channels, eliminating model choices and settings. The goal is a constant, active intelligence that knows your goals and preferences, accessible anywhere.

### Open ecosystem is the hidden hit
- **Timestamp**: [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=767)
- **Summary**: OpenAI is deeply committed to an open plugin ecosystem, with revenue sharing for popular plugins. This will be a major driver of growth and innovation, as plugins become discoverable and recommended based on quality and retention.

### Human taste and creativity remain irreplaceable
- **Timestamp**: [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=968)
- **Summary**: While AI takes over many tasks, human taste, creativity, and the desire to connect with other humans remain deeply valuable. The technology is designed as an extension of the person, amplifying their will and taste, not replacing it.

### Safety is a top investment priority
- **Timestamp**: [34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=2070)
- **Summary**: OpenAI invests heavily in model consistency, security, and safeguards, including secondary monitoring systems. They are committed to releasing only safe and reliable models, especially given the scale of their user base.
