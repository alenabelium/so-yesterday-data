## Key arguments

### Agents will dominate internet actions
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=0)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that most internet actions will soon be performed by AI agents, not humans. Products must be built with agent traffic in mind, expecting massive scale and designing for agent usability, not just human interfaces.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Agent teams expand and contract with model breakthroughs
- **Timestamp**: [02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=151)
- **Summary**: Tibo describes a cycle: as models improve, one powerful agent can replace a team of specialized agents, leading to contraction. But as tasks grow, teams expand again. The key is building systems that learn and adapt without manual tweaking.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Dots unify ChatGPT and Codex into a seamless assistant
- **Timestamp**: [07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=468)
- **Summary**: Tibo explains the convergence of ChatGPT and Codex into 'Dots', a unified assistant that eliminates model choice and settings. Users interact naturally across channels, and the assistant learns preferences and works 24/7, accessible from any device.

### Open ecosystem and plugin economy are the hidden hit
- **Timestamp**: [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=767)
- **Summary**: Tibo highlights the importance of an open plugin ecosystem, with revenue sharing for popular plugins. Quality and retention drive visibility, not SEO. This ecosystem will be crucial for distributing AI capabilities to billions of users.

### Human taste and creativity remain invaluable
- **Timestamp**: [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=968)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that human taste, creativity, and connection remain central. AI is an extension of human will, and while technical skills may shift, the ability to envision and curate will become more important. Founders and product thinkers thrive.
