## Key arguments

### Most internet actions will be agent-driven
- **Timestamp**: [31:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=1895)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that the majority of internet actions will be performed by AI agents, not humans. Products must be built with agent-scale in mind, expecting massive traffic and designing for agent usability, not just human interfaces.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### The future is a unified 'Dots' assistant
- **Timestamp**: [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=236)
- **Summary**: Tibo explains that the future is a single, always-available intelligence accessible through any client, eliminating the need to choose between Codex, ChatGPT, or models. This 'Dots' assistant learns from feedback and works 24/7, reducing complexity and setup fatigue.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### Open ecosystem and plugin economy
- **Timestamp**: [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=767)
- **Summary**: Tibo highlights the importance of an open plugin ecosystem, where partners like Notion and Figma can benefit from ChatGPT's distribution. He emphasizes a sharing economy where popular plugins earn revenue, and quality is determined by retention and success, not SEO.

### Human taste and creativity remain key
- **Timestamp**: [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=968)
- **Summary**: Tibo believes that as AI takes over more tasks, human taste, creativity, and the ability to connect with audiences become more valuable. He notes that many former founders and YC alumni thrive at OpenAI, and that product managers are well-positioned for this shift.

### Safety and security are top priorities
- **Timestamp**: [34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=2070)
- **Summary**: Tibo discusses OpenAI's heavy investment in safety, including secondary monitoring systems and safeguards. He emphasizes that with 1.2 billion users, they cannot afford to make mistakes, and they are proud of decisions like not releasing Astra 6.1 to ensure safety.
