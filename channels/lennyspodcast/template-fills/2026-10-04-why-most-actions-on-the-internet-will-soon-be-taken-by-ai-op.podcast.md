## Key arguments

### Agents will dominate internet actions
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=0)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that most internet actions will be performed by AI agents, and products must be built with scale and agent traffic in mind. He emphasizes that if you imagine the technology being ten times better in a year, you would build completely differently.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### The evolution of agent teams
- **Timestamp**: [02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=151)
- **Summary**: Tibo describes a cycle of expansion and contraction in agent teams: as models improve, one big agent can replace many specialized ones, leading to constant restructuring. He believes the future is a system that learns from feedback rather than manually tweaked loops.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Dots as the unified assistant
- **Timestamp**: [03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=236)
- **Summary**: Tibo explains that 'Dots' will become the primary interface to AI, merging ChatGPT and Codex into a single assistant that works across any device, accessible via voice, text, or in meetings. The goal is to eliminate technology friction and have AI work for you.

### Open ecosystem and plugin economy
- **Timestamp**: [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=767)
- **Summary**: Tibo highlights the importance of an open plugin ecosystem, with revenue sharing for popular plugins. He cites partnerships with Notion and Figma, and the growth of MCP, as key to enabling agents to perform real work and driving traffic.

### Human taste and creativity remain valuable
- **Timestamp**: [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=968)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that human taste, creativity, and connection remain deeply valuable. He sees AI as an extension of human will, and believes that even as roles shift, the ability to understand users and create quality products will be more important than ever.
