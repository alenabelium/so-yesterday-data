## Key arguments

### Agents will dominate internet actions
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=0)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that most internet actions will soon be performed by AI agents, so products must be built for scale and agent traffic. He emphasizes that if you imagine your product being ten times better in a year, you'd build it completely differently.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Shift from coding to orchestrating agent teams
- **Timestamp**: [02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=151)
- **Summary**: Tibo describes his workflow of building larger and larger teams of agents, then cutting them down when model breakthroughs allow one agent to do everything. He notes that tweaking loops is not the path to success; instead, you want a system that learns from feedback.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Convergence of ChatGPT and Codex into 'Dots'
- **Timestamp**: [07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=468)
- **Summary**: Tibo explains that ChatGPT and Codex will merge into a unified assistant called 'Dots', which will be accessible through any client or screen, eliminating the need to choose models or settings. The goal is to create a constant, active intelligence that knows everything it needs to do.

### Open plugin ecosystem as a hidden hit
- **Timestamp**: [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=767)
- **Summary**: Tibo highlights the importance of an open ecosystem for plugins, with revenue sharing for popular plugins. He believes this will be a major driver of growth, allowing developers to reach 1.2 billion users and benefit from distribution.

### Human taste and creativity remain valuable
- **Timestamp**: [17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=1029)
- **Summary**: Tibo argues that as AI takes over more tasks, human taste, creativity, and the ability to connect with an audience become even more valuable. He sees AI as an extension of the person, amplifying their will and taste, and believes there will always be a deeply human element in creation.
