## Key arguments

### The LOCKS Framework for CEO Evaluation
- **Timestamp**: [17:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UyitfSbY6c&t=1037)
- **Summary**: Halligan evaluates CEOs using the LOCKS algorithm: Lovable (inspiring followership), Obsessed (deep founder-market fit), Chip on shoulder (dissatisfaction with status quo), Knowledgeable (domain expertise), and Student (constant learning). He notes a new breed of 'five-tool' CEOs who can code, design, sell, recruit, and set vision, a trait rare in previous generations.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0, 1]

### Hiring Strategy: Homegrown vs. External Hires
- **Timestamp**: [06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UyitfSbY6c&t=371)
- **Summary**: Successful scaling CEOs focus heavily on building their executive team. Halligan advises against hiring 'been there, done that' executives from large tech firms due to impedance mismatch with startup realities. Instead, he recommends the '2004 Red Sox' model: mixing homegrown talent with a few key external free agents, and using blind references to assess fit.

### AI's Impact on Go-to-Market and Sales
- **Timestamp**: [28:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UyitfSbY6c&t=1680)
- **Summary**: While enterprise sales processes remain largely unchanged, the top of the funnel will shift. Buyers will use AI agents to research products before contacting humans. Halligan predicts that websites will feature high-quality avatars to handle initial conversations, which are then handed off to sales reps accompanied by their own AI agents.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### CEO Mindset: Nibbling vs. Eating the Sandwich
- **Timestamp**: [38:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UyitfSbY6c&t=2295)
- **Summary**: Halligan shares 'hagganisms' for leadership, including 'when you have to eat a sandwich, don't nibble,' urging decisive action during crises like layoffs. He also emphasizes the DRRI (Directly Responsible Individual) principle to avoid diffusion of responsibility and solving for Enterprise Value over Team Value.

### The Reality of Founder Life and Scaling
- **Timestamp**: [47:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UyitfSbY6c&t=2843)
- **Summary**: Founding is 'full contact' and requires immense obsession, often sacrificing work-life balance. Scaling requires shifting from 90% perspiration to 90% inspiration, letting go of control, and being repetitive in communication. Halligan notes that companies are more likely to die of 'overeating' (doing too much) than indigestion.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]
