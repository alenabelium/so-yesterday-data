## Key arguments

### Frontier model development is unpredictable, requiring PMs to feel the 'vibe'
- **Timestamp**: [03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=195)
- **Summary**: Tulsi explains that unlike traditional products, models cannot be guaranteed to meet goals. PMs must set a vision but also observe the outcome, find where the model surprises, and turn that into user value. It's a blend of science and intuition, like a Potions lesson.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Google's strategy spans the full stack, aiming to lead at the frontier
- **Timestamp**: [07:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=454)
- **Summary**: Tulsi argues Google's strength is covering hardware to end products, serving billions of users. They aim to lead in cutting-edge models (e.g., Gemini 4) while also building models along a cost-quality Pareto curve for diverse use cases, from search to cybersecurity.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### AI safety is built-in through monitoring and collaboration
- **Timestamp**: [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=636)
- **Summary**: Tulsi highlights that safety is integrated from training to deployment, with monitoring to track model reasoning and intervene. She also emphasizes AI's role in strengthening systems, citing partnerships with cybersecurity experts like Wiz to detect and fix vulnerabilities.

### The 60-20-20 rule: build for today while preparing for tomorrow's model
- **Timestamp**: [33:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=1993)
- **Summary**: Tulsi advises building features where 60% works now, 20% works well enough with potential, and 20% shows glimmers of future magic. This balances shipping a functional product with setting up for magical experiences as models improve.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [3]

### Product management renaissance: taste and consensus become key
- **Timestamp**: [19:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=1196)
- **Summary**: Tulsi agrees that with easy building, the unique value is deciding what's worth building and uniting people around a vision. She emphasizes hiring PMs who understand model 'vibe', are adaptable, and can bring clarity to chaos.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]
