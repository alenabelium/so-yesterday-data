## Key arguments

### Performance-based paid UGC is the growth engine
- **Timestamp**: [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=681)
- **Summary**: Hector explains how he built a paid UGC program where creators earn a percentage of ad spend (or GMV for e-commerce), not a flat fee. This aligns incentives, attracts top talent, and scales output—now running 500-600 ads monthly with creators earning up to $13k/month.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Hire young, hungry talent over credentials
- **Timestamp**: [20:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=1237)
- **Summary**: Hector argues that traditional hiring signals (grades, elite schools) fail to identify modern marketers. Instead, look for young people who have built a following (even 10k), are hardworking, and fit the culture. Give them freedom to experiment and fail—they'll become your best A-players.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Avoid AI for final creative output
- **Timestamp**: [01:04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=3883)
- **Summary**: Hector warns against using AI for the final creative product—ads, videos, design—because it looks inauthentic and backfires. Instead, use AI for efficiency behind the scenes, but keep a human at the endpoint to ensure quality and trust.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### Build a community culture with creators
- **Timestamp**: [33:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=2001)
- **Summary**: Hector emphasizes treating creators as partners, not transactions. Weekly meetings, retreats, and a referral program build loyalty. This leads to better content, lower churn, and a competitive edge as creators spread the word about your brand.

### Founder-led content is a long-term growth lever
- **Timestamp**: [01:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=4085)
- **Summary**: Hector advocates for founder-led content as a way to humanize the brand and build trust. By documenting the journey toward a mission, you attract superfans and lower paid acquisition costs over time. It's a long-term investment that compounds.
