## Key arguments

### Underestimating Business Viability
- **Timestamp**: [05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=300)
- **Summary**: Cagan admits he underestimated business viability, which includes sales affordability, legal compliance, and ethics. He notes that while his engineering background helped in developer tools, modern AI products require holistic systems thinking for viability, a blind spot in his early work.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Over-indexing on Problem Identification
- **Timestamp**: [08:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=502)
- **Summary**: He regrets emphasizing problem identification too much, arguing that teams often get stuck there. He believes the real value lies in finding solutions and understanding why customers don't use a product, rather than just validating the problem exists.

### The Danger of Predictability Artifacts
- **Timestamp**: [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=759)
- **Summary**: Cagan identifies the desire for predictability as a major regret, criticizing roadmaps and PRDs when used to assert certainty. He argues these artifacts become bottlenecks if they replace evidence-based learning, leading to wasted engineering capacity.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Neglecting Product Leadership & Politics
- **Timestamp**: [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=963)
- **Summary**: He acknowledges that focusing solely on team craft ignored the critical role of product leadership and corporate governance. He warns that empowered teams still need strong leadership to navigate politics and strategy, which he initially overlooked.

### Process as a Substitute for Thinking
- **Timestamp**: [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=1056)
- **Summary**: Cagan regrets structuring his work around process, noting that many companies use frameworks and AI tools to avoid deep thinking. He advocates for 'creating to learn' rather than just delivering features, emphasizing mindset over methodology.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]
