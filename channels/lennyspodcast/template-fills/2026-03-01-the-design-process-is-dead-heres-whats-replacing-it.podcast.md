## Key arguments

### The Death of the Traditional Design Process
- **Timestamp**: [05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo&t=350)
- **Summary**: The traditional research-diverge-converge design process is dead, forced by engineering speed. Design work stratifies into supporting fast execution with AI agents and creating short-horizon (3-6 month) visions instead of multi-year decks.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0, 1]

### Stratification of Design Work and Time Allocation
- **Timestamp**: [06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo&t=408)
- **Summary**: Design time shifts from 60-70% mocking to 30-40%, with the rest split between pairing with engineers and writing code. Designers must support implementation and create prototypes that point people in the right direction rather than blocking execution.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### Human Judgment and Accountability in AI
- **Timestamp**: [28:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo&t=1710)
- **Summary**: While AI improves at taste and judgment, humans remain accountable for decisions. The hardest part of building software is resolving disputes and deciding what matters, which AI cannot fully solve yet.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### New Archetypes for Hiring Designers
- **Timestamp**: [46:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo&t=2786)
- **Summary**: Hiring focuses on strong generalists (block-shaped), deep specialists (deep T-shaped), and craft new grads. Resilience and the ability to adapt to rapid change are critical for success in the new design landscape.

### Management and the Legibility Framework
- **Timestamp**: [55:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo&t=3349)
- **Summary**: Managers should embrace low-leverage tasks to build trust and use the Legibility Framework to spot illegible ideas with high energy. Psychological safety allows for high standards and direct feedback without fear.
