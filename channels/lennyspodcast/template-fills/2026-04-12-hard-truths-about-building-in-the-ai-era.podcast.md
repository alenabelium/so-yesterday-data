## Key arguments

### PM role is obsolete; CEO-like skill is key
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCd9ykretlg&t=0)
- **Summary**: Rabois argues that the traditional PM role makes no sense in the future because AI capabilities change so fast that year-long roadmaps are incoherent. The skill is more like being a CEO: noticing what just became possible and exploiting it within days.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### CMOs are top token consumers
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCd9ykretlg&t=0)
- **Summary**: In the best organizations, the CMO is the number one consumer of AI tokens. Intellectually curious business leaders use AI to bypass layers of deputies and ship work product directly, future-proofing their careers.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Avoid talking to customers
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCd9ykretlg&t=0)
- **Summary**: Rabois advises against talking to customers for consumer and SMB products because customers don't know what they want and often give misleading information. Instead, rely on instincts and foundational insights, as with DoorDash and Airbnb.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [2]

### Criticize in public, not private
- **Timestamp**: [00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xCd9ykretlg&t=0)
- **Summary**: Rabois advocates for criticizing people in public to optimize for the system, not the individual. It signals to the team that issues are being addressed and encourages collaboration. High-performance machines are about winning, not psychological safety.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [3]
