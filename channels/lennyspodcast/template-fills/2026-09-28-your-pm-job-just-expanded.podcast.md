## Key arguments

### PM roles expand via AI automation
- **Timestamp**: [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=150)
- **Summary**: AI tools automate routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities such as strategy, customer insight, and product-market fit rather than disappearing.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### PMs engage in code review and prototyping
- **Timestamp**: [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=386)
- **Summary**: Atlassian PMs now review PRs, build rapid prototypes using Loom and coding agents, and automate design-to-code fixes, significantly accelerating delivery cycles from months to weeks.

### Shift from rowing to steering depends on phase
- **Timestamp**: [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=628)
- **Summary**: PMs must decide whether to 'row' (code/prototype) or 'steer' (prioritize/unblock) based on the product phase; starting with coding can help understand obstacles before shifting to management.

### AI Freedom Index guides skill development
- **Timestamp**: [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042)
- **Summary**: Atlassian uses the AI Freedom Index (levels 1-5) across six technical skills to help PMs develop fluency, supported by quarterly 'Builders Weeks' for hands-on training and workflow creation.

### Measure outcomes not input metrics
- **Timestamp**: [19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1188)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed features, throughput, and time-to-value rather than traditional input metrics like written PRs or manual task counts, ensuring teams work faster and smarter.
