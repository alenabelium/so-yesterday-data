## Key arguments

### PM Roles Expand via AI Automation
- **Timestamp**: [02:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=165)
- **Summary**: AI tools automate routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities such as strategy, customer insight, and acceleration. The role is not disappearing but expanding into technical fluency areas.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Case Study: Confluence Code Review
- **Timestamp**: [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=420)
- **Summary**: A PM at Atlassian reviewed 26 PRs in a month to unblock engineers, using AI to map Figma designs to code and automate fixes. This blurred role boundaries, accelerating feature delivery from six months to six weeks.

### Case Study: Robo Claw Steering
- **Timestamp**: [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=660)
- **Summary**: For a new product, the PM started by coding ('rowing') to understand obstacles but shifted to 'steering' (direction/prioritization) once engineers joined. Using AI agents for weekly updates allowed him to focus on unblocking the team.

### Case Study: Jira Prototyping & Triage
- **Timestamp**: [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=840)
- **Summary**: In a complex legacy codebase, PMs used Loom for prototyping and AI agents to triage 900+ customer reviews. This automated feedback loop increased throughput threefold, shipping 22 features in 10 weeks without PMs touching production code.

### Measuring Success via Outcomes
- **Timestamp**: [19:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1170)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed PRs and feature throughput rather than input metrics. Atlassian uses the 'AI Freedom Index' to guide skill development, expecting PMs to reach L3 fluency in areas like prototyping and automation.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]
