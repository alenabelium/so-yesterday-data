## Key arguments

### PM Roles Expand Through AI Automation
- **Timestamp**: [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=57)
- **Summary**: AI automates routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value strategy, customer insight, and product-market fit rather than disappearing.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Three Atlassian Case Studies of PM Evolution
- **Timestamp**: [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=334)
- **Summary**: PMs at Atlassian now review code (Confluence), build from scratch with vibes (Robo Claw), and triage feedback via agents (Jira), accelerating delivery cycles by 3x to 10x.

### Skill Frameworks and Training Are Critical
- **Timestamp**: [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042)
- **Summary**: Atlassian uses the AI Freedom Index (L1-L5) to guide PM skill development in prototyping, evals, and agent creation, supported by quarterly 'Builders Weeks' for hands-on learning.

### Measuring Outcomes Over Input Metrics
- **Timestamp**: [19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1188)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed PRs, feature throughput, and idea-to-delivery speed rather than traditional input metrics, requiring new OKRs focused on customer usage.
