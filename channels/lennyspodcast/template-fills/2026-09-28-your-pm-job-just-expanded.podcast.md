## Key arguments

### PM roles expand through AI automation
- **Timestamp**: [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=150)
- **Summary**: AI tools automate routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities such as strategy, customer insight, and product-market fit rather than disappearing.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### PMs adopt technical workflows for speed
- **Timestamp**: [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=386)
- **Summary**: Atlassian PMs now review code, run evaluations, and automate design fixes. One PM reviewed 26 PRs in a month to unblock engineers, accelerating feature delivery from six months to six weeks.

### Contextual intelligence drives acceleration
- **Timestamp**: [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=334)
- **Summary**: Speed comes from combining model intelligence with organizational context. PMs must decide when to 'row' (code/prototype) versus 'steer' (prioritize/unblock) based on product phase and team needs.

### Structured training via AI Freedom Index
- **Timestamp**: [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042)
- **Summary**: Atlassian uses the AI Freedom Index to map PM skills from curious to innovative. Quarterly 'Builders Weeks' train thousands of employees, resulting in over 120 new internal workflows.

### Outcome-based metrics replace input tracking
- **Timestamp**: [19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1188)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed features and throughput from idea to customer usage, not by traditional input metrics. PMs focus on unlocking engineering capacity through automated feedback triage.
