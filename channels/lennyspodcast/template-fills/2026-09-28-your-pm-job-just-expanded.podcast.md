## Key arguments

### PM roles expand via AI automation
- **Timestamp**: [02:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=150)
- **Summary**: The Product Manager role is expanding, not disappearing. AI tools automate routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities such as strategy, customer insight, and product-market fit.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Three Atlassian case studies
- **Timestamp**: [05:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=334)
- **Summary**: Atlassian demonstrates PM expansion through three examples: Confluence PMs reviewing code and automating design fixes; Robo Claw PMs shifting from coding to steering; and Jira PMs using AI for rapid prototyping and feedback triage.

### AI Freedom Index framework
- **Timestamp**: [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042)
- **Summary**: Atlassian introduced the 'AI Freedom Index' to guide PM skill development across six areas like tool usage and prototyping. The goal is for PMs to reach level 3 capability, with training provided through quarterly AI Developer Weeks.

### Measuring outcomes over inputs
- **Timestamp**: [19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1188)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by outcomes like deployed PRs, feature throughput, and time-to-delivery rather than traditional input metrics. Organizations must track both team and organizational speed to ensure AI adoption accelerates delivery.
