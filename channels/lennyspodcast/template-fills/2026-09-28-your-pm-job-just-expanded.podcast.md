## Key arguments

### PM Roles Expand via AI Automation
- **Timestamp**: [01:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=70)
- **Summary**: AI tools automate routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities such as strategy, customer insight, and accelerating delivery cycles rather than disappearing.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [0]

### Three Case Studies of PM Evolution
- **Timestamp**: [06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=400)
- **Summary**: Atlassian PMs now review code, perform evals, and automate design fixes. In one case, a PM reviewed 26 PRs in a month; in another, they prototyped from scratch before stepping back to steer the team.

### Structured Training via AI Freedom Index
- **Timestamp**: [17:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1060)
- **Summary**: Atlassian uses an 'AI Freedom Index' (levels 1-5) to guide PMs toward technical fluency and prototyping skills, supported by quarterly 'Builders Weeks' where over 1,200 people created new workflows.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### Measuring Outcomes Over Inputs
- **Timestamp**: [19:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1170)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed features and throughput (idea to delivery) rather than traditional input metrics, ensuring teams work faster individually and organizationally.
