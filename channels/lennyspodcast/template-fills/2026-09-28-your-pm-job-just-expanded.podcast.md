## Key arguments

### PM roles expand via AI automation
- **Timestamp**: [00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=57)
- **Summary**: The PM role is expanding, not disappearing. AI automates routine tasks like note-taking and status updates, allowing PMs to focus on high-value activities like strategy, customer insight, and accelerating delivery cycles.
- **Anchor Quotes**: [1]

### PMs engage in code review and prototyping
- **Timestamp**: [06:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=386)
- **Summary**: Atlassian PMs now review code, perform evaluations, and automate design fixes. One PM reviewed 26 PRs in a month to unblock engineers, while another used Figma MCP to fix 14 errors per hour, drastically reducing iteration time.

### Shift from rowing to steering based on phase
- **Timestamp**: [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=628)
- **Summary**: PMs must know when to 'row' (code/prototype) and when to 'steer' (prioritize/unblock). In new products, PMs start by coding to understand obstacles, then shift to management as the team scales, using AI agents for administrative tasks.

### AI Freedom Index guides skill development
- **Timestamp**: [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042)
- **Summary**: Atlassian uses the 'AI Freedom Index' (levels 1-5) to help PMs develop technical fluency in areas like prototyping, evals, and agent creation. Quarterly 'Builders Weeks' provide structured training and workflow creation.

### Measure outcomes, not input metrics
- **Timestamp**: [19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1188)
- **Summary**: Success is measured by deployed features, throughput, and time-to-delivery rather than traditional input metrics. Organizations must track both team-level and organizational-level speed to ensure AI integration drives real value.
