# What it actually takes to build frontier models 

## Executive Summary

Tulsi, a product manager at Google DeepMind, discusses the unpredictable nature of frontier model development, comparing it to a Potions lesson where magic emerges from the process. She emphasizes the importance of product managers who understand the 'vibe' of models, the 60-20-20 rule for building ahead of model capabilities, and the need for adaptive planning. The conversation also covers Google's strategy across the full stack, AI safety through monitoring and collaboration, and the evolving role of product managers in an AI-driven world.

## Key Points

- Model development is unpredictable; product managers must observe outcomes and find where models excel, turning surprises into user value. [00:03:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=195)
- Google aims to lead in frontier models while covering the full stack from hardware to end-user products, serving billions of users. [00:07:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=454)
- AI safety is built into the process through monitoring and collaboration with cybersecurity experts, giving confidence in deployment. [00:10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=636)
- The 60-20-20 rule suggests building features that work now while anticipating future model improvements to create magical experiences. [00:33:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=1993)
- Product managers must understand the 'vibe' of models, be adaptable, and focus on evaluation and user feedback to thrive. [00:21:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGf2Yvan4Wg&t=1310)
