# Why AI feels anti-social 

## Executive Summary

Mark Pincus introduces the 'Proven, Better, New' framework, arguing that successful product innovation begins by mastering existing best practices ('Proven') before attempting to improve them ('Better') and finally adding novel elements ('New'). He contends that founders must overcome the 'moral arbitrage' of copying by defining ambition through consumer value rather than peer approval, often starting with humble, unambitious ideas to achieve product-market fit. Pincus also emphasizes the critical need to 'kill hope' by treating products as testable hypotheses rather than emotional bets, ensuring that teams fail fast and learn quickly.

## Key Points

- The 'Proven, Better, New' framework dictates that innovators should first master existing proven patterns, then make small, statistically validated improvements, and only then add a novel feature that is likely to fail but worth testing [00:06:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eh9C3TUotc&t=393).
- Founders should define their ambition in the eyes of their consumers, not their peers, accepting that starting with a humble, 'unambitious' idea is often the most strategic path to building a massive company [00:16:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eh9C3TUotc&t=965).
- To avoid wasting resources on bad ideas, leaders must distinguish between belief and hope, using AI as a 'failure machine' to test low-fidelity versions of products rapidly rather than building perfect MVPs [00:34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eh9C3TUotc&t=2049).
- Future social innovation lies in creating a 'rowdy cocktail party' via AI agents that broker relationships and provide productivity, moving beyond the current passive consumption of existing platforms [00:55:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eh9C3TUotc&t=3306).
- Effective leadership requires staying 'close to the metal' by micromanaging critical product details and empowering team members as CEOs of their specific domains to maintain intellectual honesty and speed [00:19:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eh9C3TUotc&t=1170).
