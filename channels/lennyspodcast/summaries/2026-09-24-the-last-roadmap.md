# The last roadmap 

## Executive Summary

The speaker argues that traditional product roadmaps are obsolete because AI has eliminated engineering scarcity, turning execution from a constraint into an infinite capability. This shift creates three dangerous traps: the lag trap of clearing backlogs without impact, the parity trap of competitors building identical features, and the attrition trap of abandoning work too quickly. Instead of listing features and dates, product leaders must adopt a 'zero-based roadmap' focused on testing strong beliefs through rapid experimentation and real customer data. The future of product strategy lies in ambition and conviction rather than speed or volume of output.

## Key Points

- AI has removed the bottleneck of engineering capacity, meaning teams can now build anything they want, which shifts the primary challenge from execution to finding meaningful ideas worth building [00:04:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VM5kuvWgwDY&t=264).
- The combination of limitless AI execution and old-school roadmaps leads to three specific traps: clearing backlogs without business impact (lag), competitors copying each other's obvious features (parity), and abandoning projects before learning from them (attrition) [00:10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VM5kuvWgwDY&t=608).
- Product strategy must shift from feature lists to 'zero-based roadmaps' that prioritize proving strong beliefs through rapid, high-stakes experiments rather than just shipping code quickly [00:13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VM5kuvWgwDY&t=793).
- Leaders should measure success by the number of ambitious experiments run and the conviction behind them, rather than by velocity or feature count, accepting that most features may be disposable while core beliefs remain enduring [00:17:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VM5kuvWgwDY&t=1037).
