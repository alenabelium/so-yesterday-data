# Elena Verna: The case for becoming a HI-IC 

## Executive Summary

Elena Verna argues that the traditional correlation between leadership influence and team size is breaking down due to AI capabilities, making the 'High Impact Individual Contributor' (HI-IC) a viable and often superior career path. She shares her personal transition from managing hundreds of people to working as an individual specialist at Lovable, highlighting how AI tools allow single experts to execute end-to-end projects that previously required large teams. Verna contends that organizations must adapt their structures by decentralizing power, ensuring free information flow, and decoupling compensation from headcount to fully leverage this shift.

## Key Points

- The traditional career ladder equating influence with the number of subordinates is obsolete because AI allows a single person to execute complex tasks that previously required large teams, creating the 'High Impact IC' role. [00:03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fn8wnmpVqeI&t=221)
- For individual contributors to thrive, organizations must dismantle hierarchical barriers by ensuring free information flow, reducing approval layers, and trusting employees to make decisions and accept responsibility for mistakes. [00:12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fn8wnmpVqeI&t=765)
- Companies should decouple pay and status from management titles, offering specialist-level compensation to high-impact ICs to prevent the proliferation of ineffective managers and allow leaders to return to their core craft. [00:14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fn8wnmpVqeI&t=865)
