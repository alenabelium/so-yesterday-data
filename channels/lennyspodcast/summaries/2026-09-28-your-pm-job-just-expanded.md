# Your PM job just expanded 

## Executive Summary

The role of the Product Manager is expanding rather than disappearing due to the integration of AI tools, which automate routine tasks and allow PMs to focus on high-value activities like strategy and customer insight. Atlassian demonstrates this shift through three case studies where PMs engaged in code review, rapid prototyping, and automated feedback triage to accelerate delivery cycles significantly. To support this evolution, organizations must implement structured training programs and new skill frameworks, such as the AI Freedom Index, to help PMs develop necessary technical fluency. Ultimately, success depends on measuring outcomes like deployed features and throughput rather than traditional input metrics.

## Key Points

- PMs at Atlassian are taking on technical tasks like code review and automated testing, which reduced feature development time from six months to six weeks in the Confluence team [00:07:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=440).
- The appropriate level of PM involvement shifts between 'rowing' (coding/prototyping) and 'steering' (strategy/unblocking) depending on the project phase and product maturity [00:10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=628).
- Atlassian uses an 'AI Freedom Index' to assess PM proficiency across six key areas, guiding targeted training during quarterly AI Developer Weeks [00:17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=1042). [MM:SS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BtK4kFI1LNo&t=SECONDS)
