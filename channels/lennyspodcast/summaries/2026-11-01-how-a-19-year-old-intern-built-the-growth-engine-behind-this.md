# How a 19-year-old intern built the growth engine behind this $2B AI company 

## Executive Summary

Hector Serrano, a 19-year-old intern at WhisperFlow, explains how he built the company's growth engine through a performance-based paid UGC program. He emphasizes that a great product is the foundation, then details how to recruit, retain, and motivate creators by giving them creative freedom and a share of ad spend. He argues that young, hungry talent is undervalued and that founders should trust them to navigate new channels. He also advises against using AI for final creative output, advocating for quality and authenticity over automation.

## Key Points

- Product-market fit is the foundation; paid advertising should be activated early to accelerate learning and validate the product. [00:04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=289)
- Performance-based UGC pays creators a percentage of ad spend (SaaS) or GMV (e-commerce), with a Smart Retainer to support new creators. [00:13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=838)
- Recruit creators manually via Instagram DMs, start with 5-6, build a strong community culture, and avoid over-briefing to keep content authentic and diverse. [00:32:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=1931)
- Hire young, hungry talent who have built a small following (10k+ on Instagram) and give them time to experiment and fail. [00:22:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=1323)
- Avoid using AI for final creative output; focus on quality and authenticity to stand out in a noisy market. [01:04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KzS5Qs7vSMg&t=3883)
