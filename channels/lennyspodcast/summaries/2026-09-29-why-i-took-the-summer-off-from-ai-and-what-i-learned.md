# Why I took the summer off from AI (and what I learned) 

## Executive Summary

The speaker argues that while AI tools are rapidly improving, organizations risk losing critical learning and intuition if they focus solely on automation and efficiency. By taking a break from AI news, the author realized that creating great products depends more on team context and customer understanding than on the speed of execution. The core thesis is that 'context becomes the product,' meaning companies should use AI to automate routine tasks so teams can dedicate more time to deep customer engagement and collective learning.

## Key Points

- Automating repetitive work with AI frees up time for engineers to engage directly with customers, which builds the intuition necessary for good product decisions rather than just increasing output speed. [00:06:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zn9NZ-r1-C4&t=382)
- To prevent teams from losing their edge through isolation, organizations must implement practices like 'quality environments' and regular critique sessions to ensure everyone learns from shared context and collective judgment. [00:10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zn9NZ-r1-C4&t=651)
- The ultimate goal for product organizations should be managing and storing institutional knowledge, as 'context becomes the product' and hiring people with good taste is more valuable than simply increasing productivity metrics. [00:15:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zn9NZ-r1-C4&t=923)
