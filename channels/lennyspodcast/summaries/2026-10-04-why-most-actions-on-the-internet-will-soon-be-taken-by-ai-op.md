# Why most actions on the internet will soon be taken by AI (OpenAI’s head of ChatGPT, Tibo Sottiaux)

## Executive Summary

OpenAI's head of ChatGPT, Tibo Sottiaux, discusses the near-future where most internet actions are performed by AI agents, emphasizing the need for products built for scale and agent traffic. He highlights the shift from manual coding to orchestrating agent teams, the convergence of ChatGPT and Codex into a unified 'Dots' assistant, and the importance of an open plugin ecosystem. Sottiaux also addresses safety investments, the evolving value of human taste and creativity, and the changing skill set for engineers and founders.

## Key Points

- Most actions on the internet will soon be performed by agents, so products must be built with scale in mind to succeed in that future. [00:31:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=1895)
- The future of AI interaction is a unified 'Dots' assistant that learns from you, works 24/7, and is accessible across any device—eliminating the need to choose models or manage complex setups. [00:03:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=236)
- OpenAI is committed to an open ecosystem with plugins and revenue sharing, where quality and retention determine visibility, not SEO. [00:13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=831)
- Safety is a top priority: OpenAI invests heavily in monitoring and security stacks, and is proud of not releasing models that don't meet their standards. [00:34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=2070)
- The most valuable skills are shifting from technical execution to taste, user empathy, and the ability to leverage AI—making founders and young talent particularly well-suited for the future. [00:23:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MM-C3JqCXBk&t=1397)
