# How to grow a career during the AI transition 

## Executive Summary

Molly Graham re-evaluates her famous 'give away your Legos' career advice for the AI era, arguing that while delegation remains crucial, humans must retain control over tasks requiring vision, taste, and trust. She highlights the emotional toll of rapid technological change, urging professionals to process their grief rather than succumb to fear-based narratives about job displacement. The core thesis is that the future belongs to those who view AI as a manageable intern and actively shape their evolving roles instead of resisting change.

## Key Points

- The 'give away your Legos' advice remains valid for growth, but in the AI era, you must distinguish between delegating execution and retaining strategic vision, taste, and trust. [01:00:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-96FyJFiCA&t=3631)
- Professionals are experiencing significant grief and burnout as their roles transform; leaders must normalize these emotions rather than promoting a toxic narrative of fear or immediate obsolescence. [00:27:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-96FyJFiCA&t=1630)
- Treat AI not as a super-intelligent replacement but as a 'lazy intern' that requires human coaching, context, and rigorous quality control to prevent the spread of low-value output. [00:35:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-96FyJFiCA&t=2143)
