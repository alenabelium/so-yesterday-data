# Marty Cagan on everything he got wrong about product management

## Executive Summary

Marty Cagan reflects on ten significant regrets from his decades-long career in product management, emphasizing that traditional frameworks often fail to address modern business complexities. He argues that product managers must prioritize business viability and holistic systems thinking over mere feature delivery or engineering-centric metrics. Furthermore, he warns against the desire for predictability through rigid roadmaps and PRDs, advocating instead for a mindset of continuous learning and strategic leadership.

## Key Points

- Cagan admits to underestimating business viability, noting that product managers must ensure solutions are legally compliant, secure, and profitable, not just usable or feasible [00:06:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=366).
- He critiques the industry's obsession with predictability via roadmaps and PRDs, arguing these artifacts often mask arrogance and hinder innovation by prioritizing schedule over actual results [00:12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=771).
- Cagan emphasizes that product leadership and corporate governance are critical missing pieces in traditional advice, as empowered teams require strong strategic context to navigate competitive markets effectively [00:17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fF3lkTCM5-c&t=1056).
