# Anthropic’s first technical PM on token maxing, the jagged edge, and living in the future

## Executive Summary

Diane Penn, Anthropic’s Head of Product for AI Research and Labs, discusses the evolution of product management in the age of frontier AI, emphasizing that 'evals are the new PRDs' for defining user value. She highlights the critical importance of hands-on experimentation, or 'token maxing,' to uncover emerging capabilities and drive innovation before these features become standard. Penn also outlines the shift in hiring and leadership, arguing that effective PMs and managers must remain deeply technical and user-centric to navigate the rapid, non-deterministic nature of AI development.

## Key Points

- Anthropic's product strategy relies on 'evals' as the primary artifact for defining user needs and measuring model improvements, replacing traditional PRDs for technical tasks. [00:01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tivaWTTVRhY&t=96)
- Product leaders must engage in 'token maxing'—spending significant resources on experimentation—to discover new use cases and maintain a competitive edge as AI capabilities accelerate exponentially. [00:01:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tivaWTTVRhY&t=96)
- Successful AI product management requires a hands-on approach where even senior managers spend time building and shipping with models to maintain intuition and empathy for user pain points. [00:51:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tivaWTTVRhY&t=3062)
- Anthropic’s Labs team fosters innovation by pursuing 'discontinuous bets' with small, autonomous pods, allowing for rapid prototyping and learning from failures without slowing down the core product. [00:25:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tivaWTTVRhY&t=1506)
- Human judgment, persistence, and the ability to maintain a distinct personal voice are becoming increasingly valuable traits as AI models improve at execution but struggle with nuanced decision-making and authentic expression. [01:12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tivaWTTVRhY&t=4360)
