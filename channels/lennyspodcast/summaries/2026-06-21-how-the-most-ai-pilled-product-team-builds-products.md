# How the most AI-pilled product team builds products 

## Executive Summary

Fiona Fung, leader of the Claude Code and Co-Work teams at Anthropic, details how AI has transformed software engineering by increasing code output eightfold and shifting the bottleneck from coding to verification and product sense. She emphasizes that the most successful teams balance high agency with high accountability, utilizing AI agents for asynchronous workflows and proactive quality monitoring rather than relying solely on traditional metrics. Fung also highlights the importance of maintaining team culture, fostering a growth mindset to overcome fear of change, and ensuring leaders remain deeply connected to the product through constant dogfooding.

## Key Points

- Anthropic engineers now ship eight times more code per quarter, shifting the primary challenge from writing code to verifying quality and managing the resulting high throughput [00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ybrl4FYM57c&t=0).
- To manage increased velocity, teams use AI routines for asynchronous work and define 'bad' versus 'sad' experience thresholds to proactively monitor product health rather than relying on lagging metrics [00:36:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ybrl4FYM57c&t=2169).
- The ideal engineering profile now consists of creative builders with strong product sense and deep systems experts for complex verification, requiring a balance of high agency and high accountability [00:17:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ybrl4FYM57c&t=1057).
- Leaders must maintain a growth mindset and engage in constant dogfooding to stay connected to the product reality, avoiding the trap of managing solely through dashboards [00:20:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ybrl4FYM57c&t=1204).
- As coding becomes automated, the role of the engineer is blurring with product management, necessitating new approaches to hiring, onboarding, and maintaining team culture during rapid scaling [01:00:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ybrl4FYM57c&t=3623).
