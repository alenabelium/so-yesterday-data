# The rise of persistent AI coworkers 

## Executive Summary

Tara Sash, OpenAI’s product lead for Codex and ChatGPT Work, outlines the evolution of AI from simple chat interfaces to persistent, collaborative coworkers capable of executing complex knowledge work. She argues that product managers must shift from theoretical planning to empirical testing, focusing on rapid iteration and elevating team ambition rather than getting bogged down in perfect documentation. Sash emphasizes that while AI handles execution, human value lies in strategic steering, artistic opinionation, and maintaining accountability for outcomes.

## Key Points

- The future of work involves 'steering' agents rather than 'rowing,' where humans define high-level goals and intuition while AI handles the tactical execution. [00:11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMvBMfj4cSQ&t=682)
- Product managers should abandon long theoretical documents in favor of building prototypes and testing hypotheses quickly, as the market moves too fast for rigid planning. [00:07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMvBMfj4cSQ&t=468)
- Human creativity and 'opinionation' remain critical differentiators in AI products, as software is an artistic expression rather than just a functional utility. [00:13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMvBMfj4cSQ&t=793)
- OpenAI’s strategy involves merging ChatGPT and Codex into a unified interface where users don't need to choose between modes, focusing instead on the underlying agent capabilities. [00:30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMvBMfj4cSQ&t=1815)
- Effective AI usage requires 'writing as thinking' manually to preserve cognitive sharpness, while automating only the 'writing as reporting' aspects like summarization. [00:53:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMvBMfj4cSQ&t=3222)
