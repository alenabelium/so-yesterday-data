# How to scale intent, quality, and artistry with Al 

## Executive Summary

The speaker warns against replicating the post-WWII architectural boom's pitfalls in the current AI development era, where speed often sacrifices intentionality and context. To avoid creating generic 'zombie UI,' builders must establish a strong brand point of view and encode those standards directly into their AI systems rather than relying on vague prompts. The talk emphasizes that while AI accelerates production, human editors and designers must rigorously refine outputs to ensure quality, soul, and genuine user care.

## Key Points

- AI models naturally default to generic patterns based on popularity, risking the creation of 'zombie UI' that lacks specific brand character or user context [00:01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLvFTMtw4Jk&t=80).
- Design standards must be encoded into machine-readable systems like CLIs and design tokens to ensure consistency when building is distributed and agentic [00:10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLvFTMtw4Jk&t=638).
- Builders must refuse to confuse 'done' with 'good,' adopting the role of an editor who scrutinizes AI outputs for coherence, problem-solving, and unexpected details [00:12:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLvFTMtw4Jk&t=745).
- AI should be used as a creative catalyst to amplify human artistry rather than just speeding up existing processes, requiring specific inputs and rigorous stress-testing of outputs [00:16:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLvFTMtw4Jk&t=978).
