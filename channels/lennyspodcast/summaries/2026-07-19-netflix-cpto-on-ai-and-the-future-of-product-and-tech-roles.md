# Netflix CPTO on AI and the future of product and tech roles 

## Executive Summary

Netflix CPTO Elizabeth Stone argues that while AI blurs functional lines by enabling product managers and designers to prototype code, deep craft excellence in engineering, data science, and design remains scarce and essential. She emphasizes that the future of tech roles requires a shift toward systems thinking and AI fluency, where specialists must broaden their scope to understand broader business contexts and infrastructure. Stone also highlights Netflix's enduring culture of 'excellence as an operating system,' which relies on high talent density, autonomy, and a resistance to excessive process to maintain innovation.

## Key Points

- AI allows non-engineers to prototype and analyze data faster, but functional expertise in engineering, data science, and design remains critical for quality and scaling [00:05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY&t=311).
- Netflix is prioritizing hiring for 'systems thinkers' who can abstract across business domains to build robust infrastructure and design systems for an AI-driven future [00:14:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY&t=893).
- The company maintains 'excellence as an operating system' through high talent density, risk-taking, and a deliberate resistance to adding process when things get difficult [00:41:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY&t=2511).
- Junior talent remains vital for their native fluency in new tools and consumer trends, but mastery of craft and understanding of system mechanics are still required to ensure quality and fixability [00:54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY&t=3295).
- Netflix's culture of high agency and autonomy mirrors the operating principles of top AI labs, focusing on excellence rather than just process or control [00:39:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t0GiTyz4syY&t=2382).
