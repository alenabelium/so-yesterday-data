# How Many Narrow AIs Could Behave Like One Superintelligence - Daniel Kokotajlo and Thomas Larsen

## Executive Summary

Daniel Kokotajlo and Thomas Larsen discuss their 'Plan A: 2040' scenario, which proposes a managed path to superintelligence through international cooperation, transparency, and control measures. They argue that AI will soon become 'people in the cloud'—human-level agents that can replace most human labor—and that this transition requires careful governance to avoid risks like loss of control, power concentration, and conflict. The conversation explores the feasibility of AI achieving general intelligence, the role of reinforcement learning, and the importance of distinguishing between alignment and control. They emphasize that while AI may initially be narrow, scaling and unification will likely lead to a single superintelligent system, and they advocate for a pause to build verification infrastructure before proceeding.

## Key Points

- The 'Plan A: 2040' scenario proposes a 6-12 month pause to build transparency and verification infrastructure, then a controlled development toward human-level AI, with a second pause at that level to ensure control. [00:58:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5Xix4h5UlU&t=3487)
- AI will likely become 'people in the cloud'—human-level agents that can replace most human labor—and even without superintelligence, exponential growth of such systems would radically transform the world. [00:35:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5Xix4h5UlU&t=2149)
- The main risks identified are loss of control, concentration of power, war, job displacement, and abuse; Plan A aims to address all five through transparency, international agreements, and control mechanisms. [00:57:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5Xix4h5UlU&t=3426)
- Transparency is crucial: making AI training and deployment visible to the public would reduce power concentration and enable international verification, even though it would hurt companies' competitive advantages. [01:07:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5Xix4h5UlU&t=4031)
- The key disagreement with skeptics is whether AI will soon achieve 100% performance on important tasks, which would make it qualitatively different from ordinary technology like electricity or the internet. [01:27:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5Xix4h5UlU&t=5237)
