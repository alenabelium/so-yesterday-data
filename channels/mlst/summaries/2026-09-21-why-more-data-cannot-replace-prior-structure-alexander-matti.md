# Why More Data Cannot Replace Prior Structure - Alexander Mattick

## Executive Summary

Alexander Mattick argues that while more data can improve models, it cannot replace the prior structure and constraints that make learning efficient and safe. He critiques energy-based models as outdated, favoring diffusion and flow matching for generative tasks, and emphasizes the importance of explicit constraints in reinforcement learning over reward engineering. The conversation covers the history of inference methods, theories of deep learning, and the practical value of constrained RL.

## Key Points

- Energy-based models are not useful anymore for most problems; diffusion and flow matching are superior for generative tasks. [00:40:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1S1B4XkFCD8&t=2403)
- Flow matching offers the flexibility of normalizing flows with the sampling efficiency of diffusion models by fixing a reference path. [01:01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1S1B4XkFCD8&t=3690)
- Constraints are essential in reinforcement learning; explicit constraints are more composable and safer than reward shaping. [01:48:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1S1B4XkFCD8&t=6496)
