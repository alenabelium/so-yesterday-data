# The Dangerous Illusion of AI Coding? - Jeremy Howard

## Executive Summary

Jeremy Howard critiques the hype around AI coding, arguing that while LLMs can generate code, they lack true understanding and are poor at software engineering, which requires deep intuition and interaction. He warns that over-reliance on AI tools like Claude Code can degrade human skills and organizational knowledge, comparing it to a slot machine that creates an illusion of control. He advocates for rich, interactive environments like notebooks that foster understanding and collaboration between humans and AI, and warns against centralizing AI power.

## Key Points

- AI coding tools create an illusion of control and can degrade human competence, as people become less engaged and lose understanding. [00:34:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHBEQ-Ryo24&t=2096)
- LLMs are not creative beyond their training data; they interpolate between existing examples, which limits their ability to handle novel engineering problems. [00:19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHBEQ-Ryo24&t=1165)
- Software engineering is more than writing code; it requires deep understanding and interaction, which is why rich environments like notebooks are superior to linear terminal interfaces for both humans and AI. [01:03:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHBEQ-Ryo24&t=3796)
