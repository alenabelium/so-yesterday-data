# Watching America Run Away With AI - Alistair Pullen (Cosine AI)

## Executive Summary

Alistair Pullen, co-founder of Cosine AI, discusses the UK's sovereign AI initiative and his company's mandate to build a sovereign LLM with limited compute compared to US labs. He explains how Cosine leverages its licensing model, architectural choices, and data strategies to compete, emphasizing the importance of active parameters, post-training data, and reinforcement learning with credit attribution. Pullen also covers agent engineering, the diminishing importance of agent harnesses, the challenges of memory and code review, and the role of synthetic data generation in training models for diverse tasks.

## Key Points

- Cosine AI received a mandate to build the UK's first sovereign LLM, supported by government compute on the Isambard supercomputer, which unlocked their ambition despite limited resources compared to US labs. [00:02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTHmrELSfvk&t=120)
- To compete with US labs, Cosine focuses on architectural choices like total and active parameters, and emphasizes the importance of post-training data and RL at scale, which are key differentiators. [00:08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTHmrELSfvk&t=500)
- Cosine addresses the 'spaghetti monster' problem in AI-generated code by using RL rewards that penalize inefficiency and by implementing credit attribution within trajectories to improve learning. [00:22:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTHmrELSfvk&t=1334)
- Agent harnesses become less important over time as models improve, but Cosine's 'swarm' approach uses many sub-agents to tackle complex tasks, though it requires careful orchestration and intervention mechanisms. [00:38:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTHmrELSfvk&t=2290)
- Cosine uses synthetic data generation to create RL training data for a wide range of tasks, including non-coding domains, by synthesizing evaluators for real-world problems rather than inventing fictional ones. [00:48:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTHmrELSfvk&t=2924)
