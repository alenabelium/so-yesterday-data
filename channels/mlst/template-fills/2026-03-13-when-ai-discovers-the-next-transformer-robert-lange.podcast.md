### Co-evolve problems and solutions
- **Timestamp**: [08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EInEmGaMRLc&t=480)
- **Summary**: Innovation often requires inventing a different problem first. Current systems take the problem as fixed, but co-evolving problems and solutions, as in POET, could unlock open-ended discovery.
