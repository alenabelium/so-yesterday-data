Applied alignment — distinct from the research program the corpus tracks as [alignment](/knowledge/alignment) — is the engineering of intended behavior in deployed systems: objective specification that resists Goodharting, human oversight loops ([human-in-the-loop](/knowledge/human-in-the-tool)) patterns), permission architectures, and evaluation suites that catch specification gaming before users do.

The corpus's practical canon reads like this platform's own governance: agents propose, humans dispose; every action audit-logged; blast radius scoped; evals treated as the operational definition of "aligned enough to ship" (see [evals](/knowledge/evals), [governance](/knowledge/ai-governance)). The honest limitation it keeps in view: applied controls bound what deployed systems can do, but don't resolve the deeper research question of whether capable optimizers stay corrigible — the two layers are complements, not substitutes.
