Open source in AI spans two related but distinct layers: open-source *software* (training and inference code, agent frameworks, tooling) and open *weights* (models whose parameters can be downloaded and run locally — see [open-weight models](/knowledge/open-weight-models)). The corpus treats open source as an economic and strategic position, not just a licensing detail: it is the foundation of data sovereignty, local inference, and cost control, and the reason a small team can operate a production AI pipeline without renting a frontier API.

The strategic reading: open-source AI compresses the advantage of any capability that has already been published. When a technique lands in an open model, it commoditizes within months — one driver of the "so yesterday" dynamic this platform tracks. Ecosystem infrastructure like [Hugging Face](/knowledge/hugging-face) and permissively licensed model families (Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama lineage) make the open stack the default substrate for [local inference](/knowledge/local-inference) and for sovereign deployments in regulated sectors.
