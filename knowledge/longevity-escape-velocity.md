Longevity escape velocity (LEV) is the scenario where medical progress adds more than a year of expected life per year elapsed, making aging effectively a solved problem for those alive at the crossover. In the corpus it belongs to the Diamandis-style abundance wing — a useful marker of maximal techno-optimism, cited more as a mood indicator than a plannable event.

The strategic treatment is scenario-hedging, not forecasting: LEV probability is unknowable, but its enabling technologies (AI-accelerated drug discovery, health-data analytics) are investable on ordinary evidence regardless. The corpus's pragmatic-operator cluster treats longevity claims the way it treats [singularity](/knowledge/singularity) claims — as tail options, while building for the mundane continuation where healthcare productivity gains arrive on ordinary timelines.
