Claude is Anthropic's model family and assistant product. In this corpus Claude appears less as a chatbot and more as an *agentic work surface*: the reference workflows for spec-driven development, long-context codebase work, and tool-heavy automation are disproportionately Claude-shaped. [Claude Code](/knowledge/claude-code) — the terminal-based agent — is the corpus's running example of an AI-native development tool that edits files, runs commands, and manages local workflows with a human reviewing the diff.

Strategically, Claude anchors one pole of the frontier-model duopoly narrative (with [OpenAI](/knowledge/openai)/GPT as the other), and its release cadence reliably resets expectations for agentic capability — see [Anthropic](/knowledge/anthropic). For practitioners the relevant question is rarely "which model is smartest" but which model-plus-tools loop fits their [specification quality](/knowledge/specification-quality) and review discipline; model advantage compounds only when the surrounding workflow captures it.
