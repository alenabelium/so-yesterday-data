Open-source models — in the corpus's loose usage, [open-weight](/knowledge/open-weight-models) families with permissive licenses — are the price floor of the industry: whatever capability a frontier lab shipped N quarters ago arrives as a freely hostable model, compressing margins on everything below it. The recurring families (Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama lineage, Mistral) anchor local deployment, sovereign stacks and the cheap tier of routing architectures.

The strategic pattern the corpus documents: open models are the default substrate for [local inference](/knowledge/local-inference) and cost-sensitive pipelines; the frontier retains a temporary premium on peak capability and novel modes; and the gap's steady shrink is the empirical basis of the "so yesterday" clock. Licensing diligence remains the hidden cost — "open" labels range from Apache-2.0 to research-only terms (see [open source AI](/knowledge/open-source-ai)).
