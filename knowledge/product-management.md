Product management is the profession the corpus documents restructuring in real time. The settled direction: the role expands rather than disappears — routine specification-to-ticket work is automated ([disposable software](/knowledge/disposable-software), [the 70% problem](/knowledge/the-70-percent-problem)), while the durable core becomes strategic coordination over agent-centric architecture: deciding what to build, encoding intent as specifications good enough for AI implementation ([specification quality](/knowledge/specification-quality)), and supplying the organizational authority and user empathy agents lack.

The corpus's markers of the shift: PMs prototyping in days, engaging directly with generated code, and the flattening of execution-focused teams around [HI-IC](/knowledge/career)-style leverage. The open question it tracks honestly: whether the title survives as a distinct function or dissolves into a general judgment-layer skill set (see [three layers of work](/knowledge/three-layers-of-work)).
