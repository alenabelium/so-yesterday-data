Enterprise AI is the corpus's name for the adoption phase most organizations are actually in: past the demo, short of the transformation. The defining datum of the period is the ROI gap — large majorities of generative-AI programs report no measurable P&L impact, and analysts attribute the failures to leadership, process and engineering discipline rather than model capability. The pattern that does work is consistent: pick workflows with measurable throughput, rebuild the workflow rather than bolting AI onto it, staff the human-validation layer, and instrument outcomes from day one.

The tactical stack is settling: [RAG](/knowledge/rag) over internal corpora, agent workflows over structured systems of record, model routing that reserves frontier capability for hard steps, and a growing [governance](/knowledge/ai-governance) layer. The strategic framing the corpus returns to is [AI-native economics](/knowledge/ai-native-economics): the competitive metric shifts from cost reduction to revenue per employee, which makes role redesign ([three layers of work](/knowledge/three-layers-of-work)) as important as tooling.
