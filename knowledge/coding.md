Coding with AI spans a maturity ladder the corpus maps explicitly: prompt-level assistance, IDE-integrated generation, [vibe coding](/knowledge/vibe-coding) for prototypes, and full [agentic coding](/knowledge/agentic-coding) where agents iterate against specifications and tests. Each rung shifts the human's job — from typing to reviewing to specifying (see [specification quality](/knowledge/specification-quality)).

The settled findings: agents reliably deliver ~70% of well-specified work ([the 70% problem](/knowledge/the-70-percent-problem)); the failure modes concentrate in unreviewed accumulation ([dark code](/knowledge/dark-code)) and unbounded permissions ([blast radius](/knowledge/blast-radius)); and the countermeasures are evals, scoped environments, and regeneration-from-spec as the recoverability test. Teams that treat the agent as a tireless junior engineer ship weekly; teams that treat it as an oracle stall.
